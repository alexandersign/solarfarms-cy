## 4. Probation

The first **six (6) months** of employment constitute a probation period. Either party may terminate during probation with **one (1) week** written notice.

## 5. Duties and Responsibilities

The Employee shall perform all duties reasonably associated with the role, including but not limited to:

**Site Installation Works**
- On-site supervision and execution of BESS and PV civil installation works.
- Management and coordination of installation teams and subcontractors on site.
- Completion of site inspection checklists, torque registers, and quality documentation.

**Operations & Maintenance**
- Preventive and corrective maintenance visits for PV systems.
- O&M call response for PV installations — fault diagnosis and small repairs.
- Service report completion and documentation.

**Coordination & Support**
- Coordination with engineering and back office on project progress and material requirements.
- Warehouse support — loading, receiving, and inventory verification of equipment.

**Health & Safety**
- Adherence to all health and safety protocols on site.
- Compliance with OEM installation and maintenance procedures.

**General**
- Any other reasonable duties related to field operations, installations, and service.

The Employee shall devote full working time, attention, and professional skill to the business of the Employer and act at all times in the Employer's best interests.

## 6. Working Hours

Normal working hours are **forty (40) hours per week**, Monday to Friday, with flexibility depending on operational requirements. Site work may require early starts, extended hours, or weekend attendance. Reasonable additional hours may be required without additional remuneration.

---

## 7. Place of Work

The Employee will work primarily on project sites across Cyprus. The Employee may also be required to attend the Employer's Limassol office or warehouse as operational needs dictate.

---

## 8. Salary and Compensation

The Employee shall receive a gross monthly salary of **EUR 1,400**, payable through payroll between the 1st and 5th day of each month following the worked month.

Salary is subject to statutory deductions for social insurance, income tax, and other applicable contributions.

The Employee is entitled to a thirteenth (13th) salary in accordance with Company practice and Cyprus norms, pro-rated where applicable.

## 9. Performance and KPIs

The Employee's performance is measured against the **Field Engineer & Service KPI Framework (LCY-KPI-002)**, attached as Annex A.

KPIs cover six categories:
- Installation Quality & Execution (30%)
- Schedule & Delivery (20%)
- Team & Subcontractor Management (15%)
- O&M & PV Service Calls (15%)
- Health, Safety & Compliance (10%)
- Documentation & Administration (10%)

KPIs are reviewed quarterly by the Director.

---

## 10. Discretionary Bonus

The Employer may award a discretionary quarterly performance bonus based on KPI scores as defined in the Field Engineer & Service KPI Framework (LCY-KPI-002).

| Overall KPI Rating | Annual Bonus Eligibility |
|--------------------|--------------------------|
| Excellent | Up to one month's salary |
| Good | Up to half month's salary |
| Needs Improvement | No bonus |
| Unsatisfactory | No bonus |

The bonus is discretionary and non-contractual. Payment in one period does not create entitlement for future periods. The bonus may be amended or withdrawn at any time.
