## 4. Services

The Subcontractor shall provide the following services:

**Documentation and Filing**
- Maintenance of complete project files including contracts, quotations, permits, and correspondence.
- Preparation of EAC submission packages and regulatory document support.
- Document version control and filing accuracy.

**CRM and Data Management**
- Data entry and maintenance of the Company CRM system.
- Pipeline status tracking and reporting.
- Lead and project record accuracy.

**Financial Administration**
- Preparation of client invoices at project milestones.
- Tracking of incoming and outgoing payments.
- Supplier invoice processing and expense tracking.

**Internal Coordination**
- Coordination between sales, engineering, and operations teams.
- Meeting support including agendas, minutes, and follow-up.
- Sales-to-engineering handoff package preparation.

**Regulatory and Compliance Support**
- Tracking of EAC, CERA, and permitting deadlines.
- Insurance certificate management and renewal tracking.

**Warehouse and Inventory Support**
- Inventory record maintenance.
- Delivery documentation and receipts.
- Spare parts reorder alerting.

**General**
- Any other reasonable administrative services as agreed.
