2. Position and Title

The Employee is employed in the position of BESS Division Lead — ETEK Licensed Engineer, Sales & Technical Consultant.

3. Commencement and Duration

Employment commences on 19 January 2026 and is for an indefinite duration, subject to termination in accordance with this Agreement and applicable law.

4. Probation

The first six (6) months of employment constitute a probation period. Either party may terminate during probation with one (1) week written notice.

5. Duties and Responsibilities

The Employee shall perform all duties reasonably associated with the role in relation to battery energy storage systems (BESS), photovoltaic systems, EPC works, and related renewable energy services, including but not limited to:

ETEK Professional Services

  • Professional sign-off and certification of BESS park installations as ETEK licensed electrician.
  • Electrical inspection, validation, and compliance verification of BESS installations against applicable laws, EAC and DSO requirements, standards, and manufacturer specifications.
  • Pre-installation park studies and site electrical assessments.
  • Refusal to sign off any non-compliant installation and prompt reporting of deficiencies.

Business Development & Sales — BESS Division

  • Business development and client acquisition for BESS projects.
  • Preparation of technical and commercial quotations and proposals for BESS systems.
  • Client engagement, relationship management, and ongoing account management for BESS clients.
  • Preliminary technical assessments, feasibility studies, and site evaluations for BESS projects.

Technical Coordination

  • Coordination with engineering, operations, and installation teams on BESS project scoping and delivery.
  • Documentation support for EAC/CERA submissions, Net Billing, and permitting.
  • Strict adherence to Company pricing and approval rules.

The Employee shall devote full working time, attention, and professional skill to the business of the Employer and act at all times in the Employer’s best interests.

5A. ETEK Licensed Electrician — BESS Sign-Off

Where legally permitted and subject to holding a valid registration with the Cyprus Scientific and Technical Chamber (ETEK), the Employee shall act as the Employer’s ETEK licensed electrician for inspection, validation, and professional sign-off of BESS installations.

The Employee shall ensure compliance with applicable laws, directives, EAC and DSO requirements, standards, and manufacturer specifications. The Employee shall not sign off any non-compliant installation and shall report deficiencies promptly.

Nothing in this Agreement requires the Employee to assume personal liability beyond mandatory law or professional regulation.

5B. Professional Indemnity Insurance

The Employer confirms that it maintains, or shall obtain prior to any ETEK sign-off activity, professional indemnity insurance and/or general liability insurance that covers the Employee while acting in the course of their duties under this Agreement, including ETEK-licensed sign-off and inspection activities.

The Employer shall provide the Employee with written confirmation of coverage upon request. The Employee shall not be required to perform ETEK sign-off duties until such insurance is confirmed in place.

6. Working Hours and Flexibility

Normal working hours are forty (40) hours per week, Monday to Friday.

6A. Hybrid Working Model

The Employee’s normal working pattern is flexible and hybrid, comprising a combination of:

  • Office-based days at the Employer’s Limassol office.
  • Site visits for inspections, sign-offs, and client meetings.
  • Remote work from the Employee’s residence or other suitable location.

The specific balance of office, site, and remote days shall be agreed between the Employee and the Employer based on operational requirements and may vary by week. Both parties shall act reasonably and in good faith in agreeing the working pattern.

6B. Additional Hours

Reasonable additional hours may be required from time to time due to operational needs. Where additional hours become systematic or frequent, the Employer and Employee shall agree on appropriate compensation, which may include:

  • Time off in lieu (TOIL) at a mutually agreed ratio; or
  • Compensation in accordance with applicable Cyprus labour law.

This provision does not create an entitlement to overtime pay for occasional reasonable additional hours inherent to a professional role.

8. Commission Structure

In addition to salary, the Employee may earn performance-based commission calculated strictly on net margin generated from projects personally closed by the Employee or formally allocated in writing by the Employer.

Participation, assistance, or technical support does not automatically create commission entitlement. Assistance alone never creates commission entitlement unless formally allocated in writing.

Net Margin = Client Contract Value (ex VAT) − Confirmed Material & Subcontractor Costs

8D. Project Allocation and Commission Attachment

Project allocation to the Employee shall be confirmed in writing by the Employer. Written confirmation may be by email, internal system record, or other documented form.

Once a project has been formally allocated to the Employee in writing, the related commission on collected funds shall remain attached to the Employee unless:

  • The Employee is removed from the project due to documented performance issues, pricing non-compliance, or client-requested reassignment;
  • The Employee voluntarily relinquishes the project in writing; or
  • The Employee’s employment is terminated prior to the relevant milestone payment.

Any reallocation of a previously assigned project must be communicated to the Employee in writing with reasons.

10. Protective Clauses

10A. Allocation Control

Any allocation of net margin to the Employee is at the sole discretion of the Employer and must be expressly confirmed in writing by management for each specific project. No verbal, implied, historical, or assumed allocation shall apply.

10B. Strategic and Utility-Scale Projects

Framework agreements, utility-scale projects, strategic partnerships, or transactions of exceptional size may be excluded from this commission structure or be subject to separate written terms at the Employer’s discretion.

10C. Pricing Compliance

Any unauthorized discounting, pricing deviation, margin breach, or post-signature correction attributable to the Employee may result in forfeiture of commission for the affected project.

10D. Adjustment and Clawback

Commission remains provisional until final project close-out. The Employer reserves the right to adjust or reclaim commission in the event of client default, cost overruns, regulatory rejection, rework, or margin erosion.

10E. No Double Compensation

Net margin shall not be used simultaneously for individual commission and team bonus calculation unless expressly approved in writing by the Employer.

11. Team Commission Pool and Exceptional Sales

11A. Team Bonus

The Employer may operate a discretionary, non-contractual team-based sales bonus scheme linked to overall collective sales performance.

Payment of any team bonus in one period does not create an entitlement or expectation for future periods. The team bonus is discretionary, non-contractual, and may be amended or withdrawn at any time.

11B. Direct Acquisition and Outbound Sales

Where the Employee independently generates BESS or other sales outside of standard inbound lead intake, including direct client acquisition, active prospecting, and project development, the Employer may award project-specific commission following the same or similar progressive structure. Such commissions must be confirmed in writing per project.

12. Social Insurance and Tax

Employer and Employee social insurance contributions shall be paid in accordance with Cyprus law.

13. Annual Leave

The Employee is entitled to twenty-one (21) working days of paid annual leave per calendar year, in addition to Cyprus public holidays. Leave accrues proportionally from the start of employment.

Sick leave is granted in accordance with Cyprus employment legislation and Social Insurance regulations.

14. Confidentiality

During and after employment, the Employee shall maintain strict confidentiality regarding all company, client, and commercial information, including pricing, margins, supplier terms, technical data, client lists, and business strategies.

15. Intellectual Property

All intellectual property, work product, designs, calculations, documentation, and materials created during employment are the exclusive property of Lighthief Cyprus Ltd.

16. Non-Competition and Non-Solicitation

During employment and for six (6) months following termination, the Employee shall not:

  • Directly solicit or attempt to solicit any client, customer, or prospective client of the Employer with whom the Employee had material contact during the last twelve (12) months of employment; or
  • Solicit or recruit any employee or contractor of the Employer to leave the Employer’s engagement.

For the avoidance of doubt: this clause does not prevent the Employee from being employed in, or providing services to, the wider solar, BESS, or renewable energy sector in general after termination. The restriction is limited to the protection of the Employer’s confidential information, client relationships, and legitimate business interests, and shall be interpreted and enforced only to the extent permitted by Cyprus law.
