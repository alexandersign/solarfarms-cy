## Background

This Amendment modifies the Employment Contract dated 2 March 2026 between **Lighthief Cyprus Ltd** ("Employer") and **Andreas Christoforou** ("Employee").

The Employee has not met the contractual minimum sales performance threshold for the review period covering March and April 2026, during which no sales were closed. Under Section 9B of the original contract, this gives the Employer grounds to initiate a revised terms process.

Rather than proceed to termination, the parties agree to restructure the compensation arrangement as set out below. This Amendment supersedes the salary and commission provisions of the original contract in their entirety. All other terms remain in force.

---

## 1. Revised Monthly Base Salary

With effect from **1 May 2026**, the Employee's gross monthly base salary is reduced from **EUR 2,500** to **EUR 1,000**, payable through payroll between the 1st and 5th day of each month following the worked month.

The revised base is subject to the same statutory deductions (social insurance, GESY, PAYE) as before.

---

## 2. Commission Structure — 18% of Net Margin

In addition to the base salary, the Employee earns commission on every project personally closed or formally allocated in writing by the Employer.

**Commission rate: 18% of net margin**, where net margin is defined as:

```
Net Margin = Client Contract Value (ex VAT) − Confirmed Material & Subcontractor Costs
```

There is **no minimum threshold** — commission is earned on the first euro of net margin from any qualifying project.

### 2A. Illustrative Commission Examples

| Project Type | Sold Price | Net Margin | Commission (18%) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Typical 5 kW residential PV | €5,500 | ~€1,850 | **€333** |
| Typical 10 kW residential PV | €9,000 | ~€1,990 | **€358** |
| Commercial 40 kW zero-export (e.g. Aurolia/Greenland type) | €40,336 | €20,336 | **€3,660** |

Four to five residential deals per month, combined with the base salary, restores the Employee's previous total earnings level.

### 2B. Payment Milestones

Commission is released only on collected funds:

- **40%** after contract signing payment received
- **50%** after installation payment received
- **10%** after commissioning or grid connection payment received

### 2C. Allocation Control

All commission allocations require written confirmation by management per project. No verbal, implied, or assumed allocation applies.

### 2D. Clawback

Commission remains provisional until final project close-out. The Employer reserves the right to adjust or reclaim commission in the event of client default, cost overruns, regulatory rejection, rework, or margin erosion.

## 3. Revised Sales Targets

Sales targets recalculate automatically against the revised base salary:

| Metric | Calculation | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Annual Sales Target (20×) | €1,000 × 12 × 20 | **€240,000** |
| 6-Month Minimum Threshold (9× ÷ 2) | €1,000 × 12 × 9 ÷ 2 | **€54,000** |

The 6-month minimum remains an **employment continuation threshold**, not a commission gate. If gross sales over any 6-month review period fall below €54,000, the Employer may initiate a further formal review, which may result in revised terms or termination in accordance with applicable law.

---

## 4. Renegotiation Clause

This compensation structure is intended as a transitional arrangement and is expressly subject to renegotiation. Either party may request a formal review of these terms at any time with **30 days' written notice**. Any agreed changes must be confirmed in a further written amendment signed by both parties.

If the Employee's sales performance during May–July 2026 demonstrates consistent pipeline conversion, the Employer is willing to discuss restoration of a higher base salary.
