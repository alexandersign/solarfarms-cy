4. Probation and Performance Review

The first six (6) months of employment constitute a probation period. Either party may terminate during probation with one (1) week written notice.

3-Month Performance Gate

A formal performance review shall be conducted at the end of the third (3rd) month of employment. The review shall assess the Employee’s performance against the 3-month sales minimum set out in Section 10.

If the Employee has not achieved gross sales of at least €67,500 in the first three months, the Employer may:

  • Terminate employment with one (1) week written notice under the probation clause; or
  • Issue a written performance improvement plan for the remaining probation period; or
  • Revise employment terms, including salary, by mutual written agreement.

If the Employee meets the 3-month target, employment continues under the same terms through the remainder of the probation period and beyond.

5. Duties and Responsibilities

The Employee shall perform all duties reasonably associated with the role, including but not limited to:

  • Technical and commercial sales of BESS and PV systems.
  • Preparation of proposals, quotations, ROI analyses, and client business cases.
  • Client engagement, preliminary technical assessments, and energy analysis.
  • Coordination with engineering and operations teams on project scoping.
  • Documentation support for EAC/CERA submissions and permitting.
  • Business development — identification and qualification of new project opportunities.
  • Strict adherence to Company pricing and approval rules.

The Employee shall devote full working time, attention, and professional skill to the business of the Employer and act at all times in the Employer’s best interests.

In addition to salary, the Employee may earn performance-based commission calculated strictly on net margin generated from projects personally closed by the Employee or formally allocated in writing by the Employer.

Participation, assistance, or technical support does not automatically create commission entitlement. Assistance alone never creates commission entitlement unless formally allocated in writing.

11. Protective Clauses

11A. Allocation Control

Any allocation of net margin to the Employee is at the sole discretion of the Employer and must be expressly confirmed in writing by management for each specific project. No verbal, implied, historical, or assumed allocation shall apply.

11B. Strategic and Utility-Scale Projects

Framework agreements, utility-scale projects, strategic partnerships, or transactions of exceptional size may be excluded from this commission structure or be subject to separate written terms at the Employer’s discretion.

11C. Pricing Compliance

Any unauthorized discounting, pricing deviation, margin breach, or post-signature correction attributable to the Employee may result in forfeiture of commission for the affected project.

11D. Adjustment and Clawback

Commission remains provisional until final project close-out. The Employer reserves the right to adjust or reclaim commission in the event of client default, cost overruns, regulatory rejection, rework, or margin erosion.

11E. No Double Compensation

Net margin shall not be used simultaneously for individual commission and team bonus calculation unless expressly approved in writing by the Employer.

12. Team Commission Pool and Exceptional Sales

12A. Team Bonus

The Employer may operate a discretionary, non-contractual team-based sales bonus scheme linked to overall collective sales performance.

Payment of any team bonus in one period does not create an entitlement or expectation for future periods. The team bonus is discretionary, non-contractual, and may be amended or withdrawn at any time.

12B. Direct Acquisition and Outbound Sales

Where the Employee independently generates sales outside of standard inbound lead intake, including but not limited to direct client acquisition, active prospecting, and project development, the Employer may award project-specific commission following the same or similar progressive structure. Such commissions must be confirmed in writing per project.

13. Social Insurance and Tax

Employer and Employee social insurance contributions, GESY healthcare contributions, and income tax withholding (PAYE) shall be administered in accordance with Cyprus law.

14. Annual Leave

The Employee is entitled to annual leave, public holidays, and sick leave in accordance with Cyprus employment legislation and Social Insurance regulations.

15. Confidentiality

During and after employment, the Employee shall maintain strict confidentiality regarding all company, client, and commercial information, including pricing, margins, supplier terms, technical data, and business strategies.

16. Intellectual Property

All intellectual property, work product, designs, calculations, documentation, and materials created during employment are the exclusive property of Lighthief Cyprus Ltd.

17. Non-Competition and Non-Solicitation

During employment and for six (6) months following termination, the Employee shall not compete with the Employer or solicit its clients or employees, to the extent permitted by Cyprus law.

18. Termination

During probation, either party may terminate with one (1) week written notice.

After completion of probation, either party may terminate employment with one (1) month written notice. Immediate termination may apply in cases of gross misconduct.

On termination, commission is payable only for milestones already triggered and client payments already received. No commission is payable for deals in pipeline or unsigned at termination date.

19. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Republic of Cyprus.

20. Entire Agreement

This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior discussions or understandings. Any amendments must be in writing and signed by both parties.
