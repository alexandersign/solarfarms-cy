## 9. Individual Commission

In addition to salary, the Employee may earn performance-based commission calculated strictly on **net margin** generated from projects personally closed by the Employee or formally allocated in writing by the Employer.

Participation, assistance, or technical support does not automatically create commission entitlement. Assistance alone **never** creates commission entitlement unless formally allocated in writing.

### 9A. Commission Structure

Commission is calculated on **net margin**, defined as:

```
Net Margin = Client Contract Value (ex VAT) − Confirmed Material & Subcontractor Costs
```

Progressive commission rates apply:

| Net Margin Band | Commission Rate |
|-----------------|-----------------|
| €0 – €10,000 | 0% |
| €10,001 – €20,000 | 5% |
| €20,001 – €35,000 | 7.5% |
| €35,001 – €50,000 | 10% |
| €50,001 – €75,000 | 12.5% |
| €75,001 and above | 15% |

Commission is released only on collected funds:
- **40%** after contract signing payment received
- **50%** after installation payment received
- **10%** after commissioning or grid connection payment received

### 9B. Sales Targets and Employment Review

Targets are derived from the Employee's gross salary using the Company's standard model:

```
Annual Sales Target   = 20 × Annual Gross Salary
Minimum Sales Target  =  9 × Annual Gross Salary (reviewed every 6 months)
```

The Employee's targets based on a gross monthly salary of {{MONTHLY_SALARY}}:

| Metric | Threshold |
|--------|-----------|
| Annual Sales Target (20×) | {{ANNUAL_SALES_TARGET}} |
| 6-Month Minimum Sales (9× ÷ 2) | {{SIX_MONTH_MIN_TARGET}} |

The minimum sales target is **not a commission gate**. Commission is earned on any qualifying project regardless of total sales volume.

The minimum sales target is an **employment continuation threshold**, reviewed every six (6) months. If gross sales over a 6-month review period fall below the minimum, the Employer may initiate a formal performance review, which may result in revised terms, a performance improvement plan, or termination in accordance with this Agreement and applicable law.

Targets recalculate automatically if salary changes. The Employer may adjust the multiplier model with 30 days' written notice.

### 9C. Allocation Control

Any allocation of net margin to the Employee is at the sole discretion of the Employer and must be expressly confirmed in writing by management for each specific project. No verbal, implied, historical, or assumed allocation shall apply.

### 9D. Strategic and Utility-Scale Projects

Framework agreements, utility-scale projects, strategic partnerships, or transactions of exceptional size may be excluded from this commission structure or be subject to separate written terms at the Employer's discretion.

### 9E. Pricing Compliance

Any unauthorized discounting, pricing deviation, margin breach, or post-signature correction attributable to the Employee may result in forfeiture of commission for the affected project.

### 9F. Adjustment and Clawback

Commission remains provisional until final project close-out. The Employer reserves the right to adjust or reclaim commission in the event of client default, cost overruns, regulatory rejection, rework, or margin erosion.

### 9G. No Double Compensation

Net margin shall not be used simultaneously for individual commission and team bonus calculation unless expressly approved in writing by the Employer.
