## 1. Scope

This policy establishes the unified commission and bonus structure for all sales personnel. It replaces any prior individual commission arrangements. All sales team members operate under identical structural terms, with targets scaled to individual salary levels.

---

## 2. Individual Commission

### 2.1 Eligibility

Commission is earned on projects **personally closed** by the Employee or **formally allocated in writing** by the Employer for each specific project.

**No commission is earned for:**
- Technical assistance or support on another team member's project
- Lead intake, routing, or CRM administration alone
- Participation in client meetings without formal allocation

### 2.2 Definition of Net Margin

Net margin is defined as:

```
Net Margin = Client Contract Value (ex VAT) − Confirmed Material & Subcontractor Costs
```

Only costs confirmed at time of contract signing are used. Post-signature cost increases are reflected through the Adjustment and Clawback mechanism (Section 6.3).

### 2.3 Progressive Commission Rates

| Net Margin Band | Commission Rate |
|-----------------|-----------------|
| €0 – €10,000 | 0% |
| €10,001 – €20,000 | 5% |
| €20,001 – €35,000 | 7.5% |
| €35,001 – €50,000 | 10% |
| €50,001 – €75,000 | 12.5% |
| €75,001 and above | 15% |

Rates are **marginal** — each band applies only to the portion of margin within that band.

**Example:** A project with €40,000 net margin:
- €0–10,000 → €0
- €10,001–20,000 → €500 (5% of €10,000)
- €20,001–35,000 → €1,125 (7.5% of €15,000)
- €35,001–40,000 → €500 (10% of €5,000)
- **Total commission: €2,125**

### 2.4 Payment Milestones

Commission is released **only on collected funds**, as follows:

| Milestone | % of Total Commission | Trigger |
|-----------|-----------------------|---------|
| Contract Signing | 40% | Client signing payment received |
| Installation | 50% | Client installation payment received |
| Commissioning | 10% | Client commissioning / grid connection payment received |

No commission is payable until the corresponding client payment is received in full.

## 3. Sales Target Model

### 3.1 Formula

Targets are derived directly from the Employee's gross salary:

```
Annual Sales Target  = 20 × Annual Gross Salary
Annual Minimum Target = 9 × Annual Gross Salary
Monthly Minimum       = Annual Minimum ÷ 12
```

### 3.2 Per-Employee Targets

| **Zinovia Efesopoulou** | €1,400 | €16,800 | **€336,000** | **€75,600** |

### 3.3 Minimum Target — Employment Continuation (Not Commission Gate)

The minimum sales target is **not a commission gate**. Commission is earned on any qualifying project regardless of total sales volume.

The minimum sales target is an **employment continuation threshold**, reviewed every **six (6) months**. If gross sales over a 6-month review period fall below the 6-month minimum, the Employer may:

- Initiate a formal performance review.
- Issue a performance improvement plan with specific targets and a defined review period.
- Revise employment terms.
- Terminate employment in accordance with the Employment Contract and applicable law.

### 3.4 Target Adjustment

Targets recalculate automatically if salary changes. The Employer may adjust the multiplier model with 30 days' written notice at the start of each calendar year.

## 4. Team Commission Pool

### 4.1 Structure

The Employer may operate a **discretionary, non-contractual team-based sales bonus** linked to overall collective sales performance.

### 4.2 Key Terms

- The team bonus is entirely at the Employer's discretion.
- Payment of a team bonus in one period **does not create entitlement or expectation** for future periods.
- The team bonus may be amended, suspended, or withdrawn at any time without notice.
- Eligibility criteria, payout amounts, and timing are determined by the Employer.

### 4.3 No Double Compensation

Net margin shall **not** be used simultaneously for individual commission and team bonus calculation unless expressly approved in writing by the Employer.

## 5. Special Commission Categories

### 5.1 Direct Acquisition / Outbound Sales

Where an Employee independently generates sales **outside of standard inbound lead intake**, including:

- Direct client acquisition
- Active prospecting and outbound sales
- Introduction and development of specific projects

The Employer may award project-specific commission, which may follow the same progressive structure in Section 2.3 or be agreed on a case-by-case basis.

Such commissions:
- May differ by project.
- Must be confirmed in writing, including scope, percentage, and payment milestones.
- Verbal understandings or informal discussions do not constitute entitlement unless formally documented.

### 5.2 Strategic and Utility-Scale Projects

Framework agreements, utility-scale projects, strategic partnerships, or transactions of exceptional size (typically exceeding €500,000 gross) **are excluded** from this commission structure.

Such projects may be subject to separate written terms at the Employer's discretion.

## 6. Protective Clauses

### 6.1 Allocation Control

Any allocation of net margin to an Employee is at the **sole discretion of the Employer** and must be expressly confirmed in writing by management for each specific project. No verbal, implied, historical, or assumed allocation shall apply.

### 6.2 Pricing Compliance

Any unauthorized discounting, pricing deviation, margin breach, or post-signature correction attributable to the Employee may result in **forfeiture of commission** for the affected project.

All quotations and pricing must be approved through the Company's standard pricing approval process before client submission.

### 6.3 Adjustment and Clawback

Commission remains **provisional** until final project close-out. The Employer reserves the right to adjust or reclaim commission in the event of:

- Client default or non-payment
- Cost overruns reducing actual net margin
- Regulatory rejection or permitting failure
- Rework or warranty claims attributable to sales commitments
- Margin erosion from any cause

### 6.4 Assistance Does Not Create Entitlement

Assistance, technical support, or participation in another team member's project **never creates commission entitlement** unless that specific project has been formally allocated in writing.

### 6.5 Manager Override

The Employer's 1/3 net margin retention rule applies to all projects. Commission structures in this policy operate on the Employee's allocable share of net margin only, after the Employer's margin retention.

---

## 7. Administration

### 7.1 Commission Calculation

- Commission is calculated by the Employer at each milestone.
- The Employee may request a written breakdown at any time.
- Disputes are resolved by the Employer's final determination.

### 7.2 Payment

- Commission is payable through standard payroll, subject to statutory deductions.
- Payment follows in the payroll cycle after the milestone trigger is confirmed.

### 7.3 Termination

- On termination, commission is payable only for milestones already triggered and client payments already received.
- No commission is payable for deals in pipeline or unsigned at termination date.
