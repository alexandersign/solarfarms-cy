# Field Engineer & Service KPI Framework

**Lighthief Cyprus Ltd**
**Document Reference:** LCY-KPI-002
**Version:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** March 2026
**Classification:** Internal — Confidential
**Applies to:** Jihat — Field Engineer, Civil Works & O&M

---

## 1. Purpose

This document establishes measurable Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) for the Field Engineer role covering site installations, team management, preventive/corrective maintenance, and O&M service calls. KPIs are reviewed quarterly and may be adjusted by management with written notice.

---

## 2. KPI Categories

### 2.1 Installation Quality & Execution

| KPI | Target | Measurement |
|-----|--------|-------------|
| Punch list items at handover | ≤ 3 minor items per site, zero critical | Per project sign-off |
| Installation checklist completion | 100% of checklists completed and signed before energisation | Per project |
| Torque register completion | 100% of connections torqued, witnessed, and photographed | Per project audit |
| Rework rate | Zero rework attributable to installation quality per quarter | Quarterly review |
| Cable entry / waterproofing compliance | 100% pass on pre-energisation walk-down | Per project |

### 2.2 Schedule & Delivery

| KPI | Target | Measurement |
|-----|--------|-------------|
| On-time start | Site works begin within 1 day of scheduled start | Per project |
| Milestone delivery | Installation milestones delivered within ±2 days of programme | Per project |
| Daily progress reporting | Daily site report submitted by 18:00 on work days | Per work day |
| Material readiness | Material shortages flagged ≥ 3 days before need date | Per project |

### 2.3 Team & Subcontractor Management

| KPI | Target | Measurement |
|-----|--------|-------------|
| Crew briefing | Safety and scope briefing conducted at start of every work day | Daily sign-off sheet |
| Subcontractor quality | Subcontractor defects flagged and documented same day | Per occurrence |
| Labour utilisation | ≥ 85% productive time on site (excluding travel, waiting) | Weekly timesheet review |
| Interface ownership | All interface ownership matrix items signed off before works begin | Per project |

### 2.4 O&M & PV Service Calls

| KPI | Target | Measurement |
|-----|--------|-------------|
| Response time | Acknowledge call within 2 hours, on-site within 24 hours (non-critical) | Per call |
| Critical response | On-site within 4 hours for critical faults | Per call |
| First-time fix rate | ≥ 80% of service calls resolved on first visit | Monthly |
| Service report completion | Report submitted within 24 hours of visit | Per visit |
| Repeat call rate | ≤ 10% of calls result in repeat visit for same fault within 30 days | Monthly |

### 2.5 Health, Safety & Compliance

| KPI | Target | Measurement |
|-----|--------|-------------|
| Safety incidents | Zero reportable incidents per quarter | Quarterly |
| PPE compliance | 100% PPE worn at all times on site | Random checks |
| Tool/equipment inspection | All tools inspected and logged before use | Weekly register |
| Near-miss reporting | All near-misses reported within 24 hours | Per occurrence |
| OEM procedure compliance | Zero deviations from Linyang installation/maintenance manual | Per project/visit |

### 2.6 Documentation & Administration

| KPI | Target | Measurement |
|-----|--------|-------------|
| Photo documentation | All required photos (serial numbers, connections, before/after) captured per checklist | Per project/visit |
| Grounding verification records | Completed and filed within 24 hours of test | Per project |
| Thermal soak test records | Completed and filed within 24 hours of test completion | Per project |
| Inventory / warehouse accuracy | Delivered items checked against delivery note, discrepancies reported same day | Per delivery |
| Timesheet submission | Weekly timesheets submitted by Monday 09:00 | Weekly |

## 3. Performance Scoring

### 3.1 Scoring Method

Each KPI is scored:

| Score | Definition |
|-------|------------|
| 3 — Exceeds | Consistently exceeds target |
| 2 — Meets | Meets target in ≥ 90% of instances |
| 1 — Below | Meets target in 70–89% of instances |
| 0 — Unsatisfactory | Meets target in < 70% of instances |

### 3.2 Overall Performance Rating

| Rating | Weighted Average Score |
|--------|----------------------|
| Excellent | 2.5 – 3.0 |
| Good | 2.0 – 2.49 |
| Needs Improvement | 1.5 – 1.99 |
| Unsatisfactory | Below 1.5 |

### 3.3 Weighting

| Category | Weight |
|----------|--------|
| Installation Quality & Execution | 30% |
| Schedule & Delivery | 20% |
| Team & Subcontractor Management | 15% |
| O&M & PV Service Calls | 15% |
| Health, Safety & Compliance | 10% |
| Documentation & Administration | 10% |

| Annual Performance Review | Annually | Director |

---

## 5. Bonus Eligibility

The Field Engineer may be eligible for a **discretionary quarterly performance bonus** based on KPI scores:

| Overall Rating | Bonus Eligibility |
|----------------|-------------------|
| Excellent | Up to one month's salary annually (paid quarterly) |
| Good | Up to half month's salary annually (paid quarterly) |
| Needs Improvement | No bonus |
| Unsatisfactory | No bonus |

The bonus is discretionary and non-contractual. Payment in one period does not create entitlement for future periods.

---

## 6. Performance Improvement

If overall rating falls to "Needs Improvement" for two consecutive quarters:

1. A written Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) is issued.
2. PIP duration: 3 months with monthly reviews.
3. Failure to improve to "Good" or above during PIP may result in reassignment or termination per Cyprus employment law.
