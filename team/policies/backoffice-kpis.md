# Back Office KPI Framework

**Lighthief Cyprus Ltd**
**Document Reference:** LCY-KPI-001
**Version:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** March 2026
**Classification:** Internal — Confidential
**Applies to:** Chris — Back Office & Operations Coordinator

---

## 1. Purpose

This document establishes measurable Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) for the Back Office & Operations Coordinator role. KPIs are reviewed quarterly and may be adjusted by management with written notice.

---

## 2. KPI Categories

### 2.1 Documentation & Filing

| KPI | Target | Measurement |
|-----|--------|-------------|
| Project file completeness | 100% of active projects have all required documents filed | Monthly audit |
| Document turnaround | Contracts, quotations, and internal documents completed within 2 business days of request | Tracked per request |
| EAC submission support | All submission packages prepared within 3 business days of engineering sign-off | Per submission |
| Filing accuracy | Zero misfiled or missing documents per quarter | Quarterly audit |

### 2.2 CRM & Data Management

| KPI | Target | Measurement |
|-----|--------|-------------|
| CRM data entry | All new leads entered within 24 hours of receipt | Daily check |
| CRM accuracy | < 2% error rate on data fields (contact, project details, status) | Monthly sample audit (20 records) |
| Pipeline status updates | All project statuses current within 48 hours of change | Weekly review |
| Duplicate prevention | Zero duplicate client or project records per quarter | Quarterly audit |

### 2.3 Financial Administration

| KPI | Target | Measurement |
|-----|--------|-------------|
| Invoice preparation | Client invoices prepared within 2 business days of milestone trigger | Per invoice |
| Payment tracking | All incoming payments logged within 24 hours of receipt | Daily |
| Supplier invoice processing | Supplier invoices processed within 5 business days of receipt | Per invoice |
| Expense report accuracy | Zero errors requiring correction per month | Monthly |

### 2.4 Internal Coordination & Response Time

| KPI | Target | Measurement |
|-----|--------|-------------|
| Internal request response | Acknowledge within 4 hours, complete within agreed timeline | Per request |
| Meeting support | Agendas distributed 24 hours before, minutes distributed within 24 hours after | Per meeting |
| Inter-team coordination | Sales-to-engineering handoff packages complete within 1 business day | Per handoff |
| Supplier/vendor follow-up | Outstanding queries followed up within 48 hours | Tracked log |

### 2.5 Regulatory & Compliance Support

| KPI | Target | Measurement |
|-----|--------|-------------|
| EAC/CERA document tracking | All regulatory deadlines tracked with 14-day advance notice | Per deadline |
| Permit status tracking | Status updated in project file within 24 hours of authority response | Per project |
| Insurance certificate management | All certificates current, renewals flagged 30 days in advance | Monthly review |
| Social insurance / payroll support | All monthly filings prepared on time | Monthly |

### 2.6 Warehouse & Inventory Support

| KPI | Target | Measurement |
|-----|--------|-------------|
| Inventory record accuracy | Physical count matches system within 2% | Quarterly count |
| Delivery documentation | All delivery receipts filed within 24 hours | Per delivery |
| Spare parts reorder alerts | Reorder flagged when stock drops below minimum threshold | Per item |

---

## 3. Performance Scoring

### 3.1 Scoring Method

Each KPI is scored:

| Score | Definition |
|-------|------------|
| 3 — Exceeds | Consistently exceeds target |
| 2 — Meets | Meets target in ≥ 90% of instances |
| 1 — Below | Meets target in 70–89% of instances |
| 0 — Unsatisfactory | Meets target in < 70% of instances |

### 3.2 Overall Performance Rating

| Rating | Weighted Average Score |
|--------|----------------------|
| Excellent | 2.5 – 3.0 |
| Good | 2.0 – 2.49 |
| Needs Improvement | 1.5 – 1.99 |
| Unsatisfactory | Below 1.5 |

### 3.3 Weighting

| Category | Weight |
|----------|--------|
| Documentation & Filing | 25% |
| CRM & Data Management | 20% |
| Financial Administration | 20% |
| Internal Coordination | 15% |
| Regulatory & Compliance | 10% |
| Warehouse & Inventory | 10% |

---

## 5. Bonus Eligibility

The Back Office Coordinator may be eligible for a **discretionary quarterly performance bonus** based on KPI scores:

| Overall Rating | Bonus Eligibility |
|----------------|-------------------|
| Excellent | Up to one month's salary annually (paid quarterly) |
| Good | Up to half month's salary annually (paid quarterly) |
| Needs Improvement | No bonus |
| Unsatisfactory | No bonus, performance improvement plan initiated |

The bonus is discretionary and non-contractual. Payment of a bonus in one period does not create entitlement for future periods.

---

## 6. Performance Improvement

If overall rating falls to "Needs Improvement" for two consecutive quarters:

1. A written Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) is issued.
2. PIP duration: 3 months with monthly reviews.
3. Failure to improve to "Good" or above during PIP may result in reassignment or termination per Cyprus employment law.
