## 1. Executive Summary

**Lighthief Cyprus Ltd** is positioning to become Cyprus's first vertically integrated renewable energy platform — spanning BESS EPC, solar PV investment, energy trading, and retail energy products.

### Four Revenue Pillars

**Pillar 1: BESS EPC (Active — 2026)**  
Turnkey battery storage for 51 solar parks. 881.78 MWh pipeline worth €97.6M installed. 10.7% EPC margin = **€11.64M gross revenue**.

**Pillar 2: Shark Energy Fund (Planned — 2026–2027)**  
€50M acquisition fund targeting operational PV+BESS parks. Buy at 7–8x EBITDA, retrofit BESS, sell at 10–12x. Target IRR: **15–20% fund-level**.

**Pillar 3: Energy Trading (Planned — 2027+)**  
When Cyprus passes DAM arbitrage legislation, exploit €71.87/MWh spread across owned and managed portfolio. Requires proprietary EMS/SCADA (LighthiefEMS).

**Pillar 4: 7Sun Retail & Wholesale (Active — 2026)**  
JV with 7Sun (Europe) for consumer panels, inverters, and small-scale battery sales via 7Sun Cyprus Ltd.

## 5. SWOT Analysis

### Strengths
- **Exclusive Linyang distributor** — sole Cyprus partner (signed Feb 2026)
- **Largest private BESS pipeline** — 881.78 MWh across 51 parks
- **Competitive pricing** — 16–18% below global benchmark
- **APG available** — 100% advance payment bank guarantee via EU bank (confirmed), matching competitors
- **Single-source EPC+O&M** — one contract, one counterparty
- **International group backing** — 11 countries, 150+ staff, NATO certified
- **Full product range** — T1 (1MW) to T8 (10MW) MV skids
- **Verified market data** — 134-day TSOC DAM dataset

### Weaknesses
- **No deployed BESS yet** — pre-revenue in BESS EPC
- **APG premium adds cost** — APG available via EU bank, but premium reduces margin slightly
- **SOH guarantee gap** — Year 10 at 75–80% vs competitors ≥80%
- **Single OEM dependency** — 100% reliance on Linyang
- **No proprietary EMS** — dependent on third-party Voltus
- **Newer entrant** — ~15% market share

### Opportunities
- **Zero deployed BESS competition** — first mover captures narrative
- **DAM arbitrage imminent** — +€71.87/MWh revenue when legislated
- **150 MW/350 MWh government support** — €35M announced
- **33 licensees = 33 potential customers**
- **€50M Shark Energy Fund** — own portfolio creation
- **Energy trading** — proprietary EMS enables new revenue

### Threats
- **TSOC state BESS** — 120 MW/400 MWh with EU funding
- **Regulatory protectionism** — 6 restrictions favouring state BESS
- **Competitor entry** — Sungrow, Sunwoda, Systems Sunlight active
- **Sunwoda stronger guarantees** — APG, 98% availability
- **Client concentration** — ABIO Power = 53% of pipeline
- **Delayed DAM legislation** — limits BESS to curtailment only

## 8. Shark Energy Fund — €50M PV+BESS Acquisition

### Investment Logic

1. **Buy** operational PV parks at 7–8x EBITDA (discounted due to curtailment)
2. **Retrofit** with Linyang BESS at group pricing (€100–113K/MWh)
3. **Optimise** with LighthiefEMS for curtailment recovery at €183/MWh peak
4. **Trade** when DAM legislation passes (+€71.87/MWh arbitrage)
5. **Exit** at 10–12x EBITDA or hold for 25-year cash flow

### Target Metrics

| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
