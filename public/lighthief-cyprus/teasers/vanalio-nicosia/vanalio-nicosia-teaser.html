Executive Summary

Four contiguous ground-mounted PV parks totalling 10.4 MWp with a 10 MW / 40 MWh (4-hour) integrated storage system, Nicosia District, Cyprus. All material permits are fully licensed — CERA production licences (extended to 31/12/2029), town planning permits, building permits, environmental approval, and land leases to 2051. Single EPC and O&M scope across all four plots. Integrated 40 MWh storage captures all curtailed generation for evening peak dispatch, providing structural revenue protection independent of grid dispatch signals.
