About Us

From Polish-British startup to pan-European renewable energy powerhouse

Founded nearly a decade ago, Lighthief has evolved from a small Polish-British company into a rapidly growing European-Asian renewable energy O&M operator. Today, we manage hundreds of megawatts of solar, wind, and biogas installations across 11 countries, providing comprehensive EPC and O&M services throughout the entire lifecycle of renewable energy projects.

Our team of over 150 renewable energy professionals combines field engineers, technical specialists, project managers, and monitoring experts—all united by hands-on experience and commitment to operational excellence. Led by Founder Dr. Arkadius Sybaris, our multinational workforce speaks the language of local markets while maintaining unified quality standards.

Our strength lies in our people: engineers who've weathered countless installations, technicians who know every panel and inverter intimately, and managers who understand that renewable energy service is ultimately about reliability, response time, and results.

Innovation Hub

Our Polish facility represents a major investment in renewable energy research and development

Located on a 23-hectare site in Częstochowa, Poland, this center combines cutting-edge research with practical applications. Our living laboratory allows us to test, optimize, and validate technologies before deploying them across our client portfolio.

Our innovative O&M solutions for PV farms have been supported by numerous EU grants for research and development, validating our approach to next-generation renewable energy services.

Our Vision

Lighthief's strategic goal is to provide comprehensive O&M coverage across the entire European Union and establish ourselves as one of the largest renewable energy service operators in the region.

Our expansion into Ukraine and Eurasian markets reflects this ambition, positioning us at the forefront of renewable energy growth in both established and emerging markets.

Our Services

End-to-end renewable energy solutions from project development through operations, maintenance, and end-of-life recycling

Solar EPC
Turnkey solar projects from concept to commissioning across Europe. Technical design, equipment procurement, construction management, grid connection, and final handover. NATO certification and EU grants validate our commitment to quality and safety.
Solar & Wind O&M
24/7 monitoring from four European control centers, preventive maintenance, rapid emergency response, thermal imaging inspections, and performance optimization. Experience spans floating PV, mountain installations, and active conflict zones.
BESS Solutions
Comprehensive battery energy storage from system design to ongoing monitoring. Large-scale commercial (MW-scale), residential battery storage, and SMB solutions tailored to business consumption patterns.
Repowering & Recycling
Extend installation lifecycles through strategic repowering—upgrading inverters, replacing underperforming panels, modernizing control systems. Complete physical recycling capabilities at our Polish facility.

EPC Deliverables

  • Complete technical design and engineering documentation
  • Equipment procurement from verified tier-1 manufacturers
  • Full construction management with certified installation teams
  • Grid connection coordination and permitting support
  • Commissioning, testing, and performance verification
  • As-built documentation and warranty management
