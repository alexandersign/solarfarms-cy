About Us

Founded nearly a decade ago, Lighthief has evolved from asmall Polish-British company into a rapidly growing European-Asian renewableenergy O&M operator. Today, we manage hundreds of megawatts of solar, wind,and biogas installations across 11 countries, providing comprehensive EPC andO&M services throughout the entire lifecycle of renewable energy projects.

Innovation Hub

Our new Polish facility represents a major investment inrenewable energy research and development. Located on a 23-hectare site, thiscenter combines cutting-edge research with practical applications, featuringour own 2x8 MW photovoltaic farm, a 1 MW biogas plant, and advanced energystorage systems. This living laboratory allows us to test, optimize, andvalidate technologies before deploying them across our client portfolio. Ourinnovative O&M solutions for PV farms have been supported by numerous EU grantsfor research and development, validating our approach to next-generationrenewable energy services.

Security & Standards

As a certified NATO service provider, Lighthief adheres tostringent security protocols and quality standards. This certification reflectsour commitment to operational excellence, data security, and reliable servicedelivery for critical infrastructure projects.

Our Vision

Lighthief's strategic goal is to provide comprehensive O&M coverage across the entire European Union and establish ourselves asone of the largest renewable energy service operators in the region. Our expansion into Ukraine and Eurasian markets reflects this ambition, positioningus at the forefront of renewable energy growth in both established and emergingmarkets.

Our Expertise

We offer end-to-end renewable energy solutions: from project development and construction through operations, maintenance, 24/7 monitoring, thermal imaging inspections, and end-of-life recycling. Our hands-on, field-tested approach ensures maximum performance and longevity of renewable energy installations.

Poland: Where It All Began

Our journey started in Poland, and today our new EPC and O&M center in Częstochowa stands as a testament to our growth. With over 1,000 m² of office space and 3,500 m² of warehouse facilities, we serve clients across the entire country.

Poland has emerged as one of the three largest energy markets in Europe, making it an ideal location for business development and our operational headquarters.

European Network

Our European presence includes strategic offices in Trieste and Salerno (Italy), Malaga (Spain), Hannover (Germany), Utrecht (Netherlands), Limassol (Cyprus), and Athens (Greece).

Each location has been carefully selected to provide optimal coverage and rapid response capabilities across the continent.

Eurasian Expansion

Our Eurasian operations are anchored by offices in Astana (Kazakhstan) and Tashkent (Uzbekistan), positioning us to capitalize on the rapidly developing renewable energy markets of Central Asia.

2026 Growth Plan

New offices in Romania, Czech Republic, Slovakia, and Switzerland are planned for 2026, further strengthening our pan-European service capabilities.

24/7 Monitoring Centers

Lighthief operates four primary monitoring centers across the EU: Częstochowa (Poland), Hannover (Germany), Trieste (Italy), and Malaga (Spain).

These hubs provide round-the-clock surveillance and rapid response coordination for our managed installations.

Renewable energy service delivery demands logistics excellence, rapid reaction times, and local market expertise. This is precisely what our partners, investors, and clients value most about working with Lighthief.
