BESS Solution Overview

How Battery Storage Solves Curtailment:

  1. Capture: Store energy during curtailment periods instead of losing it
  2. Shift: Discharge during evening/night when demand is high
  3. Monetize: Sell at premium night tariff rates (20-30% higher)
  4. Protect: Future-proof against increasing curtailment

Sizing Methodology

Factors to Consider:

  1. Curtailment Profile: Historical and projected curtailment %
  2. Park Capacity: MW size of solar installation
  3. Cycles per Day: How many charge/discharge cycles
  4. Tariff Differential: Day vs night pricing spread
