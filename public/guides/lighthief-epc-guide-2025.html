Lighthief provides complete turnkey Engineering, Procurement & Construction (EPC) services for solar photovoltaic parks and Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS) in Cyprus. Our integrated approach covers the entire project lifecycle:

1
Assessment & Design
2–4 weeks
Solar irradiation analysis, grid feasibility, optimized layout design, financial projections with realistic 8-13% IRR targets, and environmental impact assessment.
2
Engineering & Procurement
