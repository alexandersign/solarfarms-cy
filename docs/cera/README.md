# CERA / planning — official gazette forms (Κ.Δ.Π. 2026)

This folder contains PDF copies of the **Κοινά Διάταγματα Προέδρου (Κ.Δ.Π.)** from **Official Gazette 5992, 16 January 2026** (Κυπριακή Δημοκρατία — Επίσημη Εφημερίδα).

> **CERA (ΡΑΕΚ)** = electricity regulator. **Building / planning / town-planning** exemptions in these PDFs are issued under the **Roads & Buildings** and **Town Planning** laws, not CERA. Filings go to the **competent planning authority (αρμόδια αρχή)**, often via the **EOA** and/or **ΙΠΠΟΔΑΜΟΣ** as stated in each decree.

## What “EOA” means in the decrees

**ΕΟΑ** = **Επαρχιακός Οργανισμός Αυτοδιοίκησης** (Provincial / local administration organisation).

- **Κ.Δ.Π. 15/2026, Article 6:** the **Υπεύθυνη δήλωση μελετητή** is submitted to the **competent authority (αρμόδια αρχή)** **by email**; the **email address** is **published on the website of the relevant EOA** (transitional until ΙΠΠΟΔΑΜΟΣ).
- **Κ.Δ.Π. 17/2026, Article 5:** the study declaration and **Παράρτημα ΙΙ** documents are submitted through **ΙΠΠΟΔΑΜΟΣ** (not the same channel as 15/2026).

Always use the **current** submission address / portal for your **district’s** EOA or planning office.

## Authorised study (Μελετητής) — who signs (both decrees’ appendices)

The appendix forms are addressed to a **Μελετητής** (authorised designer), not generically to “CERA”.

**Typical form fields (both 15/2026 and 16/2026 style appendices):**

- **Όνομα/γραφείο**  
- **Αρ. μητρώου ΕΤΕΚ** (mandatory)  
- **Ειδικότητα** (specialty on ETEK register)  
- Contact and **professional liability insurance (Επαγγελματική ασφάλεια)** as required by ETEK  

**Κ.Δ.Π. 17/2026 Παράρτημα ΙΙ** additionally requires, among other things:

- Unofficial cadastral / property plan from DLS portal where applicable  
- **Χωροταξικό Διάγραμμα Δόμησης** with: existing licensed RES, proposed storage, **distances from plot boundaries**, **spot levels** on the development parcel and neighbours/roads, etc.  
- Copy of **planning and/or building permission** for the **existing** RES plant  
- ETEK certificate of **PI insurance** for the studyer  

The **Κ.Δ.Π. 15/2026** BESS appendix also asks for **Πολεοδομική and Οικοδομική** licence details for the RES plant (form section 5 in the gazette).

**Labour (Τμήμα Επιθεώρησης Εργασίας) — in Κ.Δ.Π. 17 Appendix I:** risk assessment for **battery** hazard must be prepared by a **Μελετητής/Μηχανικός (ΕΞΥΠΠ/ΕΣΥΠΠ)**. That is **separate** from the ETEK study declaration in many cases, but the same project may use aligned specialists.

## One-line “which law for what”

- **6 m from plot boundaries, 3 m height, paving, no topography change, 6 m fire access**, etc. → largely mirror between **Κ.Δ.Π. 15/2026 (§5)** and **Κ.Δ.Π. 17/2026 (§4)**; **Κ.Δ.Π. 15** is the **Roads & Buildings** special decree; **Κ.Δ.Π. 17** is the **town-planning** special development decree with **Ι** (conditions by Environment / Fire / Labour) and **ΙΙ** (submissions).
