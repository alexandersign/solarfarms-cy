1. The Opportunity

Cyprus is one of the most expensive places in Europe to buy grid electricity — and the sunniest. For a data centre, that combination is the entire investment case for on-site generation.

Grid power for large consumers runs at roughly €0.22–0.24 per kWh (among the highest in the EU), while Cyprus enjoys the region's best solar resource at ~1,750 kWh per kWp per year. On-site solar delivers energy at a fraction of the grid price. Lighthief packages solar PV, battery storage and intelligent microgrid controls into a single turnkey system that takes a large slice of your energy bill off the grid — and hedges it for 25+ years.

The headline: a behind-the-meter solar + storage microgrid for a Cyprus data centre typically pays for itself in about three years, then delivers two decades of low-cost, price-stable power.

3. Cooling — the Cyprus Swing Factor

In a 35–40°C climate, cooling is the single biggest driver of a data centre's total energy. It sets your PUE (the ratio of total facility power to IT power), your water demand on a water-scarce island, and how dense — and therefore how AI-ready — your racks can be. Lighthief designs the microgrid and the cooling strategy together.

Cooling methodTypical PUEWater useMax rack densityBest for
Air (evaporative)~1.45High≤ 15 kW/rackLowest capex; least efficient in Cyprus heat
Liquid — direct-to-chip~1.25Moderate≤ 80 kW/rackStrong PUE gain; handles AI density
Immersion (single-phase)~1.08Minimal≤ 200 kW/rackBest PUE & near-zero water; ideal for hot climate + AI
A free efficiency win: cooling demand peaks at midday — exactly when solar output peaks. The array feeds the chillers directly during the hottest hours, so an efficient cooling design both cuts total energy and raises the share of load your solar can cover.

6. Challenges We Solve

  • Expensive grid power — we replace up to ~45% of it with low-cost on-site solar, hedged for 25+ years.
  • Grid connection & capacity — a self-consuming microgrid reduces your dependence on scarce grid capacity and eases connection.
  • Renewable curtailment — behind-the-meter consumption avoids curtailment losses; your data centre absorbs the solar on-site.
  • Heat & water — cooling designed for 40°C summers, with low-water liquid or immersion options for a water-scarce island.
  • Resilience — battery storage provides seamless ride-through and backup, improving uptime.
