1. INTRODUCTION & PORTFOLIO OVERVIEW

Lighthief Cyprus Ltd is the EPC contractor, exclusive OEM distributor, and long-term O&M service provider for a portfolio of Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) projects across Cyprus. We are seeking proposals from qualified EMS (Energy Management System) providers for the supply, integration, and ongoing support of EMS, PPC (Power Plant Controller), RTU, and SCADA systems across the entire portfolio.

Portfolio Summary:
  • 51 Parks across 5 districts (Nicosia, Famagusta, Limassol, Larnaca, Paphos)
  • 249 MW total power capacity
  • 881.78 MWh total energy capacity
  • 251 containers (66 MV Skids + 185 BESS containers)
  • 3 client groups + 6 standalone parks
  • Target deployment: Q2 2026 – Q4 2027
