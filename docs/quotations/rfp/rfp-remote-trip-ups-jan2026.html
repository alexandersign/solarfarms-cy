Request for Proposal

Remote Trip System & UPS/Auxiliary Power Supply

RFP Issue Date: 27 January 2026
Proposal Due: 10 February 2026
Project: Cyprus BESS Portfolio - 47 Parks (863.5 MWh)
DSO: Electricity Authority of Cyprus (EAC)
⚠️ DSO REQUIREMENT
Remote trip capability is MANDATORY for all grid-connected RES/BESS installations per Cyprus DSO Technical Guide. The DSO must be able to remotely disconnect the installation in emergency situations.

1. Project Overview

Lighthief Cyprus Ltd requires remote trip systems and UPS/auxiliary power supplies for 47 BESS installations. These systems must comply with Cyprus DSO requirements and ensure reliable disconnection capability.
