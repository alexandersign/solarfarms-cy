Request for Proposal

Reinforced Concrete Base Construction for BESS Installations

RFP Issue Date: 20 February 2026
Proposal Due: 7 March 2026
Project: Cyprus BESS Portfolio — 51 Parks (881.78 MWh)
Total Equipment: ~176 Battery Containers (20HC) + ~75 MV SKIDs (40HC) = 251 units

1. Project Overview

Lighthief Cyprus Ltd is seeking proposals from qualified civil contractors for the design and construction of reinforced concrete foundations for a portfolio of 51 Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) installations across five districts of Cyprus. Total portfolio: 249 MW / 881.78 MWh.

⚠️ REGULATORY REQUIREMENT (Κ.Δ.Π. 15/2026)
Per Cyprus Law §18.4 Item 24: "Reinforced concrete foundation required" (Βάση οπλισμένου σκυροδέματος) for all BESS installations within licensed RES stations.

2. Scope of Work

2.1 Works Required Per Site

# Work Item Description Standard
1 Site Survey Topographic survey, soil investigation ETEK certified
2 Excavation Excavation to formation level (min 300mm) Per structural design
3 Sub-base Compacted aggregate base layer 130 kN/m² min bearing
4 Membrane DPM/Geotextile membrane 300gsm minimum
5 Reinforcement Steel mesh/rebar per structural design A393 mesh or equivalent
6 Concrete Base Reinforced concrete slab, max 40cm total height (10-20cm below grade with crusher run + 20cm above grade as needed) C30/37 minimum
7 Container Placement Level bearing surface for container placement; securing method TBC per OEM installation manual Per Linyang specs
8 Cable Trenches Trenches for MV/LV/control cables Per layout design
9 Drainage Surface water drainage system Per DSO §18.4
10 Earthing Grid Foundation earthing conductors <1Ω impedance

8. Deliverables Required

  1. Structural Design - Foundation design per site, stamped by ETEK engineer
  2. Method Statement - Detailed construction methodology
  3. Programme - Construction schedule per site
  4. Quality Plan - Inspection and test plan
  5. As-Built Drawings - Foundation as-built including container positions
  6. Test Certificates - Concrete cube tests, compaction tests
  7. Warranty - 10-year structural warranty

9. Contractor Qualifications

  • ETEK registered civil contractor (Category A or B)
  • Minimum 5 years experience in industrial foundations
  • Demonstrated experience with container/prefab installations
  • Full insurance coverage (€2M minimum)
  • In-house or contracted structural engineering capability
  • Ability to work across all Cyprus districts
