Request for Proposal

Protection Relay Testing & DSO Witness Testing Services

RFP Issue Date: 27 January 2026
Proposal Due: 10 February 2026
Project: Cyprus BESS Portfolio - 47 Parks (863.5 MWh)
DSO: Electricity Authority of Cyprus (EAC)

1. Project Overview

Lighthief Cyprus Ltd requires protection relay testing, commissioning, and DSO witness testing services for 47 BESS installations. All protection settings must comply with Cyprus DSO Technical Guide and EN 50549-2.
