3.7 Delay in Start-Up (DSU)

Purpose: Covers loss of anticipated revenue when a material damage event during construction delays the commercial operation date (COD) of a BESS project.
ItemRequirement
Indemnity Period12 months
Estimated Revenue at Risk€150-300 per MWh/year (curtailment avoidance revenue)
TriggerMaterial damage covered under CAR/EAR policy causing delay to COD
Waiting Period30 days
BasisLinked to CAR/EAR policy — same insurer preferred
Rationale: Lighthief's EPC contracts include delay Liquidated Damages of 0.1-0.2% per day, capped at 10% of contract value. A material damage event causing 60+ days delay on a €5M project exposes Lighthief to €500K in delay LDs. DSU coverage mitigates this risk.

5. BESS Fire Risk Engineering

Underwriters should note the following risk mitigation measures specific to BESS installations. Lighthief's portfolio uses Lithium Iron Phosphate (LFP) chemistry exclusively, which has a significantly lower thermal runaway risk than NMC chemistry.

5.1 Battery Chemistry & Certification

MeasureDetail
Battery ChemistryLFP (Lithium Iron Phosphate) — inherently thermally stable, lower energy density than NMC
Cell ManufacturerEve Energy Co., Ltd (314Ah LFP cells) — Linyang is a shareholder in Eve Energy
UL9540APASSED — no thermal propagation between modules
IEC 62619Certified — secondary lithium cells safety
IEC 63056Certified — ESS safety requirements
IEC 62933-5-2In progress — grid ESS safety
UN 38.3Certified — transport safety
UL 94 V-0Certified — fire retardant materials
FM 5-33Compliance assessed — lithium-ion ESS loss prevention guidance

5.2 Container Fire Protection

SystemDetail
Fire SuppressionIntegrated aerosol fire suppression system per container (automatic activation)
Fire DetectionMulti-zone smoke detection, heat detection, and gas detection (H₂, CO) per container
BMS MonitoringReal-time cell-level voltage, temperature, and current monitoring with automatic shutdown
Thermal SensorsPer-cell and per-module temperature monitoring with configurable alarm thresholds
Emergency ShutdownAutomatic disconnection on thermal anomaly, overcurrent, or overvoltage detection
VentilationForced ventilation system with gas concentration monitoring to prevent explosive gas accumulation
Enclosure RatingIP55 rated containers, C5 corrosion protection (coastal/industrial) standard for all Cyprus sites

5.3 Site Layout & Separation

MeasureDetail
Container SpacingMinimum 6m between BESS containers (exceeds FM 5-33 and insurer 4.5m minimum)
Infrastructure SeparationBESS containers positioned at safe distance from MV transformers, substations, and PV arrays
Fire Break Strategy"Let it burn" strategy viable — individual container fire will not propagate to adjacent units at 6m spacing
PV SeparationBESS power routed to grid separately from existing PV installation — no single point of failure
AccessMinimum 4m fire access roads around BESS installations for emergency vehicle access

5.4 Emergency Response

MeasureDetail
Emergency PlanSite-specific emergency response plan for each of the 47 parks
Fire Brigade CoordinationPre-incident plans shared with local fire services — LFP-specific response protocols
24/7 MonitoringVoltus SCADA + Lighthief NOC — critical alerts escalated within 1 hour
On-Site ResponseDedicated BESS field team — maximum 90 minutes to any site in Cyprus
Water StrategyWater cooling recommended for containing thermal events (per LEARISK and NFPA 855 guidance)
