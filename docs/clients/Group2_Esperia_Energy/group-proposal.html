Project Delivery Workflow

Each project follows a structured 6-phase delivery process from contract signing through to Final Acceptance.

Phase 1 — Contract & Procurement (Weeks 1–4)
  • EPC contract execution & advance payment received
  • Lighthief triggers OEM equipment order to Linyang factory (Nanjing)
  • DSO (EAC) grid connection application submitted per park
  • Site survey, geotechnical assessment & foundation design finalized
  • Local subcontractor mobilization (civil, electrical, mechanical)
Phase 2 — Manufacturing & Engineering (Weeks 4–17)
  • BESS containers manufactured at Linyang factory (~13 weeks)
  • Factory Acceptance Testing (FAT) — Lighthief-witnessed remote or on-site
  • Civil works begin: concrete platforms, cable trenches, drainage, fencing
  • MV protection & grid interface engineering completed
  • EMS/SCADA system configuration by Voltus Energy
Phase 3 — Shipping & Site Preparation (Weeks 17–24)
  • CIF sea transport: Nanjing → Limassol Port (~7 weeks)
  • Marine cargo insurance active (0.75% of CIF)
  • Customs clearance, import duties (2.66% weighted avg) processed
  • Warehouse staging at Limassol industrial facility
  • Site preparation complete: platforms, trenches, grid connection point ready
Phase 4 — Installation & Integration (Weeks 24–30)
  • Container delivery to site via A. Soulis (crane + inland transport)
  • Container placement on concrete platforms
  • MV/LV cabling installation, terminations, earthing grid
  • PCS & MV skid energization
  • EMS/SCADA integration & WAGO PLC commissioning
Phase 5 — Commissioning & Testing (Weeks 30–34)
  • Pre-commissioning checks (insulation resistance, earth resistance ≤4Ω)
  • Linyang factory commissioning team on-site (Nanjing HQ)
  • Protection relay testing & secondary injection
  • DSO (EAC) witness testing & protection settings verification
  • Site Acceptance Test (SAT) — full charge/discharge cycle, RTE verification
  • Provisional Acceptance Certificate (PAC) issued
Phase 6 — Handover & Operations (Weeks 34+)
  • Client handover & documentation package (manuals, as-built drawings, certs)
  • O&M team takes over 24/7 monitoring (Voltus SCADA)
  • LTSA contract activation
  • Defects Liability Period begins (12 months)
  • Final Acceptance Certificate (FAC) issued after defects period
