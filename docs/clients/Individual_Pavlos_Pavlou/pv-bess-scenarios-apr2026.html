Key Assumptions & Flags
  • AE Solar Meteor yield: 2,150 kWh/kWp south (monofacial 1,950 + 10.3% bifacial gain at 80% bifaciality / albedo 0.70); 1,450 kWh/kWp EW (~5% bifacial gain at low tilt). Based on Agios Theodoros RTB confirmed yield methodology.
  • BESS sizing: 4h × peak PV kW. All 4 systems (0.38–1.68 MWh) are sub-Linyang minimum (5.015 MWh). Practical BESS would use sub-5MWh commercial units (Huawei/Sungrow). €127k/MWh rate applied proportionally from Agios RTB confirmed stack.
  • Bank financing: 65% LTV, 5.5% fixed, 12yr annuity (factor 0.116). Cyprus green/SME loan typical range. Assumes RES license and net metering contract as collateral support.
  • O&M: PV €15/kWp/yr + BESS €2,500/MWh/yr + monitoring/insurance €2,000–2,500/yr flat. Based on Agios Theodoros opex stack (pvOm €15.15/kWp, bessOm €2,462/MWh).
  • Plot 2 title is Provisional (Προσωρινός τίτλος, Art.10 Law 44/84) — confirm upgrade before permitting. Both plots are agricultural — Town Planning Permission required.
