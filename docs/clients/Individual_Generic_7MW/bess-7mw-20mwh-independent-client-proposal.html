1. Executive Summary

This proposal presents a detailed financial analysis for deploying a 7 MW / 20 MWh Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) alongside a 7 MWp fixed-tilt bifacial PV park in Cyprus.

All market data is sourced exclusively from 134 verified TSOC Day-Ahead Market (DAM) Excel files covering every trading day from 1 October 2025 to 11 February 2026 — the complete dataset since the Cyprus Competitive Electricity Market launch.

The analysis demonstrates that BESS deployed for curtailment recovery — the only legal revenue model for BESS in Cyprus as of February 2026 — delivers a compelling return on investment, transforming otherwise-wasted solar energy into peak-priced revenue.

Key finding: A 7 MW PV park loses €419,808/year to curtailment. A 20 MWh BESS recovers this energy and generates €406,291 net income in Year 1, paying for itself in 5.9 years and producing €2.41M in pure profit over 15 years.

Additionally, Lighthief Cyprus offers to acquire Ready-to-Build (RTB) stage solar projects at a maximum price of €350,000 per MW.

3. Regulatory Context

As of February 2026, BESS in Cyprus cannot buy electricity from the DAM grid.

BESS revenue is legally limited to curtailment recovery only:

  • Charge cost = €0 — energy is stored from the park's own curtailed solar production (DSO dispatch-down orders)
  • Discharge at peak evening prices — verified average €182.99/MWh
  • Grid arbitrage (buy at midday, sell at peak) requires new legislation — expected but not yet enacted
  • Future arbitrage, when legal, will provide additional revenue on top of curtailment recovery

13. Disclaimers

  1. Market prices shown are historical averages from the first 134 days of the Cyprus open market. Future prices may vary.
  2. Curtailment rate (25.8%) is the 2024 Cyprus national average. Individual park curtailment rates may be higher or lower depending on location, DSO district, and grid conditions.
  3. BESS regulatory status is current as of February 2026. Grid arbitrage requires legislation that has not yet been enacted.
  4. Independent client pricing includes a 10% premium over group EPC rates. This covers standalone project management, SCADA, and logistics without group efficiencies.
  5. All figures exclude VAT, grid connection costs, and land-related expenses.
  6. PV revenue calculations assume open-market (merchant) selling. PPA contracts would yield different figures.
