Expandable architecture: All 8 × BCS1000K PCS units are installed at delivery. The system is software-limited to 6 MW for tender compliance (≥3h duration). Expansion to 8 MW is achieved programmatically — no hardware changes required. The T8 MV Skid with SL-8000 transformer (8,000 kVA) supports up to 16 battery containers (80 MWh) in a 1:1 PCS-to-container ratio, making the system highly flexible for future capacity expansion.

PCS-to-Container Architecture

Each PCS connects to battery containers via DC cables (1500 Vdc). The T8 skid supports flexible PCS-to-container ratios:

Phase 1 connection: 2 PCS per container (2:1 ratio). Each container receives two dedicated 1.0 MW PCS connections via DC cables. The BMS coordinates charge/discharge across both PCS units per container for balanced operation.

Expansion flexibility: When additional containers are added, the PCS-to-container ratio transitions from 2:1 to 1:1, maintaining uniform C-rates and balanced degradation across all strings.

Tender compliance: 6 MW exceeds the ≥5.4 MW requirement. 3.34h duration exceeds the ≥3h requirement. 7,000 warranted cycles is nearly double the 3,650 required (1 cycle/day × 10 years). Grid code: EN 50549-2 TÜV certified.

Export capacity: The 14 MW overhead line to PSEUDAS S/S provides headroom for combined wind + BESS export. The EMS enforces combined export limits automatically, coordinating with the existing wind farm controller via IEC 60870-5-104.

Ormazabal unit: Confirmed as spare wind turbine RMU — not used for BESS connection. BESS MV connection is via a new ABB ZX1.2-compatible bay (J04) included in this offer.
