1. Executive Summary

Lighthief Cyprus Ltd proposes a turnkey solar carport system at Limassol Tennis Center, Parekklisia: three carport sections over the main parking area (30 bays), with bifacial PV on pre-fabricated steel frames.

The club operates until 22:00 with significant evening load (floodlighting, clubhouse, pumps). We recommend Option B — PV + 264 kWh battery storage, sized to store approximately 4 hours of peak summer production and discharge into the evening session.

Estimated annual solar output is ~137 MWh. At an assessed electricity value of €0.28/kWh, the system can deliver up to ~€39,400/year in bill offset from solar generation (subject to load profile confirmation once EAC billing is available).

6. Financial Estimate — €0.28/kWh

Assessment uses €0.28/kWh as the value of each kWh offset against EAC imports. No historical EAC bills are available yet — model assumes club operations until 22:00 with high evening load during floodlit sessions.

Important: This estimate covers the solar generation value only. Evening grid imports for floodlighting (17:00–22:00) will remain significant. The battery improves capture of afternoon surplus on high-production days but cannot fully cover all evening demand year-round. Final economics will be confirmed from EAC billing data.
