2. Scope — two transformers (separate line items)

T1 — Main power transformer

  • 63 MVA continuous (ONAN/ONAF), 132/33 kV, YNd11, uk = 21% (75 °C, CMR, HV base)
  • OLTC +12.5% / −18.75%, 25 × 1.25% steps (MR/ABB vacuum type), local/remote/supervisory
  • Outdoor, oil-immersed; Ecodesign Tier 2 (EU 2019/1783), PEI compliant; EN 60076 series
  • LV insulation: 170 kV BIL / 70 kV AC (36 kV class); HV: 550 kV BIL / 230 kV AC; HVN 38 kV AC
  • 2 cooler banks @ 50% CMR; dual WTI (HV+LV hotspot); online DGA/monitoring; EN 60296 oil; EN 60137 bushings; EN 60214 OLTC; silica-gel breather; flat base + anti-vibration pads; EN 795 anchorage; impact recorders w/ GPS
  • State no-load loss, load loss and PEI %.

T2 — Earthing & auxiliary transformer (separate unit)

  • 33 kV / 400–230 V; zig-zag (interconnected-star) earthing winding establishing & solidly earthing the 33 kV neutral (no NER)
  • Earth-fault withstand ≥ 20 kA for 3 s (or 25 kA for 1 s) per EN 60076-5
  • Auxiliary winding ≥ 315 kVA @ 400/230 V, 3-ph 4-wire (upratable — quote 315 / 400 / 500 kVA, price per step)
  • 36 kV class insulation (170/70); EN/IEC 60076 + EN 60289

Return a signed clause-by-clause compliance statement (COMPLY / DEVIATE + note). Explicitly confirm: YNd11 (not Dyn11), uk 21%, 170/70 LV insulation, Tier 2 Ecodesign PEI, and that T2 is included. State no-load loss, load loss and PEI % for T1 and T2 — these drive the evaluation.
