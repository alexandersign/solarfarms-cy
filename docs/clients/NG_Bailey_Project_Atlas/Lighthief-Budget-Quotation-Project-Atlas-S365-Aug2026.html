2. Design basis (assumptions — as required by S 365)

The ITT asks for a ground-mounted PV system with a total annual output of 1,330,000 kWh and an approximate panel area of 5,900 m². Those two figures are consistent with a UK-type specific yield (~900 kWh/kWp ≈ 1.48 MWp). RAF Akrotiri is not a UK irradiance site.

  • Option A — recommended, priced below. Treat 5,900 m² as module area: approximately 1.50 MWp DC (circa 2,550–2,600 × ~580–590 W modules, fixed tilt, south). At Akrotiri we expect ~1,700–1,850 kWh/kWp, so annual generation of about 2.55–2.78 GWh — roughly double the ITT energy figure. AC export is constrained by the specified 1,000 kVA transformer; PV and BESS will be EMS-limited so simultaneous export does not exceed that rating.
  • Option B — energy-led alternative. Size PV to deliver ~1.33 GWh/year at Akrotiri: approximately 0.76 MWp (~2,900 m² modules). BESS, HV/LV plant, cable and road remain as specified. Budget €2,582,000 ex VAT (indicative £2,221,000).
Please confirm which driver is binding for the Pricing Document: the energy target, the panel area, or the 1,000 kVA grid interface. We have priced Option A so the physical plant in S 365 is covered. Downsizing to Option B is a straightforward deduction if 1.33 GWh is the actual station load requirement.

Electrical arrangement (assumed)

  • PV string inverters and 800 kW PCS on a 0.6 kV collector bus, stepping up through the specified 1,000 kVA 11/0.6 kV transformer.
  • Auxiliaries via 0.6/0.4 kV auxiliary transformer and LV board (lighting, HVAC, BESS ancillaries, small power).
  • 11 kV RMU arranged for ring-in / ring-out plus transformer feeder, with allowance to joint onto the existing HV ring.
  • BESS usable energy 3,450 kWh LFP, PCS 800 kW (~4.3 hour), remote monitoring brought to the HV intake in the generator compound.
  • Container: 20 ft ISO high-cube, C5 coating, IP54, ventilation and cooling, fire alarm, off-gas detection, gas suppression. If OEM packing cannot house 3.45 MWh and 800 kW PCS inside a single 20 ft box without derating the battery, the PCS will be an outdoor C5 cabinet immediately adjacent inside the same fenced energy compound — one system, no extra cost in this budget.

4. Equipment location — advice from the drawings issued

The Client has not marked a PV / BESS location. The four ADP GAs show two tight operational compounds (T-House and Generator / NER) and a large mast field inside the RADHAZ fence. Neither compound has a spare 5,900 m², and placing arrays among the 68 mast foundations would conflict with coaxial routes, RADHAZ access and RF clearances.

Recommended arrangement (for discussion with Ramboll / ADP / DIO at outline design):

  1. Energy compound (BESS + HV/LV plant + 75 m² switchroom) — immediately adjacent to the Generator Compound, not inside it. The generator building already contains the HV intake; S 365 requires BESS monitoring at that intake. The generator compound is already occupied (fuel store, generator, NER, UPS, standoff / loading clear zone, gravel pocket). A small fenced energy yard on the west or south side of the generator fence keeps the 20 ft container, 1,000 kVA transformer, RMU, aux TX and switchroom on short tails to the existing HV, and avoids the T-House RADHAZ / HVAC / hydrant plant.
  2. PV array — on open ground outside the occupational RADHAZ fence and outside both compounds, preferably on the more level ground west / north-west of the T-House compound as shown on the Existing Site GA. Target a compact south-facing block of about 1.5–1.8 ha including row spacing (Option A). Keep clear of:
    • the western tombolo / Akrotiri Salt Lake ecology and any Appropriate Assessment buffers;
    • the public-boundary timber RADHAZ fence and mast maintenance aggregate;
    • the existing SLS compound (S205.02 — no impact unless Client-approved);
    • the principal surfaced access to Akrotiri Village / RAF Akrotiri;
    • flood corridors — compounds on the GAs sit on perimeter embankments; the array should not reverse that drainage logic.
  3. 500 m HV cable — the dashed corridor on the Existing Site GA between the T-House plot and the Generator Compound is the natural dual-circuit route (matches the 500 m allowance). Use that corridor rather than a new cross-country trench through the mast field. Jointing to the existing HV ring should be at the generator-side RMU / intake, under a planned outage.
  4. 1,000 m × 6 m road — the Site GA already shows a proposed perimeter track and a 6 m double gate at the T-House. Before measuring 1,000 m again, confirm whether S 365 is duplicating that track or only the PV-field spur. If the perimeter track is in the main civils, drop A3 and retain only a short spur into the array (we can re-measure). If S 365 is a standalone loop around the PV field plus a link to the generator energy compound, keep A3.
We can overlay a sketch layout on the Site GA once you confirm Option A vs B and whether A3 stays in. A drone survey is not available (S205.14 — drones prohibited); layout would be from issued topographical information plus ground survey under a photo permit.
