Two points to flag before you lock the number:

1. Energy vs area. 1,330,000 kWh/year with 5,900 m² of modules is a UK-yield pair. At RAF Akrotiri the same 1.50 MWp array will generate about 2.6 GWh/year. If the binding requirement is 1.33 GWh, we can downsize PV to ~0.76 MWp (Option B, €2,582,000). The 1,000 kVA transformer also caps simultaneous PV + BESS export; we have assumed EMS limiting unless you need a 2,000 kVA unit.

2. Siting. Neither the T-House nor the Generator compound has room for 5,900 m² of modules. We recommend a small energy compound (BESS + HV/LV + switchroom) immediately outside the Generator Compound, next to the HV intake, with the PV field on open ground outside the occupational RADHAZ fence, west/north-west of the T-House plot. The 500 m HV route fits the corridor already shown between the two compounds. The 1,000 m road may duplicate the perimeter track on the Site GA — if that track is already in the main civils, omit our line A3 and the budget becomes €2,523,400.
