How revenue works

  • Uncurtailed PV sold on TSOC DAM daytime (~€141/MWh)
  • Curtailed PV (~50%) charges the BESS at €0 energy cost
  • Evening discharge at €210/MWh — the actual 18:00–21:00 DAM average from our market data
  • RTE 86.3% · curtailment capture 87.4% (Cyprus operational calibration)
  • No DAM arbitrage yet under Cyprus rules — capacity products = upside
