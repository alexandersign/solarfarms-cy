1. Executive Summary

Lighthief Cyprus Ltd submits this EPC proposal as independent EPC bidder for TOTALCON LTD's licensed solar park at Konia, Temachio 150, Ierokipia, Paphos.

Pricing is split into three distinct packages — PV equipment supply, PV EPC works, and BESS EPC works only (client-supplied battery equipment). Two PV configurations are quoted to match the existing September 2025 layout studies.

Grid connection is expected at low voltage (LV). For Cyprus RES projects up to approximately 1.5 MW, the DSO (EAC) typically provides the LV step-up transformer at the point of connection; transformer cost is charged on the EAC connection terms offer and is outside Lighthief EPC scope. Formal connection terms for this site are pending — TOTALCON expects preliminary terms within approximately one week.
