with a 13.2 MWh battery retrofit

Operational since July 2025Grid connectedFor saleCERA Ε004836/20253.3 MWp13.2 MWh retrofitNicosia District

A 3.3 MWp park at Margí in the Nicosia District, built by Lighthief Cyprus and exporting since July 2025, offered for sale with a 13.2 MWh four-hour battery retrofit. The park is already generating and already being curtailed, which means the storage case here is not a forecast: the curtailed volume is metered. For an investor who wants Cypriot cash flow this quarter rather than after a construction programme, this is the asset in our pipeline that delivers it.

 Unlike the new-build blocks, the battery here has no solar revenue to lose, so its return rises directly with curtailment. The site’s own metered rate of 47–50% is the conservative anchor; the national 2026 rate of 65% is the base case. We would expect diligence to settle on the site’s own 2026 metered data, which we will provide.
Note on scale. At 3.3 MWp this park sits inside M2’s 5 MWp envelope on its own, but combining it with Agios Theodoros would take the portfolio to 5.94 MWp, above the stated ceiling. If the ceiling is firm, the two are alternatives rather than a pair; if it is indicative, we would put the case for taking both.

Basis. Wholesale prices from published TSOC day-ahead market files, 1 Oct 2025 – 11 Feb 2026 (134 trading days, 6,432 half-hourly prints): daytime average €127/MWh, evening peak (17:00–21:00) average €183/MWh. This pack takes €175/MWh for evening capture, €8/MWh below the measured average. Curtailment base case of 65% is the published national rate for January–May 2026 (162 GWh curtailed); 2025 full year was 47.4% (306 GWh, CyprusGrid). Specific yield of 2,150 kWh/kWp · yr is the Lighthief design figure for fixed tilt 25° south with bifacial modules and white albedo; site-specific yield is confirmed by PVsyst before any commitment. Battery round-trip efficiency 87.8% AC–AC; effective capture of curtailed energy 85%, being daily-shape collection multiplied by round-trip efficiency. EPC rates €0.72/Wp (PV, turnkey) and €127,000/MWh (BESS, ex VAT) per the Lighthief EPC workbook v4, February 2026, and are indicative pending final specification and equipment pricing at the date of order. Revenues are stated after a 10% aggregator and offtake fee and before Cyprus corporate income tax, which rose from 12.5% to 15% on 1 January 2026. Figures are unlevered and exclude VAT. Merchant exposure to a day-ahead market with under two years of published price history is the single largest uncertainty in this case; no revenue is contracted.
