A packed east–west bifacial plant at Sotira, Famagusta District, sized to the 6,534 kW town-planning layout (Poleodomia). Independent check with JRC PVGIS 5.3 (SARAH3, 2005–2023) at 35.01084°N, 33.94224°E, plus a 2D rear-side model: 11,803 MWh/year AC, 1,806 kWh/kWp. Lighthief Cyprus can deliver EPC on this geometry. Not a bankable PVsyst P50/P90 and not a permitted RTB pack.

Investment highlights

  • Site-fit DC. 10,052 × 650 W modules, 359 strings of 28 — sized to the 6,534 kW Poleodomia layout.
  • No inverter clipping. 20 × Huawei 330 kW; DC/AC 0.99.
  • Measured resource, not a brochure yield. Front 1,743 kWh/kWp; +3.6% bifacial at albedo 0.25.
  • EU jurisdiction. Cyprus CIT 12.5%; wholesale DAM in operation.
  • Clear next work. Bankable PVsyst, grid application, EPC contract. Town planning is in process, not complete.
