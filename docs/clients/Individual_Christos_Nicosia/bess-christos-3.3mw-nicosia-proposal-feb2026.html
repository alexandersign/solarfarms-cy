1. Executive Summary

This proposal presents two turnkey BESS configurations designed to combat the 47% curtailment affecting your 3.3 MWp solar park in Nicosia. Both systems use 10 MWh of storage — enough to capture the vast majority of your wasted energy — with different power ratings to suit your preference.

Your park currently wastes 3,257 MWh per year to DSO dispatch-down orders, equivalent to €459,000 in lost revenue annually. A BESS system transforms this waste into peak-priced income by storing curtailed energy during the day and discharging it during the high-value evening window.

All market data is sourced from 134 verified TSOC Day-Ahead Market files — the complete dataset since the Cyprus competitive electricity market launched.

Bottom line: Both options deliver ~28% Year 1 ROI and pay for themselves in under 4 years. Over 15 years, either system generates €3.4–3.5M in pure profit from energy that would otherwise be destroyed.

Both systems share identical 10 MWh storage, identical containers, and nearly identical ROI. The difference is €62,300 in upfront cost for €127,800 more cumulative profit — an incremental return of 205%.

4. How BESS Curtailment Recovery Works

As of February 2026, BESS in Cyprus cannot buy electricity from the DAM grid.

BESS revenue is legally limited to curtailment recovery only:

  • Charge cost = €0 — energy stored from your park's own curtailed production. This energy would otherwise be completely wasted.
  • Discharge at peak evening prices — verified average €182.99/MWh (17:00–21:00, 134 days TSOC data)
  • The BESS transforms €0 waste energy into €183/MWh peak revenue
  • Future grid arbitrage will provide additional revenue when legislation permits — not included in this model
