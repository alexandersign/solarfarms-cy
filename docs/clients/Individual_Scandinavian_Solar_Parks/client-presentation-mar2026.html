What “Turnkey” Really Means — The True Cost of EPC

Beyond the Equipment Price: What Turnkey EPC Actually Delivers

Most battery integrators quote an FOB or CIF equipment price — the cost of the containers and inverters sitting on a factory floor in China. That number represents roughly 70–75% of the true installed cost. The remaining 25–30% is a complex web of logistics, permits, civil engineering, electrical works, protection systems, and insurance that the asset owner must somehow procure, coordinate, and execute. We include all of it.

⚠ The Comparison Trap: When evaluating BESS offers, clients often compare our turnkey €/kWh against an integrator’s FOB €/kWh. These are fundamentally different numbers. An FOB quote leaves you with containers on a dock and a long list of problems to solve. Our price delivers a grid-connected, commissioned, insured, and warranted system — ready to earn revenue on day one.

EPC Complexity & Subcontractor Coordination

Transport & Logistics — The Hidden Complexity

Each BESS container weighs 28–34 tonnes and is classified as dangerous goods (lithium batteries). Moving them from Limassol port to your solar park sites requires a chain of permits, specialist vehicles, and coordinated authorities — all managed by Lighthief.

RequirementDetailWho Handles It
ADR CertificationAll BESS containers contain lithium-ion batteries (UN3481/UN3536). Transport must comply with EU ADR regulations — certified vehicles, trained drivers, placarding, emergency documentationLighthief + A. Soulis
Overweight PermitsAt 28–34t per container, each load exceeds standard road weight limits. Individual permits required from Department of Public Works per route, per containerLighthief + A. Soulis
Police EscortOverweight/oversized loads require mandatory police escort on public roads. Scheduling must coordinate with Cyprus Police traffic division — 15-day advance bookingLighthief
Route PlanningBridge weight limits, road width restrictions, turning radii for 6-axle trailer, height clearances — surveyed and pre-approved for every park locationLighthief + A. Soulis
