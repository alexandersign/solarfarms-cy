## 1. Executive summary

Serbia is moving from a **relatively administrative** balancing and procurement model toward **market-based electricity trading and ancillary services**, with rule changes clustering around **2025–2026**. For battery energy storage (BESS), the combination of **renewables build-out**, **EU alignment pressure**, **interconnection and regional coupling ambitions**, and **new balancing / “active buyer” concepts** improves the **economic and bankability case** for storage versus the prior decade—though **revenue stacking, grid access, and dispatch rules** still require project-specific modelling and confirmed EMS/URS practice.

**Proposed local structure:** a Serbian limited-liability company (**`d.o.o.`**, often referred to informally as an “LTD-equivalent”) with **Mirjana Filipović (“Mira”)** as **primary local partner and proposed managing director**, leveraging long-standing **ministry-level energy-sector experience** and credibility with **state-owned utilities and regulators**. Public background: in a **2017** interview she described priorities including **security of supply**, **market development**, **RES and efficiency**, and **EU Chapter 15–related reforms** (SEEPEX, market coupling dialogue, oil reserves, EPS/Srbijagas restructuring context). Source: [CorD Magazine](https://cordmagazine.com/interview/mirjana-filipovic-state-secretary-ministry-mining-energy-energy-safety-serbias-priority/).  

**Action:** before any incorporation, run **KYC, conflicts, and current-role verification** (2017 title ≠ today’s mandate).

## 3. Market context

### 3.1 Power system and policy drivers

- **Demand growth and decarbonisation:** Regional commentary highlights **need to expand generation capacity** and integrate **hydrogen / transition** narratives into longer-term strategy (e.g. recent government statements reported in regional press; treat as directional).
- **EU accession alignment:** Energy chapter work historically emphasised **third energy package–style** rules: **liberalised supply**, **transparent markets**, **TSO/DSO roles**, and **environmental compliance** for legacy thermal assets. Mira’s 2017 interview framed **security of supply** as the **first priority**, with **market development, efficiency, RES, and environmental measures** as structural goals under the **national energy strategy** horizon to **2025 / 2030** (see [CorD](https://cordmagazine.com/interview/mirjana-filipovic-state-secretary-ministry-mining-energy-energy-safety-serbias-priority/)).
- **Regional integration:** Historical emphasis on **SEEPEX**, **WB6** cooperation, and dialogue on **market coupling** (e.g. **4M MC** references in the same period). Deeper coupling increases **price convergence risk** but also **liquidity and arbitrage** for flexible assets.

### 3.2 Market design evolution (2025–2026) — BESS-relevant

Public summaries of **late-2025** reforms point to:

- **Electricity Market Code / Energy Law** updates supporting **more competitive** participation.
- **Ancillary services:** shift toward **transparent procurement** (e.g. **auction-style** frameworks discussed for **2026**), with **published reserve dimensions** for products such as **FCR, aFRR, mFRR** (magnitudes cited in third-party analyses — validate against **EMS** official publications before bidding).
- **Balancing energy pricing:** move toward **marginal / pay-as-cleared** style activation (reports cite **April 2026** as a milestone for some balancing-energy rules — **confirm** with lawyer and EMS).
- **“Active buyer”** and **hybrid producer–consumer–storage** concepts in commentary — potential path for **industrial + BESS** behind-the-meter and **front-of-meter** flexibility if implemented as described in secondary sources.

**Sources to triangulate:** [Energy Transition Consulting — ancillary services 2026](https://energytransition.rs/the-opening-of-the-ancillary-services-market-in-serbia-2026-a-new-regulatory-and-market-framework/), [Balkan Green Energy News — market rules](https://balkangreenenergynews.com/serbias-new-electricity-market-rules-bring-important-changes/), [SEE Energy News — BESS bankability](https://seeenergy.news/bankability-of-battery-energy-storage-systems-in-serbia-grows-amid-market-changes/), [PV Europe — solar / market rules](http://www.pveurope.eu/markets/serbia-new-market-rules-support-solar-expansion).

### 3.3 Prosumer / DG transition

Commentary indicates **net metering** giving way to **net billing** toward **end-2026**, increasing **market exposure** for distributed solar and—if rules allow—pairing with **storage** for self-consumption optimisation and export behaviour. **Verify** exact deadlines and compensation formulas in **by-laws**.

### 3.4 Revenue themes for BESS (illustrative, not a forecast)

Industry commentary cites **day-ahead / intraday spreads** on the order of **tens of €/MWh** under volatile conditions, plus **balancing / ancillary** revenues once markets open competitively. **No project should rely on a single blog figure** — use **EMS historical prices**, **SEEPEX** data, and **scenario analysis**.

## 4. Competitive and ecosystem notes

- **Incumbents:** **EPS** group companies remain central to **generation, supply, and distribution**; reforms and **corporate form** evolution (historically toward **joint-stock** structures) affect **counterparty risk** and **PPA dynamics**.
- **Gas / flexibility:** **Srbijagas** and **interconnection** projects (e.g. historical **Niš–Bulgaria** corridor discussion in 2017 interview) matter for **CHP and gas peaking** competition to BESS for flexibility.
- **Developers:** Solar acceleration pulls in **regional IPPs** and **Chinese / EU EPC** tiers; BESS attach rates will rise where **curtailment, grid constraints, or hybrid incentives** exist.
