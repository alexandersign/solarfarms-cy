Investment thesis

Why Cyprus

  • Island balancing zone with liquid TSOC day-ahead market
  • Persistent evening vs daytime price spread (~€183 vs ~€141/MWh)
  • High solar resource; tracker + bifacial + white albedo at 2,400 kWh/kWp
  • Eurozone · EU rule of law · 15% CIT (from 1 Jan 2026)

Why this configuration

  • 4-hour BESS sized 4 MWh per MWp — matches hybrid curtailment capture
  • Daytime uncurtailed solar sold at DAM; curtailed energy shifted to evening peak
  • Turnkey EPC (PV trackers + BESS) with RTB ticket + EAC connection priced separately
  • Editable Excel accompanies this teaser for sensitivity work
Technology. 700 Wp N-type TOPCon bifacial modules on single-axis trackers over white ground (albedo ~0.70). BESS: Linyang LFP, ~5.015 MWh containers, AC–AC RTE 86.32%, LTSA €1,740/MWh/year (97% availability target). Revenue model applies a 87.4% curtailment-capture factor and a 280 full-cycle-day capacity constraint (Galascope-calibrated).
