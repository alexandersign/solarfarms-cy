2. Clarifications that change the price

Please confirm the following. Where the RFP gives two answers, we have used the bold assumption.

  1. Capacity. The design basis states DC 22–26 MWp; the BoQ states 52 MWp (1.30 DC/AC). We have priced 52 MWp. Which is correct?
  2. Mounting. The RFP states fixed tilt or tracker, then specifies single-axis trackers (Nextracker / Arctech), then the BoQ is fixed tilt. We have priced fixed tilt. Please confirm.
  3. Modules. The make-list states Trina 570 Wp; the BoQ assumes 620 W. We have allowed a 620 W-class bifacial monocrystalline module from the approved list (or equivalent). Please confirm acceptable makes and wattage.
  4. BESS. A Sungrow BESS plant is in the equipment list, with no MWh, duration, or use case. Is BESS in current scope? If yes: power (MW), energy (MWh), hours, and application (smoothing / shifting / grid code).
  5. Grid voltage. Technical text allows 33 kV / 66 kV; the BoQ is 33 kV. We have priced 33 kV at the plant boundary. Please confirm voltage, point of interconnection, distance, and the grid operator (UETCL / UEDCL / other).
  6. Transmission line. RoW and access-route survey are excluded. What is the line length from each plant to the POI, and who designs, procures, and builds it?
  7. Two sites or one contract. Are Busunju and Pader two separate grid connections and two Taking-Over Certificates, under one EPC contract?
  8. Land. Please provide coordinates, cadastral, title/lease, and confirmed acreage for each site. The RFP range of 80–150 acres is too wide to fix civil quantities.
  9. Wind and lighting. Wind is given as ≥150 km/h and as 180–190 km/h. Periphery lights are “No”, then 80 lights per plant. Which governs?
  10. Permits. Construction permits are in EPC scope; interconnection and development permits are excluded. Please issue a permit matrix (Employer vs Contractor) including ERA generation licence, ESIA, and grid connection offer.
  11. Performance LDs. Please state delay LD rate and cap, and performance LD rate and cap, plus the guaranteed annual energy (MWh/year per plant). “~30–60 GWh” is not a number we can guarantee.
  12. Taxes and currency. Contract currency (USD / EUR / UGX)? Is the EPC exempt from import duty and VAT on solar equipment, or must tax be added to the price?
  13. Contract form. FIDIC Yellow Book, Silver Book, or Employer draft? Please share the draft EPC agreement.
  14. Effective Date. What are the conditions precedent (land, grid offer, notice to proceed, first payment)? The 24-month clock cannot start on RFP issue.
  15. Local content. Any Ugandan content, employment, or nominated-subcontractor requirements?
