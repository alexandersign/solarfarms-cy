## B. Important — Required for Detailed Engineering & Pricing

### B1. Technical Clarifications

The specification references several **Indian standards and practices** (IS codes, BIS, DISCOM ABT metering, rooftop loading limits). For a Uganda ground-mount utility project we propose aligning to **IEC standards and Uganda grid requirements**.

**Please confirm acceptance of:**

- **String inverters** (or multiple central inverters) rather than a single central inverter per 20 MW
- **Optimal tilt for equatorial site** (~5–15° fixed tilt) rather than 28–45° "south-facing" configuration
- **IEC/IEEE** grid interconnection standards applicable in Uganda
- Target **DC capacity (MWp)** and **AC capacity (MWac)** for each site
