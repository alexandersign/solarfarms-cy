Specification received — key parameters noted:
• Capacity: 2×20 MW grid-connected solar PV
• Sites: AGOGA (~60 ac / 24.3 ha, UTM 36N) and MISOZI (boundary data incomplete in spec)
• Evacuation: 33 kV AC, metering at plant boundary
• Scope stated: EPC + commissioning + performance guarantee + comprehensive O&M (11 months post-COD)
• Equipment: Fixed-tilt structures, crystalline modules, 33 kV switchyard, SCADA, CCTV, weather station
Initial observation: The specification references several Indian standards and practices (IS/BIS codes, DISCOM/ABT metering, rooftop loading limits). For a Uganda ground-mount utility project we propose aligning to IEC standards and Uganda grid requirements. Please confirm acceptance in Section B1.
