Technical Solution

Battery Energy Storage

  • Manufacturer: Jiangsu Linyang Energy Storage Technology (China)
  • Model: Power Atlantic ME 5.015 MWh / ME 4.179 MWh
  • Container: 20HC (6,058 × 2,438 × 2,896 mm)
  • Chemistry: LFP (Lithium Iron Phosphate)
  • Cell: EVE LF314 (314Ah)
  • Cooling: Liquid cooling (45–60 kW)
  • Protection: IP55 rated, C5 corrosion (coastal)
  • Fire Suppression: Integrated, UL 9540A certified
  • Cycle Life: 7,000 cycles (90% DOD, 0.5C, 70% EOL)
  • Round-Trip Efficiency: ~87.8% (AC-to-AC system level)

Power Conversion & Grid

  • PCS Manufacturer: Xiamen Kehua Digital Energy
  • Model: BCS1250K-C-HUD (1,250 kW each)
  • Max Efficiency: ≥99%
  • Power Factor: >0.99
  • THDi: <3%
  • IP Rating: IP65, C5 corrosion
  • Grid-Forming: VSG, Black-Start, VF Mode
  • MV Transformer: Integrated in MV Skid (oil-immersed, 99%)
  • Switchgear: Schneider Electric RMU
  • Communication: Modbus TCP, IEC 104, IEC 61850
