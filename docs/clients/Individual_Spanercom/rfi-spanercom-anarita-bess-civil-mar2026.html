C. Topographic Survey & Slope Assessment CRITICAL — REGULATORY REQUIREMENT

Regulatory Notice — Κ.Δ.Π. 15/2026 & Κ.Δ.Π. 17/2026 (Official Gazette No. 5992, 16 January 2026)

The Cyprus planning decree for energy storage systems at licensed power stations includes the following conditions that directly affect civil works at Anarita:

Condition (10): "Η εγκατάσταση του συστήματος αποθήκευσης ενέργειας δεν αλλοιώνει την τοπογραφία ή/και τα υψόμετρα του φυσικού εδάφους και εντάσσεται στο περιβάλλον."
"The installation of the energy storage system does not alter the topography and/or the elevations of the natural ground and integrates into the environment."

Condition (6): Maximum height of installations must not exceed 3 metres from "the natural or the final formed ground level (whichever is lower)." The use of "τελικό διαμορφωμένο επίπεδο" (final formed level) acknowledges that some ground formation may occur.

Appendix II, Item 10 (Κ.Δ.Π. 17/2026): Requires submission of "elevations of the existing / proposed ground" (υφιστάμενου / προτεινόμενου εδάφους). Additionally:
• Plots with height differences ≥ 2 metres require a mandatory topographic and elevation survey
• Plots with slope > 5% require additional A3 drawings with detailed elevations, cross-sections, and fencing plans

Implication for Anarita: Localised foundation preparation (grading, compaction, concrete pads) under the BESS footprint is permitted, but large-scale earthworks that alter the site's overall topographic character are not. We must document existing vs proposed ground levels and demonstrate minimal alteration. If the proposed BESS area has slopes > 5% or elevation changes ≥ 2m, additional engineering documentation must be submitted to the planning authority.
Action Required: We need accurate existing topography at both proposed BESS locations to (1) design platforms that comply with the decree, (2) minimise cut & fill, and (3) prepare the mandatory planning submission if slope exceeds 5%. The TDD notes steep slopes in parts of the site — this must be confirmed with survey data.
