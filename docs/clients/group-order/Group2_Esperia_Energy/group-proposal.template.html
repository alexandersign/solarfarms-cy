EPC Scope — Esperia Energy Group

EPC Scope of Works

The turnkey prices in this proposal cover the items below. Items marked excluded are paid separately by the Client or the Client’s engineer unless expressly added in writing.

✓ Included in EPC Price

  • BESS containers (LFP battery + BMS + HVAC + fire suppression)
  • PCS/MV Skid (inverter + transformer + MV switchgear)
  • EMS system (Voltus — energy management + SCADA)
  • Shipping to Limassol + import customs clearance
  • Crane offloading + inland transport to site
  • Civil works: concrete platforms, trenches, drainage
  • LV & MV cabling (DC bus, AC feeder connections)
  • MV terminations and protection engineering (settings, coordination for BESS compound)
  • Surge protection (DEHN SPD) + earthing grid (internal compound)
  • Remote trip / SCADA communications infrastructure (hardware & integration)
  • UPS & auxiliary power supply
  • Construction insurance (CAR/EAR, TPL, PI)
  • Documentation & statutory compliance support (EAC/CERA submissions for BESS works)
  • ETEK installer sign-off — Costas Hadjikyriacou (MEng/MSc, ETEK): installation compliance declaration, protection settings attestation, and EAC liaison for the BESS installation (included in group pricing)
  • Commissioning — full cold/hot commissioning with OEM and Lighthief teams
  • DSO witness — Lighthief coordinates and attends the EAC witness session; demonstrates protection settings and SCADA per DSO requirements

✗ Excluded / Client Responsibility

  • Land / site lease and preparation beyond civil works
  • Grid connection agreement and PCC bay extension (if needed)
  • External lightning protection system (LPS — building-mounted, DEHN quoted per site)
  • EAC/DSO charges — connection fees, capital contribution, inspection/witness fees invoiced by EAC (paid by Client to EAC)
  • Third-party protection testing fees — relay testing contractor invoices where EAC requires separate certification (budget ~€1,250 per container; Client or Client’s engineer)
  • Licensed electrical design drawings — permit drawings, licensed SLD packages, and formal as-built engineering drawings for the wider site (€5,000–€15,000 per site, typical range). Not included in group pricing; separate from installer sign-off above.
  • Telecoms backhaul (SIM/data/fibre OPEX to DSO dispatch centre)
  • VAT (19%) — payable by importer of record
  • CERA licence application fees
  • Any works beyond the battery storage compound boundary
Scope clarification for contract discussion (Galascope / group pricing):
  • Included: Lighthief’s ETEK installer sign-off (Costas Hadjikyriacou) for the BESS compound installation, protection settings, and EAC submission support.
  • Excluded: Licensed electrical design / permit / as-built drawing packages prepared by the Client’s engineer (or appointed consultant) for the full site — not part of the group €/kWh price.
  • DSO witness: Lighthief attendance and coordination are included; any EAC inspection or protection-test invoices are Client-paid unless expressly added to Schedule A.

These points will be mirrored in the signed EPC Schedule A. Confirm alignment at commercial terms meeting.
