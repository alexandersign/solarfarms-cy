1. Background and Purpose

Two documents are required from Linyang as Conditions Precedent to the advance payment under the Galascope EPC Agreement (Annex V6 §1A.5): (i) the OEM Direct Warranty Undertaking (DWU) to the End-Customer, Galascope Ltd; and (ii) a 5% corporate Performance Guarantee (PG) to the same beneficiary. Draft versions of both, reflecting our required wording, are attached. This memo sets out the specific points that must match our drafts exactly before signature and sealing, and explains why each point matters.

The EPC Agreement itself gives the Client (Galascope) a 9-month corporate performance guarantee directly from Lighthief Cyprus Ltd (5% of the Contract Price, running 3 months Defects Liability Period + 6 additional months). That guarantee is intended to be backed back-to-back by Linyang's own Performance Guarantee. At present, the PG draft you have from us only runs 3 months (to the end of the DLP) — the amendments below close that gap.

2. OEM Direct Warranty Undertaking (DWU) — updated SOH curve, please sign as drafted

The attached DWU draft already reflects the manufacturing-defect carve-out we require (§5.3: the standard 10% aggregate liability cap in your Warranty Manual v2 §XI does not apply to confirmed manufacturing defects — Linyang bears full repair/replacement cost including shipping to CIF Limassol, uncapped, for confirmed manufacturing defects only). We are not requesting any further change to that wording. Please review, sign and seal the DWU as attached. This signed document is itself a Condition Precedent to our advance payment, so please prioritise turnaround.

Note — SOH floor at DWU §2.1 has been updated on our side; please confirm you agree. We cross-checked your own "Power Atlantic 5MWh Degradation Curve" reference document against Galascope's actual operating profile: both Galascope 1 (5 MW / 20.06 MWh) and Galascope 2 (2.5 MW / 10.03 MWh) are ~4-hour systems, i.e. the 0.25P, 1-cycle/day condition in your curve, not 0.5P (a prior working assumption on our side). Under that condition your own reference document shows End of Year 5 = 86.78%, End of Year 10 = 79.58%, End of Year 15 = 73.61%. We have updated DWU §2.1/§2.2 (and cycle life at §2.4, to 8,000 cycles) in the attached draft to match your own degradation-curve data exactly — these are now the same figures used in the EPC and LTSA, so the DWU is fully back-to-back with our Client-facing commitment with no residual gap. Please confirm you agree with these figures before signing; if Linyang's technical team would instead prefer to hold a more conservative floor as a deliberate margin, please tell us in writing so we can revert and record the position. This does not block signature of the DWU, but please turn it around before PAC — separately, we are still waiting on your technical team, via Costas Hadjikyriacou, on the 2.7V vs 2.8V cell-voltage threshold and pre-PAC charging responsibility in the Technical Agreements.

3. Required Amendments to the OEM Performance Guarantee (PG)

# Clause Current Draft Wording Required Wording Basis / Rationale
1 Validity period — CRITICAL Clause (6): "From execution until the end of the Defects Liability Period, being three (3) months after PAC." "From execution until twelve (12) months after PAC." If twelve (12) months cannot be agreed, nine (9) months after PAC (matching the Defects Liability Period plus six (6) additional months) is our minimum acceptable fallback. Actual dates are set by the Delivery Schedule confirmed at Connection Terms; no fixed calendar date applies at signing. Twelve (12) months is the Client's own lawyer's explicit ask (Galascope legal review, EPC comment #2: "extend validity to at least 12 months post PAC"). Nine (9) months is the floor — it matches the Lighthief Cyprus Ltd corporate performance guarantee to the Client under the EPC Agreement (§7.1(d)). At present your PG expires 6-9 months before ours does, leaving Lighthief uncovered for that period.
2 Cure period on demand Clause (7): "...a thirty (30) day cure period (immediately on insolvency or abandonment); payment within thirty (30) days." Reduce the cure period to fifteen (15) days (retain immediate demand on insolvency or abandonment). Payment within thirty (30) days of a valid demand is acceptable as drafted. 30 days is a long window before a demand can even be made; 15 days is more consistent with the risk profile of a live construction project and with market practice for corporate performance guarantees of this type.
3 Parent-company backing Clause (8): "No parent-company guarantee is provided unless separately agreed." Please confirm whether a guarantee or comfort letter from Linyang's ultimate parent or group holding entity can be provided in support of this Performance Guarantee, on terms to be agreed. If not available, please confirm this in writing so we can record the position. A corporate guarantee from a Chinese operating subsidiary, without parent backing, governed by PRC law with SHIAC arbitration, is materially weaker security than the Lighthief Cyprus Ltd guarantee it is meant to back. We are not blocking on this point, but need a clear answer either way.
4 Guaranteed Amount Clause (2): EUR 141,158 (5% of the Component A equipment price, ~EUR 2,823,169). No change requested. We note for the record that this is smaller than the 5%-of-Contract-Price basis (~EUR 173,142) used for the Lighthief Cyprus Ltd guarantee, since the OEM guarantee is tied to equipment value only (Component A), consistent with the EPC's decoupled Component A/B structure. Acceptable as drafted — flagged for transparency only, not a required change.
5 Governing law / arbitration Clause (11): PRC law, SHIAC Shanghai, in English. No change requested at this time. Accepted as the OEM's standard position, consistent with the DWU and OEM Warranty Terms.
