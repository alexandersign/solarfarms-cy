Working capital note: M1 is front-loaded at 25% (€155,283) — this funds the civil/platform works that happen before equipment arrives. Lighthief civil cost (Kouklis) is €98,420; the remaining €56,863 provides operating working capital for the pre-equipment mobilisation phase. Under the old blended 30% advance, Lighthief received €1,033,290 upfront — the decoupled model separates equipment (Component A, APG-secured) from works (Component B, milestone-based), so the works working capital is now explicit in M1 rather than hidden in a blended advance.
