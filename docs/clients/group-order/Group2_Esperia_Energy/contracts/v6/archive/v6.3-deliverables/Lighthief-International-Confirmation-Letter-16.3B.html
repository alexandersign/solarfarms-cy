Dear Sirs,

Lighthief International Ltd ("Lighthief International", the "Undertaker"), the parent company of Lighthief Cyprus Ltd (HE 477423) ("Lighthief Cyprus", the "Contractor"), hereby provides this corporate performance undertaking in favour of Galascope Ltd (the "Client") in connection with the EPC Agreement referenced above (the "Agreement"), pursuant to Annex V6 Section 16.3B of that Agreement.

1.  Scope of Undertaking

Lighthief International irrevocably undertakes to the Client that, in the event that Lighthief Cyprus fails to perform any of the following obligations under the Agreement, Lighthief International will, upon written demand from the Client and subject to paragraph 3 below, procure the performance of those obligations or itself perform them:

  1. Equipment delivery: delivery of the BESS equipment (Galascope 1 and Galascope 2) to the Project Site, CIF Limassol, in accordance with the Delivery Schedule confirmed at Connection Terms;
  2. Installation and commissioning: installation, commissioning and achievement of the Provisional Acceptance Certificate (PAC) in accordance with the Agreement;
  3. Five-year EPC warranty: performance of the Contractor's warranty obligations during the five (5) year warranty period from the Warranty Start Date, including rectification of defects in the Works and Equipment attributable to Lighthief Cyprus's EPC scope.

2.  Nature of This Undertaking

This is a direct corporate obligation of Lighthief International, governed by Cyprus law. It is not a bank guarantee and does not constitute a payment instrument. This undertaking is:

  1. limited to the obligations described in paragraph 1 — it does not extend to financial liabilities beyond the EPC Contract Price, to LTSA or EMS services (contracted separately), or to OEM obligations guaranteed under the OEM DWU and OEM Performance Guarantee;
  2. conditional upon the Client having first made a written demand on Lighthief Cyprus and afforded it fourteen (14) days to cure;
  3. in addition to, and not in substitution for, the Contractor's obligations under the Agreement.

3.  Duration

This undertaking remains in force for the duration of the five (5) year EPC warranty period and terminates automatically upon expiry of that period. Delivery of this letter to the Client before the advance payment under Annex V6 Section 1A.5(e) constitutes delivery for the purposes of the Agreement.

4.  Governing Law

This undertaking is governed by the laws of the Republic of Cyprus. Any dispute shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of Cyprus or, at the Client's election, to arbitration in accordance with Section 15 of the Agreement.
