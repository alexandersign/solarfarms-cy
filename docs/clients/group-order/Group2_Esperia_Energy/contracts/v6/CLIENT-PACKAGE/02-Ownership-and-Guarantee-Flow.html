How the >10% defect risk is covered. The manufacturer's product warranty caps its liability at 10%, so the protection against a large proportion of equipment being faulty is built in earlier and in layers: (1) Bank cover on the equipment supply — APG #1 (€705,792, 25% of the equipment supply value) is issued before the advance and APG #2 (€1,411,585, 50% of the equipment supply value) at the FAT, before the pre-shipment payment: €2,117,377 of first-demand bank cover securing the manufacturer's equipment refund. This is OEM-side equipment security — it is not 100% of each milestone: your milestone payments (30% / 55% of the total contract price) also include EPC services, and that portion (and any balance above the APG figures) is covered by the layers below — FAT before payment, the 5% performance guarantee, the 5% retention, and your possession and title of the equipment — not by the APGs; (2) FAT — defects are identified and fixed before the pre-shipment payment and before shipment; (3) APGs kept alive to PAC — because faults typically appear at commissioning, the guarantees run through to PAC (or 12 months after delivery), so a major non-conformity discovered at commissioning can be recovered; (4) extended rejection window — the client's right to reject/require remedy runs to PAC, not just 30 days after delivery. Alongside these: CAR/erection insurance (full replacement value, from port of discharge — the sea leg sits with the OEM under CIF), the 5% performance guarantee, the 5% retention, and the 5-year OEM warranty & DWU. Cover is continuous, end to end.
Title and APG release — both branches. Title passes to Galascope on the earlier of (i) PAC and the PAC payment, or (ii) 12 months after delivery to Site. The APGs expire on the same earlier-of basis (PAC or 12 months), so ownership never lags the end of APG cover on either branch. If title passes under branch (ii) before PAC, Lighthief retains a security interest in the equipment for any unpaid balance until the PAC payment is made. After the APGs release at PAC, protection continues under the 5% performance guarantee and 5% retention (to DLP end) and the 5-year OEM warranty & DWU.
