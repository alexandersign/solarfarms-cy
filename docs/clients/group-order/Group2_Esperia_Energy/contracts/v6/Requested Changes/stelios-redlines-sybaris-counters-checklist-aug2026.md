## Part 1 — Stelios asks where Sybaris pushed back with a counter-proposal

These are the items where Lighthief did **not** simply accept Stelios's wording — Sybaris proposed narrower/different terms, and that counter is what actually landed in v6.5.

- [x] **§1A.8 — Condition subsequent (Companion Documents).** Stelios/Anastasios asked: EPC becomes void *ab initio* at Client's election if any Companion Document (LTSA, DWU, APGs, OEM PG, EMS Addendum, Technical Agreements) isn't executed by the Documents Long-Stop Date — no cure, Client not obliged to accept anything, no waiver by part-performance. **Sybaris countered:** a Deficiency Notice + 30-Business-Day cure period *per document*, termination rights scoped to the individual lagging document (not the whole Agreement), and the "neither Party obliged to agree" language made mutual rather than Client-only. **Landed:** Sybaris's version, in full.

- [x] **§7.1A / §7.4(b) — Interim performance guarantee period + Lighthief International back-up.** Stelios asked: 9 → 12 months, flagged as "WAITING FOR LIGHTHIEF INTERNATIONAL BACK UP GUARANTEE" (implying an open-ended parent guarantee). **Sybaris countered:** accepted the 12-month figure, but drafted the Lighthief International guarantee (new Schedule D, §16.3B) to trigger **only on Contractor insolvency/liquidation** — not general non-performance, delay, or dispute while solvent — and capped it to the same §13 liability limits as the Contractor itself. **Landed:** 12 months in §7.1A/§7.4(b); Schedule D drafted on Sybaris's insolvency-only, capped terms.

- [x] **§7.2 / §7C — VAT clauses deleted in full.** Stelios's redline struck the entire Article 11B reverse-charge clause and the import-VAT/customs clause, leaving only a short "SECTION 11B RULES APPLY" note at §6.1(a). **Sybaris / internal counter:** keep the fuller VAT legal language rather than deleting the substantive protection, and add an express reservation for the Contractor to invoice VAT later if the Cyprus Tax Department rules against reverse-charge treatment. **Landed:** consolidated §7.2 with the reservation language; nothing deleted outright.

- [x] **§11.5 — Interpretation-on-conflict rule.** Baseline (previously accepted, logged in the response matrix): "the interpretation most favourable to the Client governs." **Sybaris countered:** replaced with a fixed order of precedence — EPC > LTSA > APGs/OEM PG > OEM DWU/Warranty Terms > EMS Addendum > Technical Agreements. **Landed:** Sybaris's hierarchy. ⚠️ *This reopens something already granted to the Client — flagged as the highest relationship-risk item in the safety analysis; watch for pushback.*

- [x] **§13.3A — Additional liability carve-outs.** Client-side pressure (Timotheos's list, B13) was pushing to *broaden* the uncapped carve-outs. **Sybaris countered the other way:** latent defects uncapped only if claimed within 2 years of PAC (then reverts to the normal §13.2 cap); the safety/grid-code carve-out narrowed to non-conformity existing as of PAC and attributable to the Contractor, excluding post-PAC law changes or Client acts; added a definition of "latent defect." **Landed:** Sybaris's narrower, time-limited version — Timotheos's list itself still went into §13.5 separately (see Part 3, B13).
