1. Background and Purpose

We have received the Bank of Communications draft Advance Payment Guarantee dated [July 2026]. Galascope Ltd (the Beneficiary) has reviewed the draft through their lawyer Stelios Constantinou and has provided agreed wording. However, his markup was made against our specimen template, not against the Bank's actual issued draft. This document reconciles the three versions and provides the Bank with a precise, clause-by-clause list of the amendments required to bring their draft into an acceptable form.

Two APGs are required under the EPC Agreement (§10.9):

  • APG No. 1 — Advance: EUR 705,792 (25% of Component A equipment supply value). Secures the advance payment.
  • APG No. 2 — Pre-Shipment: EUR 1,411,585 (50% of Component A). Issued at the pre-shipment milestone (FAT). A separate draft is required for this instrument; differences from APG No. 1 are noted in Section 4 below.

PAC-or-12-months is the agreed validity in EPC §10.9(d). Expiry date not a calendar date — event-linked. Agreed by both parties.
6 Demand mechanism — CRITICAL "must be presented in paper form through your bank by courier service… received at our counter: 11 Floor, No.218 Lushan Road, Jianye district, Nanjing, Jiangsu, China" Demand may be presented: (i) by authenticated SWIFT message from the Beneficiary's bank confirming that authorised signatories have signed the demand; or (ii) in writing by registered letter or by hand delivery to the Bank's designated European correspondent (or to the Bank's branch as notified). Nanjing-only courier delivery is not acceptable. Beneficiary is in Cyprus. Requiring courier to Nanjing makes demand practically impossible. SWIFT or EU-correspondent-bank demand is the market standard. If the Bank cannot offer SWIFT, appoint a European confirming/paying bank through which demands can be presented locally.
7 Payment timeline Not specified (implied on receipt) The Bank shall pay the demanded amount within five (5) Business Days of receipt of a complying written demand, without set-off or deduction, without requiring prior recourse to the Applicant. 5-business-day payment is standard for URDG 758 demand guarantees. "Without set-off" protects the Beneficiary from EPC disputes being used to block payment.
8 Liability reduction / coming into force "come into force from our receipt of the statement by the Applicant stating that it receives the advance payment" This guarantee comes into force on receipt by the Applicant of the Advance Payment to the Applicant's account (IBAN/SWIFT to be inserted). Our liability reduces by each amount paid or refunded under this guarantee. The Guaranteed Amount shall not be reduced merely on presentation of shipping documents or a bill of lading. No bill-of-lading reduction clause is critical — prevents the guarantee being eroded by shipment alone before delivery and inspection at Site. Agreed by Stelios.

4. Differences — APG No. 2 (Pre-Shipment, EUR 1,411,585)

APG No. 2 requires the same amendments as above (items 1–11) plus the following specific differences:

  • Amount: EUR 1,411,585 (Euro One Million Four Hundred Eleven Thousand Five Hundred Eighty-Five), being fifty percent (50%) of the Component A Equipment Supply Price.
  • Trigger: APG No. 2 comes into force on receipt by the Applicant of the pre-shipment payment; it is issued at the completion of Factory Acceptance Testing (FAT) and before shipment.
  • Engineer-certificate reduction: The Guaranteed Amount under APG No. 2 shall be reduced by amounts certified by the supervising engineer (Aioliki / Glekas — to be confirmed by Galascope) as properly applied against completed EPC works. The Guaranteed Amount shall not be reduced merely on presentation of shipping documents or bill of lading. Reduction certificates to be sent to the Bank by registered letter or SWIFT.
  • Validity: Same PAC-or-12-months trigger.
