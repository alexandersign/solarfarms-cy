2.3 Technical Boundaries and Interface Responsibility
The O&M scope covers the BESS, BMS, PCS, MV/MVS switchgear, transformers within the contractual scope,
auxiliary systems, HVAC/liquid cooling, fire detection and suppression systems, SCADA gateway, EMS, DSO
telemetry and all interconnections and control functions between these systems.

Lighthief is responsible for end-to-end BMS-PCS-SCADA-EMS compatibility, communications, command
execution and data integrity throughout the service period. Failure of an affiliate, DISPERON or a
subcontractor does not release Lighthief from this responsibility towards the Client.

    EXTERNAL BOUNDARY: Grid issues beyond the point of connection, unagreed civil works and third-party equipment may be
    excluded from the calculation only to the extent that they directly caused the relevant consequences. Monitoring, diagnosis,
    safe disconnection, impact mitigation, documentation and warranty-claim support remain required.

7.3 Incident Handling Workflow
    1. Detect and retain the alarm source timestamp.

    2. Acknowledge and classify the event and assess the risk to people, warranty and availability.

    3. Apply safe isolation or shutdown where required and authorised.

    4. Notify the Client and the relevant persons in the escalation matrix.

    5. Perform remote diagnosis, secure the logs and open a work order.

    6. Initiate local/OEM/DSO/emergency-services dispatch and confirm site access.

    7. Restore safe partial or full operating capability and document any limitations.

    8. Complete the permanent repair, recommissioning and functional test.

    9. Determine the root cause and define corrective and preventive actions.

    10. Close the incident after evidence acceptance, register updates and submission of the Client report.

7.4 Major Incident
The Major Incident Procedure applies to fire/thermal events, repeated trips, forced outages exceeding 24

     • Client notification - immediately.

    4. Provide emergency services with the location, LFP chemistry, SDS, diagram, stored energy, suppression
         status and possible adjacent hazards.
