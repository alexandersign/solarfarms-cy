## Key points we have built in for you

1. **You never pay ahead of the security.** The advance is conditional on the APG + signed DWU + performance guarantee being in your hands.
2. **APG names Galascope directly** as beneficiary (and your lender's security agent), issued by the manufacturer's bank, and is **kept alive to PAC** (or 12 months after delivery) — so if a material proportion of equipment is found faulty **at commissioning**, the advance can still be recovered.
3. **Factory Acceptance Test (FAT) is your first line of defence against major defects.** You (or your inspector) witness factory testing; any defects are fixed **before** the 55% pre-shipment payment and **before** shipment. This addresses the "what if a large share of equipment is faulty" risk up front — ahead of, and stronger than, the manufacturer's 10% warranty liability cap.
4. **Extended rejection window.** Your right to inspect, reject, and require remedy runs to **commissioning / PAC**, not just 30 days after delivery — matching when faults actually appear.
5. **Price moves both ways.** The confirmed price tracks the lithium index and EUR/CNY against January 2026 references: downward moves are passed to you; upward moves are capped at 5%; either side can walk away above the cap. If the order is more than 6 months out, the price is re-quoted on the same two-way basis (EPC §6.1(h)).
6. **Continuous protection.** APG (to PAC) → CAR/erection insurance at full replacement value from port of discharge (the sea voyage is covered by the manufacturer's CIF marine) → 5% performance guarantee → 5% retention → 5-year OEM warranty &amp; DWU. See the Ownership &amp; Guarantee Flow sheet.
7. **3-month defects period** backed by retention + performance guarantee + OEM warranty.

## Note — grid-forming (VSG) &amp; black start

The PCS supplied (Kehua C-series) is **hardware-capable** of grid-forming (VSG) and black start; these are **firmware-enabled**. The current Technical Agreement V2.1 lists them as **excluded** — we will add them by amendment to the Technical Agreement after review.

- **Hardware is identical**, so we expect **no change to the equipment price**; any OEM firmware licence fee (if any) will be confirmed.
- Whether activation happens **pre-shipment (tested at factory)** or by remote update later depends on the **DSO grid-code requirement** for this site, which we are confirming.
- We will reflect the agreed position in the Technical Agreement amendment before the order trigger.

*We have flagged this so it is transparent; it does not affect signing the EPC now.*
