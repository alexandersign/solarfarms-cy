3. Future Batches — Committed Pipeline

9 additional parks (285.5 MWh) including 5 Esperia Tseri 2028 parks

The Client confirms its intention to execute EPC Agreements for all remaining parks within six (6) months of the Batch 1 PAC date, subject to:

  • Successful commissioning and performance of the Batch 1 systems;
  • Availability of grid connection approvals from EAC/DSO;
  • Finalisation of site-specific technical designs;
  • Agreement on pricing for subsequent batches (based on the prevailing Lighthief pricing schedule or as separately agreed).

4. LTSA Framework

The Client confirms its intention to enter into a Long-Term Service Agreement (LTSA) covering all parks in its portfolio. The LTSA shall:

  • Commence for each park from its PAC date;
  • Apply a group-level 97% availability guarantee calculated across all active parks;
  • Accumulate parks into the availability calculation as they are commissioned;
  • Be priced per MWh per year in accordance with the agreed LTSA pricing schedule.

5. Purpose of this LOI

This LOI is provided to Lighthief Cyprus Ltd to:

  • Confirm the Client's commitment to the full portfolio (not just Batch 1);
  • Support Lighthief's procurement planning with its OEM equipment supplier;
  • Evidence the pipeline for financing and insurance purposes;
  • Establish the basis for the group-level LTSA framework.
Non-Binding: This LOI is a statement of intent and does not constitute a binding obligation to execute EPC contracts for future batches. The binding commitment for Batch 1 is contained in the EPC Agreement executed concurrently. Future batch EPC contracts will be executed separately upon mutual agreement.
