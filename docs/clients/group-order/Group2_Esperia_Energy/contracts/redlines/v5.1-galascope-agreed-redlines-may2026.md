## 1. Force Majeure — §8.4.7A (replace / supplement)

**Principle:** Only **true** FM under §12.1 may flow through from OEM. Excluded events (commercial/production delay, financing, port congestion, etc.) **cannot** be passed through.

**Replace §8.4.7A with:**

> **8.4.7A Upstream Force Majeure (OEM Flow-Through).**
>
> (a) Where the OEM has formally notified the Contractor in writing of an event it claims is Force Majeure under the upstream supply contract, the Contractor may notify the Client within seven (7) Business Days, attaching a copy of the OEM notice.
>
> (b) The Client’s Target PAC Date shall extend **only if** the notified event **constitutes Force Majeure under Section 12.1** of this Agreement (as reasonably demonstrated by reference to the §12.1 definition and exclusions). **No extension** applies where the event falls within the exclusions in Section 12.1 (including ordinary commercial or production delays, raw material sourcing difficulties, port congestion, or financing difficulties).
>
> (c) If the Client reasonably contends that the event is **not** Force Majeure under Section 12.1, the Client shall notify the Contractor in writing within **ten (10) Business Days** of the Contractor’s notice under (a). The Target PAC Date shall **not** extend during any dispute period.
>
> (d) If the Parties do not agree within **thirty (30) days** of the Client’s notice under (c), either Party may refer the matter to dispute resolution under **Section 15.3**. No Target PAC Date extension is deemed agreed unless and until resolved in favour of FM qualification.
>
> (e) Where the event **is** agreed or determined to be Force Majeure, the Target PAC Date extends day-for-day for the period of the qualifying Upstream Force Majeure. **No Delay LDs** accrue during that period.
>
> (f) The Contractor shall use reasonable endeavours to challenge any OEM notice that clearly does not meet Section 12.1.

**§8.4.6(vii):** Retain — Upstream FM per §8.4.7A is an exclusion from Delay LDs only when (b)–(e) satisfied.

## 4. Planning — §5A.1(f) (new)

> **(f)** The Client shall use **reasonable endeavours** to obtain planning permissions and landowner consents within **sixty (60) days** of the Effective Date. Where delay by a planning or competent authority beyond the Client’s reasonable control causes delay to the Target PAC Date, the Target PAC Date extends day-for-day. The Contractor shall not be entitled to additional cost reimbursement beyond **reasonable and documented standby costs** directly caused by such planning delay. The Client shall notify the Contractor promptly of material planning delay.

> **13.2** The Contractor’s aggregate liability shall be:
>
> **(a)** For claims relating to **Contractor’s own EPC services, installation, and non-OEM works** (excluding OEM equipment): **ten percent (10%)** of the Contract Price;
>

> **(c)** For **manufacturing defects in OEM equipment** and fraud or wilful misconduct: **uncapped**, with the Contractor’s right to pursue OEM recovery per Section 13.4.
