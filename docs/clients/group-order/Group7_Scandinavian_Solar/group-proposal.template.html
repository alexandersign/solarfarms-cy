EPC Scope & Specifications

What’s Included

✓ Included in turnkey price

  • Battery storage containers (LFP chemistry with integrated cooling & fire suppression)
  • Power conversion system (inverter, transformer & MV switchgear / skid)
  • Energy management & monitoring (Voltus — DSO compliant)
  • Shipping to Limassol, import duties & customs clearance
  • Crane offloading & transport to your site
  • Civil works: concrete platforms, cable trenches & drainage (within BESS compound)
  • Electrical cabling, terminations & protection engineering
  • Surge protection & earthing system
  • SCADA communications infrastructure & site UPS
  • Construction-phase insurance (CAR/EAR scope as per contract)
  • Regulatory documentation support (EAC/CERA pathway)
  • Commissioning & DSO witness testing

✗ Not included

  • Land or lease
  • Grid connection works beyond the BESS MV point of coupling (if applicable)
  • External lightning protection (quoted separately per site)
  • DSO inspection & protection testing fees (client-paid item)
  • VAT (19%)
  • CERA licence fees
  • Licensed electrical drawings for building permit (typically €5,000–€15,000 per site)
  • Works outside the battery compound boundary
