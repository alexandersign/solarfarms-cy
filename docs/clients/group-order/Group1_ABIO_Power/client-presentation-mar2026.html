EPC Scope & Technical Specifications — ABIO Power

EPC Scope of Works

✓ Included in EPC Price

  • BESS containers (LFP battery + BMS + liquid cooling + fire suppression)
  • PCS/MV Skid (Kehua inverter + transformer + MV switchgear)
  • EMS/SCADA system (Voltus — energy management + monitoring)
  • CIF shipping to Limassol + import duties + customs clearance
  • Crane offloading + inland transport to site
  • Civil works: reinforced concrete platforms, trenches, drainage
  • LV & MV cabling (DC bus, AC feeder connections)
  • MV terminations and protection engineering
  • Surge protection (DEHN SPD) + earthing grid
  • Remote trip / SCADA communications infrastructure
  • UPS & auxiliary power supply
  • Construction insurance (CAR/EAR, TPL, PI) via Marsh
  • Documentation & statutory compliance (EAC/CERA)
  • Full commissioning, DSO witness testing & grid synchronisation

✗ Excluded / Client Responsibility

  • Land / site lease and preparation beyond civil works
  • Grid connection agreement and PCC bay extension (if needed)
  • External lightning protection system (LPS — DEHN quoted per site)
  • Protection testing / DSO inspection fees (€1,250 per container)
  • VAT (19%) — payable by importer of record
  • CERA licence application fees
  • Licensed electrical drawings (€5,000–€15,000 per site)
  • Any works beyond the battery storage compound boundary
