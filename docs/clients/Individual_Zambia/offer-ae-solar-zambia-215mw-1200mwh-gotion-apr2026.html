Zambia Distribution Grid Code (May 2016) — Compliance Matrix

Validated against the Zambia Distribution Grid Code (May 2016). Plants >10 MVA must comply with both this Distribution Code and SI-79 Zambian Grid Code per §2.7.5.1.1.
RequirementCode Clause & Exact RequirementLighthief / Gotion / Sineng Compliance
SI-79 Applicability §2.7.5.1.1Embedded Generators >10 MVA shall comply with SI-79 Zambian Grid Code in addition to this Distribution Code.At 215 MW, full SI-79 compliance mandatory. Assessment included in engineering scope. ZESCO/SO connection agreement defines site-specific requirements.
Nominal Frequency §7.3.5.1Nominal fundamental frequency shall be 50 Hz. Units shall remain in synchronism for ≥5 s.System set for 50 Hz. All Sineng EH-1250kW PCS units factory-configured for 50 Hz Zambia grid.
Over/Under Frequency Protection §7.3.10.5U/F and O/F relays disconnecting outside 48.75–51.25 Hz (ZS 387).Sineng PCS includes two-stage U/F and O/F relays. Trip at 48.75 / 51.25 Hz per ZS 387. Settings submitted to DNSP/SO before commissioning.
Active Power Ramp Rate §7.3.8.1.1 & 7.3.8.1.3Limit both increase and decrease to ≤10% of nominal per minute.EMS ramp-rate limiter: ≤21.5 MW/min (10% of 215 MW). Programmable per dispatch instruction.
Active Power Curtailment §7.3.8.2Accept absolute, delta, and gradient constraints. Commence within 2 s, complete within 30 s.EMS supports all three modes via IEC 60870-5-101 to ZESCO SCADA. Response: <2 s commence, <30 s complete.
Voltage Regulation §7.3.4.7Maintain voltage between 0.90 pu and 1.10 pu at interconnector bus.Sineng PCS continuous voltage regulation. MV/HV design maintains POC voltage within 0.90–1.10 pu.
Phase Jump Ride-Through §7.3.4.4Withstand phase jumps up to 20°; resume normal production within 5 seconds.Sineng EH-1250kW PCS rated for phase jump ride-through per IEC standards. EMS ensures recovery within 5 s.
Reactive Power & Voltage Control §7.3.6.3–7.3.6.6Capable of voltage, power factor, or Q/MVAr control modes; one mode active per DNSP/SO instruction.Sineng PCS supports all three modes. Active mode and setpoints agreed with DNSP at connection agreement stage.
Harmonics §7.3.7.1THD of voltage and TDD of current at POC shall not exceed ZS 387 limits.Sineng PCS output THD <3% (IEC 61000-3-2). Harmonic study and commissioning measurement included.
Voltage Unbalance §7.3.7.2Voltage unbalance at POC not to exceed ZS 387 limits.Balanced three-phase output by design. Negative sequence <1% (per §7.3.10.3). Measured at commissioning.
Negative Sequence Voltage §7.3.10.3Contribution to negative sequence voltage at POC shall be less than 1%.Protection study included. Negative sequence contribution verified during commissioning <1%.
Synchronising Facilities §7.3.3.1–7.3.3.2Synchronising relay and sync-check relay per unit; settings submitted to SO.Sync and sync-check protection included. Settings submitted to SO before commissioning.
Over/Under Voltage Protection §7.3.10.6U/OV relays disconnecting on abnormal voltages.Sineng PCS includes two-stage U/OV relays. Settings agreed with DNSP.
OC & Earth Fault Protection §7.3.10.4OC and EF protection to trip on fault.OC/EF protection at PCS and POC level. Coordination study submitted to DNSP per §2.7.5.1.4.
Reverse Power Protection §7.3.10.8Trip on power flow from grid to generator (except agreed charge mode).BESS bidirectional: discharge = generation (reverse power applies). Charge = intentional agreed mode. EMS manages with DNSP-agreed settings.
Fault Ride-Through §7.3.10.9FRT required; DNSP defines specific fault scenarios.FRT capability included in Sineng EH-1250kW PCS. Ride-through curves defined with DNSP at connection agreement stage.
Fault Levels §7.3.10.10Fault level assessment required before connection; RPP may bear mitigation costs.Fault level study included. Results submitted to DNSP before connection agreement.
Grounding §7.3.10.11Equipment rated for max surge during ground faults per DNSP earthing method.Grounding design coordinated with DNSP. Equipment rated accordingly.
Black Start §7.3.9.1Generator shall have black start capability.Black start capability included in scope. Requirements agreed with DNSP/SO at connection agreement stage.
DC Current Injection §7.3.4.6DC content shall not exceed 0.5% of rated nominal current.Sineng EH-1250kW PCS DC injection <0.5% by design; verified in factory type test certificates.
Protection Testing §7.3.10.13DNSP to conduct periodic testing within two years.FAT (China) and SAT (site) records provided at commissioning. Ongoing 2-yearly testing is DNSP’s responsibility; RPP provides access per §8.4.2.
