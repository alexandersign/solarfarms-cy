1. Executive Summary

This proposal presents a turnkey battery energy storage system (BESS) designed for your 2.64 MWp solar park in Agios Theodoros, Larnaca — planned to energize in December 2026.

Every solar park in Cyprus today faces curtailment — periods when the grid operator (DSO) forces your park to reduce or stop production because the grid has too much solar power. When your park is curtailed, your solar panels are producing electricity, but you are not allowed to sell it. That energy is destroyed.

A BESS solves this by capturing curtailed energy that would otherwise be wasted and storing it in batteries. The stored energy is then discharged and sold during the high-price evening window when electricity is most valuable.

Bottom line: Your park will produce ~4,752 MWh per year. With a projected 55% curtailment rate, roughly €368,000/year of your energy will be wasted — unless you install a BESS. This system recovers that lost revenue and turns it into €360,000 annual net income, paying for itself in just 4 years.

2. The Problem: Solar Curtailment in Cyprus

2.1 What Is Curtailment?

Curtailment occurs when the Cyprus DSO (Distribution System Operator) orders your solar park to reduce or stop feeding electricity into the grid. This typically happens during peak sunshine hours (10:00–14:00) when too many solar parks are generating at the same time and the grid cannot absorb all the power.

When you are curtailed, your park is producing electricity but you earn zero revenue from it. The energy is simply lost.

2.2 Why Curtailment Is Increasing

Cyprus has been rapidly adding solar capacity. The TSOC Day-Ahead Market data shows that 42.5% of all trading days (57 out of 134 days since the market launched) have had periods where the electricity price dropped to €0/MWh during solar hours. This is a clear signal of growing oversupply during daytime — exactly when your park produces.

With more solar parks coming online every quarter, curtailment rates are widely expected to increase from current levels. Parks energizing in late 2026 will face higher curtailment than those that started years ago.

3. The Solution: Battery Energy Storage (BESS)

3.1 How a BESS Makes Money

A BESS is a large-scale battery system installed next to your solar park. It works in a simple cycle every day:

1
DSO Curtails

Your park is ordered to reduce output during peak solar hours

2
BESS Charges

Instead of wasting the energy, it flows into the battery — at zero cost

3
Evening Arrives

Electricity prices peak between 17:00–21:00 as demand rises

4
BESS Discharges

Stored energy is sold at peak prices — avg €183/MWh

3.2 Why the Economics Are So Attractive

Charge at €0, discharge at €183. Because you are storing your own curtailed energy (not buying from the grid), the BESS effectively creates revenue from nothing. Every kWh stored is a kWh that would have been destroyed.
Important regulatory note: As of February 2026, BESS in Cyprus cannot buy electricity from the DAM grid — revenue is limited to curtailment recovery only. When future legislation enables grid arbitrage, your system will generate additional revenue not included in this financial model.
