## BATTERY ENERGY STORAGE (BESS)

### Why do I need battery storage?

Cyprus faces significant **solar curtailment** - the grid operator reduces solar output when supply exceeds demand:
- 2021: 0% curtailment
- 2023: 25% curtailment
- 2025: **45%+ curtailment** on peak days

Without BESS, you lose up to 45% of potential revenue. With BESS:
- Store excess energy during curtailment
- Discharge when grid accepts power
- **Recover 50%+ of curtailed revenue**
- Earn premium rates for evening dispatch

### What battery technology do you use?

We supply **Linyang LFP (Lithium Iron Phosphate)** battery systems:

| Round-Trip Efficiency | 87.8% system (AC-AC) |

| Fire Safety | UL 9540A certified |

### How long do batteries last?

With proper operation:
- **15-18 years** to 70% State of Health (SOH)
- **Base Warranty**: 5 years included (manufacturer)
- **Extended Warranty**: Up to 15 years with LTSA (Long-Term Service Agreement)
- **Degradation**: ~2-2.5% capacity loss per year (operating condition dependent)

> **Note**: Coastal installations (< 500m from sea) may have reduced warranty periods for PCS/transformer components unless C5-rated enclosures are specified.

Our LTSA packages include SOH performance guarantees with module replacement or augmentation remedies if degradation exceeds OEM specifications.

## GRID CONNECTION & REGULATIONS

### What grid connection categories exist in Cyprus?

| Category | Capacity | Operator |
|----------|----------|----------|
| A | Up to 8 kW | DSO (EAC) |
| B | 8 kW - 100 kW | DSO (EAC) |
| C | 100 kW - 8 MW | DSO (EAC) |
| D | Above 8 MW | TSO (TSOC) |

Most utility-scale projects (1-10 MW) fall under **Category C** with DSO connection.

### What certifications are required?

Cyprus requires **EN 50549-2** certification for grid-connected systems:
- Voltage and frequency ride-through
- Reactive power capability
- SCADA communication (IEC 60870-5-104)
- Protection coordination

All our systems are pre-certified for Cyprus grid requirements.

### How long does grid connection take?

Typical timelines:
- **Application Processing**: 2-4 months
- **Technical Studies**: 1-2 months
- **Construction Permit**: 1-2 months
- **Connection Works**: 2-4 months

Total: **6-12 months** from application to energization.

## LANDOWNERS

### I own land - can I host a solar farm?

Yes! We offer several options for landowners:

1. **Land Lease**: Receive 6-8% of annual revenue as lease payment
2. **Land Sale**: Sell land with development rights at premium
3. **Joint Venture**: Partner in the project for equity stake
4. **Self-Development**: We build on your land, you own the asset

### What land is suitable for solar?

Ideal characteristics:
- **Size**: Minimum 1.5-2 hectares per MW
- **Terrain**: Flat or gently sloping (under 10%)
- **Access**: Road access for construction vehicles
- **Grid**: Proximity to MV/HV grid infrastructure
- **Zoning**: Agricultural or industrial (not residential)

We provide free land assessments including:
- Satellite imagery analysis
- Grid connection feasibility
- Capacity estimation
- Revenue projections

### What areas are restricted?

Development is restricted in:
- Natura 2000 protected areas
- Forest zones
- Military buffer zones
- Cultural heritage sites
- Flood plains and wetlands

Our assessment identifies any "no-go zones" on your land.
