This Model Works: Commission Extractable at Every Client Payment, VAT Always Positive, Zero Debt

By combining April 1 start (clean Q1), client-as-importer (zero import VAT), and 30/50/15/5 client terms vs 25/50/20/5 Linyang terms, the operating cash position stays positive every month. Commission of 30% of net margin (€3,492K total) can be safely extracted at each client payment milestone.

Cyprus Import VAT: Deferred Payment Is NOT Available — Client-as-Importer Is the Only Clean Solution

Cyprus has not implemented EU VAT Directive Article 211(b) (postponed accounting). Import VAT must be paid in cash at customs clearance. There is no deferment scheme for standard importers. A bonded warehouse can suspend VAT until release, but adds €30–90K in costs and logistics complexity. The only way to eliminate the €16.8M import VAT drain is to have the client named as importer of record — they pay import VAT at customs and recover it on their own VAT return.

2 Commission Extraction Model

Commission = 30% of net margin. Net margin per milestone = (client payment − Linyang payment − subcon costs) × (margin rate). Paid pro-rata with each client payment.

Commission Basis: 30% × Net Margin Rate × Amount Paid

Total project margin = €11,641K (10.7% of €109.2M revenue). Commission = 30% × 10.7% = 3.2% of each client payment. This ensures total commission (€3,492K) never exceeds 30% of realised profit, and the company retains 70% (€8,149K) as working capital + reserve.
