Overall finding: The contract stack is commercially sound and substantially aligned, but has three gating issues that must be resolved before committing Batch 1 capital. The Linyang Sales Contract must be signed; the APG/Performance Bond must be received before the advance payment is made; and insurance coverage must be confirmed for construction risk. Subject to those three gates, Batch 1 can proceed.

Three Gating Conditions Before Batch 1 Advance Payment

Gate 1 — Linyang Sales Contract signed (not yet executed): Until signed, there is no contractual commitment from Linyang for B1 pricing, delivery timeline, APG, or performance bond. All downstream client commitments are exposed without this upstream anchor.

Gate 2 — APG received from Linyang: EPC v5.0 §10.9 and Sales Contract §9B.4 both make advance payment a CP on APG receipt. Do not remit the €2.44M advance to Linyang until the bank-guaranteed APG for 70% of Component A is in hand.

Gate 3 — CAR/EAR insurance evidence: EPC v5.0 §14.4 requires insurance evidence within 14 days of first payment. Marsh rough NBI is not sufficient. Firm placement or binding indication needed by the time client first invoices (1 April 2026 target — now passed; confirm status with Marsh).
