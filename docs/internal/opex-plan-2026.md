## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This document outlines the Operational Expenditure (OPEX) plan for Lighthief Cyprus Ltd's BESS O&M operations for the calendar year 2026. The plan covers staffing, vehicles, equipment, warehouse, and all recurring costs associated with delivering preventive and corrective maintenance.

> **REVISED 6 March 2026:** Updated to reflect confirmed Batch 1 only (12 parks / 131.28 MWh). The previous plan assumed 51 parks commissioning progressively through 2026. The confirmed batch arrives CIF August 2026 with commissioning in Q4 2026. Subsequent batches are pipeline/unplaced — O&M scales up as they confirm.

## 15. KEY ASSUMPTIONS & RISKS

### Assumptions

| # | Assumption | Impact if Wrong |
|---|-----------|-----------------|
| 1 | All 5 engineers deploy to Cyprus Q1 2026 | Delayed arrival → understaffed for commissioning support |
| 2 | First parks commissioned Q2 2026 | Revenue delay → larger H1 funding gap |
| 3 | 10 maintenance days/park/year required | Fewer days = overcapacity; more days = capacity pressure |
| 4 | 2 engineers per maintenance visit (HV safety) | Single-engineer visits would double capacity but increase risk |
| 5 | Forklift rented, not purchased | Purchase in 2027 would reduce long-term costs |
| 6 | Warehouse shared 50/50 with another tenant | If sole occupant, cost doubles to €10,000/month |
| 7 | Vehicle lease includes basic maintenance | If not, add ~€100/month per vehicle |
| 8 | No major equipment failures in Year 1 | Warranty covers equipment; labor costs borne by Lighthief |

### Risks

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|--------|------------|
| Commissioning delays push revenue to H2/2027 | Medium | High — team cost runs from Q1 with no revenue offset | Q1–Q2 team supports installation/commissioning (separate revenue) |
| Engineer attrition | Low | High — specialized BESS skills, hard to replace locally | Competitive salary, accommodation included, clear career path |
| Maintenance overrun (>10 days/park) | Low | Medium — would consume float capacity | Monitor actuals vs. plan; adjust rotation if needed |
| Currency fluctuation (PLN wages paid in EUR) | Low | Medium | Engineers are EUR-contracted in Cyprus |
| Forklift underutilization | Medium | Low — €7,200 annual cost | Monitor usage; switch to per-day rental if <10 days/month |
| Warehouse underutilization in H1 | Medium | Low | H1 used for receiving/staging commissioning equipment |
