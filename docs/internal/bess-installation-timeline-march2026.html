⚠️ Scope Responsibility — Linyang vs Lighthief EPC
Lighthief EPC team performs ALL physical installation: civil works, container placement (crane rigging), MV skid/transformer positioning, MV/LV cabling, cable terminations, earthing, grounding, EMS/SCADA hardware mounting.
Linyang team provides on-site supervision during equipment unloading, visual/mechanical checks after placement, and verifies equipment condition. Linyang does NOT perform cabling, placement, or any physical installation work.
DEHN / StrikeRA installs lightning protection systems (LPS/SPD).

5. Critical Dependencies & Risks

🔴 CRANE — Single Point of Failure
Only 1 heavy-duty crane on the island. Need exclusivity Jul 12 – Oct 12 (~51 crane-days). No backup if unavailable. Must pre-book by end of February.
✅ MV Skid Datasheets — Complete
All 4 MV skid models received: T1 (1-1.25MW), T2 (2MW), T4 (4MW), T8 (8-10MW). Only these 4 skid types exist. Park MW sizes are achieved by combining skids with multiple 1.00MW or 1.25MW PCS units (e.g. 5MW = T4 skid + 4× 1.25MW PCS). Protection coordination studies can now proceed for all configurations.
⚠️ Transport Fleet — Partially Confirmed
A. Soulis confirmed 6 trucks for 20ft containers (43 tonnes each). 40ft container truck capacity TBC. 144 containers need transport from Limassol port to 29 sites across 5 districts (Jul–Oct). At 6 trucks × ~2 trips/day = ~12 containers/day → ~21 working days for B1 (80 containers). Confirm 40ft capability before Batch 1 CIF.
⚠️ Building Permits — Variable Lead Time
Permit processing varies by municipality (Famagusta, Larnaca, Limassol, Nicosia, Paphos). Some may take 3+ months. Critical path for Batch 1 civil works starting June 1.
⚠️ Civil Works — Lighthief Subcontractors — Start Before Equipment Arrives
Batch 1 civil works (foundations, drainage, fencing) must begin June 1 — before equipment CIF. Lighthief subcontractors (NOT Linyang) perform all civil works. Subcontractor must be awarded by April 30.
