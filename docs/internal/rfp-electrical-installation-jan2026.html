Electrical Installation Services - MV/LV Cabling & Terminations

RFP Issue Date: 27 January 2026
Proposal Due: 10 February 2026
Project: Cyprus BESS Portfolio - 47 Parks (863.5 MWh)
Total Equipment: 176 Battery Containers + 70 MV SKIDs

1. Project Overview

Lighthief Cyprus Ltd is seeking proposals from qualified electrical contractors for the supply, installation, and termination of MV and LV power cables for 47 Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) installations across Cyprus.

5. Technical Requirements

  • All cables must be CE marked and IEC certified
  • MV cables must be type-tested per IEC 60502-2
  • Terminations per manufacturer instructions
  • Cable loss calculations to be provided
  • Test certificates for all installed cables
  • Minimum 20-year cable life expectancy

6. Contractor Qualifications

  • ETEK registered electrical contractor (Class A or B)
  • MV cable jointing/termination certification
  • Minimum 5 years experience with industrial installations
  • Full insurance coverage (€2M minimum)
  • Ability to work across all Cyprus districts
