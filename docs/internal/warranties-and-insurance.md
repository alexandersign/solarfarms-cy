### 1.2 Business Model

Lighthief operates as a **dedicated BESS EPC contractor and O&M service provider**. The company does **not** manufacture any equipment. All BESS equipment is manufactured by Linyang (OEM) and installed/commissioned by Linyang's factory-trained technicians. Lighthief provides:

- **EPC Services:** Project management, engineering design, civil works, cabling, grounding, grid compliance, authority liaison
- **O&M Services:** 24/7 remote monitoring, preventive/corrective maintenance, availability guarantees, SOH performance warranties
- **Dedicated BESS Focus:** The entire engineering team is trained exclusively on BESS systems — this is not a sideline to solar PV

### 7.2 Availability Guarantee (Tier C — Standard)

|-----------|-------|

| **Max Operational Downtime** | 48 hours per park per year |
| **Scheduled Maintenance** | ≤ 10 days/year (midnight–6am, excluded from downtime) |
| **LD Rate** | €30/day/MWh of unavailable capacity |

**Availability LD Schedule:**

| Achieved Availability | Service Fee Reduction |
|----------------------|----------------------|
| 95% to < 97% | 5% |
| 93% to < 95% | 10% |
| 90% to < 93% | 15% |
| Below 90% | 20% (maximum) |

### 7.3 Excluded from Downtime Calculation

- Scheduled maintenance within allowance (midnight–6am window)
- Grid outages, curtailment, DSO/TSO instructions
- Force Majeure events
- Client-caused issues or unauthorised modifications
- Third-party EMS or software failures
- Periods when BESS is not dispatched

## 11. LEG3 — CAR INSURANCE DEFECTS CLAUSE

### 11.1 Recommendation

For the CAR/EAR insurance covering Lighthief's BESS portfolio, the **LEG3/06 (Defects Exclusion)** clause is recommended as the standard defects cover.

### 11.2 Why LEG3 for BESS Projects

| Clause | What It Covers | Suitability for BESS |
|--------|---------------|---------------------|
| **LEG1** | Resultant damage to sound property only — excludes defective item itself | ❌ Inadequate — defective battery module could damage adjacent modules |
| **LEG2** | Resultant damage + making good, but only if defect found during construction | ⚠️ Partial — misses post-commissioning discovery |
| **LEG3** | Cost of making good the defective part **AND** all resultant damage to other parts of the insured works. Only *improvement* costs excluded. | ✅ **Recommended** — provides broadest cover for high-value BESS installations |

### 11.3 BESS-Specific Scenarios Where LEG3 Applies

| Scenario | LEG1 | LEG2 | LEG3 |
|----------|------|------|------|
| Defective cable joint causes fire damage to adjacent container | Covers adjacent container only | Covers if found during construction | ✅ Covers cable repair + all resultant damage |
| Foundation defect causes container settlement and cable stress | Adjacent damage only | Limited | ✅ Covers foundation repair + cable damage |
| MV switchgear fault during testing damages PCS | PCS repair only | Full if during construction | ✅ Covers switchgear + PCS repair |
| Grounding defect found at commissioning causes equipment damage | Resultant damage only | Full cover | ✅ Full cover including grounding repair |

### 11.4 LEG3 — Insurer Consideration

The BESS risk profile supports LEG3 adoption:

- **Low fire risk:** LFP battery chemistry, UL9540A certified (no propagation)
- **OEM installation:** Equipment installed by Linyang factory technicians (not Lighthief subcontractors)
- **Dedicated O&M team:** 5 BESS-specialist engineers, 24/7 monitoring, 97% availability SLA
- **Quality components:** Eve Energy cells (Linyang is shareholder), Kehua PCS (EN 50549-2 certified)
- **C5 corrosion protection:** Standard for all Cyprus installations
