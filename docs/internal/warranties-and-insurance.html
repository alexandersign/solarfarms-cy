7. LEG3 — CAR Insurance Defects Clause

Recommended defects exclusion clause for BESS construction all risks coverage

LEG1
Resultant damage to sound property only. Excludes defective item itself.
Inadequate for BESS
LEG2
Resultant damage + making good, but only if defect found during construction.
Partial Cover
LEG3
Cost of making good defective part AND all resultant damage. Only improvement costs excluded.
Recommended for BESS

Why LEG3 for BESS Projects

ScenarioLEG1LEG2LEG3
Defective cable joint causes fire damage to adjacent containerAdjacent onlyIf during constructionFull cover
Foundation defect causes container settlement and cable stressAdjacent onlyLimitedFull cover
MV switchgear fault during testing damages PCSPCS onlyFull if during constructionFull cover
Grounding defect found at commissioning causes equipment damageResultant onlyFull coverFull cover
RISK PROFILE SUPPORTS LEG3 Low fire risk: LFP battery chemistry, UL9540A certified (no propagation). OEM installation: Equipment installed by Linyang factory technicians. Dedicated O&M: 5 BESS-specialist engineers, 24/7 monitoring, 97% availability SLA. Quality components: Eve Energy cells (Linyang is shareholder), Kehua PCS (EN 50549-2). C5 corrosion: Standard for all Cyprus installations.

8. Key Warranty Wording (Extracts)

OEM Base Warranty (Linyang → Lighthief, passed to Client)

“The Seller warrants that the Products supplied under this Agreement shall be free from defects in materials and workmanship under normal operating conditions for a period of five (5) years.” — Sales Agreement, Section 12.1

No Cross-Liability

“(i) The Contractor shall NOT be liable for defects in OEM equipment or OEM installation; (ii) The OEM shall NOT be liable for defects in the Contractor’s EPC works; (iii) Each party warrants only its own scope of work.” — EPC Agreement, Section 10.7(c)

Manufacturing Defect Indemnification

“The Company indemnifies the Distributor against all claims arising from manufacturing defects. The Distributor is NOT liable for Product performance issues, liquidated damages, or losses attributable to manufacturing defects or software failures within the Company’s Product stack.” — Distribution Agreement, Section 19.3

Availability Guarantee

“The Service Provider guarantees that the BESS shall achieve a minimum annual Availability of ninety-seven percent (97%), calculated in accordance with Schedule 4.” — LTSA, Section 9.1

SOH Guarantee — OEM Pass-Through

“The SOH Guarantee is a pass-through guarantee provided by the OEM (Linyang) and is backed by the OEM’s warranty reserve. The Service Provider’s obligation under this Section is to facilitate the OEM’s warranty and ensure proper maintenance is performed.” — LTSA, Section 10.1(b)
