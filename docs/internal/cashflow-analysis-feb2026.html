CRITICAL: €28M Cashflow Deficit Without Restructuring

The project faces a severe cashflow deficit driven by a double VAT cash drain. Cyprus has NOT implemented postponed accounting for import VAT. Under the 8-month carry-forward rule (VAT Law Section 20), excess input VAT credit of ~€7.8M cannot be refunded until H2 2027.

  • €15.2M import VAT payable in cash at Limassol customs (Jul–Sep 2026)
  • €8.4M output VAT remitted to tax dept on client advances (Q1–Q2) before any imports
  • Linyang 20% DAP (€15.5M) due at delivery — no matching client payment until PAC 2–3 months later
  • September 2026 has -€13M outflow with zero revenue inflow

APRIL 1 STRATEGY: First Invoice April 1 — Q3 VAT Credit Drops 91%

If no invoice is issued and no payment received before April 1 (both required under Article 65), the Q1 return is clean (€0). With a full April 1 project start, B1 pre-ship moves from Q2 to Q3 — where all three pre-ship invoices (€9.7M output) offset import VAT (€10.5M input). Q3 credit collapses from €9.2M to €0.85M. Total locked credit drops from €7.8M to €4.8M. Trade-off: project completes 1 month later.

BEST OUTCOME: April 1 Start + Client-as-Importer = Peak Deficit -€3M

Combining the April 1 start with client-as-importer restructure (EPC split into Equipment Supply + Installation Services) eliminates the import VAT problem entirely. Peak negative drops from -€28M to -€3M, VAT locked = €0, no bank facility needed. Must be decided before contracts are signed.

9 April 1 Invoice Strategy — VAT Game-Changer

If no invoice is issued and no payment received before April 1, Q1 is clean. Combined with a 1-month project shift, B1 pre-ship moves into Q3 — where all three pre-ship invoices offset import VAT.

Legal Requirement: Both Invoice AND Payment Must Be April 1+

Under EU VAT Directive Article 65 (Cyprus Law 95(I)/2000 §11), the tax point is the earliest of: date of supply, date of invoice, or date payment is received. If the client pays in March but the invoice is April 1, the tax point is still March (Q1). Both must fall on or after April 1.
