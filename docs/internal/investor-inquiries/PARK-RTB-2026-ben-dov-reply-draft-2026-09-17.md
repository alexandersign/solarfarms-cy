**6. BESS warranty, augmentation, and dispatch study**

The BESS system uses LFP cells (Tier-1 OEM, TÜV EN 50549-2 certified) with the following warranty and performance terms:

- **Cell warranty:** 7,000 cycles at 90% DoD, guaranteed to 70% residual capacity at end of warranty period
- **System availability:** guaranteed O&M availability contract (Lighthief long-term O&M)
- **Augmentation:** capacity augmentation provision included in the O&M contract to maintain contracted throughput over the project life
- **Round-trip efficiency (AC-AC):** 86.32% as modelled

The dispatch model follows a peak-shifting strategy: charge from curtailed solar midday (65% curtailment assumed), discharge at evening peak (17:00–21:00). The revenue derivation is shown in full in the Excel Revenue_Model sheet. A more detailed dispatch study with hourly profiles from the TSOC DAM dataset can be provided under NDA.
