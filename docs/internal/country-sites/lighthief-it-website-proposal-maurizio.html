1. Why this, and why now

lighthief.com already works as the group shop window: services up front, proof numbers, process, ask-an-expert form, and a live blog. Italy needs the same door — in Italian, with Italian offers and your contact — without waiting for a new WordPress site that will drift from Poland and Germany.

Germany launches on the same system. One design, two country doors first. Cyprus and Poland stay as they are until later. solarfarms.cy is not touched.

You are not paying to invent a new brand. The €2,500 is the Italy front door and Italian content. CRM and HR are optional extras at €1,000 each.
