1. What is BESS? — The Fundamentals

A Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) is an integrated system that stores electrical energy in batteries and releases it when needed. Think of it as a giant rechargeable battery that connects to the electrical grid or a solar farm.

Why Does BESS Exist?

The Problem

Solar panels only generate power when the sun shines. The grid needs power 24/7. When solar production exceeds demand, energy is wasted (curtailment). When demand exceeds supply, expensive fossil fuel plants must run.

The Solution

BESS stores excess solar energy during the day and discharges it during peak demand (evenings). It also provides critical grid stability services that keep the lights on — services that today require fossil fuel plants to run continuously.

Why Does BESS Matter for Cyprus?

  • Island grid — Cyprus is not connected to any other country's electricity grid, making stability critical
  • Curtailment crisis — Solar farms are being told to switch off (curtailed) because the grid can't absorb all the solar energy
  • Single point of failure — Vasilikos power station generates the majority of Cyprus's electricity. If it goes down, the whole island is at risk
  • EU mandates — The EU requires Cyprus to adopt ancillary services markets (FCR, aFRR) that BESS can provide
  • Revenue opportunity — BESS owners can earn money by providing grid services, not just by storing solar energy
Key Selling Point: BESS transforms a solar farm from a "use it or lose it" asset into a 24/7 dispatchable power plant with multiple revenue streams.

2. How a BESS Works — Energy Flow & Operating Modes

2.1 The Energy Path

Energy flows through several components between the grid and the batteries:

During charging, energy flows left-to-right: grid AC power is stepped down, converted to DC, and stored in batteries. During discharging, it reverses: battery DC is converted to AC and fed back to the grid.

2.2 Key Electrical Concepts

ConceptExplanationAnalogy
AC (Alternating Current)The type of electricity used by the grid. Current changes direction 50 times per second (50 Hz in Cyprus).Like water flowing back and forth in a pipe
DC (Direct Current)The type of electricity batteries store. Current flows in one direction only.Like water flowing one way through a hose
PCS / InverterThe Power Conversion System converts between AC and DC. This is the "translator" between batteries and grid.Like a currency exchange booth between two countries
TransformerSteps voltage up or down. The grid runs at 22 kV (high voltage), the PCS operates at 690 V.Like a gear box — changes speed/torque ratio
MV (Medium Voltage)Voltage range of 1 kV to 36 kV. Cyprus grid is 22 kV MV.The "highway" voltage level
kW vs kWhkW = power (rate of energy flow). kWh = energy (total stored). A 5 MW / 20 MWh system can discharge 5 MW for 4 hours.kW = how wide the tap is. kWh = how big the tank is.

5.6 Round-Trip Efficiency (RTE)

RTE measures how much energy you get back out compared to what you put in. Our system's efficiency chain:

ComponentCharging Eff.Discharging Eff.Round-Trip Loss
HV Transformer99.95%99.50%0.55%
MV Cable99.95%99.50%0.55%
MV Transformer99.00%99.00%1.99%
LV Cable99.95%99.95%0.10%
PCS (Kehua)98.20%98.50%3.28%
DC Cable99.80%99.80%0.40%
Battery (Linyang)96.50%97.50%5.91%
Full System AC-AC86.32%13.68%
In plain English: For every 100 kWh of energy you put into the battery, you get ~86 kWh back out. The biggest losses are in the battery itself (chemical conversion) and the PCS (power electronics conversion).

13. Glossary of BESS & PV Market Terms

AC
Alternating Current — Electricity where current periodically reverses direction. The grid runs on AC at 50 Hz in Europe.
aFRR
Automatic Frequency Restoration Reserve — A grid balancing service where the TSO sends automated signals to generators/batteries to restore system frequency over 5–15 minutes. Higher duration than FCR.
Ancillary Services
Services beyond basic energy supply that maintain grid reliability: frequency regulation, voltage support, reserves. BESS can provide these and earn revenue.
Arbitrage
Buying electricity when cheap (low demand) and selling when expensive (peak demand). The core revenue model for BESS.
Availability
The percentage of time a BESS system is operational and ready to dispatch. Our LTSA guarantees 97%.
BAMS
Battery Stack Management System — The top-level (Level 3) BMS controller that aggregates data from all battery clusters and communicates with the EMS.
BCMU
Battery Cluster Management Unit — Level 2 BMS that manages a cluster of battery packs, controls contactors, and calculates SOC.
BESS
Battery Energy Storage System — An integrated system of batteries, inverters, transformers, and controls that stores and releases electrical energy.
Black Start
The ability to restart a power grid after a total shutdown without relying on external power. Grid-forming BESS can provide this.
BMS
Battery Management System — Electronics that monitor cell voltage, temperature, and state of charge, and protect against overcharge/overdischarge.
BMU
Battery Management Unit — Level 1 BMS at the module level. Monitors individual cell voltages and temperatures.
BOL / EOL
Beginning of Life / End of Life — BOL is when the battery is new (98.5% SOH). EOL is defined as 70% SOH (the battery still works, but has degraded).
C-Rate
The rate at which a battery charges or discharges relative to its capacity. 1C means full charge/discharge in 1 hour. 0.5C = 2 hours. Our standard operation is 0.5C.
CAR
Construction All Risk — Insurance policy covering damage during construction phase of the EPC project.
CERA
Cyprus Energy Regulatory Authority — The regulator that issues licenses for energy storage and generation in Cyprus.
CIF
Cost, Insurance & Freight — Incoterm meaning the seller (Linyang) covers cost, insurance, and freight to the destination port (Limassol). The buyer (Lighthief) takes responsibility once goods are at port.
COD
Commercial Operation Date — The date when the BESS is fully commissioned and starts earning revenue.
CPD
Cycles Per Day — How many full charge/discharge cycles the battery performs daily. 1 CPD = once per day. 2 CPD = twice per day.
CT Class
Current Transformer accuracy class — Determines metering accuracy for billing. Class 0.2S required for BESS in Cyprus (DSO requirement).
Curtailment
When a solar farm or BESS is instructed by the grid operator to reduce output because the grid cannot absorb more power. A major problem in Cyprus.
DAP
Delivery at Place — Incoterm where the seller delivers goods to the buyer's nominated location. Used in our Linyang payment terms.
DC
Direct Current — Electricity flowing in one direction. Batteries store and release DC power.
DLP
Defects Liability Period — Period after commissioning during which the contractor must fix any defects at their cost. Our contracts have a 24-month DLP.
DoD
Depth of Discharge — How much of the battery's capacity is used. 90% DoD means discharging to 10% SOC. Higher DoD = more usable energy but faster degradation.
DSO
Distribution System Operator — The company operating the local electricity distribution network. In Cyprus, this is EAC (Electricity Authority of Cyprus).
EAC
Electricity Authority of Cyprus — Cyprus's electricity company, acting as both DSO and the main power generator.
EMS
Energy Management System — Software that optimises BESS operation: when to charge, discharge, and which grid service to provide. The "brain" of the system.
EN 50549-2
European grid connection standard for generating plants connected to MV distribution networks. The mandatory compliance standard for BESS in Cyprus.
EOL
End of Life — When battery SOH reaches the defined threshold (typically 70%). The battery still works but has reduced capacity.
EPC
Engineering, Procurement & Construction — A turnkey contract where the contractor (Lighthief) handles everything from design to handover.
FAC
Final Acceptance Certificate — Issued when all project obligations are complete, including DLP.
FAT
Factory Acceptance Test — Testing performed at the manufacturer's factory before shipping to verify equipment meets specifications.
FCR
Frequency Containment Reserve — The fastest grid balancing service. BESS must respond within 30 seconds to stabilise frequency. Highest-value ancillary service. BESS is ideal for this due to millisecond response time.
Grid-Forming
A mode where the inverter creates its own voltage and frequency reference (like a generator), rather than following the grid. Essential for island grids and black start.
Grid-Following
Standard inverter mode where the inverter synchronises to the existing grid voltage and frequency. Cannot operate without a grid reference.
HVRT / LVRT
High/Low Voltage Ride-Through — The ability to remain connected during grid voltage disturbances. Required by EN 50549-2.
IEC 104
IEC 60870-5-104 — Communication protocol used by grid operators (DSO/TSO) to monitor and control equipment. The standard SCADA protocol in Cyprus.
Inertia
The resistance to frequency changes provided by spinning generators. BESS can provide "synthetic inertia" electronically, reducing the need for fossil fuel plants to be running.
IP Rating
Ingress Protection — How well equipment is sealed against dust and water. IP55 = dust-protected + water jet protected. IP65 = dust-tight + water jet protected. IP67 = dust-tight + immersion-proof.
LFP
Lithium Iron Phosphate (LiFePO4) — Battery chemistry used in our Linyang containers. Safer, longer-lasting, and cheaper than NMC. Preferred for utility-scale storage.
LTSA
Long-Term Service Agreement — Lighthief's ongoing O&M contract with clients. €1,740/MWh/year. Includes preventive maintenance, remote monitoring, SLA guarantees, and 97% availability target.
mFRR
Manual Frequency Restoration Reserve — Grid balancing service activated manually by the TSO. Slower than aFRR (15+ minutes).
MV
Medium Voltage — Voltage range of 1 kV to 36 kV. The Cyprus distribution grid operates at 22 kV. Our MV skid converts PCS output (690 V) to grid voltage (22 kV).
MV Skid
A pre-assembled container housing PCS inverter units plus an MV transformer. Converts battery DC to grid-level AC. Ships as a single unit.
NMC
Nickel Manganese Cobalt — Alternative battery chemistry with higher energy density but shorter life and higher fire risk. We don't use it.
O&M
Operations & Maintenance — Ongoing service to keep the BESS running: inspections, preventive maintenance, alarm response, performance optimisation.
PAC
Provisional Acceptance Certificate — Issued when the BESS is commissioned and grid-connected. Triggers the 10% PAC payment and starts the DLP.
PCS
Power Conversion System — The bidirectional inverter that converts between AC (grid) and DC (battery). Our PCS is the Kehua BCS1250K-C-HUD (1.25 MW per unit).
Peak Shaving
Using BESS to reduce power demand during peak hours, lowering electricity costs or grid charges.
PV
Photovoltaic — Solar panel technology that converts sunlight directly into electricity. Our BESS systems are co-located with existing PV parks.
RES
Renewable Energy Sources — Solar, wind, and other non-fossil energy. Cyprus has a high RES penetration target under EU directives.
Revenue Stacking
Earning from multiple services simultaneously: arbitrage + FCR + aFRR + curtailment recovery. BESS uniquely enables this.
RoCoF
Rate of Change of Frequency — How quickly grid frequency is changing. High RoCoF indicates instability and is a trigger for synthetic inertia response.
RTE
Round-Trip Efficiency — The ratio of energy out to energy in. Our full system AC-AC RTE is 86.32%. For every 100 kWh in, you get ~86 kWh out.
SAT
Site Acceptance Test — Testing performed on-site after installation to verify the system meets contractual performance specifications.
SCADA
Supervisory Control and Data Acquisition — System for monitoring and controlling the BESS remotely. Provides dashboards, alarms, and data logging.
SLD
Single Line Diagram — Simplified electrical drawing showing the main power flow path. Required for DSO grid connection applications.
SOC
State of Charge — How full the battery is, as a percentage. 100% = fully charged, 0% = empty. Managed by the BMS.
SOH
State of Health — The current capacity of a battery relative to its original capacity. A new battery starts at 98.5% SOH. At 70% SOH it reaches EOL.
SPD
Surge Protection Device — Equipment that protects against voltage spikes from lightning or grid switching. Part of our DEHN protection scope.
Synthetic Inertia
The electronic emulation of rotational inertia by a grid-forming inverter. Provides the same frequency stabilisation as a spinning generator, but without the generator.
THDi / THDu
Total Harmonic Distortion — Measure of power quality. THDi = current harmonics (<3% for our PCS). THDu = voltage harmonics (<3%). Low THD = clean power.
TSO
Transmission System Operator — Operator of the high-voltage transmission grid. In Cyprus, this is TSOC. The TSO dispatches ancillary services.
TSOC
Transmission System Operator Cyprus — The entity responsible for operating Cyprus's high-voltage transmission network and managing grid stability.
UPS
Uninterruptible Power Supply — Backup power for critical systems (BMS, SCADA, fire suppression) that keeps them running during grid outages.
VPP
Virtual Power Plant — A network of distributed energy resources (like our 51 BESS sites) aggregated and controlled as a single entity.
VSG
Virtual Synchronous Generator — A PCS operating mode that mimics the behaviour of a traditional synchronous generator, providing inertia and frequency support.
