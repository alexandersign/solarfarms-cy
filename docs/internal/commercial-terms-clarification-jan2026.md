## 2. VAT & CUSTOMS HANDLING

### 2.1 Cyprus Import VAT Rules

| Item | Rate | Payment Timing |
|------|------|----------------|
| **Standard VAT** | **19%** | At customs clearance |
| **Import Duty (Weighted)** | ~0.9% | At customs clearance |

### 2.2 VAT Cash Flow Issue

**Example: €3.5M CIF Import (Large Project)**

| Tax | Amount | When Due | Issue |
|-----|--------|----------|-------|
| Import VAT (19%) | **€665,000** | At port clearance | Immediate payment required |
| Import Duty (~0.9%) | €31,500 | At port clearance | Immediate payment required |
| **TOTAL** | **€696,500** | Day 1 at port | Large cash outlay |

> **Problem:** You pay €665K VAT on import, but you don't collect VAT from client until you invoice. Standard VAT return cycle is quarterly.

### 2.3 Cyprus VAT Deferment Options

| Option | Description | Availability | Benefit |
|--------|-------------|--------------|---------|
| **VAT Deferment Account** | Defer VAT payment with customs guarantee | ✅ Available | ~30-day deferral |
| **Postponed VAT Accounting** | Account for VAT on VAT return, not at import | ❌ NOT Available in Cyprus | Would be ideal |
| **Customs Warehouse** | Defer duty/VAT until goods released | ✅ Available | Useful if staging |

### 2.4 Recommended VAT Strategy

**Option A: Standard (No Deferment)**
```
Day 1: Pay €665K VAT at customs
Day 30: Invoice client €2M + 19% VAT (€380K)
Day 60: Client pays invoice (receive €380K VAT)
Day 90: VAT return filed, claim €665K - €380K = €285K refund
Day 120: Receive VAT refund

Net: ~90-120 day cash cycle, €285K-665K cash tied up
```

**Option B: VAT Deferment Account (Recommended)**
```
Apply for Cyprus Customs Deferment Account
Provides ~30 days additional grace on VAT payment
Reduces cash flow pressure

Requirements:
- Apply to Cyprus Customs
- Bank guarantee may be required
- Good tax compliance history
```

**Option C: Structure Per Project (Alternative)**
```
Structure EPC so equipment title passes to client at port
Client becomes importer of record
Client pays import VAT directly
Client claims own VAT refund

Benefit: Removes €665K VAT liability from our cash flow
Risk: Client must be VAT registered and willing
```

## 4. LIQUIDATED DAMAGES (LDs)

### 4.1 LD Structure (Per DINO Clarification)

| LD Type | Rate | Cap | Applies To | Exclusions |
|---------|------|-----|------------|------------|
| **Production Delay** | 0.1-0.2% per day | 10% | Equipment not ready by Day 90 | Design changes by client |
| **Installation & Commissioning Delay** | 0.1-0.2% per day | 10% | I&C exceeds 30 working days | - |

### 4.2 Exclusions from LDs

| Exclusion | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Shipping Time** | Variable ~50 days, no LD |
| **DSO Scheduling Delays** | EAC approval/inspection delays |
| **Force Majeure** | Natural disasters, war, pandemic |
| **Client-Caused Delays** | Late design approval, site not ready, late payments |

### 4.3 Example LD Calculation

**Production Delay Example:**
- Equipment value: €2,000,000
- LD rate: 0.15% per day
- Delay: 31 days
- LD = €2M × 0.15% × 31 = **€9,300**
- Cap (10%) = €200,000 — not exceeded

> **Note:** Do NOT show delays > cap (10%) in examples as it confuses clients.

## 6. END-OF-LIFE (EOL) RECYCLING

### 6.1 EOL Program (Lighthief + BSi Partner)

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Partner** | BSi Cyprus (local recycling/refurbishment partner) |
| **Client Cost** | **Net €0 to client** |
| **Possible Cashback** | Based on EOL battery value at time of decommissioning |

### 6.2 EOL Value Matrix

| SOH at EOL | Typical Use Case | Client Outcome |
|------------|------------------|----------------|
| 70-80% | Second-life (low-cycle backup) | €0 cost + possible cashback (15-25% of original) |
| 60-70% | Off-grid residential/commercial | €0 cost + possible cashback (10-15% of original) |
| < 60% | Material recycling only | €0 cost (recycling credit offsets transport) |

### 6.3 Contractual Language

> "At end of BESS operational life, Lighthief Cyprus Ltd, in partnership with BSi Cyprus, will:
> 1. Collect and transport batteries at no cost to client
> 2. Assess residual value for second-life or recycling
> 3. Provide cashback to client based on EOL market value, OR
> 4. Recycle at net zero cost to client
> 
> Client is not responsible for battery disposal, recycling costs, or regulatory compliance for EOL management."
