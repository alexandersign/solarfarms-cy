1. Introduction & Purpose

What this manual covers and who it applies to

Purpose

This manual provides Lighthief Cyprus employees with comprehensive guidance on:

  • Meeting OEM warranty requirements (Linyang Energy Storage)
  • Fulfilling LTSA service obligations
  • Proper documentation and claim procedures

  • Cyprus regulatory requirements

Scope

This manual applies to all employees involved in:

  • Project installation and commissioning

  • Customer service and support
  • Warranty claim processing

Every action taken during installation, commissioning, and service must preserve the OEM warranty. Unauthorised modifications, improper handling, or inadequate documentation can void the warranty and expose Lighthief to significant liability.

2. OEM Warranty Terms (Linyang)

Jiangsu Linyang Energy Storage Technology Co., Ltd — Reference: LYCN/WI-3410

Standard Warranty Periods

ProductStandard Warranty
Power Conversion System (PCS)5 years
Battery Container5 years
HVAC System5 years
Fire Suppression System (FSS)5 years
Transformer5 years
Medium Voltage Switchgear5 years
DC/AC Container/Enclosure5 years
Uninterruptible Power Supply5 years
WARRANTY START DATE

Warranty starts on the EARLIER of: (1) Commissioning date (PAC issued), OR (2) 6 months after shipment from Linyang factory. Track shipment dates carefully!

Warranty Conditions — Warranty Applies ONLY If:

1Full payment made for all amounts due
2Products used per user manuals and maintenance guidelines
3Only Linyang-certified personnel perform service
4No unauthorised modifications or repairs
5All serial numbers visible and unaltered

Warranty Termination Events

WARRANTY IMMEDIATELY TERMINATED IF:

1. Serial number altered or unidentifiable
2. Access refused for inspection
3. Product relocated without Linyang written approval

Linyang Liability Cap

Maximum liability = 10% of product value. If warranty claims exceed 10% of what was paid for defective products, Linyang is not obligated to pay more.

Coastal Installation Restrictions

CYPRUS STANDARD: C5-Rated Enclosure — mandatory for all Cyprus installations due to island geography and coastal climate.

Distance from SeaWith C5 Enclosure
>500 mFull 5-year warranty
<500 mReduced to 2 years (PCS, transformer, switchgear)
Distance from SeaWithout C5 (non-Cyprus)
>5 kmFull 5-year warranty
2–5 kmReduced to 2 years
<2 kmWARRANTY VOID

Warranty Exclusions — DO NOT Submit Claims For:

Consumables and wear parts
Cosmetic defects not affecting function
Damage from improper transport/handling/storage
Installation not following Linyang manual
Unauthorised commissioning, repair, or modification
Natural disasters (floods, earthquakes, etc.)
Biological damage or industrial chemical exposure
Salt spray/corrosion exceeding specifications
Operation exceeding voltage/wind/snow specifications
Human damage or accidents
Third-party equipment damage
Force majeure events

7. Safety Precautions

Non-negotiable OEM safety requirements — Linyang Power Atlantic 5MWh User Manual

LETHAL VOLTAGES PRESENT

Lethal voltages (up to 1,497.6V DC) are present inside the BESS container at all times. Touching contact points or terminals may cause electric shock or death. Wait 5 minutes after power disconnect before any work. ALWAYS confirm zero voltage with a calibrated meter.

Warranty-Voiding Conditions

ConditionThresholdAction
Low Cell Voltage<2.8V for 120 hrsCharge immediately
Zero SOC0% for 120 hrsCharge immediately
Under-VoltageCell ≤2.5V (any)Escalate to OEM

Set CRITICAL alert at cell voltage <2.9V and SOC <5%. Never leave BESS in low SOC for extended periods. Before planned shutdown, charge to 30–50% SOC.

Personnel Requirements (Per OEM)

Qualified professional electricians
Electronic, electrical wiring & mechanical expertise
Familiar with ESS composition & principles
Professional training on ESS installation/commissioning
Able to respond to hazards or emergencies
Familiar with Cyprus/EU regulations and standards

Building Permit Exemption

BESS in Cyprus does NOT require a building permit when installed within an already licensed RES station, provided all safety, fire, access, and environmental conditions are met.

If conditions are violated, exemption is automatically invalidated.
