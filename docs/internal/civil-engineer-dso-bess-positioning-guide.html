DSO Regulations for BESS Positioning

Civil Engineer Reference Guide — Spacing, Setbacks, Foundations & Site Layout

Audience: Civil Engineer / Site Layout Designer
Purpose: Single reference for all physical positioning and spacing rules that affect foundation design, site layout, and container placement for Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS) in Cyprus.
Regulatory Sources: Κ.Δ.Π. 15/2026 (Building Permit Exemption), Κ.Δ.Π. 17/2026 (Fire Conditions), DSO Technical Guide for Storage 2025.1, TSO BESS Specification (ANNEX-II-Appendix-2)

1 Minimum Distances & Setbacks

These are the mandatory minimum distances that govern where BESS containers and equipment can be placed on a solar park site.

Requirement Minimum Distance Source Notes
Plot boundary setback 6 m from all plot boundaries Κ.Δ.Π. 15/2026 §12 Applies to all equipment (BESS containers, MV skids, any structure)
Fire vehicle access perimeter 6 m clear access around installation Κ.Δ.Π. 15/2026 §18 Unobstructed path for fire trucks around the entire BESS installation
Container-to-container spacing (sides) ≥ 1 m TSO ANNEX-II §13.3 Between side walls of adjacent containers
Container-to-container spacing (at doors) ≥ 3 m TSO ANNEX-II §13.3 In front of access doors for maintenance and emergency access
Distance to occupied buildings ≥ 30 m TSO ANNEX-II §13.3 From any BESS container to any occupied building/dwelling
Distance to control room / switchgear building ≥ 6 m TSO ANNEX-II §13.3 From BESS container to on-site control/electrical rooms
Fire access road width ≥ 5 m TSO ANNEX-II §13.3 Dedicated emergency vehicle access road serving the site
Augmentation space ≥ 20% additional capacity area TSO ANNEX-II §13.2 Reserve area on site for future battery augmentation
CRITICAL: The 6 m boundary setback and 6 m fire access perimeter are the two constraints that most limit usable site area. On smaller plots, verify there is sufficient space for equipment placement after applying both setbacks before proceeding with design.

2 Height & Visual Constraints

Requirement Limit Source Notes
Maximum overall height 3.0 m Κ.Δ.Π. 15/2026 §6 Includes container, supports, roof-mounted HVAC, any ancillaries
Container height (Linyang 20HC) 2.896 m OEM datasheet 104 mm clearance to 3 m limit — foundation level must not push total above 3 m
Color / finish Neutral / earth-tone colors only Κ.Δ.Π. 15/2026 §7 No bright colors, no reflective materials
Visual impact Must not alter area character or cause visual disturbance Κ.Δ.Π. 15/2026 §11 May affect fencing, screening, landscaping choices
FOUNDATION HEIGHT WARNING: The Linyang container is 2,896 mm tall. With only 104 mm of vertical clearance to the 3 m legal limit, the concrete slab surface must be at or very near existing ground level. Any raised platform or plinth that pushes total height above 3,000 mm will violate the permit exemption.

3 Foundation Requirements

Parameter Requirement Source
Foundation type Reinforced concrete (Βάση οπλισμένου σκυροδέματος) — MANDATORY Κ.Δ.Π. 15/2026 §24
Concrete grade C30/37 minimum (higher for coastal sites) EN 1992-1-1
Slab thickness 200–300 mm (per structural design & soil conditions) Structural design
Reinforcement A393 mesh or T12@200 both ways, top and bottom mats Structural design
Cover 50 mm minimum (75 mm for coastal exposure) EN 1992-1-1
Bearing capacity ≥ 150 kN/m² (after sub-base preparation) Structural design
Sub-base Compacted aggregate, 130 kN/m² minimum bearing Structural design
Membrane DPM / Geotextile, 300 gsm minimum Structural design
Surface finish Power floated, non-slip, with falls to drainage Best practice
Minimal concrete paving Limit paved area to equipment footprints only Κ.Δ.Π. 15/2026 §8
Seismic design ZPA: 3.55 m/s² horizontal, 1.78 m/s² vertical TSO ANNEX-II §13.3
Corrosion protection C3 (medium), durability H per ISO 12944 TSO ANNEX-II §13.3
Topography: No alteration of existing ground levels is permitted (Κ.Δ.Π. 15/2026 §10). Foundation design should work with existing terrain — cut-and-fill must be minimized. Slab should be partially below grade (10–20 cm below with crusher run + 20 cm above) to stay within height limits.

5 Fire Safety & Separation Requirements

Requirement Specification Source
Container wall fire resistance 1-hour fire rating OR 6 m spacing between containers TSO ANNEX-II §13.3
Pressure relief vents Required — must NOT face walkways or access paths TSO ANNEX-II §13.4
External beacon Required on each battery container (visual alarm) TSO ANNEX-II §13.4
Dry vegetation clearance All dry vegetation must be cleared within the installation perimeter Κ.Δ.Π. 15/2026 §20
Planting near fence Limited to 1 m height and 1 m width from perimeter fence Κ.Δ.Π. 15/2026 §21
Fire-break zone Width proportional to surrounding vegetation height Κ.Δ.Π. 17/2026 Appendix I
Water supply Permanent supply OR ≥ 20 m³ on-site water tank TSO ANNEX-II §13.4
Fire hydrants Required if water network is available at site Κ.Δ.Π. 17/2026
Portable fire extinguishers Quantity and type per Fire Service requirements Κ.Δ.Π. 17/2026
Vent orientation matters for layout: Battery containers have pressure relief vents that must not discharge toward walkways. This affects which direction containers face and where access paths are located.

9 Required Submissions to Authorities

Before commencing civil works, the responsible ETEK-registered engineer must submit the following:

  1. Site Layout Plan — showing existing RES installation, proposed BESS location, and distances from all boundaries
  2. Foundation Design — structural calculations stamped by ETEK engineer
  3. Informal Cadastral Plan — showing BESS location on the land parcel
  4. Photographs — of site with existing RES and proposed BESS location
  5. Responsible Engineer Declaration (Υπεύθυνη Δήλωση Μελετητή) — confirming compliance with all Κ.Δ.Π. 15/2026 conditions, submitted to the competent authority
  6. ETEK Professional Liability Insurance certificate for the responsible engineer
Permit Exemption: BESS installations within licensed RES stations are exempt from building and planning permits, provided all Κ.Δ.Π. 15/2026 conditions are met. The permit is deemed issued automatically. If any condition is violated, the exemption ceases and enforcement action may follow.
