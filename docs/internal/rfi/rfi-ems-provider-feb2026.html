REQUEST FOR INFORMATION

EMS / PPC / RTU / SCADA — Cyprus BESS Portfolio

1. INTRODUCTION

Lighthief Cyprus Ltd is developing a portfolio of Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) projects across Cyprus. We are seeking proposals from qualified EMS (Energy Management System) providers for the supply and integration of EMS, PPC (Power Plant Controller), RTU, and SCADA systems across our portfolio.

Portfolio Overview:
  • 42 Projects across 12 client groups
  • ~220 MW total power capacity
  • ~863.5 MWh total energy capacity
  • ~156 BESS containers + ~56 MV Skid containers
  • Implementation: Q4 2025 – Q4 2027

1.1 BESS Equipment

Component Supplier Details
Battery Containers Linyang Energy 20HC, 5.015 MWh LFP, EVE 314Ah cells, Liquid cooling, Active BMS
PCS Kehua (via Linyang) BCS-C-HUD series (1000K, 1250K, 2500K), EN 50549-2 certified
MV Transformer Kehua SL-10000 series (part of MV Skid)
