EPC Agreement — Key Protections (client_sales.html v3.0)

🔒 Performance Bond

V3 CONFIRMED
Amount: 5% of Contract Price
Form: Bank guarantee (OEM corporate-backed)
Validity: From 1 month before delivery → PAC
Call conditions: Material failure, abandonment, insolvency
EPC Ref: Section 7.6

⏱ Delay LDs — Full vs Partial

V3 MODIFIED
Full Park Delay (Critical Path — PCS, MV): LD base = entire park value
Partial Delay (Battery containers): LD base = delayed goods value only
Rates: 0.1%/day (d1–30) · 0.15%/day (d31–60) · 0.2%/day (d61+)
Cap: 10% of delayed/affected goods value
EPC Ref: Section 8.4

🏦 Advance Payment Guarantee

V3 CONFIRMED
Coverage: 100% of advance payment
Form: Bank guarantee (international bank)

LTSA Tier C — O&M Guarantees (ClientLTSA.html v3.0)

📊 97% Availability Guarantee

V3 CONFIRMED
Target: 97% annual availability
Direct LD: €30/day/MWh for shortfall below 97%
Fee Reduction: 5% (95–97%) · 10% (93–95%) · 15% (90–93%) · 20% (<90%)
Maintenance Allowance: 10 days/year excluded
LTSA Ref: Sections 9.1–9.6, 9.4A

🔋 SOH Guarantee

V3 CONFIRMED
Year 5: ≥ 85%
Year 10: ≥ 79.58%
Year 15: ≥ 70% (maximum warranty period)
Backed by: OEM warranty reserve (~1.9%)
LTSA Ref: Section 10

🔄 SOH Remedies

V3 CONFIRMED
Isolated (<5%): Module replacement within 60 days
Systemic (≥5%): Cell augmentation within 120 days or LDs
LD Cap: 50% of total service fees paid
LTSA Ref: Section 10.4

📉 SOH Capacity Shortfall LD (Bankable)

NEW — BANKABLE
Formula: Daily LD = Lost Capacity (MWh) x €30/MWh/day
Lost Capacity: (Guaranteed SOH% − Actual SOH%) x System Capacity
Trigger: Annual SOH test shows ≥1% shortfall vs guarantee curve
Grace Period: 90 days to remedy (augmentation or replacement)
Accrual: Day 91 if unremedied; stops when SOH restored
Annual Cap: 20% of annual LTSA fee
Lifetime Cap: 50% of total service fees paid
Combined Cap: SOH + Availability LDs ≤ 30% of annual fee
Rate Consistency: Same €30/MWh/day as Availability LD — single metric for bank models
LTSA Ref: Section 10.5

⚡ Cycle Life

V3 MODIFIED
Guarantee: 15 years or 7,000 cycles @ 70% EOL
Conditions: 90% DOD, 25±2°C, whichever comes first
Previous: 8,000 cycles at 1C
