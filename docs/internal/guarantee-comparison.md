## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The competitor offers a comprehensive risk mitigation package including APG, step-in rights, delay LDs, performance bonds, availability warranty, and SOH performance warranty. Lighthief can **match most items** through existing client contracts and upstream Linyang agreements, with **3 critical gaps** requiring attention.

## 2. DETAILED ANALYSIS BY GUARANTEE TYPE

### 2.1 ADVANCE PAYMENT GUARANTEE (APG)

**Competitor Offer:**
> An on-demand guarantee for 100% of the deposit value, issued by Trygg Hansa or Atradius

**Lighthief Current Position:**
- ❌ **NOT OFFERED** in current EPC template
- No APG insurance policy in place

**Linyang Upstream:**
- ❌ No APG provided to Lighthief
- Linyang receives 30% advance payment without guarantee

**Gap & Risk:**
| Risk | Impact | Probability |
|------|--------|-------------|
| Client loses deposit if project fails to start | High (30% of contract) | Low |
| Competitive disadvantage vs Sunwoda | Medium | High |

**Recommended Action:**
1. Source APG insurance from:
   - Trygg Hansa (Sweden - referenced by competitor)
   - Atradius (Netherlands - referenced by competitor)
   - Euler Hermes (Germany)
   - Coface
   - Local Cyprus insurers (Bank of Cyprus Insurance, Hellenic Bank)
2. Estimated cost: 0.5-1.5% of deposit value annually
3. Include APG cost in project pricing

### 2.2 STEP-IN RIGHTS / DIRECT AGREEMENTS

**Competitor Offer:**
> Step-in Rights via Direct Agreements with key suppliers allowing contract inheritance if EPC fails

**Lighthief Current Position:**
- ⚠️ **PARTIAL COVERAGE**
- EPC Agreement Section 13A.3: OEM claims facilitation, rights assignment to client
- Client LTSA Section 10.1(c): OEM warranty passthrough
- Distribution Agreement Section 19.3: €5M product liability indemnification

**Linyang Upstream:**
- ✅ Distribution Agreement Section 13.5: Survival of client/project protections
- ✅ Sales Agreement Section 14.1: Manufacturing defect indemnity
- ⚠️ No formal "step-in" rights for end customers

**Gap & Risk:**
| Risk | Impact | Probability |
|------|--------|-------------|
| Client stranded if Lighthief fails mid-project | Medium-High | Low |
| No direct contractual privity between client and Linyang | Medium | N/A |

**Recommended Action:**
1. Consider tripartite agreement structure for large projects (>€2M)
2. Include explicit collateral warranty from Linyang to end customer
3. Document formal step-in procedure in EPC Schedule

### 2.3 DELAY LIQUIDATED DAMAGES

**Competitor Offer:**
> 0.5% of contract price per week after grace period, capped at 10%

**Lighthief Current Position:**
- ✅ **FULLY MATCHED** (actually more favourable structure)
- EPC Agreement Section 8.4:
  - 0.1%/day for first 30 days
  - 0.15%/day for days 31-60
  - 0.2%/day from day 61
  - **Capped at 10%** of Contract Price

**Comparison:**
| Metric | Competitor | Lighthief |
|--------|-----------|-----------|
| Rate | 0.5%/week (~0.07%/day) | 0.1-0.2%/day |
| Cap | 10% | 10% |
| Grace Period | Yes (unspecified) | No explicit grace |
| Maximum exposure | Same | Same |

**Linyang Upstream:**
- ❌ **NO DELAY LDs** from Linyang
- Sales Agreement: No delay penalties
- Lighthief bears full delay risk

**Risk Assessment:**
| Risk | Impact | Probability | Mitigation |
|------|--------|-------------|------------|
| Linyang delivery delay | High (10% exposure) | Medium | Buffer in project schedule |
| Installation delay | High | Low | OEM supervision included |

**Recommended Action:**
1. Build 4-6 week buffer into Target PAC Date
2. Consider negotiating delay protection in next Distribution Agreement amendment
3. Price delay risk into margin (suggest 1-2% contingency)

### 2.4 PERFORMANCE GUARANTEE / BOND

**Competitor Offer:**
> On-demand bank guarantee (10% of contract value) held until SAT passed

**Lighthief Current Position:**
- ✅ **MATCHED** (lower percentage)
- EPC Agreement Section 7.4: 5% Retention until FAC
- EPC Agreement Section 7.1(d): 5% released upon FAC

**Comparison:**
| Metric | Competitor | Lighthief |
|--------|-----------|-----------|
| Amount held | 10% | 5% |
| Release trigger | SAT passed | FAC issued |
| Type | Bank guarantee | Retention (not bank guarantee) |

**Linyang Upstream:**
- ❌ No performance bond provided
- Sales Agreement: 10% paid at PAC, no retention

**Gap Assessment:**
- Competitor offers 10% vs our 5%
- Competitor uses bank guarantee vs our simple retention
- Bank guarantee is more liquid/secure for client

**Recommended Action:**
1. Consider increasing retention to 10% for competitive parity
2. Alternatively, offer optional Performance Bond (bank guarantee) at client request
3. Estimated cost of 10% bank guarantee: 0.5-1% per annum
