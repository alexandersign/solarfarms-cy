**Calculation:**
```
BESS:    €1,740 × (€1,157.62 ÷ €2,469.59) = €1,740 × 0.4688 = €815.72
PCS+MVS: €1,740 × (€1,311.97 ÷ €2,469.59) = €1,740 × 0.5312 = €924.28
                                                    TOTAL = €1,740.00 ✓
```
