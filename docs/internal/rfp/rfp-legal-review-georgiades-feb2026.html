EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Lighthief Cyprus Ltd (HE 477423) is the exclusive Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) distributor, EPC contractor, and O&M service provider for Linyang Energy in Cyprus. We are preparing to execute a group purchase order for 51 utility-scale BESS projects totalling 881.78 MWh / 249 MW with an aggregate EPC contract value of ~€109 million.

Before placing the order, we require an independent legal review of our entire contract suite — upstream (OEM supply), downstream (client EPC and service), and third-party (EMS/SCADA) — to ensure our contractual position is sound, risk allocation is appropriate, and terms are enforceable under Cyprus law and applicable foreign jurisdictions.

We are inviting Y. Georgiades & Associates LLC to quote for this review engagement. All contracts will be provided in full upon engagement.

5. Scope of Legal Review Requested

We are requesting a comprehensive legal review of the above contract suite, with particular attention to the following areas:

5.1 General Review (All Contracts)

  • Enforceability under Cyprus law and the designated governing law for each agreement
  • Consistency and alignment of terms across the entire contract suite (upstream ↔ downstream)
  • Risk allocation — identification of any disproportionate or unfavourable risk assumptions by Lighthief
  • Adequacy of limitation of liability and indemnity provisions
  • Termination rights, consequences, and survival clauses
  • Force majeure provisions — scope and adequacy
  • Intellectual property and data ownership provisions
  • Adequacy of dispute resolution mechanisms

5.2 Upstream Agreements — Specific Issues

  • Split jurisdiction: Singapore (SIAC) for general disputes vs Cyprus Courts for client protection (Sections 7–9) — is this enforceable and practical?
  • Anti-circumvention: Review penalty fee structure (€75K–€500K) for enforceability in Cyprus and Singapore
  • Exclusivity provisions: Adequacy of the 12-month post-termination tail and territory protection
  • Back-to-back alignment: Do the upstream supply terms adequately protect Lighthief against downstream EPC commitments (delay LDs, warranty, performance)?
  • Payment flow risk: Lighthief pays 25% advance + 50% ex-works to Linyang but only collects 30% advance + 55% pre-shipment from clients — cashflow gap analysis
  • Performance bond: 5% of contract price — appropriateness and form
  • SOH guarantees: Enforceability of state-of-health performance commitments across 15 years
  • Coastal warranty restrictions: C5-rated enclosures required for installations within 5km of coast — contractual implications

5.3 Downstream Agreements — Specific Issues

  • Template suitability: Are the EPC and LTSA templates suitable for use across 51 separate project contracts with different counterparties?
  • Delay liquidated damages: Review graduated LD structure (0.1%–0.2%/day, 10% cap) for enforceability as a genuine pre-estimate of loss
  • Retention mechanism: 5% retention held for 24-month DLP — adequacy and client objection risk
  • LTSA availability guarantees: 97% availability commitment with LD exposure (€30/MWh/day) — commercial risk assessment
  • Client SPV risk: Many clients are special purpose vehicles — parent company guarantee requirements?
  • PAC/FAC definitions: Clarity and completeness of acceptance criteria
  • Insurance requirements: Do downstream contracts adequately require client cooperation on insurance and site access?

5.4 Third-Party Agreements — Specific Issues

  • Voltus terms alignment: Do the Voltus EMS/SCADA terms integrate with the EPC and LTSA obligations?
  • Liability gaps: If the EMS fails, is Lighthief exposed under the LTSA availability guarantee without adequate recourse against Voltus?
  • IP ownership: Who owns the data, SCADA configurations, and EMS algorithms?

5.5 Cross-Cutting Issues

  • Cyprus regulatory compliance: Gazette No. 5992 (16/01/2026) — any contractual implications of the new BESS regulations?
  • VAT treatment: 19% VAT on EPC contracts — any VAT structuring considerations?
  • Import duty: 2.66% weighted average import duty — any customs classification risks?
  • Insurance requirements: Alignment between contractual insurance obligations and actual coverage being procured
  • Data protection / GDPR: Any data sharing or processing provisions that need attention?
