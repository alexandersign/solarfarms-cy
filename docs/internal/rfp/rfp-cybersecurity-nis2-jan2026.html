Cybersecurity Hardening & NIS2 Compliance Services

RFP Issue Date: 27 January 2026
Proposal Due: 14 February 2026
Project: Cyprus BESS Portfolio - 47 Parks (863.5 MWh)
Regulatory Driver: EU NIS2 Directive / KRITIS Classification
⚠️ REGULATORY REQUIREMENT
Under EU NIS2 Directive, energy storage facilities connected to the grid are classified as critical infrastructure. Cybersecurity measures are MANDATORY for grid connection approval.

1. Project Overview

Lighthief Cyprus Ltd requires cybersecurity hardening and NIS2 compliance services for 47 BESS installations. Each installation includes SCADA/EMS systems connected to the Cyprus DSO (EAC) via IEC 60870-5-104 protocol.
