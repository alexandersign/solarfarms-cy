1. Introduction

Lighthief Cyprus Ltd is developing a utility-scale Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) portfolio in Cyprus: 47 parks across all districts, totalling approximately 863.5 MWh. Equipment is supplied by Linyang Energy (battery containers, BMS, cooling) with Kehua Tech PCS (power conversion systems) and integrated MV SKIDs (transformer + RMU + switchgear).

We are seeking proposals from INTESO GROUP for two closely related scopes of work:

SCOPE A Electrical Design Drawings

Detailed single-line diagrams, protection schemes, cable schedules, and layout drawings for DSO application and EPC execution — covering the full BESS-to-grid connection for each park type.

SCOPE B CT Specification Consultation

Expert consultation on Current Transformer (CT) class, ratio, location, and accuracy to satisfy Cyprus DSO (EAC) requirements — including direct coordination with DSO if required.

4. Scope B — CT Specification Consultation

Background: The Current Transformer (CT) class, ratio, and mounting location need to be defined and agreed with the DSO (EAC) as part of the electrical design for this portfolio. The CT specifications will feed into the equipment production order and must align with DSO requirements for protection, metering, and ancillary services.

4.1 Current Situation

The DSO (EAC) Technical Guide for Storage 2025.1 specifies what CTs must do but does not prescribe specific CT class, ratio, or location. These are project-specific decisions that require DSO agreement. The DSO single-line diagrams show:

CT SetConfigurationPurposeFeeds
Set 1 — Protection 3 × CTs + 3 × VTs Grid protection relay Overcurrent (I>>), earth fault, ROCOF, U<, U>, f<, f>
Set 2 — Metering / PQR 3 × CTs + 3 × VTs Power Quality Recorder + SCADA metering P, Q, S, V, I, f, PF, cosφ, THDi → IEC 104 addresses 500–511

4.2 Open Questions Requiring Expert Resolution

#DecisionOptionsConsiderations
1 CT Location MV side vs LV side MV side is preferred due to space constraints on the LV busbar. DSO confirmation of placement is required.
2 CT Metering Accuracy Class Class 0.2S / 0.5S / 1.0 Depends on DSO revenue metering requirements vs operational metering. Class 0.2S typically required for billing; 0.5S may suffice for PQR/SCADA.
3 CT Protection Class 5P10 / 5P20 / 10P10 Must match protection relay burden requirements. Depends on relay make/model (ABB or Schneider in MV SKID).
4 CT Ratio Based on system rated current Depends on system rated current at the CT location (MV or LV side) and must be calculated per park type. Ratio to be agreed with DSO.
5 CT Burden (VA) Based on lead length + relay/meter burden Depends on distance between CT and relay panel, cable cross-section, and connected instruments.
6 Combined CT viability Separate vs combined protection/metering CTs Can a single CT with dual-core (protection + metering) be used, or does DSO require separate CT sets?
7 Ancillary Services CTs CT requirements for FCR/aFRR participation TSO requires sub-second response and accurate metering for grid services. CT specification must support ancillary service market participation.

4.3 Consultation Deliverables

#DeliverableDescription
B1 CT Specification Document Complete CT/VT specification per park type: class, ratio, burden, accuracy, insulation level, mounting arrangement. Suitable for inclusion in equipment procurement specifications.
B2 CT Burden Calculations Detailed burden calculation per CT set: relay/meter burden + cable burden + safety margin. Confirm CT knee-point voltage is adequate.
B3 DSO Coordination Letter / Report Written technical proposal to submit to EAC/DSO for CT class and location agreement. If consultant has existing DSO relationship, direct coordination is preferred.
B4 CT Location Recommendation Technical recommendation on MV vs LV placement with justification, assessment of design implications for each option, and mitigation approaches (e.g. external CT cabinet if LV space is constrained).
B5 Protection Relay CT Compatibility Confirm CT specifications are compatible with protection relay (ABB/Schneider in MV SKID). Include relay burden data and coordination study inputs.
