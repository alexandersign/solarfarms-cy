### 3.4 DIRECTORS & OFFICERS LIABILITY (D&O) + LEGAL PROTECTION

> **Purpose:** Protects directors and officers from **personal liability** and provides **full legal defense** for any lawsuits arising from their role.

| Item | Requirement |
|------|-------------|
| **Limit** | €2,000,000 |
| **Defense Costs** | In addition to limit (not eroding) |
| **Directors Covered** | 2 (current), all future directors |
| **Officers Covered** | All managers, company secretary |
| **Territory** | Worldwide (excluding USA - optional) |
| **Claims Basis** | Claims-made |
| **Prior Acts Coverage** | Full retroactive coverage |

**Coverage Components:**

| Side | Coverage | Description |
|------|----------|-------------|
| **Side A** | Direct D&O | Pays director directly when company cannot/won't indemnify |
| **Side B** | Company Reimbursement | Reimburses company when it indemnifies director |
| **Side C** | Entity Coverage | Protects company itself for securities/regulatory claims |

**🔴 LEGAL PROTECTION FOR DIRECTORS - MUST INCLUDE:**

| Protection | Requirement |
|------------|-------------|
| **Defense Costs** | 100% coverage for attorney fees, court costs, expert witnesses |
| **Pre-Claim Costs** | Coverage for legal advice when threat of claim arises |
| **Investigation Costs** | Legal representation during regulatory/criminal investigations |
| **Civil Lawsuit Defense** | Full defense against any civil claims |
| **Criminal Proceeding Defense** | Defense costs (not fines) for criminal allegations |
| **Extradition Costs** | Legal costs to contest extradition proceedings |
| **Appeal Costs** | Costs to appeal adverse judgments |
| **Bail Bond Costs** | Up to €250,000 |
| **Asset Protection** | Costs to protect personal assets during proceedings |

**Types of Lawsuits Covered:**

| Claimant | Lawsuit Type | Example |
|----------|--------------|---------|
| **Shareholders** | Mismanagement, breach of duty | Investor claims company was mismanaged |
| **Creditors** | Wrongful trading, insolvency | Supplier claims directors traded while insolvent |
| **Employees** | Wrongful termination, discrimination | Employee sues director for unfair dismissal |
| **Clients** | Breach of contract, negligence | Client sues director personally for failed project |
| **Regulators** | Non-compliance, violations | Tax authority, labor inspector, environmental agency |
| **Competitors** | Unfair competition, IP theft | Competitor claims unfair business practices |
| **Third Parties** | Personal injury, property damage | Person injured by company operations sues director |
| **Government** | Criminal allegations | Fraud, tax evasion, health & safety violations |

**Scenarios Covered:**
- Shareholder claims for mismanagement
- Creditor claims in insolvency
- Employment practices claims (wrongful termination, discrimination, harassment)
- Regulatory investigations (tax, labor, environmental, health & safety)
- Client lawsuits naming directors personally
- Criminal defense (defense costs only, not fines/penalties)
- Defamation claims against directors
- Breach of fiduciary duty allegations
- Antitrust/competition law proceedings
- Breach of fiduciary duty claims
- Contractual disputes involving director decisions
