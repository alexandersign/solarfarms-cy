## 5. BESS FIRE RISK ENGINEERING

> Underwriters should note the following risk mitigation measures specific to BESS installations. Lighthief's portfolio uses **Lithium Iron Phosphate (LFP)** chemistry exclusively, which has a significantly lower thermal runaway risk than NMC chemistry.

### 5.1 Battery Chemistry & Certification

| Measure | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Battery Chemistry** | LFP (Lithium Iron Phosphate) — inherently thermally stable, lower energy density than NMC |
| **Cell Manufacturer** | Eve Energy Co., Ltd (314Ah LFP cells) — Linyang is a shareholder in Eve Energy |
| **UL9540A** | **PASSED — no thermal propagation between modules** |
| **IEC 62619** | Certified — secondary lithium cells safety |
| **IEC 63056** | Certified — ESS safety requirements |
| **IEC 62933-5-2** | In progress — grid ESS safety |
| **UN 38.3** | Certified — transport safety |
| **UL 94 V-0** | Certified — fire retardant materials |
| **FM 5-33** | Compliance assessed — lithium-ion ESS loss prevention guidance |

### 5.2 Container Fire Protection

| System | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **Fire Suppression** | Integrated aerosol fire suppression system per container (automatic activation) |
| **Fire Detection** | Multi-zone smoke detection, heat detection, and gas detection (H₂, CO) per container |
| **BMS Monitoring** | Real-time cell-level voltage, temperature, and current monitoring with automatic shutdown |
| **Thermal Sensors** | Per-cell and per-module temperature monitoring with configurable alarm thresholds |
| **Emergency Shutdown** | Automatic disconnection on thermal anomaly, overcurrent, or overvoltage detection |
| **Ventilation** | Forced ventilation system with gas concentration monitoring to prevent explosive gas accumulation |
| **Enclosure Rating** | IP55 rated containers, C5 corrosion protection (coastal/industrial) standard for all Cyprus sites |

### 5.3 Site Layout & Separation

| Measure | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Container Spacing** | Minimum 6m between BESS containers (exceeds FM 5-33 and insurer 4.5m minimum) |
| **Infrastructure Separation** | BESS containers positioned at safe distance from MV transformers, substations, and PV arrays |
| **Fire Break Strategy** | "Let it burn" strategy viable — individual container fire will not propagate to adjacent units at 6m spacing |
| **PV Separation** | BESS power routed to grid separately from existing PV installation — no single point of failure |
| **Access** | Minimum 4m fire access roads around BESS installations for emergency vehicle access |

### 5.4 Emergency Response

| Measure | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Emergency Plan** | Site-specific emergency response plan for each of the 47 parks |
| **Fire Brigade Coordination** | Pre-incident plans shared with local fire services — LFP-specific response protocols |
| **24/7 Monitoring** | Voltus SCADA + Lighthief NOC — critical alerts escalated within 1 hour |
| **On-Site Response** | Dedicated BESS field team — maximum 90 minutes to any site in Cyprus |
| **Water Strategy** | Water cooling recommended for containing thermal events (per LEARISK and NFPA 855 guidance) |

### 8.4 Linyang AXA Policy — Confirmed Exclusions (Received 9 March 2026)

| **Sanctions Exclusion** | No coverage if claim would violate PRC, EU, UK, or US sanctions | Low risk for Cyprus BESS installations. No action required. |
