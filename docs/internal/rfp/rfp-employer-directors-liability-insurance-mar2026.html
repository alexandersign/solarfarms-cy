1. Introduction & Background

Lighthief Cyprus Ltd invites proposals from licensed Cyprus insurance providers for its corporate insurance programme covering employer liability, directors & officers liability, professional indemnity, and ancillary covers. This RFP is for general corporate insurances only and explicitly excludes project-specific covers.

Out of Scope: Contractor's All Risks (CAR/EAR), Delay in Start-Up (DSU), Marine Cargo, and project-specific Third Party Liability are procured separately under the BESS project insurance programme and are not part of this RFP.
