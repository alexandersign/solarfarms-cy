Request for Proposal

Earthing Grid & Grounding System Installation

RFP Issue Date: 27 January 2026
Proposal Due: 10 February 2026
Project: Cyprus BESS Portfolio - 47 Parks (863.5 MWh)
Standard: IEC 62305 / IEEE 80
⚠️ DSO & MANUFACTURER REQUIREMENT
Earth resistance must be ≤ 1Ω per Linyang specifications and Cyprus DSO requirements. Earthing enhancement may be required at sites with high soil resistivity.

1. Project Overview

Lighthief Cyprus Ltd requires earthing grid design, installation, and testing for 47 BESS installations. The scope includes primary earth grid, equipment bonding, and earthing enhancement where soil conditions require.

5. Technical Requirements

  • All materials to IEC 62561 (LPS components)
  • Copper tape: 25×3mm minimum or equivalent cable
  • All connections: exothermic weld or approved clamps
  • Corrosion protection for direct burial
  • Touch and step voltage within safe limits
  • Grid design per IEEE 80 or IEC 62305
