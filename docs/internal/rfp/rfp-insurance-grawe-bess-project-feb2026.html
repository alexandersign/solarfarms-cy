1.1 Project Description

Lighthief Cyprus Ltd is installing 29 utility-scale Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS) within existing licensed photovoltaic (PV) parks across all five districts of Cyprus. Each BESS system is installed on a separate plot within the PV park boundary, connected to the existing grid connection point. The BESS systems are containerised (20ft and 40ft shipping containers) placed on reinforced concrete foundations.

Important distinction: Lighthief is the EPC contractor and O&M manager, not the asset owner. The BESS systems are owned by independent client SPVs (solar park project companies). We require insurance for our contractual liabilities and professional responsibilities in both phases.

Why LEG3/06 for BESS: A defective battery module could damage adjacent modules within the same container. LEG3 covers the cost of making good the defective part AND all resultant damage — critical for high-value containerised BESS where component interdependence is high. LEG1 (resultant damage only) would be inadequate.

BESS-specific safety measures beyond PV park prerequisites: In addition to meeting all PV park requirements above, the BESS installations include per-container fire suppression (aerosol + water backup), H₂/CO gas detection, BMS cell-level thermal monitoring, explosion vent panels, forced ventilation, and UL9540A-certified no-propagation battery chemistry (LFP).
