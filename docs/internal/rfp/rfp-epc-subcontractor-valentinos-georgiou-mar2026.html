EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Lighthief Cyprus Ltd is the exclusive BESS distributor, EPC contractor, and O&M service provider for Linyang Energy in Cyprus. We are delivering 46 utility-scale Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) parks across five districts, totalling 798 MWh / 249 MW, in three phased batches between July 2026 and February 2027.

We are seeking a qualified EPC subcontractor to provide on-site services across four scope areas: civil works, lightning protection, DSO compliance, and logistics. This RFP requests pricing per park, per container, and per MW/MWh to enable direct comparison and batch-level budgeting.

A further 5 parks (87.5 MWh) are planned for 2028 and may be awarded as a follow-on engagement.

3. Scope of Work

We are inviting proposals across four EPC scope areas. You may quote for any or all scopes. Each scope should be priced independently to allow flexible award.

3.1 Scope A — Civil Works CIVIL

Design and construction of reinforced concrete foundations for all BESS and MV SKID containers.

#Work ItemDescriptionStandard / Requirement
A1Structural DesignFoundation design per park, ETEK-stamped structural drawingsEN 1992-1-1, Eurocode 8 (seismic)
A2Site Survey & Setting OutTopographic survey, soil investigation, layout markingETEK certified
A3Excavation & Sub-baseExcavation to formation (min 300mm), compacted aggregate base≥130 kN/m² bearing capacity
A4Reinforced Concrete SlabC30/37 slab (200–300mm), reinforcement mesh (A393 or T12@200), DPM membraneC30/37 min, 50mm cover (75mm coastal)
A5Container Placement PreparationLevel bearing surface for container placement; container securing method TBC per OEM installation manualPer Linyang specs
A6Cable TrenchesTrenches for MV, LV, and control cables between containers and to POC600 × 600mm typical
A7DrainageSurface water drainage, falls from slabΚ.Δ.Π. 15/2026 §18.4
A8Earthing Grid (in slab)Foundation earthing conductors, bonding to containers<1Ω impedance target
A9As-Built & Test CertsAs-built drawings, concrete cube tests, compaction testsETEK structural sign-off
Regulatory: Per Κ.Δ.Π. 15/2026 §24, reinforced concrete foundations are mandatory for all BESS installations within licensed RES stations. Seismic design required (ZPA: 3.55 m/s² horizontal, 1.78 m/s² vertical). Minimum 6m setback from boundaries; 6m fire access perimeter.

3.2 Scope B — Lightning Protection, SPD & Earthing LPS

Complete lightning protection system (LPS), surge protection devices (SPD), and earthing system for all BESS sites.

#Work ItemDescription
B1Lightning Risk AssessmentPer IEC 62305-2 for each park — determine LPS class required
B2External LPS Design & SupplyAir termination network, down conductors, earth termination system. Material supply and installation.
B3SPD Supply & InstallationSurge protection devices for MV, LV AC, LV DC, and communication circuits
B4Earthing SystemEarth electrode installation, bonding network, earth resistance testing (<1Ω)
B5Installation LabourFull installation of LPS, SPD, and earthing across all sites
B6Testing & CertificationEarth resistance tests, continuity tests, LPS certification per IEC 62305
Note: If you prefer to supply specific LPS/SPD brands (e.g. DEHN, OBO Bettermann, Citel), please specify. Alternatively, we can supply the material (DEHN) and engage you for installation labour only. Please quote both options if possible.

3.3 Scope C — DSO Compliance & Documentation DSO

Preparation and submission of all regulatory documentation required for DSO (EAC) grid connection approval and building/planning permits.

#Work ItemDescription

C3CT/VT SpecificationCurrent Transformer class, ratio, burden calculation, and DSO coordination for CT specification agreement
C4Protection Relay SettingsRelay settings per DSO requirements (U< 0.9Un/0.2s, U> 1.1Un/0.2s, f< 47Hz/0.2s, f> 52Hz/0.2s, LoM/ROCOF)
