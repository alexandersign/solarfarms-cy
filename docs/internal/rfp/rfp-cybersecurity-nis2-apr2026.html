Lighthief Cyprus Ltd

Scope: Group-wide NIS2 cybersecurity compliance covering the Cyprus BESS portfolio (47 parks, 863.5 MWh) and the Disperon EMS cloud/OT infrastructure operated by Lighthief EU BESS.

Regulatory Driver: EU Directive 2022/2555 (NIS2), transposed in Cyprus by Law 60(I)/2025 (in force 25 April 2025). Lighthief entities are classified as Essential/Important Entities under Annex I — Energy (Electricity Producers & Storage Operators). The Digital Security Authority (DSA) of Cyprus is the competent authority.
⚠ REGULATORY REQUIREMENT
Under NIS2 / Law 60(I)/2025, energy storage facilities and EMS platforms connected to the grid are classified as critical infrastructure. Cyprus applies a 6-hour early-warning requirement for significant incidents (stricter than the EU 24-hour baseline). Cybersecurity measures are mandatory for continued DSO (EAC) grid connection approval and regulatory standing.

This RFP is issued jointly by Lighthief EU BESS and Lighthief Cyprus Ltd to procure a single integrated cybersecurity and NIS2 compliance programme covering both entities' systems, networks, and physical sites.

6. Vendor Qualification Requirements

  • ISO/IEC 27001 certified (organisation) or evidence of equivalent ISMS maturity
  • Demonstrated experience with IEC 62443 (industrial / OT cybersecurity) — mandatory for Stream A and Stream B
  • Demonstrated knowledge of IEC 60870-5-104 protocol and energy/utility SCADA security
  • Active NIS2 compliance project experience (references in energy, utilities, or critical infrastructure sector)
  • Ability to deploy and support across 47 geographically dispersed sites in Cyprus
  • Capability for multi-jurisdiction NIS2 advisory (EU HQ Poland + Cyprus operating entity)
  • CREST membership or equivalent for penetration testing delivery (preferred)
  • Financial stability: provide last 2 years' audited accounts or equivalent evidence

7. Proposal Requirements

All proposals must include the following sections:

  1. Technical Approach — methodology per stream; article-by-article NIS2 Article 21 mapping table
  2. Project Plan — Gantt chart showing pilot site, phased rollout, and governance deliverable milestones
  3. Team CVs — lead consultant, OT security engineer, and project manager CVs
  4. References — minimum two energy/utility sector NIS2 or IEC 62443 references with contact details
  5. Certifications — copies of ISO 27001 certificate, IEC 62443 credentials, CREST or equivalent
  6. Commercial Proposal — completed pricing template (sections 5.1–5.6 above) in € excl. VAT
  7. Exceptions — any deviations from this RFP scope, clearly listed
