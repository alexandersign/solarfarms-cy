1. Project Overview

Lighthief Cyprus Ltd is the exclusive distributor and EPC contractor for a portfolio of 28 Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) parks being delivered across Cyprus. This RFP covers Batch 1 only — the first confirmed order of 9 parks (36 containers). All equipment ships CIF Limassol from Linyang Energy (Shanghai, China) and requires crane unloading and heavy haulage transport from Limassol Port to solar park sites across 4 Cyprus districts.

Scope Change Notice: This RFP supersedes LCY-RFP-CRANE-2026-001 (26 February 2026). The previous 46-park / 251-container scope included ABIO Power, who have since selected an alternative supplier (March 2026). The batch plan has been rebuilt from confirmed commitments only. Batch 1 is the confirmed scope; subsequent batches will be issued as separate RFPs when confirmed.

2. Container Specifications — Types, Sizes & Weights

There are two container types across the portfolio. Both require crane placement on prepared reinforced concrete foundations at each site.

External Dimensions: 6,058 mm × 2,438 mm × 2,896 mm
Height: 2,896 mm (9 ft 6 in)

Crane Requirement: 160-tonne mobile crane (Soulis proposal)

External Dimensions (20HC): 6,058 mm × 2,438 mm × 2,896 mm
Weight (loaded): ~35 – 38 tonnes (40HC) / ~28 – 32 tonnes (20HC)
Contents: PCS Inverter (Kehua) + MV Transformer + MV Switchgear
Crane Requirement: 160-tonne mobile crane (Soulis proposal — covers both sizes)
Note: *Smaller parks (≤2.5 MW) use 20HC skids; larger parks use 40HC

3. 2026 Batch Schedule & Port ETA

Batch 1 containers arrive at Limassol Port (ECTL terminal) in a single shipment. Port clearance typically takes 5–7 working days. Containers can remain in port for up to 10 days (confirmed by A. Soulis, March 2026). Transport from port to sites should commence promptly after clearance to utilise the 10-day window.

Batch Description Parks Containers ETA Limassol Port Port Clearance Complete Transport Window Destination Districts
Batch 1 Galascope + Timotheos + Lampros + Spanercom 9 36 20 August 2026 ~27 August 2026 27 Aug – 15 Sep 2026 Famagusta, Nicosia, Limassol, Paphos
Scheduling Note: Batch 1 ETA is based on production start 1 April 2026, 90-day production (FAT by 30 June), ship 1 July, and 50-day shipping from Shanghai. Actual arrival may vary ±7 days. Lighthief will provide 14 days advance notice of confirmed vessel arrival. Crane and transport must be available within 48 hours of port clearance completion.

Permit Timing: Heavy goods transport permits require 15 days lead time and are valid for 30 days. Permit applications should be submitted as soon as vessel ETA is confirmed to ensure permits are active during the transport window. With a 10-day port storage allowance, the 30-day permit validity comfortably covers the full Batch 1 delivery programme (~3 weeks).

CRITICAL — Port Clearance Payment: Port clearance fees (via Interfreight) must be paid at least 10 working days before vessel arrival. Late payment will delay container release from port, causing truck idle charges and port parking costs. Lighthief finance must coordinate payment with Interfreight as soon as vessel ETA is confirmed.

5.3 Crane Specifications — Soulis Proposal (March 2026)

Soulis Crane Proposal: A. Soulis proposes a 160-tonne mobile crane for all container types (BESS 20HC and MV SKID 40HC).
