1. Project Overview

Lighthief Cyprus Ltd is the exclusive distributor and EPC contractor for a portfolio of 46 Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) parks being delivered across Cyprus in 2026. All equipment ships CIF Limassol from Linyang Energy (Shanghai, China) and requires crane unloading and heavy haulage transport from Limassol Port to solar park sites across 5 Cyprus districts.

Soulis provided a reference quotation (November 2025) for Limassol Port → Ayios Kendeas: 6× 20HC (43t) + 4× 20' Standard (15t), transport + crane unload = €23,600 (~€2,360/container). Fleet confirmation received 22 February 2026: 6× 20ft trucks (43T capacity) confirmed. Current transport budget: €604,160 (251 containers × €2,360). This RFP seeks formal pricing for the full 2026 programme across all routes and batches.

2. Container Specifications — Types, Sizes & Weights

There are two container types across the portfolio. Both require crane placement on prepared reinforced concrete foundations at each site.

TYPE A — Battery Container (BESS) ISO Type: 20' High Cube (20HC)
External Dimensions: 6,058 mm × 2,438 mm × 2,896 mm
Height: 2,896 mm (9 ft 6 in)
Weight (loaded): ~41.5 – 43 tonnes
Contents: LFP Battery Cells (EVE 314Ah) + BMS + HVAC Cooling + Fire Suppression
Capacity: 5.015 MWh per container
Crane Requirement: Minimum 50-tonne mobile crane
IMO Class: Class 9 (Lithium batteries)
TYPE B — MV SKID / PCS Container ISO Type: 40' High Cube (40HC) or 20' High Cube (20HC)*
External Dimensions (40HC): 12,192 mm × 2,438 mm × 2,896 mm
External Dimensions (20HC): 6,058 mm × 2,438 mm × 2,896 mm
Weight (loaded): ~35 – 38 tonnes (40HC) / ~28 – 32 tonnes (20HC)
Contents: PCS Inverter (Kehua) + MV Transformer + MV Switchgear
Crane Requirement: Min 50T (20HC) / Min 80T (40HC)
Note: *Smaller parks (≤2.5 MW) use 20HC skids; larger parks use 40HC
