1. Fire Safety Engineering — Expanded Detail

All 144 battery containers deploy a multi-layer fire protection architecture designed to detect, suppress, contain, and prevent propagation. The system uses LFP (Lithium Iron Phosphate) chemistry, which is inherently thermally stable — the most fire-safe lithium chemistry commercially available.

1.2 Fire Alarm Escalation Process

Alarm LevelTriggerAutomatic Action
Level 1 — PrimaryAny single detector triggeredAudible/visual alarm activated; BMS alert raised
Level 2 — SecondarySmoke + heat detector simultaneouslyOutdoor alarm activated; aerosol system armed
Level 3 — Gas ThresholdCombustible gas concentration detectedExplosion-proof exhaust fans activated; emergency ventilation
Level 4 — Pack AlarmPack-integrated device alarm (CO + temperature)30-second countdown → aerosol spray discharged

1.3 Fire Suppression Sequence

Automatic sequence (no human intervention required):
1. Detector triggered → Primary alarm → Audible/visual alarm
2. Smoke + heat → Secondary alarm → Outdoor alarm activated
3. Gas fire suppression system armed
4. 30-second countdown begins (allows evacuation)
5. Aerosol extinguishing agent discharged (FK-5-1-12 / Novec 1230)
6. Gas release indicator activated
7. Fans auto-shutdown when gas concentration drops below threshold
