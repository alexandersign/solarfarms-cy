1. Purpose & EPC Context

Lighthief Cyprus Ltd is the local EPC partner for two utility-scale Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) installations co-located with existing PV parks in Cyprus (Category B — RES + integrated storage). Equipment is supplied CIF Limassol by the OEM (Linyang battery containers, Kehua PCS, integrated MV SKIDs).

This RFP requests priced proposals for two separate scopes that sit within the wider EPC delivery boundary but are not included in the OEM CIF supply:

LINE 1 Electrical Design Works

Licensed electrical design, drawings, CT/VT specification, protection coordination, and DSO application packages — per park.

LINE 2 DSO Witnessing & Protection Testing

Protection relay testing, secondary injection, DSO witness attendance, formal test reports, and grid-connection clearance support — per park.

Please quote Line 1 and Line 2 separately. Lump-sum options per park and portfolio discount are welcome. Do not combine design and witnessing into a single undifferentiated price unless you also provide the split.
