## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Lighthief Cyprus Ltd is the exclusive EPC contractor and O&M service provider for Linyang Battery Energy Storage Systems in Cyprus. We are constructing and commissioning **51 utility-scale BESS projects** totalling **881.78 MWh** with an aggregate EPC contract value of **~€109 million** across all five districts of Cyprus, with peak construction activity in Q3–Q4 2026.

We are seeking Marsh Specialty's expertise to design and place a **comprehensive construction and project insurance program** covering the full lifecycle of our BESS portfolio — from equipment arrival through construction, testing, commissioning, and the extended maintenance period.

This RFP covers **project-specific insurance lines only**. General business insurance (Employers' Liability, D&O, Motor, etc.) is being placed separately through a local Cyprus broker.

**Why Marsh:** We understand Marsh Specialty supports over 650 GW of renewable energy projects globally with 400+ dedicated specialists, and has specific expertise in BESS construction risk, thermal runaway modeling, and bespoke policy wording accepted by major lenders. We believe Marsh is best positioned to secure competitive terms for our LFP BESS portfolio in the London and specialist markets.

### 3.1 CONSTRUCTION ALL RISKS (CAR) / ERECTION ALL RISKS (EAR)

| Item | Requirement |
|------|-------------|
| **Structure** | Single Annual Policy with Declaration Basis |
| **Sum Insured (Any One Project)** | €50,000,000 |
| **Aggregate Limit** | €109,000,000 |
| **Period** | 1 April 2026 – 31 March 2028 (covering all 3 batches + FAC) |
| **Extended to** | 31 March 2029 (for Esperia 2028 batch) |
| **Basis** | Declarations — new projects reported monthly |
| **Premium** | Deposit + adjustable based on declarations |
| **Defects Clause** | **LEG3/06 REQUIRED** |
| **Maintenance Period** | 24 months post-PAC |
| **Peak Exposure** | ~€98M (Q4 2026 — all 3 batches in construction) |

**Material Damage Coverage:**
- All equipment during erection and installation
- BESS containers, PCS/inverters, MV transformers, switchgear
- Cabling, grounding, civil works
- Testing and commissioning (hot testing, grid synchronisation)
- Existing property at site (PV installations) — €1M per occurrence

**Special CAR/EAR Requirements:**
- LFP lithium battery coverage — **no lithium/battery exclusion**
- LEG3/06 defects clause (broadest cover — see justification below)
- Fire and explosion coverage for battery thermal events
- No exclusion for electrical/mechanical breakdown during testing
- Coverage for DSO-mandated testing requirements
- Debris removal: 10% of loss
- Professional fees for reinstatement: €100,000
- Sub-contractor coverage included
- Lighthief named as insured; clients as additional insured per project

**Why LEG3/06 for BESS:**

| Clause | Coverage | BESS Suitability |
|--------|----------|-----------------|
| LEG1 | Resultant damage only — excludes defective item | Inadequate — defective battery module could damage adjacent modules |
| LEG2 | Resultant damage + making good, but only during construction | Partial — misses post-commissioning discovery |
| **LEG3** | **Cost of making good defective part AND all resultant damage** | **Recommended — broadest cover for high-value BESS** |

### 3.2 DELAY IN START-UP (DSU)

| Item | Requirement |
|------|-------------|
| **Indemnity Period** | 12 months |
| **Revenue at Risk** | €150–300 per MWh/year (curtailment avoidance revenue) |
| **Trigger** | Material damage covered under CAR/EAR causing delay to COD |
| **Waiting Period** | 30 days |
| **Basis** | Linked to CAR/EAR — same insurer |

**Exposure:** Lighthief's EPC contracts include delay LDs of 0.1–0.2% per day, capped at 10% of contract value. A material damage event causing 60+ days delay on a €5M project exposes Lighthief to ~€500K in delay LDs. With all 3 batches overlapping in Q3–Q4 2026, a port incident or customs delay could affect multiple projects simultaneously.

### 4.2 Fire Protection & Detection (Per Container)

| System | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **Fire Suppression** | Integrated aerosol fire suppression (automatic activation) |
| **Gas Detection** | H₂ and CO gas concentration monitoring per container |
| **Smoke Detection** | Multi-zone smoke detection per container |
| **Heat Detection** | Thermal sensors per cell and per module |
| **BMS** | Real-time cell-level voltage, temperature, and current monitoring; automatic shutdown on anomaly |
| **Ventilation** | Forced ventilation with gas concentration monitoring to prevent explosive gas accumulation |
| **Enclosure** | IP55 rated, C5 corrosion protection (coastal/industrial standard for all Cyprus sites) |

### 4.3 Site Layout & Separation Distances

| Measure | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Container Spacing** | Minimum 6m between BESS containers (exceeds FM 5-33 and typical insurer 4.5m minimum) |
| **Infrastructure Separation** | BESS at safe distance from MV transformers, substations, PV arrays |
| **Fire Break Strategy** | "Let it burn" viable — individual container will not propagate to adjacent units at 6m spacing |
| **PV Separation** | BESS routed to grid separately from PV — no single point of failure |
| **Fire Access** | Minimum 4m access roads for emergency vehicles around all BESS installations |
| **PML Reduction** | 6m spacing significantly reduces Probable Maximum Loss vs industry-standard 3m spacing |

### 4.4 Emergency Response

| Measure | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Emergency Plans** | Site-specific plans for all 51 parks |
| **Fire Brigade** | Pre-incident plans shared with local services; LFP-specific response protocols |
| **24/7 Monitoring** | Voltus SCADA/EMS + Lighthief NOC; critical alerts escalated within 1 hour |
| **On-Site Response** | Dedicated BESS field engineers; maximum 90 minutes to any site in Cyprus; 4-hour response SLA |
| **Water Cooling** | Recommended for thermal event containment (per LEARISK and NFPA 855) |

### 4.5 Risk Assessment Summary

| Risk Factor | Assessment | Detail |
|-------------|------------|--------|
| **Fire / Thermal Runaway** | LOW | LFP chemistry, UL9540A no-propagation, 6m spacing, gas detection |
