1. Project Overview

Lighthief Cyprus Ltd is importing 251 containerised Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) units from Linyang Energy (Shanghai, China) under CIF Limassol terms. Interfreight Logistics is requested to provide comprehensive port clearance, customs brokerage, and IMO container handling services for the full 2026 import programme across 3 delivery batches.

Previous Engagement: Interfreight provided an indicative quotation (31 January 2026, Ref: LCY-LOGISTICS-INTERFREIGHT-2026) covering customs clearance at €85/declaration, landing charges €450–€650/container, and inland transport rates. This RFP seeks a formal binding proposal for the full 2026 programme with confirmed volumes, CIF values, and batch schedule.
Scope Boundary: Heavy transport from Limassol Port to site and crane unloading is handled separately by A. Soulis Special Lifting & Heavy Haulage under contract LCY-RFP-CRANE-2026-001. Interfreight's scope covers all port-side services: customs clearance, port landing coordination, ECTL storage management, import duty facilitation, and IMO/DG handling.

2. Container Specifications — Types, Sizes & Weights

All containers are classified as IMO Class 9 (Lithium Batteries, UN 3536 — Lithium batteries installed in cargo transport unit). Two container types are used across the portfolio.

TYPE A — Battery Container (BESS) ISO Type: 20' High Cube (20HC)
External Dimensions: 6,058 mm × 2,438 mm × 2,896 mm
Weight (loaded): ~41.5 – 43 tonnes
Contents: LFP Battery Cells (EVE 314Ah) + BMS + HVAC + Fire Suppression
Capacity: 5.015 MWh per container
HS Code: 8507 60 00 90
IMO Class: 9 (Lithium batteries)
UN Number: UN 3536
TYPE B — MV SKID / PCS Container ISO Type: 40' High Cube (40HC) or 20' High Cube (20HC)*
Dimensions (40HC): 12,192 mm × 2,438 mm × 2,896 mm
Dimensions (20HC): 6,058 mm × 2,438 mm × 2,896 mm
Weight (loaded): ~28 – 38 tonnes
Contents: PCS Inverter (Kehua) + MV Transformer + Switchgear
HS Codes: 8504 40 (PCS), 8504 21/23 (Transformer), 8537 (Switchgear)

*Note: Smaller parks (≤2.5 MW) use 20HC skids
