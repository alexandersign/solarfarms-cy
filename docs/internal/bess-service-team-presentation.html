About Lighthief

European-Asian Renewable Energy EPC & O&M Operator — 11 Countries, 150+ Professionals

Founded nearly a decade ago, Lighthief has evolved from a small Polish-British company into a rapidly growing European-Asian renewable energy EPC and O&M operator. Today, we deliver and manage hundreds of megawatts of solar, wind, biogas, and BESS installations across 11 countries, providing comprehensive turnkey solutions from project development through long-term operations.

Our strength lies in vertical integration: we design, procure, build, commission, and maintain energy installations with our own teams. No subcontracted engineering — every project is delivered by Lighthief professionals who understand the full lifecycle of renewable energy assets.

Rotation Schedule
