About Lighthief

European-Asian Renewable Energy O&M Operator — 11 Countries, 150+ Professionals

Founded nearly a decade ago, Lighthief has evolved from a small Polish-British company into a rapidly growing European-Asian renewable energy O&M operator. Today, we manage hundreds of megawatts of solar, wind, biogas, and now BESS installations across 11 countries.

Our Cyprus division — Lighthief Cyprus Ltd (HE 477423) — serves as the exclusive Linyang BESS distribution partner for the Republic of Cyprus, deploying an 863.5 MWh battery energy storage portfolio across 51 parks island-wide.
