## 7. IMPORTANT NOTES & ASSUMPTIONS

1. **Rates are exclusive of VAT** and any applicable import duties or taxes
2. **Standard container weights** and direct access to delivery sites assumed
3. **Special handling requirements** (cranes, side loaders, escorts, restricted access, DG handling for lithium batteries) quoted separately
4. **Waiting time:** 2 hours free for unloading, thereafter €30/hour
5. **Customs inspections, scanning, or documentary queries** may result in additional charges
6. **Final pricing depends on:**
   - Confirmed container type (20' / 40' / HC)
   - Gross weight
   - UN classification (if applicable)
   - Delivery windows

7. **CE Marking:** Ensure all products have applicable compliance documents and are properly CE marked for EU importation
