## Transaction Structure — Four Separate Agreements

At the Landlord/Seller's instruction, the rental and sale arrangements are **completely independent** agreements. Each stands on its own merits and can be enforced independently.

| Agreement | Type | Description |
|-----------|------|-------------|
| **A — NDA** | Standalone | Protects Matero Ltd's shareholder information. **€250,000 breach penalty.** |
| **B — Commercial Lease** | Standalone | Pure rental — 100% office + 50% warehouse, €10,000/month, 1 year + 5-year auto-renewal. No reference to any sale. |
| **C — Option to Purchase** | Standalone | Irrevocable option to buy at locked price of €1,764,000. Deposited at Land Registry. |
| **D — Post-Sale Leaseback** | Conditional | Matero retains 50% warehouse for 3 years post-sale. Deposited at Land Registry. |

### Why Separate?

- Lease does not depend on or reference the purchase option
- Termination of lease does not automatically affect purchase option (and vice versa)
- Each agreement can be enforced independently in court
- NDA stands alone and survives regardless

## Agreement A — Non-Disclosure Agreement

### Parties
- **Disclosing Party:** Matero Ltd
- **Receiving Party:** Lighthief Cyprus Ltd

### Purpose
Protects confidential information disclosed during negotiations, specifically the Landlord's internal **shareholder restructuring** matters.

### Key Terms

#### Confidential Information
- Shareholder identity, holdings, disputes, buy-out terms, restructuring
- Financial condition, valuations of Matero Ltd
- Terms and existence of the Transaction
- Business plans, strategies, client/supplier relationships

#### Enhanced Protection — Shareholder Matters
- Access restricted to: (a) Lighthief directors directly involved, (b) legal counsel retained for the Transaction
- NOT to be disclosed to any employee, contractor, consultant, investor, lender, or other person
- No storage on unsecured devices or shared cloud services

#### Breach Penalty: €250,000
- **Agreed liquidated damages** — not a punitive sum
- Represents genuine pre-estimate of minimum loss considering:
  - Disruption to shareholder restructuring
  - Loss of negotiating position
  - Reputational damage
  - Potential property value diminution
  - Third-party claims triggered by disclosure
- Does NOT relieve continuing obligations
- Disclosing Party may also seek:
  - **Injunctive relief** (court order restraining further disclosure)
  - **Additional damages** if actual loss exceeds €250,000
- Payable within 30 days of written demand

#### Duration
- Agreement lasts 5 years from signing
- Shareholder Matters obligations and penalty survive in perpetuity

### Legal Basis
- Cap. 149 (Contract Law)
- §74 — liquidated damages enforceable if genuine pre-estimate of loss
- Governing law: Republic of Cyprus
- Jurisdiction: District Court of Limassol
- Injunctive relief available without prior mediation

## Agreement B — Commercial Lease Agreement

### Parties
- **Landlord:** Matero Ltd, represented by **Christodoulos Aristocleous** (Χριστόδουλος Αριστοκλέους), Director & UBO
- **Tenant:** Lighthief Cyprus Ltd

### Leased Premises
- 100% office space + 50% warehouse space
- Matero retains 50% warehouse (Retained Premises)
- Location: Ypsonas, Limassol District

### Key Terms

#### Rent & Payment
- **Monthly Rent:** €10,000 (€4,000 office + €6,000 warehouse)
- **On Signing:** €80,000 (6 months advance rent + 2 months deposit)
- **Month 7 onwards:** €10,000/month in advance
- **Security Deposit:** €20,000 — returned within 30 days of lease end

#### Partition Wall
- Tenant erects mid-section wall within 60 days at own cost
- Must meet fire-rating standards per Cyprus Building Regulations
- Building permit required — Landlord cooperates
- Becomes permanent fixture of the Property

#### Annual Rent Adjustment (from Year 2)
- **Year 1:** Fixed at €10,000/month — no adjustment
- **From Year 2 (Renewal Period):** Annual adjustment on each anniversary:
  - Increase by the **greater of**: annual CPI change (CYSTAT) **or** 2% minimum
  - **Capped at 6% per annum** (in line with Cyprus regulatory guidance)
  - Landlord must give 30 days' written notice with CPI calculation
  - If Landlord fails to notify, Tenant continues at existing rent; no retroactive adjustment
  - CYSTAT published CPI figure is conclusive in case of dispute

#### Lease Term
- **Initial Period:** 12 months
- **Automatic Renewal:** 5 years at adjusted rent per annual adjustment clause (unless 90 days' notice given before expiry)

#### Governing Law
- Republic of Cyprus
- District Court of Limassol
- Mediation required before court (60 days)

## Notes for Legal Counsel

1. **Independence of Agreements:** B and C must be standalone. Lease references nothing about purchase. Option references Lease only for rent credit calculation.
2. **NDA Penalty (€250K):** Under Cap. 149 §74, must be genuine pre-estimate of loss. Frame as "agreed damages" with supporting rationale. Court may reduce if deemed punitive.
3. **Property Search Certificate:** Mandatory under Law 132(I)/2023 — within 5 working days of signing Agreement C.
4. **Land Registry Deposits:** Agreement C under specific performance law (6 months). Agreement D as memo of encumbrance (below 15-year threshold).
5. **Stamp Duty:** Abolished from 1 Jan 2026 (Law 239(I)/2025). None payable.
6. **VAT:** Verify if 19% applies. Existing commercial property >5 years may be exempt.
7. **Transfer Fees:** ~€54,290 after 50% discount on €1,764,000. Confirm allocation.
8. **Corporate Authority:** Board resolutions from both parties. Critical for Matero given shareholder situation.
9. **Partition Wall Permit:** Ypsonas Municipal Authority. Landlord must cooperate.
10. **Greek Translation:** May be required for Land Registry. English version prevails.
11. **Pre-emption Rights:** Confirm none on the Property.
