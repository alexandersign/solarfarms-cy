Transaction Structure — Four Separate Agreements

At the instruction of the Landlord/Seller, the rental relationship and the sale/purchase arrangement are structured as completely independent agreements. This ensures that the lease stands on its own merits and the purchase option is a separate commercial undertaking, giving both parties flexibility and clean legal separation.

  1. Agreement A — Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) Standalone
    Protects confidential information disclosed during negotiations, specifically the Landlord's internal shareholder matters. Penalty for breach: €250,000.
  2. Agreement B — Commercial Lease Agreement Standalone
    Pure rental of 100% office + 50% warehouse for 12 months at €10,000/month, with automatic 5-year renewal. No reference to any sale or purchase.
  3. Agreement C — Option to Purchase Agreement Standalone
    Separate agreement granting Lighthief Cyprus Ltd an irrevocable option to buy the Property at a locked price of €1,764,000. Deposited at Land Registry under Specific Performance Law.
  4. Agreement D — Post-Sale Leaseback Agreement Conditional
    Conditional on the sale completing. Matero Ltd retains 50% warehouse for 3 years post-sale. Deposited at Land Registry for Matero's protection.
Why Separate Agreements: The Landlord/Seller has requested that the lease and sale arrangements be entirely independent. This means:
  • The lease does not depend on or reference the purchase option
  • Termination of the lease does not automatically affect the purchase option (and vice versa)
  • Each agreement can be enforced independently in court
  • The NDA stands alone and survives regardless of the outcome of either agreement

Cyprus Law Analysis

Applicable Legislation

Contract Law Cap. 149 — Governs enforceability of all four agreements.
Immovable Property Cap. 224 — No estate or interest in immovable property can be created except under this law. Transfer must be registered at DLS.
Specific Performance Law 81(I)/2011 as amended by Law 132(I)/2023 — Protects the Buyer. Agreement C (Option to Purchase) must be deposited at Land Registry within 6 months.
Transfer Law Law 9/1965 — Governs mechanics of title transfer upon option exercise.
Stamp Duty Law 239(I)/2025 — Stamp Duty abolished from 1 January 2026. No stamp duty on any agreement.
NDA Enforcement Cap. 149, §74 — Liquidated damages clause (€250,000 penalty) is enforceable under Cyprus law provided it represents a genuine pre-estimate of loss. Courts may review if penalty is disproportionate.
Transfer Fees DLS transfer fees: 3% on first €85K; 5% on €85K–€170K; 8% above €170K. Currently 50% discount. VAT at 19% may apply if property is new commercial; exempt if second-hand.

Notes for Legal Counsel

  1. Independence of Agreements: Agreements B and C must be drafted so that neither is conditional on the other. The lease operates independently. The option to purchase references the lease only to calculate the rent credit deduction — it does not make the lease a condition of the option or vice versa.
  2. NDA Penalty (€250,000): Under Cyprus law (Cap. 149 §74), a liquidated damages clause must represent a genuine pre-estimate of loss. Counsel should assess whether €250,000 is defensible as proportionate to the potential harm from disclosure of the shareholder buy-out information. If deemed a penalty rather than liquidated damages, a court may reduce it. Consider framing as "agreed damages" with supporting rationale.
  3. Property Search Certificate: Mandatory under Law 132(I)/2023 — must be obtained within 5 working days of signing Agreement C.
  4. Land Registry Deposits: Agreement C deposited under specific performance law within 6 months. Agreement D registered as memo of encumbrance (3-year term is below 15-year DLS threshold for lease registration).
  5. Stamp Duty: Abolished from 1 January 2026 (Law 239(I)/2025). None payable on any agreement.
  6. VAT: Verify if Property is subject to 19% VAT. Existing commercial property >5 years may be exempt.
  7. Transfer Fees: On €1,764,000: approx. €108,580 before discount, approx. €54,290 after 50% discount. Confirm allocation.
  8. Corporate Authority: Board resolutions required from both parties. Critical for Matero Ltd given the shareholder situation.
  9. Partition Wall Permit: Building permit from Ypsonas Municipal Authority required. Landlord must cooperate.
  10. Greek Translation: May be required for Land Registry deposit. English version prevails in case of discrepancy.
  11. Pre-emption Rights: Confirm no Matero shareholders hold first-refusal rights over the Property.

NOW IT IS HEREBY AGREED AS FOLLOWS:

1. Definitions

  1. "Confidential Information" means all information disclosed by the Disclosing Party to the Receiving Party, whether orally, in writing, electronically, or by any other means, in connection with the Transaction, including but not limited to:
    1. The Shareholder Matters — all information relating to the identity, holdings, disputes, negotiations, buy-out terms, or restructuring of shareholders of the Disclosing Party;

  2. "Confidential Information" does not include information that:
    1. Is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the Receiving Party;
    2. Was in the Receiving Party's possession prior to disclosure, as evidenced by written records;
    3. Is independently developed by the Receiving Party without reference to the Confidential Information;
    4. Is disclosed to the Receiving Party by a third party who is not under an obligation of confidentiality to the Disclosing Party;
    5. Is required to be disclosed by law, regulation, or court order — provided the Receiving Party gives the Disclosing Party prompt written notice and cooperates in seeking protective measures.

2. Obligations of the Receiving Party

  1. The Receiving Party shall keep all Confidential Information strictly confidential and shall not disclose, publish, or disseminate it to any third party without the prior written consent of the Disclosing Party.
  2. The Receiving Party shall use the Confidential Information solely for the purpose of evaluating and performing the Transaction. No other use is permitted.
  3. The Receiving Party shall restrict access to Confidential Information to its directors, officers, employees, and professional advisors (legal, financial, tax) who have a need to know and who are bound by obligations of confidentiality at least as restrictive as those in this Agreement.
  4. The Receiving Party shall take all reasonable precautions to prevent unauthorised disclosure, including but not limited to physical and electronic security measures appropriate to the sensitivity of the information.
  5. The Receiving Party shall not make copies, reproductions, or summaries of Confidential Information except as reasonably necessary for the purpose of the Transaction.
  6. The Receiving Party shall be liable for any breach of this Agreement by its directors, officers, employees, or advisors as if the breach were committed by the Receiving Party itself.

3. Shareholder Matters — Enhanced Protection

Special Confidentiality — Shareholder Matters: The following enhanced obligations apply specifically to the Shareholder Matters, in addition to the general obligations in Clause 2.
  1. The Receiving Party acknowledges that the Shareholder Matters constitute highly sensitive commercial information and that any unauthorised disclosure could cause severe and irreparable harm to the Disclosing Party, its shareholders, and the value of the Property.
  2. Information relating to the Shareholder Matters shall be disclosed only to the following persons within the Receiving Party's organisation:
    1. The director(s) of Lighthief Cyprus Ltd directly involved in the Transaction;
    2. Legal counsel retained by Lighthief Cyprus Ltd in connection with the Transaction.
  3. The Shareholder Matters shall not be disclosed to any employee, contractor, consultant, investor, lender, or any other person not listed in Clause 3.2, regardless of any general need-to-know justification.
  4. No written record, email, memo, or other documentation containing the Shareholder Matters shall be stored on any unsecured device, shared drive, or cloud service accessible to persons not authorised under Clause 3.2.

4. Agreed Damages for Breach — €250,000

  1. The parties acknowledge and agree that a breach of this Agreement, and in particular any unauthorised disclosure of the Shareholder Matters, would cause the Disclosing Party substantial and difficult-to-quantify harm, including but not limited to:
    1. Disruption to the ongoing shareholder restructuring process;
    2. Potential loss of negotiating position with the affected shareholder(s);
    3. Reputational damage to the Disclosing Party and its directors;
    4. Potential diminution in the value of the Property or the Disclosing Party's business;
    5. Third-party claims or litigation triggered by the disclosure.
  2. In view of the foregoing, the parties agree that in the event of any breach of this Agreement by the Receiving Party (including breach by its directors, officers, employees, or advisors), the Receiving Party shall pay to the Disclosing Party the sum of Two Hundred and Fifty Thousand Euro (€250,000) as agreed and liquidated damages (the "Agreed Penalty").
  3. The Agreed Penalty represents a genuine pre-estimate of the minimum loss that the Disclosing Party would suffer from a breach, taking into account the factors listed in Clause 4.1 and the overall value of the Transaction (€2,100,000). The parties confirm that the Agreed Penalty is not a punitive sum and is proportionate to the potential harm.
  4. Payment of the Agreed Penalty shall not relieve the Receiving Party of its continuing obligations under this Agreement, nor shall it limit the Disclosing Party's right to seek:
    1. Injunctive relief — an order from the District Court of Limassol restraining further disclosure;
    2. Additional damages — if the Disclosing Party's actual loss exceeds the Agreed Penalty, the Disclosing Party may claim the excess.
  5. The Agreed Penalty shall be payable within thirty (30) days of written demand by the Disclosing Party, accompanied by reasonable particulars of the breach.

5. Return and Destruction of Information

  1. Upon the earlier of: (a) completion of the Transaction, (b) termination of negotiations, or (c) written request by the Disclosing Party, the Receiving Party shall promptly return or destroy all Confidential Information in its possession, including all copies, notes, and summaries.
  2. The Receiving Party shall provide written confirmation of destruction within fourteen (14) days of the request.
  3. The obligation to maintain confidentiality shall survive the return or destruction of materials.

6. Term and Survival

  1. This Agreement shall come into force on the date of execution and shall remain in effect for a period of five (5) years from the date of signing, regardless of whether the Transaction proceeds to completion.
  2. The obligations concerning the Shareholder Matters (Clause 3) and the Agreed Penalty (Clause 4) shall survive the expiry or termination of this Agreement and shall continue in perpetuity or until the Shareholder Matters become publicly available through no fault of the Receiving Party.

7. No Licence or Obligation to Transact

  1. Nothing in this Agreement grants the Receiving Party any licence, right, or interest in the Confidential Information, the Property, or any intellectual property of the Disclosing Party.
  2. This Agreement does not oblige either party to proceed with the Transaction or any part thereof.

8. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

  1. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Republic of Cyprus, including the Law of Contract (Cap. 149).
  2. Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the District Court of Limassol.
  3. The Disclosing Party may seek interim or injunctive relief from any court of competent jurisdiction without the need for prior mediation.

9. General

  1. This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties regarding confidentiality. No prior oral or written agreements on confidentiality shall have effect.
  2. No amendment shall be valid unless in writing and signed by both parties.
  3. This Agreement is independent of any lease, sale, or other agreement between the parties. Its validity and enforceability are not contingent on any other arrangement.
  4. If any provision is found invalid, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force.

WHEREAS:

  1. The Landlord is the registered owner of warehouse and office premises at Ypsonas, Limassol District (the "Property").
  2. The Tenant wishes to lease the Leased Premises for commercial purposes.
  3. The Landlord wishes to retain and continue occupying the Retained Premises for its own commercial purposes.

NOW IT IS HEREBY AGREED AS FOLLOWS:

1. Definitions

  1. "Commencement Date" means the date of execution of this Agreement.
  2. "Initial Lease Period" means twelve (12) consecutive calendar months from the Commencement Date.
  3. "Monthly Rent" means Ten Thousand Euro (€10,000), comprising €4,000 for the office space and €6,000 for the warehouse space.
  4. "Renewal Period" means five (5) years from the expiry of the Initial Lease Period.
  5. "Partition Wall" means the mid-section dividing wall separating the Leased Premises from the Retained Premises.

2. Grant of Lease

  1. The Landlord leases the Leased Premises to the Tenant for the Initial Lease Period, commencing on the Commencement Date.
  2. The Tenant shall have unrestricted access to the Leased Premises twenty-four (24) hours per day, seven (7) days per week.
  3. The Landlord retains the right to occupy the Retained Premises for its own commercial purposes throughout the lease term.
  4. Both parties shall share common areas (access roads, loading bays, parking) in accordance with the shared-use protocol in Schedule 1.

3. Rent and Payment

  1. Monthly Rent of €10,000 is payable in advance on the first business day of each calendar month by bank transfer.
  2. Advance Payment: Upon execution, the Tenant shall pay €80,000:
    1. Six (6) months' rent in advance: €60,000;
    2. Two (2) months' security deposit: €20,000.
  3. The advance covers Months 1 through 6. The next payment (Month 7) is due on the first business day of the seventh month following the Commencement Date.
  4. Months 7 through 12 are payable monthly in advance.
  5. Security Deposit: The €20,000 deposit shall be returned without interest within thirty (30) days of the end of the lease, less any amounts lawfully deducted for damage, unpaid rent, or other liabilities.
  6. All payments are in Euro (€), exclusive of VAT where applicable.

4. Partition Wall

  1. The Tenant shall erect the Partition Wall at its own cost within sixty (60) days of the Commencement Date.
  2. The wall shall meet fire-rated standards per Cyprus Building Regulations.
  3. The Tenant shall obtain all necessary building permits. The Landlord shall cooperate in the permit process as property owner.
  4. The cost is borne entirely by the Tenant and is not deductible from rent.
  5. The Partition Wall becomes a permanent fixture. If the lease terminates, the wall remains the Landlord's property unless the Landlord requests removal, in which case the Tenant shall remove it and restore the premises at its own cost.
  6. Other tenant improvements require the Landlord's prior written consent.

5. Annual Rent Adjustment

  1. Initial Lease Period: The Monthly Rent shall remain fixed at €10,000 for the duration of the Initial Lease Period (12 months). No adjustment shall apply during this period.
  2. Annual Adjustment from Year 2: On each anniversary of the Commencement Date falling within the Renewal Period, the Monthly Rent shall be adjusted upwards by the greater of:
    1. The annual percentage change in the Consumer Price Index (CPI) as published by the Statistical Service of the Republic of Cyprus (CYSTAT) for the twelve-month period ending on the most recent published index date prior to the adjustment date; or
    2. Two percent (2%) per annum (the "Minimum Increase").
  3. Cap: The annual increase shall not exceed six percent (6%) per annum, in line with prevailing Cyprus regulatory guidance on commercial rent adjustments.
  4. Calculation: The adjusted Monthly Rent shall be calculated as: New Monthly Rent = Previous Monthly Rent × (1 + Adjustment Rate), rounded to the nearest whole Euro.
  5. Notification: The Landlord shall notify the Tenant in writing of the adjusted Monthly Rent at least thirty (30) days before the anniversary date, specifying the CPI figure relied upon and the calculation applied.
  6. Failure to Notify: If the Landlord fails to deliver timely notice, the Tenant shall continue paying the existing Monthly Rent until notice is received. The adjusted rent shall apply from the first month following receipt of notice; no retroactive adjustment shall be charged for months before notice was received.
  7. Dispute: If the Tenant disputes the calculation, the parties shall attempt to resolve the dispute within thirty (30) days. If unresolved, the CPI figure published by CYSTAT shall be conclusive.

6. Automatic Renewal

  1. Unless either party gives the other ninety (90) days' written notice before expiry of the Initial Lease Period, the lease shall automatically renew for a Renewal Period of five (5) years.
  2. During the Renewal Period, all terms of this Agreement continue to apply, including the annual rent adjustment mechanism in Clause 5.
  3. At the end of the Renewal Period, the lease shall terminate unless the parties agree in writing to a further extension.

7. Landlord's Obligations

  1. Deliver the Leased Premises in good and tenantable condition on the Commencement Date.
  2. Structural repairs and maintenance of the building exterior, roof, and common areas.
  3. Maintain building insurance covering the structure. The Tenant shall be added as an interested party.
  4. Not mortgage, charge, or encumber the Property in a manner that would materially interfere with the Tenant's quiet enjoyment, without the Tenant's prior written consent.

8. Tenant's Obligations

  1. Pay the Monthly Rent punctually.
  2. Use the Leased Premises for lawful commercial purposes only.
  3. Internal repairs and maintenance, including utilities consumed by the Tenant.
  4. Contents insurance and public liability insurance.
  5. Erect the Partition Wall per Clause 4.
  6. No sub-letting or assignment without the Landlord's prior written consent.

9. Utilities and Shared Costs

  1. Each party bears its own utility costs. Shared meters shall be split 50/50 or by sub-metering installed by the Tenant.
  2. Municipal taxes, refuse, and common area maintenance apportioned by occupied space (50/50 warehouse; 100% office to Tenant).

10. Termination

  1. Not terminable during the Initial Lease Period except:
    1. By mutual written agreement;
    2. For material breach, following thirty (30) days' written notice and failure to cure;
    3. Insolvency, winding-up, or receivership of either party.
  2. Upon termination, the security deposit shall be returned per Clause 3.5.

11. Governing Law and Disputes

  1. Governed by the laws of the Republic of Cyprus.
  2. Exclusive jurisdiction of the District Court of Limassol.
  3. Parties shall attempt mediation under the Cyprus Mediation Law (L.159(I)/2012) before court proceedings. Mediation period: sixty (60) days.

12. General

  1. Entire Agreement: This Agreement is the entire agreement between the parties concerning the lease. No oral or prior written agreement modifies these terms.
  2. Amendments must be in writing and signed by both parties.
  3. Notices in writing to addresses in Schedule 2. Email with delivery confirmation is acceptable.
  4. Severability, binding effect, waiver, counterparts, and language provisions apply in standard form.
  5. Where a Greek translation is required for any purpose, the English version shall prevail in case of discrepancy.
