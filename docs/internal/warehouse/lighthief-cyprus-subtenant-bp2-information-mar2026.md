## 4. Activities — Linyang, 7SUN, and core operations

Use the following **narrative descriptions** on the application (adapt Greek as needed):

### 4.1 Core activity (Lighthief Cyprus)

- **Αιτούμενη χρήση / δραστηριότητα:** Commercial **storage, logistics hub, and technical support base** for **utility-scale Battery Energy Storage System (BESS)** equipment and spare parts; **field service coordination** for EPC and **operations & maintenance (O&M)** on **grid-connected solar + BESS** installations in the Republic of Cyprus.
- **Nature of work at the warehouse:** Receipt and storage of **containers, skids, spare parts, tools, test equipment**; indoor activities only — **no heavy manufacturing**; **no emissions** beyond normal building use; **wastes** limited to **commercial / packaging / EEE recycling** via licensed collectors (to be detailed in environmental section).

### 4.2 Linyang Energy (current & expanding)

- **Lighthief Cyprus Ltd** is the **exclusive authorised distributor / EPC partner** for **Linyang** grid-scale BESS products in **Cyprus** under group commercial arrangements with **Linyang** and EU logistics via **Solarfun / Linyang European HQ (Poland)**.
- **Current:** Procurement, customs, installation supervision, commissioning support, warranty coordination, and O&M for **BESS plants** across Cyprus.
- **New / scaling:** Additional **equipment inbounds**, **spare-parts stocking**, and **field team staging** as the **Cyprus BESS portfolio** is delivered through **2026–2028**; warehouse supports **supply chain and O&M**, not factory production.

### 4.3 7SUN Cyprus JV (current & retail / wholesale)

- **7SUN** operations in Cyprus (JV with **7sun.eu** / Lighthief International) cover **wholesale and retail** solar equipment and related products, **warehousing and pickup** in **Limassol**, and **coordination with EU stock** (e.g. Poland).
- **Relationship to this site:** The **Ypsonas warehouse** is intended to support **shared logistics** where agreed (storage, **cross-docking**, **last-mile** to installers) — **non-industrial processing**; office and trade coordination may be ancillary.

### 4.4 One-line summary for “reason for this industrial zone”

*Central Limassol / **Ypsonas** industrial access, **loading bays**, **power**, and **highway links** to all districts where BESS and PV sites are located; compliant **industrial zoning** for logistics and technical operations supporting the national energy-transition build-out.*
