3. Critical Flags & Pricing Impact

✅ FLAG #1 — Civil Works Rate: CONFIRMED €2,000/MWh
Kamil mentioned "2 Million for 800 MWh" and "€2,500/MWh" in passing during the meeting, but the confirmed rate remains €2,000/MWh (per direct prior confirmation). The €2,500 figure referenced broader scope including additional cable connection lines beyond the agreed scope. Model uses €2,000/MWh = €1,763,560 portfolio total. No change required.
FLAG #2 — CT Decision is Production-Blocking
Current transformers must be specified before Kehua production order. Kehua cannot modify on site. CT class needs DSO agreement. If DSO requires LV-side CT, there is insufficient busbar space. This must be resolved BEFORE any manufacturing begins.
FLAG #3 — Lighthief Manpower Requirement: 30 People
Kamil stated Lighthief needs 5 teams × 6 persons = 30 people for installation support. This is a significant resource commitment not currently budgeted. Need to assess:
  • Hiring/subcontracting plan for 30 site workers
  • Duration and overlap with Linyang's 12-person team
  • Cost impact on Lighthief supervision budget (currently €5,000/park = €255,000)
FLAG #4 — NEW Cost Items Not in Model
  • Fencing / entrance control — not currently priced
  • WAGU spare controllers — 5–10 units + memory cards (10% spares rule)
  • Internet connections — separate from Ethernet, per site
  • ADR truck compliance — may impact transport costs vs A. Soulis quote
  • NIS2 cyber security hardening — no quote, no ownership assigned
FLAG #5 — Earthing Rooftop Risk
Previous Linyang project: crane crashed and made a hole in container rooftop during earthing bolt installation. Took 3 weeks to repair. Welding bolts for earthing can damage rooftop. Need 1 week advance notice; planning and execution in 1 day. Recommendation: Add contingency time in project schedule + verify earthing attachment method with DEHN.
RESOLVED — PPC Not Needed
PPC (Power Plant Controller) is NOT a separate item. It is WAGU, integrated into EMS in the Voltus quote. ITC gateway also included in Voltus quote. This eliminates a potential scope gap.
CONFIRMED — Marine Insurance
CIF Incoterms include marine insurance. This is Linyang's responsibility. Confirms our model assumption of 0.75% of CIF insurance being on Lighthief's account (CAR/EAR, not marine transit).
