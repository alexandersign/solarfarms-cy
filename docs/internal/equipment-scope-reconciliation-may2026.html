6. Component A / Component B Split for Client Invoicing

The EPC v5.0 (§6.2, §6.3, Schedule A) requires both components to be stated per contract. This is how to derive them:

ComponentDefinitionFormulaB1 ExampleAPG Coverage?
Component A
Equipment Supply Price		 CIF Limassol value of Linyang hardware only (battery containers + PCS + MV skids + all equipment within CIF quotation) = CIF from Linyang quotation for that specific park B1 parks: €12,177,400 total (≈€1,353,044 per park avg) YES — APG = 70% of Component A (advance 20% + pre-ship 50%). B1 APG = €8,524,180
Component B
EPC Services Price		 All EPC adder costs: logistics, electrical, civil, DEHN, EMS/SCADA, insurance, docs, project management = Installed Cost − CIF
= (Physical Adders + EMS) per park		 B1: €13,868,100 − €12,177,400 = €1,690,700 (≈€187,856 per park avg) NO — APG does NOT cover Component B per EPC §10.9(e)
Contract Price A + B — the price invoiced to client = Client Revenue per park B1: €15,067,200 (client pays this — inclusive of margin) Client payment milestones apply to total Contract Price
Practical implication for Schedule A population: For each client EPC, Component A = the CIF quoted by Linyang for that park (from LY202601271 or LY202602111). Component B = Contract Price − Component A. Both must be declared in Schedule A. The APG is then sized at 70% of Component A only, not 70% of total Contract Price. For a €3M park: Component A ≈ €2.5M, APG = €1.75M. Component B ≈ €0.5M, no APG coverage.
