## KEY OBSERVATIONS

### ✅ HIGH MARGIN PARKS (>15%)
- **5MW/10MWh configurations** yield best margins (~22%) due to high €/MWh pricing
- **Small systems** (<5 MWh) have highest €/MWh but also higher costs
- **Easy Power 1-3, Renergetic, Solarity** are the most profitable

### ⚠️ LOW MARGIN PARKS (<12%)
- **Large systems** (25+ MWh) have lowest margins due to aggressive volume pricing
- **Dianary 2 plot15,157** (35 MWh) - 9.3% margin
- **Agios Sozomenos** (25 MWh) - 9.1% margin
- These need economies of scale to work

### 💡 MARGIN OPTIMIZATION OPPORTUNITIES

| Option | Impact | Risk |
|--------|--------|------|
| Increase large system pricing by €2,000/MWh | +€120,000 margin | May lose deal |
| Bundle more parks per order (logistics savings) | +1-2% margin | Coordination risk |
| Negotiate Linyang volume discount | +2-3% margin | Already at group rate |
| Reduce group discount from €500 to €300/MWh | +€45,000 margin | Competitive pressure |
