3. Request

We request a short-term credit facility (overdraft or revolving facility) to bridge the period from payment of VAT at customs (August 2026) until receipt of the VAT refund from the Tax Department (expected mid-November 2026).

The facility would be secured / supported by the expected VAT refund of approximately €2.6M (Batch 1), with repayment from the refund proceeds once received.

We can provide cashflow schedules, contract summaries, and confirmation of the VAT refund process with the Limassol office on request.
