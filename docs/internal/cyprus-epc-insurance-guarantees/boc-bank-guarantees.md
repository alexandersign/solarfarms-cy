## Recommended Department

### Primary: Wholesale Banking — Εμπορικές Διευκολύνσεις (Commercial Trade Facilities)

This is BOC's **trade finance / bank guarantee desk** for corporate clients. The product page (Greek, verified June 2026) states:

> *Προσφέρουμε βραχυπρόθεσμες χρηματοδοτήσεις εισαγωγών, **εγγυητικές επιστολές**, πιστώσεις εισαγωγής και εξαγωγής, γραμμάτια προς είσπραξη...*

Translation: BOC offers short-term import finance, **letters of guarantee**, import/export credits, bills for collection, foreign exchange, and factoring.

**URL:** https://www.bankofcyprus.com/wholesale/daily-banking/day-to-day/wholesale_emporikes_diefkolinsis/

Wholesale → Daily Banking → Day-to-Day → Εμπορικές Διευκολύνσεις

## How to Initiate a Guarantee Request (Existing BOC Client)

### Step 1 — Relationship Manager (fastest route)

As an existing BOC corporate client, **start with your assigned Relationship Manager (RM)** in Wholesale Banking. They will:

1. Confirm your **guarantee limit / line** (often part of overall credit facility)
2. Route the request to **Εμπορικές Διευκολύνσεις / Trade Finance operations**
3. Coordinate margin/collateral requirements with credit

If you do not know your RM: call business line below and ask for **Wholesale Banking — Trade Finance**.

### Step 2 — Prepare documentation

Typical BOC requirements (standard Cyprus banking practice — confirm with RM):

| Document | Notes |
|----------|-------|
| Board resolution | Authorising guarantee issuance |
| Contract copy | Underlying EPC/construction agreement |
| Guarantee wording | Often employer's template — bank may amend |
| Financial statements | Last 2–3 years audited |
| Company KYC | HE 477423 registry extract, UBO declarations |
| Collateral / margin | Cash cover, lien on deposits, or unsecured if limit approved |

### Step 3 — Guarantee types to request

| Type | EPC use |
|------|---------|
| **Bid bond / tender guarantee** | Pre-award |
| **Performance bond** | 5–10% contract value through defects liability |
| **Advance payment guarantee** | Matches advance payment % |
| **Retention guarantee** | In lieu of cash retention |
| **Warranty / maintenance guarantee** | Post-completion O&M period |

## Typical Costs & Timelines (Industry Benchmarks — Confirm with BOC)

| Item | Typical range | Notes |
|------|---------------|-------|
| Issuance fee | 0.5%–2.0% p.a. on guarantee amount | Varies by credit rating, collateral, tenor |
| Minimum commission | €500–€2,000 per instrument | Bank-specific |
| Cash margin | 0%–100% of guarantee value | Unsecured lines for established clients reduce margin |
| Processing time | 5–15 business days | First-time setup longer; renewals faster |
| Validity | Matches contract (often 12–24 months + claim period) | Extensions require amendment |

*These are market benchmarks for Cyprus corporate banking — BOC will quote specifically.*

## Counter-Guarantee / Collateral

BOC may require one or more of:

- **Cash collateral** (blocked deposit in guarantee currency)
- **Lien on existing deposits** with BOC
- **Parent company counter-guarantee** (if applicable)
- **Credit facility allocation** from approved limit
- **Personal guarantees** (rare for established corporates)

Existing banking relationship and track record with BOC should reduce friction.
