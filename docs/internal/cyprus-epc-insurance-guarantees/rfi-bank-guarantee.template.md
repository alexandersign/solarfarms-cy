## 2. Guarantee Requirements

We request terms for the following instruments supporting **multi-MW BESS EPC contracts** in Cyprus:

| # | Guarantee type | Purpose | Typical amount | Typical tenor |
|---|----------------|---------|----------------|---------------|
| 1 | **Bid bond / tender guarantee** | Pre-qualification & tender submission | 2–3% of tender value | 90–180 days |
| 2 | **Performance bond** | Contract performance through defects period | 5–10% of contract value | 12–24 months post-COD |
| 3 | **Advance payment guarantee** | Securing employer advance payments | Up to 20% of contract | Until advance fully recovered |
| 4 | **Retention guarantee** | Release of contract retention | 5–10% of contract | Defects liability period |
| 5 | **Warranty / maintenance guarantee** | Post-completion O&M obligations | Per contract | 2–5 years |

**Aggregate annual throughput:** Multiple guarantees per year across several projects; please quote both **individual instrument pricing** and **umbrella / line facility** options.
