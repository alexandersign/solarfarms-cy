## 2. Project Scope (Generic)

We request indicative terms for insurance and surety products supporting **utility-scale BESS and solar+storage EPC projects** in Cyprus, typically comprising:

- Containerised BESS equipment supply and installation
- MV/LV electrical works, grid connection support
- Civil works, foundations, fencing, security systems
- Commissioning and handover to employer/operator

Individual project values are in the **multi-million EUR** range. We anticipate **multiple projects per year** rather than a single policy.

## 3. Coverage Types Required

Please provide availability, indicative premiums, deductibles, and standard wordings for:

### 3.1 Construction Phase

| # | Coverage | Required | Notes |
|---|----------|----------|-------|
| A | **Contractors All Risks (CAR)** | Yes | Including transit, storage, installation |
| B | **Erection All Risks (EAR)** | Yes | Plant & equipment erection |
| C | **Delay in Start-Up (DSU)** | Optional | Please quote separately |
| D | **Cross-liability / Third Party CAR extension** | Yes | Subcontractor coordination |

### 3.2 Liability

| # | Coverage | Required | Notes |
|---|----------|----------|-------|
| E | **Public / General Liability** | Yes | Min. EUR 1,000,000 per occurrence |
| F | **Employer's Liability** | Yes | Per Cyprus statutory requirements |
| G | **Professional Indemnity** | Yes | EPC design/engineering errors & omissions |
| H | **Product Liability** | Yes | BESS equipment post-handover (if applicable) |

### 3.3 Bonds & Financial Guarantees (Insurance-Backed)

| # | Instrument | Typical amount | Typical duration |
|---|------------|----------------|------------------|
| I | **Bid bond / tender guarantee** | 2–3% of tender value | 3–6 months |
| J | **Performance bond** | 5–10% of contract value | Through defects liability (12–24 months post-COD) |
| K | **Advance payment guarantee** | Up to 20% of contract | Until advance recovered |
| L | **Retention guarantee** | 5–10% of contract | Defects period |
| M | **Warranty / maintenance bond** | Per contract | 2–5 years post-COD |

*If your firm does not underwrite surety bonds, please indicate partner surety markets or decline those lines.*
