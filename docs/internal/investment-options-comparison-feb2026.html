11. MANDATORY Protection Measures — Before Shares Transfer

ALL measures must be completed BEFORE the 25% share subscription completes. Once the investor has shares, their consent is needed for most changes — and they will never agree to protections that reduce their leverage.
🔐
1. Shareholder Agreement — Pre-Existing Obligation
The SHA must include a clause where the new shareholder explicitly acknowledges and agrees not to challenge the Moi Ostrov Ltd team consultancy (30% net margin) as a "Pre-Existing Obligation." This must be a condition precedent in the share subscription agreement — no acknowledgment, no shares.
🔐
2. External Consultancy Structure
Formalize the 30% as a team operational consultancy agreement between LCY and Moi Ostrov Ltd (Alexander Papacosta's existing company). This frames it as an arm's-length commercial contract for team management services — not director compensation. Covers project management, team coordination, subcontractor supervision, and operational oversight. Must be executed BEFORE investor due diligence.
🔐
3. Articles of Association — Protected Matter
Amend the Articles BEFORE share transfer to classify the Moi Ostrov Ltd consultancy agreement as a "Protected Matter" requiring unanimous shareholder consent (not just 75%) to modify or terminate. This gives the Director effective veto power over changes to the team contract even after the investor enters.
🔐
4. Minimum Term + Termination Penalty
The Moi Ostrov Ltd agreement must have a 5-year minimum term with automatic renewal. Early termination by LCY triggers a penalty equal to 3 years' average fee (~€1.2M+). This creates a massive financial disincentive for the investor to push for termination — it would cost LCY more than it saves.
🔐
5. Fund Revenue Ringfence
The Moi Ostrov Ltd agreement must explicitly state that all revenue streams — including fund-generated EPC margins, management fees, BESS supply margins, and O&M — are included in the team 30% calculation base. Without this clause, the investor could argue the 30% only applies to "organic" business, excluding the €9.98M of fund-related profit.
🔐
6. UBO Undertaking Letter
Dr. Sybaris provides a personal undertaking committing to vote against any shareholder resolution that would modify, reduce, or terminate the Moi Ostrov Ltd consultancy agreement for the full 5-year term. This binds the UBO's 75% voting power and prevents the "UBO caves to investor pressure" scenario.
Protection Value: These 6 measures increase Moi Ostrov Ltd's expected 5-year income by €1,351K (from €4,793K unprotected to €6,144K protected). The legal costs to implement them are estimated at €15K-25K. Return on legal investment: 54x to 90x.
