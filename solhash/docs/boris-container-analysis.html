4. Halving Calendar

Halving: ~13 April 2028, block 1,050,000. Block subsidy 3.125 → 1.5625 BTC. Effect on annual revenue by year (uptime 92.5% Y1 / 85% Y2–5):
Year 1 (Sep 2026–Sep 2027): 365 days pre-halving → 100% subsidy. Year 2 (Sep 2027–Sep 2028): 208 pre + 158 post → 78.4% blended. Years 3–5: fully post-halving → 50% of Year 1. Without a compensating BTC price rise, revenue roughly halves in Year 3 and stays there.

8. What Is Not in the Numbers

Excluded costs / risks

  • No replacement of the 15% Year-1 failures
  • Difficulty growth (~5% rise queued for 19 Sep alone)
  • Park EPC (PV panels, inverter, civil, wiring)
  • Starlink + monitoring ≈ $100–120/mo per site
  • 8543 reclassification: adds ~$2,500 duty + $500 extra VAT
  • BTC price is a scenario, not a forecast
  • Post-2028 difficulty growth if BTC price rises

Upside not modelled

  • 24/7 with BESS ≈ 3× revenue (machines run at night too)
  • BTC post-halving bull run historically 1.5–3× Y2–Y3 price
  • Container is mobile — moves to next park at connection
  • JV fee if Lighthief supplies the kit to park owners
  • Difficulty could fall if marginal miners exit post-halving
