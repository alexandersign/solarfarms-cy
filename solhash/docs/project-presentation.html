1. The Opportunity

Solar parks in Cyprus face 3–5 year delays before grid connection terms are issued. During this period, installed PV panels generate energy that cannot be exported and is entirely wasted. Park owners pay land lease and O&M on an asset producing zero revenue.

Solhash converts stranded solar into Bitcoin revenue. Containerised miners are deployed behind-the-meter, consuming on-site PV. Electricity cost is effectively €0 — no grid tariff, no PPA. Both parties earn while waiting for connection.

13. Site Electrical Architecture, BESS & Automation

Off-grid parks cannot use standard grid-tied inverters. Without a grid connection, PV string inverters have no voltage/frequency reference and will not start. A grid-forming BESS + microgrid controller is required to create a “virtual grid” that string inverters sync to. This same BESS provides post-run cooling, overnight auxiliary power, and PV transient buffering.

Summer mitigation: Evaporative cooling pads (“swamp coolers”) are highly effective in Cyprus’s dry heat, dropping container intake air by 10–15°C. This keeps S21+ at full performance even when ambient exceeds 40°C. Cost: ~€2,000–3,000 per container.

Post-Run Cooling Protocol (Critical)

When hashing stops at sunset, S21+ chips are at 75–80°C internally. If power is cut instantly, heat “soaks” into control boards and solder joints, causing premature failure.

  1. Braiins OS triggers Soft Shutdown — stops hashing, keeps fans running.
  2. Container fans run at full speed for 20–30 minutes (powered by battery).
  3. Once chip temps drop to ambient, fans shut off.
  4. Battery powers only Starlink + controller overnight (~2–4 kWh total).

Why Starlink Works for Mining

  • Latency: 25ms median (US peak-hour). Mining needs <100ms to avoid stale shares. Stale rate at 25–50ms is ~0.1–0.3% — negligible.
  • Bandwidth: Mining uses a few MB/day (<1 Mbps). Starlink is ~100,000× overspec’d. Same connection handles CCTV, remote management.
  • Remote deployment: Solar parks lack fibre. Starlink operational within hours of dish install. Clear sky view at park is guaranteed.
  • 24/7 on battery: Starlink Mini draws only 15–40W — easily sustained by the 15 kWh site battery overnight, providing continuous remote monitoring.
  • Pool sync: When S21+ units boot each morning, they sync with the mining pool in 5–10 minutes via Starlink. Brief daily handshake is normal and expected.

Why FPPS Matters for the JV

FPPS (Full Pay Per Share) pays a fixed amount per valid share submitted, regardless of whether the pool actually finds a block. This provides predictable daily revenue — essential for the 30/70 monthly revenue split. PPLNS pays more on average but with high variance (lucky/unlucky streaks), making JV accounting unpredictable.

Stratum V2 Advantage

Braiins Pool supports Stratum V2 — a modern mining protocol that reduces data transfer, lowers latency, and decreases stale share rates. On Starlink (25–50ms latency), Stratum V2 reduces stale shares from ~0.3% to ~0.1%, recovering ~0.2% of revenue vs legacy Stratum V1.

16. Taxation (Cyprus 2026)

Mining profits are taxed at 15% CIT — NOT the 8% crypto rate. Cyprus introduced an 8% flat tax on crypto disposals from 1 Jan 2026, but mining is explicitly excluded as “active business income.” The 8% rate applies only when mined BTC is subsequently sold for EUR.

What if BTC crashes?Your downside is €0 (back to status quo). You bear no BTC price risk — no capex, no operations. Mining Partner bears all market risk.
Does mining damage my panels?No. Miners consume electricity via LV connection. PV degradation is natural (~0.5%/yr) and compensated at €10/MWh.
Do I need a licence?No. Behind-the-meter self-consumption. No grid export, no trading, no licence.
What happens when connection comes?30-day exit clause. Equipment removed. Site restored. You transition to grid PPA immediately.
Can I get more than 30%?The 30/70 split reflects tied-up equity and opportunity cost. Alternative structures (PPA floor, dynamic BTC-linked split) are available.

Operations & Tax

Do I need a battery for overnight?No. Miners run daytime-only and shut down at sunset. Cyprus night temps (15–25°C) are within S21+ idle tolerance. A small UPS (€3K/container) handles graceful shutdown during cloud transients. No standalone BESS needed.
How do containers connect to the internet?Starlink (primary) + 4G SIM (failover), bonded via Peplink router. ~€1,560/yr per site. Mining needs <1 Mbps — Starlink delivers 100–400 Mbps with 20–50ms latency. Stale shares negligible (~0.1%).
Which mining pool?Braiins Pool (2% FPPS) recommended. Predictable daily income for 30/70 split accounting. Stratum V2 protocol reduces stale shares on Starlink. Lightning payouts minimise on-chain fees.
How is mining taxed in Cyprus?Mining = active business income at 15% CIT (not the 8% crypto disposal rate). When BTC is sold for EUR, the disposal gain is taxed at 8%. VAT: 0% on mining (EU CJEU ruling). No stamp duty (abolished 2026). Dividends to CY individuals: 5% SDC.
Is there VAT on the electricity?No. Behind-the-meter self-consumption — no supply occurs, no VAT, no excise duty. Input VAT on equipment (EU-sourced) is recoverable.
What about the 8% crypto tax?The 8% flat rate applies only to disposal of crypto (sale/exchange). Mining income itself is taxed at 15% CIT. The 8% applies when you convert mined BTC to EUR — on the gain above your mining cost basis.
