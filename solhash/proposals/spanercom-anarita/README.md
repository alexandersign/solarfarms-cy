# Spanercom (Anarita) — JV Mining Proposal

**UNIQUE STRUCTURE**: Lighthief is BOTH EPC contractor (at self-cost) AND co-investor (50/50 equity partner).

## Park Details

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Location | Anarita, Paphos district (two adjacent plots) |
| Capacity | 2 MWp (2 × 1 MW) |
| PV yield | 1,800 kWh/kWp/yr |
| Annual energy | 3,600 MWh/yr |
| Contact | Muminjon / Spanercom Ltd |

## JV Structure

- **Spanercom** = Park Partner (land, panels, site access)
- **Lighthief** = EPC at self-cost (no margin) + 50% co-investor in mining equipment
- Mining equipment capex split **50/50**
- Revenue split: **50/50** after deducting 5% management fee for Lighthief
- Lighthief receives: 50% of net revenue + 5% management fee on gross
