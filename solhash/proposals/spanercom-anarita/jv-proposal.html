Lighthief is NOT just an intermediary. In this deal, Lighthief co-invests €689K of its own capital (50% of mining equipment + EPC at cost). This creates true alignment: if the project succeeds, both parties win. If it underperforms, both parties share the downside. EPC at self-cost — zero margin — proves Lighthief’s commitment as a partner, not a vendor.

10. Risk Analysis

RiskProbabilityImpactMitigation
BTC price declineMediumHigh€0 electricity cost = positive margin even at €30K BTC. Both parties share risk 50/50.
2028 halvingCertainMediumHistorically offset by price increases. Fast payback (~2yr) means most capex recovered before halving.
Network difficultyMediumMediumS21+ is latest-gen. Zero energy cost preserves advantage vs grid miners.
Equipment failureLowLowUPS for graceful shutdown. Monitoring via Starlink 24/7. Insurance available.
Summer heatCertainLowEvaporative cooling. S21+ auto-throttles at >40°C. ~10% revenue impact Jun–Aug.
Grid connection grantedLowPositiveContainers relocate. Spanercom transitions to grid PPA (upside).
RegulatoryLowLowBehind-the-meter self-consumption. No licence required in Cyprus.
Risk symmetry: Unlike a standard 30/70 deal where only the mining partner bears BTC risk, here both parties share market risk equally through the 50/50 equity structure. This alignment means neither party is incentivised to exit early.
