# Esperia Tseri Pipeline — JV Mining Proposal (Phase 3)

**Partner:** Dino Constantinou / Esperia Energy Group
**Scope:** 5 parks, 27.5 MW BESS-matched PV, Nicosia district
**Connection:** ~2028 (3–5 year mining window)

## Three-Party Structure

| Party | Role | Capex | Revenue |
|-------|------|-------|---------|
| **Dino (Esperia)** | Park Partner — provides 29 MWp PV + permitted sites | €0 new capex (PV already committed) | 30% of net after mgmt fee |
| **Mining Partner** | Equipment + EPC funder — 6,350 S21+ / 38 containers | €15,240,000 | 70% of net after mgmt fee |
| **Lighthief** | Arranger + JV manager — finds Mining Partner, manages ops | €0 | 5% management fee on gross |

## Key Economics (BTC €100K)

- Gross revenue: €9,179,118/yr
- Net after costs: €7,968,193/yr
- Lighthief mgmt fee (5%): €458,956/yr
- Net distributable: €7,509,237/yr
- Dino (30%): €2,252,771/yr → ~4.55¢/kWh
- Mining Partner (70%): €5,256,466/yr → payback 2.90 yr, ~22% IRR
