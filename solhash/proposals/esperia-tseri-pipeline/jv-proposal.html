2. The Opportunity — 27.5 MW at Scale

Cyprus solar parks face 3–5 year delays before grid connection terms are issued by EAC/CERA. During this stranded period, installed PV panels generate energy that cannot be exported. Park owners pay land lease and O&M on an asset producing zero revenue.

Esperia Tseri is uniquely positioned. Five parks, all in the Nicosia district, are expected to connect around 2028. Together they represent 29 MWp of PV and 87.5 MWh of BESS capacity. The BESS MW rating (27.5 MW) sets the maximum behind-the-meter mining load.

Why €0/kWh Matters

Electricity is 60–80% of Bitcoin mining cost. By deploying behind-the-meter on pre-connection solar parks, the Mining Partner eliminates the largest operating expense. There is no grid tariff, no PPA, no energy bill. This is the lowest-cost mining location in Europe.
