# Christos Nicosia 3.3 MW — Park Study

**Project:** Christos Nicosia  
**Site:** Nicosia district, Cyprus  
**Total park:** 3.3 MWp  
**Pre-connection PV:** 1.7 MWp (space without connection terms)

## Overview

Pre-connection mining study for the Christos 3.3 MWp solar park in Nicosia. 1.7 MWp of PV capacity is available in the area without connection terms. Bifacial panels at 1900 kWh/kWp/year.

**Model:** 25% revenue share for Park Partner (≈4.6 c/kWh at BTC €100k). Alternative: fixed 5 c/kWh PPA.
