2. Per-EPC contract insurance — our intended structure

Each EPC contract will be insured separately. We do not intend one undifferentiated policy limit for the whole portfolio. For every executed EPC agreement between Lighthief Cyprus Ltd and the relevant client SPV, we will:
  • Submit a separate insurance declaration (or policy schedule / attachment) naming that EPC contract, site, contract value, construction period, and client SPV;
  • Apply Section 1 (CAR) to the full EPC contract price of that project only;
  • Apply Section 2 (DSU) to the anticipated revenue / delay exposure of that project only (€30/MWh/day; indemnity capped at 10% of that EPC price, per your endorsement);
  • Apply Section 7 (TPL) pro-rata to the contract value of that project;
  • Name the client SPV as additional insured under Sections 1 and 7 for that declared project;
  • Pay premium attributable to that contract at your quoted rates (see §5) when the declaration is accepted and cover incepts;
  • Trigger cover on the inception / construction start date of that EPC (or as agreed with insurers), not before the EPC is signed.

We are content for this to sit under a master CEAR policy with automatic additions/deletions memoranda, provided each EPC remains a distinct declared risk with its own limits, deductibles, DSU waiting period, and maintenance (DLP) tail from Commercial Operation Date of that BESS.
