01 Executive Summary

What we're building, why it matters, and what it means for 7sun's Cyprus expansion.

The Opportunity

Cyprus's solar market is booming (~120 MW/year growth) with limited online distribution. 7SUN.cy will be the first AI-powered solar e-commerce platform in Cyprus, leveraging 7sun.eu's 5,000+ product catalog, competitive pricing, and EU supply chain to capture both B2B wholesale and B2C retail markets.

What We're Proposing

🛒 E-Commerce

Full online store with B2C retail, B2B wholesale portal, developer kits. Products auto-synced from 7sun.eu XML feed including images, specs, pricing, stock levels.

🤖 AI Automation

AI chatbot (GPT-4o) handles 80%+ of customer queries. AI voice agent (ElevenLabs) handles phone calls 24/7 in Greek + English. AI generates newsletters automatically.

⚡ Zero Friction

3-click checkout. RES Fund installers auto-verified for wholesale pricing. CSV bulk upload. One-click reorder. Net-30/60 credit terms via Stripe.

Why NOT WooCommerce?

7sun.eu runs on WordPress/WooCommerce — a proven platform. However, for a new market-specific B2B/B2C operation with heavy AI integration and XML automation, WooCommerce's limitations become significant. We propose a modern headless stack that is faster, cheaper to operate, and purpose-built for this use case.

04 XML Feed Integration — Zero Manual Work

7sun.eu's DataFeedWatch XML feed powers the entire product catalog. No manual product entry ever.

How It Works

Every 4 hours, our sync worker fetches the XML feed from DataFeedWatch, compares it to the current catalog, downloads new images, translates descriptions to Greek via AI, calculates 6 price tiers, and updates the live store. Zero human intervention required.

What Gets Synced Automatically

DataSourceProcessing
Product name & descriptionXML <title> + <description>Clean + AI translate to Greek
All product imagesXML <image_link> + additional imagesDownload → optimize (WebP/AVIF) → CDN
B2B pricing (6 tiers)XML <price>Calculate H1–H5 + retail markup
Stock levelsXML <availability> + <quantity>Real-time stock display on product page
SpecificationsXML custom labels + attributesParse wattage, weight, dimensions
Brand & categoryXML <brand> + <product_type>Map to store categories
Product datasheetsXML <link> (7sun.eu product page)Link to 7sun.eu PDF or mirror locally
EAN / GTIN codesXML <gtin>Direct map for barcode/inventory

Catalog Filtering

7sun.eu has 5,000+ products. We filter to ~400–600 Cyprus-relevant products:

  • Brands: Jinko, JA Solar, Longi, Aiko, Jolywood, Canadian Solar, Huawei, SolarEdge, FoxESS, Sofar, Fronius, Solax, Envertech, Tigo
  • Panels: ≥400W only (smaller not relevant for Cyprus market)
  • Inverters: All string + micro + hybrid
  • Exclude: Mounting hardware, DC cables (source locally — too heavy to ship)
For 7sun.eu: No changes needed on your side. We consume the existing DataFeedWatch XML feed as-is. If product data, images, or pricing change on 7sun.eu, they appear on 7sun.cy within 4 hours automatically.
