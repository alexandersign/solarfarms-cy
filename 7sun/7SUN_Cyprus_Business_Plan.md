## 1. Executive Summary

**7SUN Cyprus** will be a photovoltaic wholesale and retail distribution operation based in Ypsonas, Limassol, leveraging 7sun.eu's massive European B2B supply chain and Lighthief.cy's established local presence in Cyprus's booming solar market.

### Key Value Proposition
- **30–50% lower wholesale prices** than the dominant local competitor (Trikkis Energy)
- **Direct EU supply chain** from 7sun.eu (Poland HQ) with 500+ product catalogue
- **Zero stock investment** — 7sun.eu provides all stock as JV contribution
- **Dual-channel sales**: Wholesale to installers + Retail to end consumers
- **50/50 Joint Venture**: 7sun.eu (stock + EU supply chain) + Lighthief International Ltd (local operations + market access)

**Market Sizing:**

| | MW | Revenue (at ~€250K/MW) |
|---|---|---|
| **TAM** (Total Addressable Market — all equipment distribution, res + comm) | 100 MW | **~€25–28M/year** |
| Trikkis share (claimed 60%) | 60 MW | ~€15–17M/year |
| Other distributors + direct imports (40%) | 40 MW | ~€10–11M/year |
| **TSM** (Total Serviceable Market — non-Trikkis + brand-unique + growth) | ~56–72 MW | **~€14–18M/year** |
| **SOM** (Serviceable Obtainable Market — 7SUN Year 1 target) | ~3.5 MW | **~€888K/year** |
| **SOM** Year 2–3 target (7–12% share) | 7–12 MW | **€2–3M/year** |

> **TSM rationale:** Non-Trikkis 40MW (~€10M) + brands we exclusively carry that can win Trikkis customers (SolarEdge*, FoxESS, Sofar, Deye, Jolywood, Longi, Aiko — brands Trikkis doesn't stock) adds ~€2–4M addressable + annual market growth of 5–10% adds ~€1.5–3M. Battery storage penetration is rising fast (grid curtailment 47%), expanding equipment value per MW. TSM grows to ~€18–22M by 2028.
>
> **Implication:** Even capturing just 3–5% of the distribution TAM in Year 1 (our SOM target of ~€888K) represents a conservative entry into a market where a single dominant player (Trikkis) holds 60%. Our price advantage (30–50% below on panels, exclusive brands) and zero-stock-investment JV model make rapid share gains achievable.

### 2.2 Market Drivers

1. **Exceptional solar resource**: ~340 sunny days/year, one of the highest irradiance levels in Europe (~1,750–1,900 kWh/m²/year)
2. **High electricity prices**: Cyprus has among the highest retail electricity costs in the EU, making solar payback attractive (3–5 years)
3. **Self-consumption boom**: ~80% of 2025 installations were self-consumption; ~12,000 net-metering applications filed in Dec 2025 before scheme transition
4. **Policy shift (Jan 2026)**: Net-metering/net-billing replaced by market-based self-consumption model — creating urgency and new demand patterns
5. **EU funding & fast-track permitting**: Accelerated project approvals and EU-funded incentives
6. **Growing battery/storage demand**: Grid curtailment hit 47% in 2025, driving demand for battery storage solutions

### 2.3 Market Segmentation

| Segment | Share | Key Products | Avg. System Size |
|---------|-------|-------------|-----------------|
| Residential Self-Consumption | ~60% | Panels, hybrid inverters, batteries | 5–10 kWp |
| Commercial/Industrial | ~25% | Panels, string inverters, monitoring | 10–100+ kWp |
| Solar Farms / PPA | ~15% | Panels, large inverters, mounting | 500+ kWp |

### 2.4 Regulatory Environment

- **VAT**: Standard 19% on imports; potential reduced 5% on qualifying renewable energy equipment (verify with Cyprus Customs for specific CN/HS codes)
- **Customs Duty**: EU internal market — 0% duty on goods originating from EU member states (7sun.eu ships from Poland/EU)
- **2026 Change**: EU removal of €150 low-value consignment duty exemption (minimal impact for bulk B2B shipments)
- **Subsidies**: The RES Fund Grant Scheme 2024–2025 (€375/kW, max €1,500 for first 4kW) has **closed** — applications ended 31 Dec 2025. No 2026 scheme announced yet. Historically renewed in new rounds; future grants should not be relied upon in pricing or business case

3. **Dedicated warehouse** — 700 sqm in Ypsonas (€7,000/mo), expandable to second section (€10,000/mo)
