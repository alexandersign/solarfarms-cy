4.5 Key Pricing Insights

1. Panels: Massive advantage. 7sun's panel prices are 8–37% below Trikkis wholesale. At retail, savings reach 35–60%.

2. Huawei Inverters: Mixed. Competitive on 1-phase domestic and large commercial, but Trikkis has slightly better pricing on some 3-phase domestic models. Strategy: Focus on alternative inverter brands (SolarEdge, FoxESS, Sofar) where 7sun has supply and significant price advantages. (Note: SolarEdge CY exclusivity pending verification — BIGSOLAR/Epiphaniou Group may already represent.)

3. Wider brand portfolio = key differentiator. 7sun offers SolarEdge, FoxESS, Sofar, Fronius, Canadian Solar, Longi, Aiko, Hyundai, Jolywood — NONE of which Trikkis carries. (Note: SolarEdge may already have a Cyprus channel via BIGSOLAR/Epiphaniou Group — exclusivity pending verification.)

4. Battery storage: At par to slightly better. The growing storage market is a key opportunity.

7 Launch Plan & Timeline

Phase 0: Foundation (Weeks 1–4)

  • Register Cyprus company (Ltd) or branch of EU entity
  • Sign warehouse lease agreement (shared with Lighthief.cy)
  • Open Cyprus business bank account
  • Register for Cyprus VAT (VIES for intra-EU B2B)
  • Engage accountant/tax advisor for CY compliance
  • Set up warehouse infrastructure (racking, forklift, security)

Phase 1: Pre-Launch (Weeks 5–8)

  • Place initial stock order from 7sun.eu (see Section 8)
  • Build website (7sun.cy or 7suncyprus.com)
  • Build installer contact database (200+ contacts)
  • Create bilingual marketing materials (Greek + English)
  • Set up Google Business Profile and social media
  • Arrange transport logistics (Poland → Cyprus, truck or container)
  • Hire 1 warehouse operative + 1 sales rep (bilingual)

Phase 2: Soft Launch (Weeks 9–10)

  • Receive and warehouse initial stock
  • Soft launch: Lighthief.cy as first wholesale client
  • Invite 10–15 key installers for private preview
  • Test order/delivery/invoicing workflow
  • Collect feedback and adjust pricing if needed

Phase 3: Official Launch (Weeks 11–12)

  • Grand opening event at Ypsonas warehouse
  • Activate Google Ads and Social Media campaigns
  • Send launch mailer to full installer database
  • Begin retail walk-in sales
  • Activate "Price Match Guarantee" campaign

Phase 4: Growth (Month 4–12)

  • Monthly stock replenishment (based on sell-through)
  • Expand product range (batteries, EV chargers, mounting)
  • Build installer loyalty/credit program
  • Target commercial/industrial projects
  • Evaluate satellite pickup point in Nicosia
