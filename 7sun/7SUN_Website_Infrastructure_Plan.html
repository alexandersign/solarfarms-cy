3 E-Commerce Engine — Medusa.js v2

Why Medusa.js

  • Open-source — No license fees, full source code control
  • B2B Starter Kit — Pre-built Next.js storefront with company accounts, spending limits, quote flows
  • Multi-Channel — Separate B2C retail and B2B wholesale with different pricing
  • Customer Groups — Auto-assign pricing tiers (Retail, H1–H5, Developer, Partner)
  • Quote System — RFQ → negotiate → approve → order
  • API-First — Every feature accessible via REST/GraphQL
  • Plugin System — Extensible with custom modules (AI, XML, RES Fund)

8 Auto-Matched Wholesale Accounts

Installers registered with the RES Fund (390+ companies) get instant wholesale access. No manual approval needed.

Registration Flow

User registers → "Are you a solar installer?" → [Yes]
    │
    ├── Check company/phone/email against RES Fund DB (390+ entries)
    │   └── Fuzzy match (Levenshtein distance < 3)
    │
    ├── MATCH FOUND:
    │   ├── Auto-assign "Verified Installer" (H1 tier)
    │   ├── Unlock wholesale pricing immediately
    │   ├── Enable Net-30 credit application
    │   ├── Welcome email: "Your wholesale account is ready"
    │   └── Slack notification → sales team follow-up
    │
    └── NO MATCH:
        ├── Flag for manual review (24h)
        ├── Email: "We're verifying your status"
        └── Manual verification by sales team
