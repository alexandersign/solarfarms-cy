5. Micro Inverters — Verified Cost €5,444 | 65+ Units | Apartment Segment

The Apartment Building Opportunity

Cyprus has tens of thousands of apartment buildings with flat rooftops. Micro inverters allow each apartment to have its own independent 2–4 panel system, connected to its own meter. No shared inverter, no legal headaches, each unit independent.

Grant Status: The RES Fund Grant Scheme 2024–2025 (€375/kW for first 4 kW = max €1,500) has closed. Historically renewed in new rounds. When a new scheme opens, each apartment owner with a micro inverter system qualifies individually — maximizing total grant capture for the building.
⚠️ Regulatory Warning: Our local Electrical Engineer has flagged that micro inverters may not be fully legislated in Cyprus. Standard inverters can be programmed with EAC's specific grid settings and requirements, but micro inverters may not support this. Verification with EAC is required before committing stock or marketing this segment. All micro inverter quantities and stock value figures below are PROVISIONAL pending this confirmation.
