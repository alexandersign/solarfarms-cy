# Lighthief Greece IKE — Business Plan

## BESS EPC, O&M & Consulting — Greek Market Entry

**Prepared:** March 2026
**Entity:** Lighthief Greece IKE (Proposed)
**Parent:** Lighthief Cyprus Ltd (HE 477423)
**Horizon:** 3-Year Plan (2026–2029)

---

## 1. Executive Summary

Lighthief Greece IKE will be established as a wholly-owned subsidiary of Lighthief Cyprus Ltd to capture the rapidly expanding Greek battery energy storage market. Greece has allocated **4.7 GW of standalone BESS capacity** (€1.9 billion market) with regulatory support now in place, yet faces a severe grid connection bottleneck and renewable curtailment crisis (1,867 GWh wasted in 2025) that demands urgent storage deployment.

Lighthief enters Greece with a decisive advantage: **881 MWh of delivered BESS portfolio in Cyprus** (51 parks, 249 MW), exclusive OEM relationships with Linyang Energy, Kehua PCS, and Voltus EMS, and a trained 6-engineer field team. No other new Greek market entrant offers this combination of scale, OEM access, and operational track record.

**Year 1 target:** Secure first contract (≥€500K) through sub-EPC partnerships, consulting mandates, or equipment supply agreements with established Greek developers.

**3-year target:** €5–15M cumulative revenue, 3–5 active BESS projects, established reputation as the go-to BESS technical partner in Greece.

### 2.3 Curtailment Crisis — The Demand Driver

| Year | Curtailment Rate | GWh Wasted | Solar Revenue Impact |

Each 1 GW of battery storage reduces curtailment by 0.62–1.18 TWh. Greece's installed renewable base is growing faster than its grid can absorb — making BESS deployment structurally urgent.

## 3. Business Model

### 3.1 Revenue Streams

| Stream | Description | Margin | Timeline |
|--------|------------|--------|----------|
| **A. Sub-EPC Services** | Partner with Greek developers as BESS technical subcontractor — engineering, commissioning supervision, protection systems | 15–25% | Month 6+ |
| **B. Technical Consulting** | Feasibility studies, technical due diligence, grid connection analysis, tender preparation for investors/developers | 40–60% | Month 3+ |
| **C. Equipment Supply** | Supply Linyang BESS containers, Kehua PCS, Voltus EMS to Greek projects via intercompany procurement | 10–15% | Month 6+ |
| **D. O&M Contracts** | Long-term service agreements (LTSA) for operating BESS assets — preventive/corrective maintenance, SCADA | 30–40% | Month 18+ (post-commissioning) |
| **E. Full EPC** | Turnkey BESS project delivery (design-build-commission) | 8–12% | Year 2+ (once established) |

| Legal | Retainer with energy-sector lawyer (hourly) |
| IT | Laptop, mobile, cloud tools (MS365/Google Workspace) |
| Insurance | Professional indemnity + employer liability |

### 4.2 Operational Workflow

```
1. PIPELINE DEVELOPMENT
   Country Lead identifies projects → qualifies → initial meetings
   
2. PROPOSAL
   Technical design (Country Lead + CY engineering) → pricing (CY SSOT) → proposal submission

3. CONTRACT
   Greek lawyer reviews → commercial negotiation → contract signing

4. EXECUTION
   Equipment procurement (via Lighthief CY / Linyang) → local subcontractors (civil, electrical)
   → Lighthief PL engineers seconded for commissioning → TEE engineer supervises on-site

5. HANDOVER & O&M
   System acceptance → LTSA contract → ongoing maintenance (local team + SCADA remote)
```

### 4.3 Intercompany Model

| Transaction | From → To | Basis |
|-------------|----------|-------|
| Equipment procurement | Lighthief CY → Lighthief GR | CIF + arm's length margin |
| Engineering support | Lighthief CY → Lighthief GR | Cost-plus or time & materials |
| Field engineers (commissioning) | Lighthief PL → Lighthief GR | Posted workers (EU Directive) + cost recharge |
| Management fee | Lighthief GR → Lighthief CY | % of revenue or fixed monthly fee |
| SCADA / EMS services | Voltus → Lighthief GR | Direct contract or via CY |

All intercompany transactions documented under arm's length transfer pricing.

## 6. Risk Analysis

| Risk | Impact | Probability | Mitigation |
|------|--------|------------|------------|
| Grid connection delays | Projects stall, revenue deferred | High | Focus on consulting/advisory (not dependent on grid); diversify services |
| TEE engineer hire failure | Cannot bid on tenders | Medium | Start recruiting immediately; backup: engage TEE-licensed subcontractor |
| Greek regulatory changes | Market rules shift | Medium | Active RAAEY engagement; lawyer monitors regulatory pipeline |
| Competitor undercutting | Price pressure on EPC | Medium | Differentiate on track record and OEM access, not price |
| Intercompany transfer pricing challenge | Tax dispute | Low | Prepare robust TP documentation from day one |
| Cyprus portfolio delays | Weakens reference story | Low | Portfolio is already 881 MWh — track record is established |
| Currency risk | N/A | None | EUR-to-EUR across all entities |
