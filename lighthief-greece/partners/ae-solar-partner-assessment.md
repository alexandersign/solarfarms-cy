## 4. Partnership Models Available

AE Solar offers two distinct partnership tracks:

### A. Distribution / Exclusive Distribution Partnership

- Exclusive rights to distribute AE Solar modules in a designated territory
- Customised products designed for specific regional markets
- Example: OSW (Australia's largest solar distributor) secured exclusive Australian distribution
- Likely structure: volume commitments in exchange for territory exclusivity and preferential pricing

### B. Manufacturing Franchise

- Manufacture PV modules under the AE Solar brand in your own facility
- Comprehensive technical training and production process transfer
- Exclusive territory rights with no franchisee competition
- Preferential pricing on raw materials (BOM supply available)
- Sales & marketing support, legal guidance
- Can onboard existing factories or build greenfield
- Technologies available: TOPCon, HJT, PERC, BC
- ROI analysis and feasibility study provided by AE Solar team

**Assessment:** The **distribution partnership** is the relevant model for Lighthief Greece. The manufacturing franchise is interesting longer-term but requires significant CAPEX and is not aligned with our immediate market entry strategy.

## 5. Relevance to Lighthief Greece

### Potential Fit

| Factor | Assessment |
|--------|-----------|
| **Greek PV market** | Greece has ~6 GW installed PV, growing ~1 GW/year. AE Solar does not appear to have a strong Greek distribution presence — opportunity for exclusive territory |
| **BESS complementarity** | AE Solar is PV-only (no BESS/storage products). Partnership would complement our BESS focus by adding PV to our Greek offering |
| **Bankability** | Tier 1 + Grade A status is excellent for project finance and investor confidence |
| **European manufacturing** | "German brand" positioning is valuable in Greece where Chinese module scepticism exists in some market segments |
| **Price competitiveness** | TOPCon at €0.17–0.19/Wp is competitive with Jinko, Longi, JA Solar at wholesale |
| **Existing Greek competitor** | Nanodomi appears to be a Greek distributor of AE Solar — need to verify if territory is already allocated |

### Strategic Scenarios

**Scenario 1: Greek Exclusive Distribution**
- Lighthief Greece IKE becomes exclusive AE Solar distributor for Greece
- PV module distribution adds a revenue stream alongside BESS EPC
- Synergy: offer combined BESS + PV projects (co-located storage + solar)
- Risk: PV distribution is volume-dependent and margin-thin; diverts focus from BESS

**Scenario 2: Project-Level Supply Agreement**
- Non-exclusive supply agreement for AE Solar modules on Lighthief's own Greek projects
- No distribution commitment — buy modules per project
- Lower risk, no territorial obligation
- Less strategic value — just another supplier

**Scenario 3: 7SUN Integration**
- Explore AE Solar as an additional brand for the 7SUN Cyprus JV portfolio
- 7SUN already distributes 15+ PV brands — adding AE Solar's Tier 1 German brand strengthens the premium tier
- Could negotiate dual-territory deal (Cyprus + Greece)

## 8. Verdict

**Overall: Interesting but secondary to BESS priority.**

AE Solar is a credible, well-certified European PV manufacturer with competitive pricing and a broad product range. The main question is whether Greek exclusive distribution territory is available (Nanodomi may already hold it).

For Lighthief Greece, the **primary value** would be as a complementary PV offering alongside our core BESS business — e.g., co-located solar + storage projects, or adding a PV revenue stream once the Greek entity is established. It should **not** distract from the BESS market entry which is the primary reason for establishing Lighthief Greece IKE.

The **most actionable opportunity** may actually be through the **7SUN Cyprus JV** — adding AE Solar as a premium German Tier 1 brand to the 7SUN product catalogue, potentially with a dual Cyprus + Greece distribution agreement.

**Recommended first step:** Email sales@ae-solar.com to check Greek territory availability and request distributor terms.
