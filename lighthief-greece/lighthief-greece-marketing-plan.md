# Lighthief Greece IKE — Marketing Plan

## B2B Go-to-Market Strategy for the Greek BESS Market

**Prepared:** March 2026
**Market:** Greece — Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS)
**Annual Marketing Budget (Year 1):** €5,000 – €10,000

---

## 1. Market Positioning

### 1.1 Value Proposition

**"European BESS expertise, proven at scale — now in Greece."**

Lighthief Greece is the only market entrant that combines:
- **881 MWh of delivered BESS portfolio** (Cyprus — largest private BESS programme)
- **Exclusive OEM access** to Linyang Energy, Kehua PCS, and Voltus EMS
- **Integrated EPC + O&M** capability with a 6-engineer field team
- **European engineering standards** (EN 50549-2, TÜV certified grid code compliance)

### 1.2 Target Positioning Statement

> For Greek energy developers and investors who need a technically credible BESS EPC and O&M partner, Lighthief Greece offers proven execution capability backed by an 881 MWh portfolio and direct OEM relationships — unlike local competitors who lack utility-scale BESS track record.

### 1.3 Competitive Differentiation

| Factor | Lighthief Greece | Local Greek Competitors | Large International EPC |
|--------|-----------------|----------------------|------------------------|
| BESS track record | 881 MWh (51 parks) | Limited / none | Strong but expensive |
| OEM pricing | Direct Linyang/Kehua | Third-party markup | Direct but different OEMs |
| O&M capability | In-house (6 engineers) | Outsourced or none | In-house |
| Local presence | TEE-licensed engineer | Full local teams | Remote / fly-in |
| Cost structure | Lean, competitive | Variable | High overhead |
| Response time | Fast (dedicated team) | Variable | Slow (bureaucratic) |

## 2. Target Segments

### Segment 1: BESS Project Developers (Primary — 70% of effort)

**Who:** Companies developing standalone BESS projects under the 4.7 GW programme or auction winners needing EPC partners.

**Key Targets:**
| Company | Profile | Approach |
|---------|---------|----------|
| METLEN (Mytilineos) | Market leader, 330 MW Thessaly project, 2.2 GWh pipeline | Sub-EPC partner / equipment supply |
| PPC Group | State-backed, 860 MW planned (Western Macedonia) | Technical consulting / sub-EPC |
| Terna Energy | Major renewables developer | EPC / O&M partnership |
| Motor Oil (MORE) | 72 MW/144 MWh completed | O&M services / next projects |
| Karatzis Group | Investor, partnered with METLEN | Technical advisory |
| Independent developers | Small-medium SPV developers | Full EPC + O&M offering |

**Message:** "We've built 881 MWh in Cyprus. Let us bring that expertise to your Greek BESS project — from engineering to commissioning to 15-year O&M."

### Segment 2: Financial Investors & Funds (Secondary — 20%)

**Who:** Infrastructure funds, PE firms, and family offices investing in Greek BESS projects who need technical due diligence or turnkey EPC partners.

**Message:** "Bankable BESS delivery. Tier 1 OEMs, TÜV-certified equipment, and a proven O&M framework with 97% availability SLA."

### Segment 3: Industrial Off-takers (Opportunistic — 10%)

**Who:** Energy-intensive industries (aluminium, cement, steel) eligible for the 500 MW industrial coupling programme with 8-year offtake agreements.

**Message:** "Behind-the-meter BESS for energy cost optimisation — design, build, operate."

## 4. Sales Process

### 4.1 Sales Pipeline Stages

```
PROSPECT → QUALIFY → PROPOSAL → NEGOTIATE → CONTRACT → DELIVERY
   │          │          │           │           │          │
   │          │          │           │           │          └─ EPC/O&M execution
   │          │          │           │           └─ Contract signing
   │          │          │           └─ Commercial & legal negotiation
   │          │          └─ Technical + commercial proposal
   │          └─ Project specs, budget, timeline, decision-maker
   └─ Identified developer, project, or tender
```

### 4.2 Sales Cycle

| Stage | Duration | Activity |
|-------|----------|----------|
| Prospect | Ongoing | Event networking, LinkedIn, referrals, tender monitoring |
| Qualify | 1–2 weeks | Initial meeting, project brief review, go/no-go |
| Proposal | 2–4 weeks | Technical design, pricing, reference portfolio |
| Negotiate | 2–6 weeks | Contract terms, scope, payment schedule |
| Contract | 1–2 weeks | Legal review, signing |
| **Total cycle** | **2–4 months** (shorter for consulting, longer for EPC) |

### 4.3 Key Sales Tools

| Tool | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| **Cyprus portfolio deck** | 881 MWh showcase with photos, specs, and performance data |
| **OEM capabilities brochure** | Linyang, Kehua, Voltus specs — bankability evidence |
| **Reference letters** | From Cyprus clients (ABIO, Esperia, etc.) |
| **Technical case studies** | Specific project examples (protection systems, commissioning, O&M) |
| **Standardised proposal template** | Customisable per project — scope, price, timeline, warranty |
