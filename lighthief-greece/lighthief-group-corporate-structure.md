## Strategic Notes

1. **Cyprus remains a tax-efficient operating entity** (15% CIT from 1 Jan 2026, up from 12.5%; 0% dividend WHT to treaty/EU; stamp duty abolished) — route profits through Lighthief CY where commercially justified.

2. **Greece IKE dividends to a Cyprus/EU parent qualify for 0% WHT** under the EU Parent-Subsidiary Directive (≥10% holding, ≥24 months).

3. **Poland entities** benefit from 9% small taxpayer CIT rate if annual revenue < 2M EUR equivalent.

4. **Bretwalt** as the ultimate holding company provides group-level flexibility for future M&A, financing, and IP structuring.

5. **7SUN JV** is operationally separate — 7sun.eu controls stock supply, Lighthief controls local ops. Profit split follows 50/50 JV terms.
