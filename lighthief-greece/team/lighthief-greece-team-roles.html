Role 2: TEE-Licensed Engineer / Country Lead

TitleCountry Lead & Chief Engineer (Υπεύθυνος Μηχανικός & Επικεφαλής Ελλάδας)
Reports toAlexander Papacosta (Managing Director)
LocationAthens, Greece
EmploymentFull-time employee
Salary Range€3,300 – €4,700/month gross (€40,000 – €56,000/year)
Employer Cost€4,070 – €5,690/month (incl. ~22% employer SI)
PriorityFIRST HIRE — mandatory for tender participation

Requirements

  • Graduate of Greek polytechnic (ΕΜΠ, ΑΠΘ, Πολυτεχνείο Κρήτης) or EU-recognised equivalent
  • Active TEE membership with practice licence (Άδεια Ασκήσεως Επαγγέλματος)
  • 5–10+ years in energy sector (BESS, RES, grid connection, MV/HV systems)
  • Familiarity with RAE/RAAEY permitting, ADMIE grid code, HEDNO processes
  • English fluency (for internal Lighthief communication)

Responsibilities

Technical Authority (50%)
  • Sign off on all engineering designs and technical studies
  • Prepare and submit tender documents to RAE/RAAEY
  • Grid connection applications to ADMIE (transmission) and HEDNO (distribution)
  • Technical due diligence on BESS projects
  • Protection system design and review
  • MV/HV electrical engineering, single-line diagrams, relay settings
  • Compliance with Greek grid code and EN standards
  • Interface with Lighthief Cyprus engineering team and OEMs (Linyang, Kehua, Voltus)
  • Commissioning supervision and technical acceptance
Business Development (30%)
  • Market intelligence — track Greek BESS pipeline, tenders, and regulatory changes
  • Relationship building with key developers (METLEN, PPC, Terna Energy, Motor Oil/MORE)
  • Attend RAE/RAAEY consultations and industry events (e.g., TIF-Helexpo, Athens Energy Forum)
  • Prepare proposals and RFP responses
  • Identify co-investment and partnership opportunities
  • Build local subcontractor network (civil, electrical, crane/transport)
Country Management (20%)
  • First point of contact for all Greek operations
  • Coordinate with accountant, lawyer, and registered office provider
  • Budget tracking and monthly reporting to Managing Director
  • Recruitment of additional team members as pipeline grows
  • Health & safety compliance for Greek operations

Role 3: Energy Engineer / Project Manager

TitleEnergy Engineer / Project Manager (Μηχανικός Ενέργειας / Υπεύθυνος Έργου)
Reports toCountry Lead
LocationAthens, Greece (with site travel)
EmploymentFull-time employee
Salary Range€2,200 – €3,200/month gross (€26,000 – €38,000/year)
Employer Cost€2,640 – €3,860/month (incl. ~22% employer SI)
TimingSecond hire — when first project is secured or pipeline exceeds €10M

Requirements

  • Engineering degree (electrical, mechanical, or energy engineering)
  • TEE registration preferred (or eligible)
  • 3–5 years experience in energy/renewables projects
  • Familiarity with BESS technology, grid connection, and construction processes
  • AutoCAD/ETAP proficiency
  • English fluency

Responsibilities

  • Detailed project planning, scheduling, and cost tracking
  • Grid connection process management with ADMIE/HEDNO
  • Subcontractor coordination (civil works, electrical installation, crane/transport)
  • Site supervision during construction and commissioning
  • Quality assurance and inspection documentation
  • Environmental permit and building permit coordination
  • Interface with Linyang installation crew during commissioning
  • O&M setup for completed projects (procedures, SCADA handover)
  • Health & safety on-site compliance

Reports toCountry Lead or Project Manager
