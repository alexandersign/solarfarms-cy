## 10. Comparison: Greece PV vs. Cyprus BESS

| Metric | Greece 10 MWp PV Park | Cyprus 10 MWh BESS Park |
|--------|----------------------|------------------------|
| CAPEX | €5.6M (€560/kWp) | ~€1.1M (€110K/MWh) |
| Annual revenue | €781K | ~€400K (DAM arbitrage) |
| EBITDA | €596K (76%) | ~€300K (75%) |
| EBITDA yield | 10.6% | ~27% |
| Equity IRR | 13–16% | 15–25% |
| LCOE / cost of energy | €33–35/MWh | N/A (storage) |
| Revenue certainty | High (PPA) | Medium (merchant) |
| Curtailment risk | High (7%+, rising) | Low (storage absorbs curtailment) |
| Grid connection | Bottlenecked | Bottlenecked |
| O&M complexity | Low | Medium-High |

**Strategic insight:** Greek PV parks offer stable, PPA-backed returns but face rising curtailment risk. BESS projects have higher returns but merchant risk. The ideal Lighthief Greece play is **PV + BESS co-location** — PV provides baseload revenue while BESS captures curtailed energy and arbitrage spread.
