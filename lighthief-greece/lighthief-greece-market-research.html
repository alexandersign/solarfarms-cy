Greece is different from Germany/UK: Most balancing revenue is energy-based (paid per MWh delivered), not capacity-based. This means batteries need to actually cycle, not just reserve capacity. Early entrants capture outsized returns — the market is shallow and will compress as more BESS enters.

Verdict: 2-hour systems deliver 56% higher revenue per MW and ~3× the EBITDA yield. The evening peak is only 4 hours — a 2h battery captures the most expensive 2 hours and still has time for a second partial cycle. Recommended sizing: 5–10 MW / 10–20 MWh per project.

Key trend: The Greek market is moving from subsidised (CfD) to merchant. The 4.7 GW BESS programme is 100% merchant — no government support. PV PPAs are falling (~€52.50/MWh and declining). Energy trading capability is becoming as important as generation.
