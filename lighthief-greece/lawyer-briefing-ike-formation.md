### Director & Liability

8. Can liability exposure be limited through corporate structure (e.g., appointing a local nominee director)?

### Tax & Compliance

10. Confirm the 0% dividend withholding tax to Lighthief Cyprus under the EU Parent-Subsidiary Directive (≥10% holding, ≥24 months). Any filing requirements to claim this?
11. What are the transfer pricing documentation requirements for intercompany transactions (management fees, equipment procurement margin)?
12. Is there an annual audit requirement for a small IKE? What are the revenue/asset thresholds?
13. Are there any tax incentives or grants available for energy storage / renewable energy companies establishing in Greece?

### Energy Sector

14. Does the company need a specific licence from RAE (Ρυθμιστική Αρχή Ενέργειας) to provide BESS EPC or O&M services?
15. What permits are required to develop, own, or operate a battery energy storage project in Greece?
16. Are there restrictions on foreign-owned companies participating in BESS tenders or receiving grid connection priority?
17. What is the process for obtaining a storage permit (Άδεια Αποθήκευσης) from RAAEY?

### TEE Registration & Professional Licensing

18. Confirm: does the company need at least one TEE-registered engineer on staff to participate in BESS EPC tenders and submit engineering designs to RAE/RAAEY?
19. Can a TEE-registered engineer employed by the IKE sign off on designs and tender submissions on behalf of the company, or does the company itself need a separate contractor classification (e.g., MEEP/ptychion)?
20. If we hire an engineer with a Cyprus (ETEK) or EU engineering degree, what is the process for TEE recognition under EU Directive 2005/36/EC? How long does recognition take?
21. Are there specific contractor classification or licensing requirements (MEEP categories) for BESS EPC work in Greece? If so, what class/category is needed and what are the prerequisites?

### Posted Workers

22. If we send engineers from our Polish entity to work on Greek projects temporarily (1–3 months), what are the obligations under the EU Posting of Workers Directive?
23. What Greek labour law minimums apply to posted workers (minimum wage, working hours, safety requirements)?
24. What notifications/filings are required with ERGANI for posted workers?
