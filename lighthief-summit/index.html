About the Summit

The premier energy summit for the Eastern Mediterranean

The Cyprus Energy Summit 2026 is a high-level, invite-only conference bringing together energy ministers, policymakers, investors, and leading industry professionals to shape the energy transition across Cyprus, Greece, and the broader Eastern Mediterranean.

Organised by Lighthief Cyprus Ltd — an EPC and BESS specialist with an active 249 MW portfolio — the summit provides a unique platform for decision-makers to exchange intelligence, forge partnerships, and drive investment in renewable energy and grid infrastructure.

With Cyprus at the crossroads of EU policy ambitions and regional geopolitics, and the EuroAsia Interconnector poised to transform the island's energy landscape, 2026 is the defining year for Eastern Mediterranean energy.

Key Themes

Discussion Topics

01

BESS & Grid-Scale Storage

Battery energy storage as the enabling technology for island grid stability, RES curtailment reduction, and frequency regulation.

02

EuroAsia Interconnector

The strategic undersea cable linking Cyprus, Greece, and Israel — timeline, investment implications, and regional power market integration.

03

RES Policy & Permitting

Regulatory developments in Cyprus and Greece: grid connection queues, permitting acceleration, and NECP compliance pathways.

04

Green Finance & Investment

EU taxonomy, green bonds, project finance structures, and the role of development banks in accelerating Eastern Mediterranean decarbonisation.

05

Hydrogen & New Technologies

Hydrogen production and export potential for Cyprus; electrolysers, offtake agreements, and the EU Hydrogen Bank framework.

06

Poland–Cyprus Investment Corridor

Bilateral investment flows, diplomatic energy partnerships, and the role of European funds in cross-border RES project development.
