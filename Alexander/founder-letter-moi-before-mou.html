1. What is being formalised

The Team Operational Consultancy Agreement between Lighthief Cyprus Ltd and Moi Ostrov Ltd (reference LCY-MOI-TOCA-2026-001) records:

  • Part A — Origination Fee: 30% of Net Margin on the Schedule 1 BESS portfolio (the 51-park Cyprus book I originated and structured).
  • Part B — Team Operations Fee: 30% of Net Margin on future Cyprus BESS business conducted through LCY, as defined in the agreement.
  • Fees are payable pro-rata when clients pay the Company (not from founder capital or investor equity).

This is operational and origination compensation through Moi Ostrov Ltd. It is not a fee on any shareholder injection, equity subscription, or external investment proceeds.

2. Why this must be signed before any memorandum or share transfer

The consultancy agreement is drafted as a pre-existing obligation of the Company. If it is fully executed first, any later memorandum, cap-table change, or share issue is subject to explicit protections in the deed:

Provision Effect
Clause 5.3(b) The agreement is a Protected Matter — it cannot be amended, terminated, or overridden without unanimous written consent of all shareholders (including my 10% if issued).
Clause 6.3 No shares may be issued, transferred, or allotted to any new shareholder unless, as a condition precedent, that person has executed a deed of acknowledgement confirming the agreement is a pre-existing obligation and that they will not challenge or seek to vary it.
Clause 6.8 The Articles of Association must be amended before any transfer to a new shareholder to embed Protected Matter status and the Clause 6.3 acknowledgement requirement.
Clause 6.7 The Company must notify Moi Ostrov immediately of any proposed share transfer, new investor, or articles change.
If a memorandum or share transfer is signed or completed before this package is closed, the new partner may refuse the Clause 6.3 deed — and we will be in a commercial dispute instead of a clean execution path.

Signing the consultancy after a memorandum gives any incoming shareholder leverage to demand a fee cut or removal as a condition of closing. The correct sequence is: consultancy closing package first; memorandum / share transfer second.

3. Closing package required (same signing round)

The main deed alone is not sufficient. The following must be executed together, before any Ben-Haim MOU completion, share transfer, or third-party subscription:

  1. Team Operational Consultancy Agreement (LCY ↔ Moi Ostrov) — executed as a deed.
  2. Annex A — Shareholder Resolution — signed by you (or authorised signatory) for Lighthief International Ltd, approving and ratifying the agreement and instructing Articles amendment.
  3. Annex B — UBO Personal Undertaking — signed by you personally, including your undertaking to procure the Clause 6.3 deed from any transferee and to vote against any action that would prejudice the agreement.
  4. Articles of Association amendment — filed at the Registrar to record Protected Matter status and the new-shareholder acknowledgement requirement (Clause 6.8).
  5. Board minutes — related-party approval documented (I will recuse conflict items; you chair as Lighthief International Ltd director).
  6. Schedule 1 — locked to the current park register (all groups, including Galascope).
Without Annex A, Annex B, and the Articles change, the agreement exists only as a contract between the Company and Moi Ostrov. It does not have the shareholder-level lock or personal UBO backstop the draft is designed to provide.

4. Interaction with the Ben-Haim / investor memorandum

For clarity:

  • A memorandum proposing 45% / 45% / 10% or any change to my shareholding does not, by itself, cancel or reduce the Moi Ostrov fee — provided the consultancy package above is executed first and any new 45% holder executes the Clause 6.3 deed as a condition precedent to receiving shares.
  • Any proposal that my 10% carries no vote or no dividend is a separate shareholder matter. It does not replace or satisfy the Company's contractual obligation to Moi Ostrov under LCY-MOI-TOCA-2026-001.
  • You must not complete, sign, or implement any share transfer, allotment, or binding SHA under the Ben-Haim proposal until items 1–6 above are complete.
  • If you intend to proceed with Ben-Haim, you must procure that the incoming shareholder executes the deed of acknowledgement under Clause 6.3(b) before any shares move. That deed is not optional and is not subject to side negotiation after closing.

  • I will not treat verbal assurances on commission or MD terms as sufficient for bank, client, or execution purposes.
  • I will treat any share transfer or memorandum completion without the Clause 6.3 deed as a breach of the Company's pre-existing obligations once the consultancy is signed, and Moi Ostrov Ltd will rely on its contractual, shareholder, and personal undertaking rights accordingly.

This is not a threat to the pipeline — it is the minimum governance required for me to continue executing as MD and for any third party to rely on clean corporate authority.
