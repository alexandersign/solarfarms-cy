## Operative terms (heads)

1. Adhering Entity acknowledges the Consultancy Agreement (LCY-MOI-TOCA-2026-001) and agrees that any receipt by Adhering Entity in respect of a **Cyprus Protected Project** is a **Deemed Group Receipt** under Clause 12.3.

3. Adhering Entity joins the fee calculation and payment mechanics: Company remains primary payor; Adhering Entity covenants to cooperate with margin reporting and audit.

4. Adhering Entity acknowledges Protected Matter status and agrees not to take action to frustrate fees properly accrued under signed client contracts.

6. Term: co-terminous with Consultancy Agreement Minimum Term / per-project survival on signed contracts.
