## Deed of adherence — enforceability note

The deed binds only **signing** Group entities. The Consultancy Agreement can **require** adherence (12.3(d)), but an entity that refuses to sign and contracts anyway leaves you with:

- A **breach claim against Sybaris** under Annex B (personal), not direct enforcement against that entity  
- Possible **specific performance / injunction** only if counsel structures it — entity is still not a fee debtor until it adheres  

**Annex B enforceability matters more than the deed template sitting unsigned in the pack.**
