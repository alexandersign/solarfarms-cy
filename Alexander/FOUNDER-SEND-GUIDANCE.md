## 3. Map Shapiro pushbacks → your response

### Pushback #3 — Cash waterfall / use of proceeds

**His line:** “My €1M funds your 30% on every milestone.”

**Reality you should own in the conversation:**

- Commission is **not** 30% of client cash — it is **30% of Net Margin** on each milestone, **after** Linyang/CIF and **project EPC costs** in the margin definition.
- On Galascope-scale margin (~€250k), your take is ~€75k on the **signed book**, not 30% of every euro that hits the bank.
- Investor capital is **equity**, not a substitute for **client receipts** — fee still mirrors **client payments**, not investor wire.

**Proposed waterfall (for future SHA — acceptable compromise):**

```
Client receipt (Galascope / portfolio)
  → Linyang & confirmed equipment/adders (project COGS)
  → Project EPC subcontractors & direct delivery costs
  → Net Margin calculated (per park, per SSOT / v5 adders)
  → Consultancy fee (30% of Net Margin) within 14 days
  → Working capital reserve (board-approved %)
  → Retention / warranty holdbacks
  → Dividends / shareholder distributions (reserved matter)
```

**Do not agree:** fee **subordinate to investor dividends** or “capped until investor repaid.”

**Do agree (if needed):** **transparency** — monthly margin bridge + quarterly SHA reporting for investor **observer**.
