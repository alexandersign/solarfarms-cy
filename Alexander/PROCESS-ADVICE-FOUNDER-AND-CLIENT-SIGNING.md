# Process advice — founder alignment before client contract

**Audience:** Managing Director  
**Objective:** Reduce personal, corporate, and reputational risk by securing **written** founder (and, if applicable, board) commitments **before** the company binds itself to the client.

---

## 1. Principles

1. **Company first:** Your director duties run to **Lighthief Cyprus Ltd**, not to any single shareholder. Document how runway will be funded so the company can **perform** the client contract.
2. **Separate tracks:** Do not merge (a) **company liquidity**, (b) **your compensation / commission**, and (c) **cap table / investor** into one informal conversation. Each needs clarity.
3. **Written beats verbal:** Board resolutions, signed heads of terms, or short-form agreements beat email promises for anything material (runway, your %, repayment, exclusivity).
4. **Condition precedent:** Where appropriate, make **founder funding** or **investor term sheet** a **condition** (or at least a documented parallel covenant) to **your** recommendation to proceed with client execution — subject to lawyer advice on how to phrase this under Cyprus law and the articles.

---

## 2. Recommended sequence (high level)

| Step | Action | Owner |
|------|--------|--------|
| 1 | **Cash model:** 4-month (or longer) **company** burn post client signature until first milestone cash — include payroll, premises (e.g. office/warehouse), VAT timing if relevant, and any performance instruments. | MD + accountant |
| 2 | **Board / written shareholder note:** Circulate **one page** — funding gap, options (Founder A vs Investor B), deadline for founder response. | MD |
| 3 | **Founder meeting:** Present Option A and Option B using the draft packs in this folder; agree **which path** and **by what date** commitments must be signed. | MD + Founder |
| 4 | **Lawyer review:** Send chosen draft(s) to Cyprus counsel; revise for companies law, tax, and enforceability. | MD / Company |
| 5 | **Sign founder-facing documents** (amended by counsel) **before** or **simultaneously with** client contract — counsel will advise on **condition precedent** vs **covenant** vs **side letter**. | Founder + Company + MD as needed |
| 6 | **Client contract:** Company signs only when **counsel** is satisfied that authority, capacity, and funding plan are consistent with director duties. | Board / MD per articles |

---

## 3. What to avoid

- Signing the **client** contract while relying on **undocumented** founder promises for runway.
- **Diverting** or restructuring the client relationship toward a third party **without** explicit legal review of **all** agreements (employment, directorship, client LOI/contract, fiduciary duties).
- Treating **“30% of €1M”** or **commission** as certain cash until **net-of-tax** and **trigger** clauses are written.

---

## 4. Documentation checklist (minimum)

- [ ] Current **articles of association** and **register of members**
- [ ] **Board minutes** (or written resolutions) authorising client contract and any **guarantees / BGs**
- [ ] **Employment / service agreement** for MD (base, commission mechanism, social contributions)
- [ ] **Accrued pay / loan to company** schedule if you have been deferring compensation (accountant-shaped)
- [ ] **Founder** agreement pack (Option A or B from this folder) — **after** lawyer review
- [ ] **Client** contract + any **NDA / exclusivity** that affects other options

---

## 5. After client signature

- Track **milestones** and **cash** against the model weekly during the gap to first payment.
- Refresh **board** if covenant breach or funding shortfall appears likely (early warning, not last-minute crisis).
