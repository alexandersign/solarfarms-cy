# Future Business — How the Agreement Protects Work Not Yet Existing

**Ref:** LCY-FUTURE-BIZ-2026-001  
**Audience:** Founder · MD · Cyprus counsel  
**Cross-ref:** Consultancy Agreement Parts 1–7 (LCY-MOI-TOCA-2026-001)

---

## 1. The problem you are solving

Much of the value is **not** only the 51 parks in Schedule 1 today. You are also protecting:

- The **nine Esperia pipeline** parks (LOI quoted, EPC not yet signed)  
- **New clients** you originate after signing  
- Revenue if Sybaris routes work through **another Group company**  
- **O&M / LTSA** on delivered BESS  
- Deals introduced by a **future investor** but executed through LCY  

The agreement uses **four layers** — not a single “list of parks” clause.

---

## 2. Four layers of protection

| Layer | What it covers | Key clause / doc |
|-------|----------------|------------------|
| **A — Schedule 1 (51 parks)** | Everything already in the portfolio | Part A — 30% Net Margin; Schedule 1 groups G1–G6 |
| **B — Pre-sign origination add-on** | Parks you added to LCY **before** deed date but not yet in Schedule 1 table | Definition **Existing Project** — “any additional BESS parks … originated … prior to the date of this Agreement” |
| **C — Protected Project (ongoing)** | Any **LCY business** you originate (EPC, PV, advisory, supply, O&M — not only BESS) | Definition §1.1 · Clause 12 |
| **D — Anti-circumvention** | Stops Group / Sybaris routing Cyprus work outside LCY to avoid fees | **Clause 12** + **Annex B** personal undertaking + deed of adherence |

---

## 3. Part A vs Part B — what applies to “new” revenue

**Important:** Part A remains on the **BESS park portfolio** (Schedule 1 / 1A) using the Net Margin formula (Client − CIF − EPC). **Part B** covers **all other Company revenue** in Cyprus — not limited to BESS.

| Revenue type | Fee | When it applies |
|--------------|-----|-----------------|
| Schedule 1 / 1A park EPC (BESS) | **Part A** — 30% Net Margin | Existing Project |
| **Any other** LCY contract (PV, advisory, supply, new EPC, etc.) | **Part B** — 30% Net Margin or Net Revenue | Future Business |
| Cyprus O&M / LTSA / consultancy | **Part B** | Schedule 3 services |
| Fund **EPC revenue** through LCY | **Part B** | Yes |
| Fund GP **carry / AUM** only | **Excluded** | Clause 3.1A(i) |
| Equity subscriptions to LCY | **Excluded** | Clause 3.1A(ii) |
| Energy trading | **Excluded** unless written scope | Clause 3.1A(iii) |
| Non-Cyprus principal performance | **Excluded** unless agreed | Clause 3.1A(iv) |

**Non-BESS revenue is explicitly not excluded** by technology alone (Clause 3.1A).

**Galascope (2 parks):** already in Schedule 1 G2; separate **Galascope LOI** (Part L1) + EPC triggers **10%** equity and €5k salary.

## 5. Investor or third party introduces a deal later

**Clause 12.7:** Future business from a **third-party investor** remains subject to **Part B** when executed through the Company, **unless** the Parties sign a **written carve-out for a named project before** contract signature.

So an investor does **not** automatically wipe fees — but you should **disclose** the side letter to investors and expect SHA negotiation at scale.

**Equity side letter** is separate: investor cash → **30% participation fee**; MD equity **capped at 15%** if investor path (see Part 10).

---

## 6. What is *not* automatically protected

| Gap | Mitigation |
|-----|------------|
| New park originated **by someone else** (not you) | Part B only if you deliver ops; Part A only if in Schedule 1 / your prior origination |
| Work forced through **non-Group** SPV without you | Commercial + LOI; legal review per deal |
| Founder refuses **deed of adherence** | **Annex B** personal claim against Sybaris |
| **Broad** “Future Business” definition in recitals | **Operative** scope is Part B + Protected Project + Clause 12 — counsel narrowed Part B in 3.1A |

---

## 7. Practical checklist when a new opportunity appears

1. **Name the park/client** in writing to the Company (email/minutes).  
2. Confirm contracting entity = **Lighthief Cyprus Ltd** (Clause 12.2).  
3. If a **Group company** is involved → **deed of adherence** before they touch the client.  
4. If **investor** involved → written carve-out only if you agree; else Part B + disclosure.  
5. Update **Schedule 1** at next amendment if counsel advises (optional; Protected Project may suffice).

BESS is the core today; the fee is on the **whole company undertaking**, with narrow exclusions for equity, fund carry, and offshore work.”
