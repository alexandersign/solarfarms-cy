## 4. Team Operational Consultancy Agreement (Moi Ostrov Ltd)

**4.1** The Board received and considered the Team Operational Consultancy Agreement between the Company and Moi Ostrov Ltd (ref **LCY-MOI-TOCA-2026-001**), including Schedules 1–3 and Annexes A and B.

**4.2** **Alexander Papacosta**, having declared his interest, **withdrew from the meeting** for this item and did not participate in deliberations or voting.

**4.3** The Chair (Dr. Sybaris) noted the commercial rationale: (a) the Cyprus BESS pipeline was originated and structured by Alexander Papacosta; (b) Part A compensates origination on the Schedule 1 portfolio; (c) Part B provides operational services at market rates; (d) shareholder approval would be sought via written resolution of Lighthief International Ltd (Annex A).

**4.4** **RESOLVED** that:

(a) subject to shareholder approval, the Company enter into the Consultancy Agreement substantially in the form circulated;  
(b) **Alexander Papacosta** be authorised to execute the Consultancy Agreement on behalf of the Company as a deed, notwithstanding his directorship of Moi Ostrov Ltd, such execution to occur only after Annex A (shareholder resolution) and Annex B (UBO personal undertaking) are executed by Dr. Arkadiusz Sybaris;
