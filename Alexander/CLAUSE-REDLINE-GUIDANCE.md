### Clause 5 — Duration & protection

**5.3(a) — "This Agreement is irrevocable"**

```
CURRENT:  cannot be terminated… without prior written consent of the Consultant
PROPOSED:  During the Minimum Term, the Agreement may not be terminated by the Company 
           except for material uncured breach by the Consultant (30-day cure) or 
           mutual written agreement. After the Minimum Term, either Party may terminate 
           on [12] months' written notice, subject to accrued fees and Clause 5.5 (as amended).
```

**5.3(b) — Protected Matter / unanimous consent**

```
CURRENT:  unanimous written consent of all shareholders… not merely 75%
PROPOSED:  Amendment or termination of this Agreement during the Minimum Term requires 
           consent of shareholders holding [75]% of voting shares OR unanimous consent if 
           fewer than [3] shareholders. To be reflected in Articles as a class/consent 
           provision [counsel to draft — not a contractual override of Cap. 113].
```

**5.4 — Supremacy / non-supersession**

```
CURRENT:  shall not be superseded… by any change in articles… any shareholder resolution
PROPOSED:  DELETE sub-clauses (b) and (c) entirely. Replace with:
           "This Agreement is a material pre-existing obligation disclosed to new shareholders 
           pursuant to Clause 6.3. Nothing in this Agreement limits statutory rights of 
           shareholders under the Companies Law, Cap. 113."
```

**5.5 — Termination penalty**

```
CURRENT:  three (3) years' average… whether lawfully, by court order, or in breach
PROPOSED:  Liquidated Damages: If the Company repudiates or terminates without cause 
           (other than Consultant material breach) before expiry of the Minimum Term, 
           the Company shall pay an amount equal to the **average annual fees paid in the 
           preceding 24 months**, capped at **[EUR ___ / 12 months' fees]**, as a genuine 
           pre-estimate of loss. No liquidated damages apply to termination following 
           expiry of the Minimum Term or on Consultant material breach.
```

**5.6 — void if amended without consent**

```
SOFTEN:  "Without Consultant consent, any purported variation shall not bind the Consultant 
          and shall not reduce fees accrued prior to the purported variation."
DELETE:  "void and of no legal effect" as to the whole agreement vs future shareholders.
```

### Clause 6 — Consultant protection

**6.5 — Priority of payment**

```
CURRENT:  first-priority… ahead of dividends, management fees, shareholder loans
PROPOSED:  Fees accrued in respect of revenue received shall be paid before the Company 
           declares discretionary dividends or makes discretionary distributions to 
           shareholders in respect of that revenue. This clause does not create security 
           or priority over statutory or secured creditors.
```

**6.3 — New shareholder ack**

```
KEEP — this is the enforceable investor hook. Ensure deed of acknowledgement is separate 
       one-page instrument counsel prepares.
```

### Clause 3 — Part B scope

DELETE rows: fund management fees, carried interest, energy trading (unless Consultant
