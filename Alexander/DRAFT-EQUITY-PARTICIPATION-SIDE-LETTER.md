**Purpose:** Convert founder’s verbal package into one coherent instrument:

3. **Three-year dividend lock** (company-wide) from first Fallback Grant or first Qualifying Equity Closing — whichever is earlier.

**Separate from:** Moi Ostrov commission deed (30% **Net Margin** on EPC operations). The two regimes coexist; they are different economic bases.

## 1. Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Equity Pool Cap** | **25%** of the fully diluted ordinary share capital of the Company (as at each Qualifying Equity Closing). |
| **Qualifying Equity Closing** | Completion of a bona fide subscription or transfer of ordinary shares to a **Third Party Investor** (not MD, not Founder, not any Group company) against payment of **cash subscription moneys** to the Company, pursuant to executed subscription / SHA documents. |
| **Gross Subscription Amount** | Total cash received by the Company from the Third Party Investor in respect of that Qualifying Equity Closing (excluding expense reimbursements). |
| **Cumulative Sold Percentage** | Sum of all percentages issued or transferred to Third Party Investors under Qualifying Equity Closings, measured on a fully diluted basis. |
| **Unsold Pool Percentage** | **Equity Pool Cap** minus **Cumulative Sold Percentage** (minimum zero). |
| **Share Sale Participation Fee** | **30%** of the **Gross Subscription Amount** for each Qualifying Equity Closing. |
| **Fallback Grant Date** | The date on which Unsold Pool Percentage is transferred or allotted to MD under Clause 3. |
| **Longstop Date** | **[180] days** after Galascope EPC Effective Date (signed and stamped by Lighthief Cyprus Ltd and Galascope Ltd), unless extended in writing by Founder and MD. |
| **Group** | Lighthief International Ltd and any entity Sybaris Controls (>50% vote or UBO). |

## 2. Share Sale Participation Fee (30%)

**2.1 Amount.** On each **Qualifying Equity Closing**, the **Founder** shall pay (or cause to be paid) to the MD (or MD Nominee) the **Share Sale Participation Fee** equal to **30%** of the **Gross Subscription Amount** for that closing.

**2.2 Timing.** Within **14 calendar days** after the Company’s receipt of the Gross Subscription Amount (same cadence as Moi Ostrov milestone fees).

**2.3 Payor (critical for investor optics).** Primary obligor is **Founder / Lighthief International Ltd**, **not** the Company’s project working capital. If counsel structures payment via the Company, it shall be documented as a **pre-disclosed success fee** in the investor term sheet and shall rank **after** Linyang / project delivery obligations for that tranche — *counsel to confirm*.

**2.4 No duplication.** The Share Sale Participation Fee is calculated **only** on **Gross Subscription Amount**. It is **not** calculated on: (a) Net Margin or EPC revenue; (b) client milestone receipts; (c) shareholder loans. Operating margin remains governed by the Moi Ostrov Consultancy Agreement.

**2.5 Partial tranches.** Each tranche (e.g. €500,000 for 12.5%) is a separate Qualifying Equity Closing with its own fee: **30% × €500,000 = €150,000**.

**2.6 Survival.** Fee applies to all Qualifying Equity Closings occurring while MD is engaged as Managing Director, or within **12 months** after MD ceases for **Good Reason** (as defined in counsel’s employment / services agreement).

**2.7 Tax.** MD obtains independent tax advice. Parties cooperate on lawful structuring (personal receipt vs MD Nominee company).

## 3. Fallback Equity Grant (unsold portion of 25% pool)

**3.1 Principle.** The **Equity Pool Cap** is **25%**. Third parties may acquire up to **25%**. Any **Unsold Pool Percentage** is granted to MD — not retained by Founder.

**3.2 Worked examples**

| Cumulative Sold % | MD Participation Fee (30% of cash) | MD Fallback Equity |
|-------------------|--------------------------------------|--------------------|
| **0%** (no investor by Longstop) | None | **25%** |
| **12.5%** (€500k) | **€150,000** | **12.5%** (25% − 12.5%) |
| **25%** (€1M) | **€300,000** | **0%** |

**3.3 Trigger — per tranche + longstop**

(a) **On each Qualifying Equity Closing:** within **30 days**, Founder causes register update so MD holds fallback equity equal to **Unsold Pool Percentage** **immediately after** that closing *(optional — counsel may prefer single grant at longstop)*; **OR**

(b) **Recommended:** **Incremental grant** — after each closing, MD receives **Unsold Pool Percentage** as at that date (e.g. after 12.5% sale, MD gets 12.5% shares within 30 days).

(c) **Longstop top-up:** If **Cumulative Sold Percentage < 25%** on **Longstop Date**, Founder shall within **30 days** grant MD the remaining **Unsold Pool Percentage** so that **Cumulative Sold Percentage + MD holding = 25%** (plus Founder’s retained balance).

**3.4 Consideration.** Nominal / nil subscription or set-off against documented MD amounts — **as tax counsel advises**.

**3.5 Grantor.** Lighthief International Ltd (transfer) or Company (capital increase) — counsel to choose.

**3.6 Recipient.** Alexander Papacosta or **Moi Ostrov Ltd** (MD Nominee).

**3.7 No extra fee on fallback shares.** Fallback equity is **not** subject to Share Sale Participation Fee (no Gross Subscription Amount on nil grant).

## 4. Dividend restriction (3 years)

**4.1** From the **earlier** of (i) first **Fallback Grant Date** or (ii) first **Qualifying Equity Closing**, until the **third anniversary**, the Company shall not declare or pay **dividends** or make **shareholder distributions** except:

(a) payments under the Moi Ostrov Consultancy Agreement (operating fees — **not** distributions);  
(b) payroll and arm’s-length director/employee remuneration;  
(c) project delivery costs and confirmed equipment/adders;  
(d) repayment of **documented** shareholder loans per a **pre-agreed schedule** attached as Annex C;  
(e) amounts required by law.

**4.2** This restriction binds the Company and Founder shall vote shares accordingly.

## 5. Conditions & protections

**5.1 MD engagement.** MD must be Managing Director (or equivalent) at each Qualifying Equity Closing, except Good Reason leaver per employment terms.

**5.2 Founder undertakings.** Founder shall not circumvent this Side Letter by: (a) issuing shares above **Equity Pool Cap** to Group entities without MD consent; (b) routing Cyprus BESS revenue outside LCY to avoid margin or equity value (consistent with Consultancy Agreement Clause 12).

**5.3 Investor disclosure.** Before each Qualifying Equity Closing, Founder shall disclose existence of this Side Letter to Third Party Investor under NDA. **Share Sale Participation Fee** should appear in term sheet if paid from Company.

**5.4 Protected Matter.** Amendment or termination of this Side Letter requires **unanimous** written consent of Founder and MD — mirror Consultancy Agreement Protected Matter in Articles.

**5.5 Anti-dilution (MD fallback shares only).** Until a bona fide third-party round above **Equity Pool Cap**, MD’s **Fallback Equity** shall not be diluted by founder-only issuances without MD consent.
