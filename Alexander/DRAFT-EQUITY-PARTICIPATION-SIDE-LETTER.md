# DRAFT — MD Equity Participation Side Letter (heads + clause wording)

**Status:** TEMPLATE — Cyprus legal review required before signature  
**Ref:** LCY-MD-EQ-2026-001  
**Parties:** Dr. Arkadiusz Sybaris / **Lighthief International Ltd** (“**Founder**”) · Lighthief Cyprus Ltd (“**Company**”) · Alexander Papacosta (“**MD**”)

**Purpose:** Convert founder’s May 2026 verbal package into one coherent instrument:

1. **EPC Milestone Equity** — **10%** on Galascope EPC signing + **10%** on Esperia EPC signing (up to **20%** total on the delivery path).  
2. **Share Sale Participation Fee** — **30%** of gross cash on each third-party Qualifying Equity Closing (Founder payor preferred).  
3. **Investor-path equity cap** — if a third-party investor closes, MD’s **aggregate equity** is capped at **15%** fully diluted (EPC grants already issued count toward the cap).  
4. **Three-year dividend lock** from first EPC equity grant or first Qualifying Equity Closing — whichever is earlier.

**Separate from:** Moi Ostrov commission deed (30% **Net Margin** on EPC operations). Different economic bases.

## Recitals (counsel to adapt — contribution narrative)

WHEREAS:

(a) The MD has originated and manages the Company’s Cyprus BESS pipeline (including Galascope, Esperia, and the wider portfolio), such that execution of major EPC contracts is predominantly attributable to MD-led origination and delivery;

(b) The Founder group (Dr. Arkadiusz Sybaris, as UBO of Bretwald Sybaris Holding Ltd, which owns 100% of Lighthief International Ltd) holds **90%** of the Company through Lighthief International Ltd (900 shares), and Alexander Papacosta holds **10%** (100 shares), prior to any further MD equity grants or Qualifying Equity Closing;

(c) The Founder has indicated that, on signing key EPC contracts, the MD shall receive **at least 10%** equity alignment per major client execution, and the Parties wish to record **10% on Galascope EPC** and **10% on Esperia EPC**, subject to the **Investor Path Cap** in Clause 4;

(d) The Parties may also admit a **Third Party Investor**; in that case MD shall receive **30% of Gross Subscription Amount** as a participation fee, while MD **equity** shall not exceed **15%** in aggregate;

(e) This Side Letter is entered into alongside the Moi Ostrov Consultancy Agreement, which governs **operating margin** only.

## 2. EPC Milestone Equity (10% + 10%)

**2.1 Galascope — 10%.** On **Galascope EPC Effective Date**, Founder shall cause **EPC Milestone Grant A** ( **10%** ) to be issued or transferred to MD (or MD Nominee) within **30 calendar days**.

**2.2 Esperia — 10%.** On **Esperia EPC Effective Date**, Founder shall cause **EPC Milestone Grant B** ( **10%** ) to be issued or transferred to MD within **30 calendar days**, **unless** Clause 4 prevents the full second tranche.

**2.3 Consideration.** Nominal / nil subscription or set-off against documented MD amounts — **as tax counsel advises**.

**2.4 Grantor.** Lighthief International Ltd (transfer) or Company (capital increase) — counsel to choose.

**2.5 No cash fee on EPC grants.** EPC milestone equity is **not** subject to Share Sale Participation Fee.

**2.6 Employment cross-ref.** Galascope EPC Effective Date also triggers **€5,000 gross** monthly salary under LCY-EMP-AP-2026-001.

**2.7 Order of operations.** Esperia EPC may execute before or after Galascope EPC; each tranche is independent except for the Investor Path Cap in Clause 4.

---

## 3. Share Sale Participation Fee (30% — investor cash)

**3.1 Amount.** On each **Qualifying Equity Closing**, Founder shall pay (or cause to be paid) to MD (or MD Nominee) the **Share Sale Participation Fee** equal to **30%** of the **Gross Subscription Amount**.

**3.2 Timing.** Within **14 calendar days** after the Company’s receipt of the Gross Subscription Amount.

**3.3 Payor.** Primary obligor is **Founder / Lighthief International Ltd**, **not** Company project working capital. If payment is routed via the Company, it must be **pre-disclosed** in the investor term sheet.

**3.4 No duplication.** Fee applies **only** to Gross Subscription Amount — not Net Margin, EPC revenue, or client milestones (Moi Ostrov deed governs margin).

**3.5 Partial tranches.** Each tranche is a separate closing (e.g. €500,000 for 12.5% → **€150,000** fee).

**3.6 Survival.** Applies while MD is Managing Director, or within **12 months** after Good Reason cessation.

---

## 4. Investor Path Cap (15% total equity)

**4.1 Cap.** If **any** Qualifying Equity Closing occurs, **MD Equity Percentage** shall **not exceed 15%** at any time thereafter.

**4.2 EPC grants count toward cap.** Any shares already issued under Clause 2 (EPC Milestone Grant A and/or B) **count fully** toward the **15%** cap.

**4.3 Second EPC tranche when investor precedes or overlaps Esperia signing.**

| MD equity already held | Investor closed? | Maximum additional equity to MD |
|------------------------|------------------|----------------------------------|
| **0%** | Yes (before any EPC grant) | Up to **15%** total (structure as counsel advises — may combine EPC + investor mechanics) |
| **10%** (Galascope only) | Yes | Up to **5%** further equity only (not full second 10% if that would exceed 15%) |
| **10%** | No | Entitled to full **EPC Milestone Grant B** (10%) → **20%** total |
| **20%** (both EPCs) | Yes later | **No further equity** to MD; **fee only** on subscription cash |

**4.4 No “25% minus sold” pool.** The Parties **replace** any prior discussion of a 25% equity pool or unsold-pool fallback with this **EPC milestone + investor cap** structure.

**4.5 Fee not reduced by cap.** Share Sale Participation Fee is **unaffected** by the Investor Path Cap (cash on subscription remains **30%**).

## 6. Dividend restriction (3 years)

**6.1** From the **earlier** of (i) first EPC Milestone Grant A or (ii) first Qualifying Equity Closing, until the **third anniversary**, no dividends or shareholder distributions except:

(a) Moi Ostrov fees; (b) payroll; (c) project delivery costs; (d) documented shareholder loan repayments per Annex C; (e) amounts required by law.

**6.2** Founder shall vote shares accordingly.

## 7. Conditions & protections

**7.1 MD engagement.** MD must be Managing Director at each grant/closing (except Good Reason leaver).

**7.2 Founder undertakings.** No circumvention via Group entities or off-LCY contracting (consistent with Consultancy Agreement Clause 12).

**7.3 Investor disclosure.** Disclose Side Letter to Third Party Investor under NDA before closing; disclose participation fee in term sheet if Company pays.

**7.4 Protected Matter.** Amendment requires unanimous Founder + MD written consent — mirror Consultancy Agreement in Articles.

**7.5 Anti-dilution (MD shares only).** MD equity shall not be diluted by founder-only issuances without MD consent until a bona fide third-party round.
