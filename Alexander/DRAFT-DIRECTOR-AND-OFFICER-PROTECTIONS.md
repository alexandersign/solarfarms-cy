## 1. Why this is separate from EPC professional indemnity

| Cover | What it protects | Typical payor | In current pack? |
|-------|------------------|---------------|------------------|
| **EPC professional indemnity (PI)** | Client claims for design/EPC negligence | Lighthief Cyprus Ltd | Yes — EPC template §14 (€1–2M); Marsh programme in progress |
| **Public / employer liability** | Third-party injury, employee claims | Company | Partially — EPC / Marsh |
| **D&O / management liability** | Breach of duty, wrongful trading, shareholder claims, regulatory, related-party challenge | Company (or shareholder) | **No** |
| **Statutory indemnity (Cap. 113)** | Defence costs / losses from acts in office (within legal limits) | Company | **No — check Articles** |

**PI does not substitute for D&O.** Client PI responds when the **company** is sued for project delivery. D&O responds when **you** (or Lighthief International’s representative) are sued as **officers** — e.g. approving related-party contracts, dividend policy, insolvency, filing defaults, employment disputes.

## 3. Company covenants (heads — counsel to expand)

**3.1 Directors’ and officers’ liability insurance**

The Company shall, within **[60]** days of execution (or **[30]** days before Galascope EPC Effective Date, whichever is earlier):

(a) procure and maintain **D&O / management liability** insurance with minimum limit **€[1,000,000]** per claim and in the aggregate (counsel + Marsh to confirm BESS-appropriate limit);

(b) name as **insured persons**: Alexander Papacosta (director and secretary); each authorised representative of Lighthief International Ltd acting as corporate director; and any future executive directors approved by the Shareholder;

(c) provide **certificates of insurance** and policy summaries to each insured person on placement and renewal;

(d) **not cancel or materially reduce** cover without **[30]** days’ prior written notice to insured persons and Shareholder consent while any Protected Matter under LCY-MOI-TOCA-2026-001 remains in force;

(e) where the policy permits, ensure **defence costs are advanced** and **retroactive date** covers acts from **1 July 2025** (or earlier if available).

**3.2 Statutory indemnity (Cap. 113)**

To the fullest extent permitted by Cyprus law (including Section 197, Cap. 113 and the Company’s articles):

(a) the Company **indemnifies** each Beneficiary against liabilities and reasonable defence costs arising from acts or omissions in the proper performance of duties as director or secretary, **excluding** fraud, wilful default, or conduct known to be in breach of duty;

(b) indemnity includes proceedings brought by the Company or Shareholder **unless** final non-appealable judgment finds fraud or dishonesty;

(c) the Company shall **advance** reasonable defence costs subject to undertaking to repay if indemnity excluded.

**3.3 Secretary-specific**

Alexander Papacosta’s indemnity and D&O cover extend to duties as **company secretary** (registers, filings, minutes) on the same basis as directorship.

**3.4 Related-party transactions**

The Shareholder acknowledges that Beneficiaries may participate in related-party transactions (Moi Ostrov deed, employment, side letter) **only** where disclosed and approved per Section 191, Cap. 113. The Company and Shareholder **shall not** deny indemnity or insurance solely because a claim arises from a transaction that was **duly disclosed and shareholder-approved**, unless counsel determines indemnity is legally prohibited.

**3.5 Run-off**

On cessation of office (other than summary removal for fraud/dishonesty), D&O **run-off** cover of **[6] years** (or policy maximum) at Company expense unless termination is for cause defined in counsel’s final draft.

**3.6 No personal guarantee of company PI**

Nothing here requires Beneficiaries to **personally** underwrite the Company’s EPC professional indemnity obligations to clients. Client-facing PI remains a **Company** obligation.
