## Resolution

**THE SOLE SHAREHOLDER** resolves:

**1. D&O insurance.** The Company is **directed and authorised** to procure and maintain directors’ and officers’ liability insurance naming **Alexander Papacosta** (director and company secretary) and each **authorised representative** of Lighthief International Ltd acting as corporate director, on terms no less favourable than the heads set out in the Director & Officer Protections deed (LCY-DIR-IND-2026-001), including minimum limit **€[1,000,000]** and retroactive coverage from **1 July 2025** where available.

**2. Premium.** Insurance premiums and run-off premiums are **approved Company expenses**.

**3. Statutory indemnity.** The Company is **authorised** to execute a deed of indemnity in favour of the Beneficiaries under LCY-DIR-IND-2026-001 to the maximum permitted by Section 197, Cap. 113 and the articles.

**4. Secretary appointment.** **Alexander Papacosta** is **confirmed** as **company secretary** of the Company and all filings and minutes executed by him in that capacity from **[appointment date / 1 July 2025]** to date are **ratified**, subject to correction of any technical filing errors identified by counsel.

**5. Articles.** The directors are **instructed** to procure such **amendments to the articles** as counsel advises are necessary to give full effect to the indemnity and insurance obligations.

**6. Related-party.** The Shareholder acknowledges that D&O and indemnity cover related-party claims **only** where disclosure and approval requirements were satisfied; this Resolution does not waive any right to pursue claims for fraud or dishonesty.
