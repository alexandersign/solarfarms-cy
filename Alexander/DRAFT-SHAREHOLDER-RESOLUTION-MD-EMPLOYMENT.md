## Resolution

**THE SHAREHOLDER(S)** of Lighthief Cyprus Ltd (the “**Company**”) hereby resolve (Lighthief International Ltd holding **90%** of issued share capital; Alexander Papacosta holding **10%**):

**1. Approval of employment.** The Contract of Employment between the Company and **Alexander Papacosta** (Cyprus ID: 1241318) as **Managing Director**, reference **LCY-EMP-AP-2026-001** (the “**Employment Agreement**”), is **approved and ratified** in its entirety.

**2. Remuneration terms.** The Shareholder approves:

| Period | Remuneration |
|--------|----------------|
| From **1 July 2025** until the day before **Galascope EPC Effective Date** | **€2,000 net per month** (with PAYE/NIS on gross equivalent per payroll adviser) |
| From **Galascope EPC Effective Date** | **€5,000 gross per month** |

**Galascope EPC Effective Date** means the date on which the Galascope Ltd BESS EPC contract (LCY-EPC-GAL-B1-2026 or successor) is **signed and stamped** by both the Company and Galascope Ltd.

**3. Ratification and payroll reconciliation.** The Shareholder ratifies managing director remuneration paid at **€2,000 net per month** for the period **1 July 2025** to the date of this Resolution. **Ratification alone does not satisfy tax or social-insurance obligations.** The Director shall cause the Company’s payroll adviser to reconcile historic PAYE and NIS for that period and remediate any underpayment before execution of the Employment Agreement where practicable.

**4. Related-party / conflict waiver.** The Shareholder acknowledges that Alexander Papacosta is a director of the Company and will be party to the Employment Agreement as Employee. The Shareholder has considered the conflict of interest and **waives objection**, confirming the terms are in the Company’s commercial interests given the Employee’s executive role in building and delivering the Cyprus BESS platform.

**5. Authority to sign.** Alexander Papacosta is **authorised** to execute the Employment Agreement on behalf of the Company notwithstanding his interest as Employee, provided he executes separately in that capacity.

**6. Separate from introducer fee.** The Shareholder acknowledges that introducer / consultancy fees under the Moi Ostrov agreement (LCY-MOI-TOCA-2026-001) and any equity side letter are **separate** from employment salary and shall not be set off against salary without written consent.

**7. Filing.** The Company Secretary shall file this Resolution with the statutory books.
