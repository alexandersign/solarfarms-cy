## Call-Out Rate Schedule (VAT exclusive)

### Urgent Call
> Definition: Any call regarding an error that creates, or appears to create, a loss of energy production due to a failure on the **Medium Voltage Network**.
> Response: **The soonest possible and NO later than 6 hours from the call.**

| Timing | Hourly Rate | Minimum Charge per Visit |
|---|---|---|
| Working Days 08:30–17:00 | €100/hr | **€250** |
| Non-Working Days 17:00–08:30 & Saturdays | €150/hr | **€375** |
| Sundays & Bank Holidays | €200/hr | **€500** |

### Normal Call
> Definition: Any other call (non-MV, non-urgent).
> Response: **The soonest possible and NO later than 72 hours from the call, or based on mutual agreement.**

| Timing | Hourly Rate | Minimum Charge per Visit |
|---|---|---|
| Working Days 08:30–17:00 | €100/hr | **€250** |

**Note:** Legal VAT is added to all above charges.
