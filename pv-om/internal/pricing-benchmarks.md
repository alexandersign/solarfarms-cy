- Emergency site visit: **PLN 1,950 (~€459)** per visit

- SCADA hardware integration: PLN 6,000 per transformer substation (~€1,412)

| Metric | Poland (10 MW) | Cyprus (10 MW) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Annual fee ex. VAT | ~€52,000 | **€56,400** | +8.5% premium |
| Per MW | ~€5,200 | **€5,640** | Within normal range |
| Cleaning sessions | 1x | **2x** | Cyprus includes more |
| Emergency response | 12h, extra cost | **4h, 6 incl.** | Cyprus SLA is far stronger |
| GOLD equivalent (10 MW) | ~€112,565 | N/A | Our Custom is 50% of GOLD |

**Conclusion:** Cyprus Custom pricing is ~8.5% above Polish Custom on a per-MW basis, fully justified by:
1. Stronger SLA (4h vs 12h response)
2. Double cleaning frequency
3. 4x vegetation sessions (vs 1x)
4. 6 included call-outs (vs 0)
5. LESA/MV coverage included
