Grid Resilience: Centralised Generation vs Distributed Storage

Why battery storage across the island is more resilient than a single power station

Centralised thermal (single fuel source)
Distributed BESS (grid-forming, solar-charged)
Grid mesh (redundant connections)

Battery storage systems with grid-forming inverters (such as the Kehua BCS series deployed in Cyprus) can provide synthetic inertia, FCR, and aFRR services — the same stability services that currently require fossil fuel plants to run 24/7. Distributing these systems across the island eliminates the single-point-of-failure risk inherent in centralised generation.
