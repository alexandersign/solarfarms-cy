1. Project Overview

GreenVolt Power Group sp. z o.o. (Warsaw) is tendering O&M services for the HV electrical infrastructure of two massive BESS projects — each 200 MW / 800 MWh. This summary covers the Ełk site. A parallel tender exists for Turośń Kościelna (identical scope, 110/33 kV station).

4. Quotation Basis — Suggested Lighthief Pricing

Pricing approach: ~2% of BESS asset value per year = ~€2,600/MWh/year. For 800 MWh this gives ~€2,080,000/year. However, the tender scope is HV station O&M only (not full BESS O&M). We should price the HV station component separately and offer BESS O&M as an upsell.
