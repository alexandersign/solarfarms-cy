1. Executive Summary

Lighthief proposes a comprehensive Operation & Maintenance programme for the HV electrical infrastructure supporting the Ełk 200 MW / 800 MWh battery energy storage installation. Our scope covers the MEE Ełk 220/110/33 kV substation, the 220 kV cable line to the PSE grid connection point, and all associated auxiliary systems — delivered by a dedicated on-site team with 24/7/365 coverage.

Lighthief operates 11 offices and a 150+ engineer team across 7 European countries, with our EU headquarters and 3,500 m² spare-parts warehouse in Poland. We bring deep BESS and HV infrastructure experience from our 486 MWh deployed portfolio in Cyprus and growing operations across the EU.

2. Scope of Services

Full-scope O&M for the HV station and cable connection, aligned with PSE S.A. (TSO) requirements and Polish energy regulations.

1 HV/MV Substation Maintenance (220/110/33 kV)

  • Scheduled inspections and preventive maintenance of HV switchgear (220 kV, 110 kV bays)
  • Power transformer servicing — oil analysis, Buchholz relay testing, cooling system maintenance, DGA monitoring
  • MV switchgear maintenance (33 kV panels connecting to BESS arrays)
  • Protection relay testing, calibration, and firmware management (IEC 61850 / IEC 60870-5-104)
  • Switching apparatus maintenance — circuit breakers, disconnectors, earthing switches
  • Power quality monitoring and measurement
  • Auxiliary systems — DC supply, UPS, station lighting, HVAC, security

2 220 kV Cable Line Maintenance

  • Cable condition monitoring (thermal imaging, partial discharge testing)
  • Cable termination inspections and maintenance
  • Route inspections (vegetation management, access road maintenance)
  • Joint bay inspections and thermal monitoring
  • Fault location and repair readiness

3 24/7 Monitoring & Emergency Response

  • Round-the-clock substation supervision via SCADA and local control room
  • Alarm handling, event logging, and incident classification
  • Emergency fault response — 30-minute on-site mobilization
  • Switching operations under PSE coordination (IRiESP compliance)
  • Liaison with PSE S.A. dispatch center

4 Planned Maintenance Programme

  • Annual maintenance calendar aligned with PSE outage windows
  • Transformer oil testing (quarterly DGA, annual full analysis)
  • Protection relay testing programme (annual secondary injection, functional testing)
  • HV equipment thermographic surveys (quarterly)
  • Earth resistance measurements (annual)
  • Battery bank maintenance (station DC supply)

5 Compliance, Reporting & Documentation

  • Monthly operational reports and KPI dashboards
  • Annual technical reports for PSE S.A. (IRiESP compliance)
  • Equipment condition reports and remaining-life assessments
  • Regulatory compliance monitoring (URE, PSE, grid code)
  • Technical documentation maintenance and as-built updates
  • H&S compliance — drills, training, incident reporting

6 Spare Parts & Logistics

  • Critical spare parts inventory maintained on-site
  • Backed by Lighthief's 3,500 m² EU warehouse in Poland
  • OEM-certified replacement components sourced directly
  • Target: critical spares available within 4 hours, major spares within 48 hours
