Requested Scope of Work

The Asset Management scope covers five workstreams across the full operational life of the installation. Services may start in the pre-operation phase (before 1 Nov 2026).

1 Operational Management

  • Supervision of day-to-day plant operations
  • Coordination with the technical O&M contractor
  • Real-time monitoring of plant performance and availability
  • Optimization of BESS dispatch and cycling strategy
  • Oversight of OEM warranty obligations and claims

2 Financial Management

  • Operating cost control and budget management
  • Project budget planning and variance tracking
  • Preparation of financial reports for investors / co-owners
  • Cash flow monitoring and forecasting

3 Contract & Energy Market Management

  • Management of commercial and service contracts
  • Coordination with energy trading entities (arbitrage, balancing market, system services)
  • Offtake and PPA management (if applicable)

4 Reporting & Compliance

  • Operational and financial reporting to stakeholders
  • Preparation of reports for lending institutions (bank covenants, NFOŚiGW grant conditions)
  • Regulatory compliance monitoring (URE, PSE, grid code)
  • Support for audits (financial, technical, environmental)

5 Risk Management

  • Identification and monitoring of operational risks
  • Insurance oversight — policy adequacy, renewals, claims
  • Emergency and incident management
  • Claims management and dispute support
