1. Executive Summary

Lighthief is pleased to submit this turnkey EPC quotation for the Mūša 500 MW / 1,000 MWh battery energy storage project in Lithuania, in response to your project requirement checklist (rev. 2026.04.08) and preliminary site plan. The plant comprises a 200 MW block and a 300 MW block of containerised LFP storage, connected via two new producer step-up stations to the existing Mūša 330 kV Litgrid substation.

Our scope delivers a complete, grid-code-compliant BESS on a DDP project-site basis, engineered for the ENTSO-E Continental Europe synchronous area and the full Lithuanian revenue stack (FCR, aFRR, mFRR, day-ahead, intraday and the capacity market). The design meets the specified −25 °C to +60 °C ambient range, C5 anti-corrosion class and IEEE 693-2018 (moderate) seismic level, targeting COD in Q4 2028 / Q1 2029.

Lighthief operates 11 offices and a 150+ engineer team across 11 European countries, with our EU headquarters and a 3,500 m² spare-parts warehouse in Poland — enabling delivery and long-term service in Lithuania without cross-border mobilisation delays.

3. Technical Solution

Containerised LFP DC blocks with bidirectional PCS and integrated MV step-up, controlled by a grid-code-compliant EMS/SCADA.

DC Battery System

  • Outdoor liquid-cooled LFP containers (~5 MWh class, ~200 units) — packs, racks, BMS, DC cabling & internal comms
  • Integrated thermal management (HVAC / liquid cooling) rated for −25 °C to +60 °C
  • 4-level fire safety per NFPA 855: smoke detection, H₂ off-gas detection, aerosol suppression and water sprinkler/deluge readiness
  • C5 anti-corrosion coating (ISO 12944) for coastal / moderate-industrial exposure

Power Conversion & MV

  • Bidirectional PCS totalling 500 MW with integrated MV step-up transformers (MV-skid)
  • 33 kV MV collection network, switchgear, RMUs and auxiliary transformers
  • Grid-connected switchgear provided within EPC scope
  • Reactive power / voltage regulation and LVRT per Litgrid grid code (VDE-AR-N 4120 as reference)

EMS / SCADA

  • Plant controller and energy management system with market-ready dispatch (FCR / aFRR / mFRR / arbitrage)
  • BMS↔PCS↔EMS integration; Modbus TCP / IEC 61850 / IEC 60870-5-104 to TSO
  • Real-time SoC/SoH monitoring, alarm handling and reporting

Balance of Plant & Civil

  • Foundations, internal roads, drainage, earthing and lightning protection
  • Perimeter fencing, CCTV, access control and site lighting
  • Control / auxiliary building; cable trenching and containment
