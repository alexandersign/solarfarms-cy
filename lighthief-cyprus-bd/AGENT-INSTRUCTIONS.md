## 2. Cyprus Market Context

### BESS Regulatory Framework

- Cyprus Electricity Authority (EAC) is the grid operator
- Cyprus Energy Regulatory Authority (CERA) issues licenses
- TSOC (Transmission System Operator Cyprus) manages grid dispatch
- BESS is co-located with existing solar PV farms (not standalone — yet)
- No day-ahead market (DAM) arbitrage legislation yet (expected 2027+)
- Revenue model: curtailment recovery, peak shaving, ancillary services
- 19% VAT applies to EPC services
- 2.66% import duty on BESS equipment (weighted HS codes)

### Market Pain Points (Opportunities for Lighthief)

1. **Curtailment crisis** — Solar farms losing 15–25% revenue from forced curtailment by TSOC
2. **No storage mandate yet** — But CERA is moving toward storage requirements for new licenses
3. **Grid congestion** — Especially Nicosia and Paphos districts
4. **PV owners need BESS** — To protect revenue, extend export hours, future-proof for DAM
5. **Financing gap** — Banks unfamiliar with BESS bankability; need education and credible EPC partners
6. **O&M void** — No established BESS O&M providers in Cyprus; Lighthief fills this gap

### Prospect Research Tips

- Most PV plants are **SPVs** (Special Purpose Vehicles) registered as "HE" (Limited Company)
- Search keywords on company register: "Solar", "Photovoltaic", "PV", "Energy", "Power", "Renewable"
- Directors of SPVs often sit on the board of the parent group — reveals group ownership structures
- Cross-reference director names to find all related companies (one investor may own 5–15 parks)
- CERA license data shows capacity, location, license holder — cross-reference with company register for owners
- **Target parks: 1–10 MW** (the sweet spot for BESS co-location in Cyprus)

## 4. Writing & Research Guidelines

### Tone & Style

- **Professional but direct** — No corporate jargon or filler. Say what you mean.
- **Data-driven** — Every claim should be backed by a source, number, or citation
- **Action-oriented** — Documents should tell the reader what to DO, not just what exists
- **Cyprus-specific** — Generic BESS content is useless. Everything must reference Cyprus regulation, pricing, players, and market conditions
- **Bilingual awareness** — Some prospects prefer Greek; note when Greek-language versions are needed

### Research Standards

- Always cite sources with URLs and access dates
- Distinguish between **confirmed data** (CERA, TSOC, official) and **estimates** (analyst reports, press)
- Flag data older than 6 months as potentially stale
- Cross-reference at least 2 sources for market sizing claims
- Use tables for comparative data (competitors, pricing, features)
- Include "Last Updated" dates on all living documents

## 7. Do's and Don'ts

### Do

- Research using official Cyprus sources (CERA, TSOC, EAC, company register)
- Create structured, reusable templates
- Track all leads with status, next action, and owner
- Quantify everything — MWh, €, MW, % — no vague claims
- Build materials that a non-technical sales person can use confidently
- Consider the prospect's perspective: "Why should I add BESS to my solar farm?"
- Include ROI arguments: curtailment savings, revenue uplift, asset value increase

### Don't

- Use generic renewable energy content — everything must be Cyprus-specific
- Make up data — if you don't know, say so and suggest where to find it
- Create documents longer than necessary — brevity wins in B2B
- Ignore Greek language needs — flag when bilingual versions are required
- Share confidential pricing (per-client margins, Linyang CIF costs) in external-facing materials
- Assume prospects understand BESS — many PV owners have never seen a battery system
