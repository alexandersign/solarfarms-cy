## 6. LINYANG PAYMENT TERMS — Negotiation Brief

**Internal Brief — For Alexander's Use in Negotiation**

### Current State

Linyang proposed **30/60/10** in their V1 contract draft (6 March 2026).
Portfolio-data.ts (SSOT) shows **25/50/20/5** as the previously negotiated terms.

### Lighthief's Position: Push for 25/50/20/5

**Arguments to present:**

1. **Counterparty risk:** Under 30/60/10, Lighthief pays 90% (EUR 11.9M) before delivery. Under 25/50/20/5, only 75% (EUR 9.9M) is pre-delivery. The EUR 2M difference is meaningful leverage.

2. **DAP alignment:** A 20% DAP milestone aligns with client delivery milestones. Under 30/60/10, there is no delivery-linked payment — Linyang has no financial incentive to deliver on time.

3. **SAT holdback:** The 5% SAT holdback (EUR 660K) is Lighthief's primary quality lever. Without it, Lighthief has no mechanism to withhold payment for commissioning issues.

4. **Industry standard:** Major EPC equipment supply contracts (Siemens, ABB, GE) typically use 20-30% advance, 40-50% pre-shipment, 15-25% delivery, 5-10% acceptance. The 25/50/20/5 structure is within industry norms; 30/60/10 is aggressive.

5. **APG sizing:** A 25% advance requires a EUR 3.3M APG from Linyang's bank. A 30% advance requires EUR 4.0M. The smaller APG is easier for Linyang to arrange.

### Fallback Position

If Linyang insists on 30/60/10:
- Accept, BUT require:
  - Performance bond increased from 5% to **10%** (EUR 1.32M)
  - Delay LDs from day 1 (no grace period) — per proposed Section 9C
  - APG covers 100% of ALL pre-delivery payments (EUR 11.9M), not just advance
  - Retention guarantee: Lighthief retains right to hold 10% until FAC if quality issues arise at delivery

### Impact on Batch 1 Cashflow

| Month | 25/50/20/5 | 30/60/10 | Difference |
|-------|----------:|----------:|----------:|
| Apr (advance) | -3,303K | -3,963K | -660K worse |
| Jun (pre-ship) | -6,605K | -7,926K | -1,321K worse |
| Aug (DAP/delivery) | -2,642K | -1,321K | +1,321K better |
| Dec (SAT/holdback) | -660K | 0 | +660K better |

Net effect: 30/60/10 front-loads EUR 1,981K more before delivery, but saves the same amount post-delivery. The risk profile is worse because more cash is at risk before goods arrive.
