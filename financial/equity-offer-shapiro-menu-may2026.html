The three offers

Offer 1 — Secured project equity
€500,000
12.5% ordinary shares

Collateral-first

  • Equity: 12.5% post-money implied €3.5M on this tranche alone
  • Security: Assignment / charge on Galascope Batch 1 receivables (cap €500k) — documented in SHA
  • Cash sweep: 100% of Galascope post-pool distributable margin (after 10% WC holdback) paid pro-rata until you receive €540,000 (capital + 8% simple return on project cash), then standard distribution policy
  • Governance: Board observer; monthly Galascope cash report
  • Upside: Retain 12.5% for Esperia pipeline and quoted book
Like buying one productive parcel with a mortgageable asset — small cheque, cash back from that asset first.
Offer 2 — Dividend covenant equity
€1,500,000
18% ordinary shares

Income-first · Recommended if one cheque

  • Equity: 18% · implied post-money €6.8M
  • Distribution covenant: Minimum 50% of Distributable Project Cash from Galascope + Esperia Schedule 1 parks paid to shareholders quarterly for 5 years (after origination pool, delivery costs, and agreed retention)
  • Option: +€500k → +4% (22% total) on first Esperia pipeline EPC at same pre-money
  • Governance: Board seat; reserved matters (related-party >€25k, new debt, Poland cross-charge, dividends)
  • Transparency: Quarterly margin bridge; audit rights on project cash
Closest to a profitable operating company — policy-driven cash to shareholders, not founder discretion.
Offer 3 — Platform strategic minority
€3M – €5M
22% – 28%

Scale & governance

  • €3,000,000 → 24% (post-money €12.5M) or €5,000,000 → 28% (post-money €17.9M)
  • Reinvestment: No mandatory dividend — capital deployed into backlog execution and WC (max upside on €65M quoted book)
  • Governance: Board seat; full reserved matters; tag-along; pre-emption; LCY/Poland firewall in SHA
  • People: Right to nominate one operating executive (employment terms; outside origination pool)
  • Trigger: Galascope EPC + completed data room under NDA
Full-scale strategic cheque — you participate in platform value, not single-project yield.
