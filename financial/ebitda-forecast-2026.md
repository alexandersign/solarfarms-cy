## Executive Summary

Lighthief Cyprus Ltd is executing the largest utility-scale Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) deployment in Cyprus — 28 parks totalling 134 MW / 486.5 MWh across 5 districts. The company operates as an asset-light EPC integrator with exclusive distribution rights for Linyang Energy BESS systems in Cyprus.

The company holds a contracted EPC portfolio worth **€60.6M** generating a project EBITDA of **€6.7M (11.1% margin)**, with an additional **€847K/yr recurring service revenue** under 15-year LTSA contracts (51.5% EBITDA margin).

## 5. Recurring Revenue Model (Post-Completion)

Upon completion of all 28 parks, Lighthief transitions to a recurring service business under 15-year Long-Term Service Agreements. The OPEX model uses real-world, fully loaded costs with deliberate overestimates.

### Planning Assumptions

- 6 field engineers (1 buffer over 5-minimum workload model)
- 2 engineers per HV maintenance visit (safety requirement)
- Fully loaded engineer cost = €4,500/mo (incl. accommodation, social insurance)
- 10 planned maintenance days per park per year (280 total)
- 80–150 reactive calls per year across 28 parks
- Warehouse at 50% share (€5,000/mo rent)
- 4 service vehicles (3 shift + 1 logistics/driver)
- 10% contingency on all recurring OPEX
- Spare parts budget increases ~20% after Year 5 as fleet ages

### LTSA vs Extended Warranty — Payment Structure

The LTSA and extended warranty are **separate commercial arrangements**:

| Item | Paid By | Paid To | Lighthief P&L Impact |
|------|---------|---------|---------------------|
| LTSA O&M Fee (€1,740/MWh/yr) | Client | **Lighthief** | Revenue — retained in full |
| Base Warranty (Years 1–5) | — | — | Included in CIF price (no additional cost) |
| Extended Warranty Yr 6–10 (€1,662/MWh/yr) | Client | **Linyang directly** | None — does not flow through Lighthief |
| Extended Warranty Yr 11–15 (€2,084/MWh/yr) | Client | **Linyang directly** | None — does not flow through Lighthief |

Lighthief's LTSA margin is **constant across all 15 years** — it is not compressed by Linyang extended warranty costs, which are a separate client obligation paid directly to the OEM.

## 9. Key Investment Highlights

1. **Contracted Revenue:** Portfolio of €60.6M at fixed prices with 6 client groups. Batch 1 (9 parks, €14.2M) confirmed; Batches 2–3 pipeline.

2. **Exclusive Distribution:** Lighthief holds exclusive rights for Linyang Energy BESS systems in Cyprus — no competitor can offer the same product.

3. **High EBITDA Margin:** 11.1% on €60.6M turnkey contracts, with 96%+ of costs confirmed or formally quoted.

4. **Asset-Light Model:** Minimal capex (offices, tools). Near-100% EBITDA-to-FCF conversion. No debt required under optimised structure.

5. **Recurring Revenue:** 15-year LTSA contracts generate €847K/yr at 51.5% EBITDA margin (€436K/yr) — €3.7M NPV at 8% discount. LTSA revenue is retained by Lighthief in full; extended warranty is a separate client → Linyang arrangement.

6. **Market Timing:** Cyprus BESS market is at inflection point. CERA regulatory framework supports utility-scale storage. First-mover advantage in a market with limited competition.

7. **Proven Team:** ETEK-licensed lead engineer, established subcontractor network, field engineers for O&M.

8. **Pipeline:** Esperia Tseri 2028 (5 parks, 87.5 MWh) in pre-order. Soteria committed to sign. Additional client enquiries ongoing.
