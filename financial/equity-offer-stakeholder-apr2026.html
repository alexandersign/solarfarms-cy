Executive Summary

Lighthief Cyprus Ltd is a Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) EPC contractor operating in Cyprus, with a confirmed and near-confirmed order book totalling €38.6 million in revenue and €4.46 million in gross profit. The company holds an exclusive long-term EPC relationship with Esperia Energy — a major RES licence holder — covering 317.5 MWh of projects spanning 2026 through 2029, all with EPC V4 contracts in final review. Batch 1 (45 MWh) is confirmed for delivery in 2026 under a signed supply agreement with Linyang Energy (China).

This document presents five equity participation tiers for an incoming strategic or financial investor. The investment is denominated in Euros and is structured as a direct shareholding in Lighthief Cyprus Ltd.

Why Invest

Locked Pipeline — Not Indicative
The Esperia relationship represents 317.5 MWh of BESS EPC projects with signed EPC V4 contracts in final legal review. This is not pipeline — it is a committed multi-year programme with a single counterparty controlled by Dino Constantinou.
Batch 1 in Execution Now
45 MWh (Galascope 1 & 2 + Timotheos) in active delivery. Linyang supply agreement signed. CIF Limassol port August 2026. PAC December 2026. Revenue €5.43M cash-realised within 12 months.
Scalable Platform — €61M+ Potential
Additional groups (Timotheos remaining, Kerasi, Karis) represent a further €13.6M in B3 revenue. ABIO/ELESTORE re-engagement adds potential for 200 MWh at 20% probability. Platform is established and repeatable.
