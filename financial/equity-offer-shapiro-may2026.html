Executive Summary

Lighthief Cyprus Ltd is a Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) EPC contractor operating in Cyprus, with a quoted order book of approximately €65.5 million in client revenue and an estimated €7.6 million in gross profit across 546 MWh of quoted projects (plus a further 120 MWh standalone opportunity under study). The company is the appointed EPC for Galascope Ltd and Esperia Energy Group — two of Cyprus's largest BESS programmes — plus a growing portfolio of standalone and group-order clients.

This menu offers Michael Shapiro a choice of five structures in Lighthief Cyprus Ltd, from €300,000 for 7.5% (Offer 0) through €5,000,000 platform participation (Offer 3). All equity closes on the Galascope Ltd EPC signed and stamped by Lighthief Cyprus Ltd and Galascope Ltd (not LOI alone). Offer 0L is an optional €300,000 equity-linked loan at 8% p.a. converting to the same 7.5% stake.

Galascope Batch 1 alone: €3.44M ex VAT / ~€251k estimated net margin (~€175k post-pool to shareholders). Illustrative returns in the menu above; formal subscription and SHA by Cyprus counsel.

Closing trigger (all offers): Initial closing when the Galascope Ltd EPC (LCY-EPC-GAL-B1-2026) is signed and stamped by both Lighthief Cyprus Ltd and Galascope Ltd. Offer 0L may draw on signing for project working capital; conversion to 7.5% equity on the same EPC Effective Date unless investor elects repayment. This proposal does not take effect on LOI alone.

Anticipated Questions

Short answers to points likely raised in diligence. Not binding — for alignment before term sheet.

Why €4M valuation against a €65M quoted backlog?
Early entry before first EPC cashflow. Most of the table is LOI or quoted, not signed EPC — we haircut accordingly. €4M prices the platform at first contract execution, not full backlog conversion. Offers 0 and 0L (€300k / 7.5%) and Offer 1 (€500k / 12.5%) provide lower entry if you prefer less capital at Galascope close.
How much of the backlog is actually committed?
Near-term: Galascope (LOI + EPC in legal review, ~€3.4M). Esperia pipeline: LOI covering 315 MWh. Other lines are quoted or high-probability proposals. HESS is study-only and excluded from the total. Closing trigger is Galascope EPC signed & stamped — not LOI alone.
What is the 30% origination pool — who participates?
As previously discussed, up to 30% of net margin on executed projects rewards the existing team that sourced and quoted the current backlog — Cyprus origination, engineering, and delivery under current management. It is funded from project margin on client cash, not from your equity. Personnel you place in the company would be on separate terms and outside this pool unless expressly agreed. Shareholders participate in the remaining ~70% of net margin after project costs and pool allocation.
Why wire on Galascope EPC if it is only €3.4M?
Galascope de-risks the platform (signed EPC, Linyang supply executed, first revenue path). You are buying into the EPC machine that carries the Esperia LOI pipeline and quoted book — not only one park. Longstop date and conditions to be set in term sheet if EPC slips.
What is Offer 0L — equity-linked loan vs Offer 0 equity?
Same economic outcome if you convert: 7.5% at €4M post-money. The loan pays 8% p.a. simple from draw, secured on Galascope receivables and VAT refund. On Galascope EPC signing you convert to ordinary shares (accrued interest settled at conversion or from first distributable project cash). If you do not convert, principal plus interest is repaid from Galascope cash within 9 months of draw — debt return only, no equity upside. Use 0L if you want WC deployed before close with a defined debt floor; use Offer 0 for straight equity.
What governance do I get at each ticket size?
Offer 0 / 0L (7.5%): information rights, no board seat. Offer 1 (12.5%): board observer. Offer 2 (18%): board seat and reserved matters on material spend, debt, and dividends. Offer 3 (24–28%): full strategic minority package including tag-along and LCY/Poland firewall. Term sheet and SHA to formalise — expect heads of terms with counsel.
Where does investment capital go?
Primarily Galascope working capital — OEM deposit, adders, import VAT timing, delivery — and company runway. Not the origination pool (funded from project margin on client payments). Offer 0L draw schedule tied to Galascope milestones. Use-of-proceeds schedule in subscription agreement.
Cyprus only, or Poland / group?
This investment is in Lighthief Cyprus Ltd — Cyprus BESS EPC. Poland and other group activities are separate; no cross-charge to your stake unless agreed later.
Are margin figures audited?
Management estimates, ex VAT. Data room under NDA: adder stack, Linyang supply contract, sample EPC/LOI, per-park pricing. TIM/LAM/KER/KAR re-quotes at 12.5% to be re-issued after missing group pricing window.
