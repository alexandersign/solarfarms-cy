Executive Summary

Lighthief Cyprus Ltd is a Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) EPC contractor operating in Cyprus, with a quoted order book of approximately €65.5 million in client revenue and an estimated €7.6 million in gross profit across 546 MWh of quoted projects (plus a further 120 MWh standalone opportunity under study). The company is the appointed EPC for Galascope Ltd and Esperia Energy Group — two of Cyprus's largest BESS programmes — plus a growing portfolio of standalone and group-order clients.

This proposal offers [Investor Name] a 25% equity stake in Lighthief Cyprus Ltd for an investment of €1,000,000, implying a post-money valuation of €4.0 million. The offer takes effect and share transfer is triggered only upon the Galascope Ltd EPC being signed and stamped by both Lighthief Cyprus Ltd and Galascope Ltd — expected within approximately 10 days following completion of client legal review.

An optional staged structure is also available: 12.5% for €500,000 on Galascope EPC signing and stamping (both parties), with a call option to acquire a further 12.5% for €500,000 upon signing of the first Esperia pipeline EPC. Detailed commercial terms are set out below; formal subscription and shareholder agreements to be prepared by Cyprus counsel.

Anticipated Questions

Short answers to points likely raised in diligence. Not binding — for alignment before term sheet.

Why €4M valuation against a €65M quoted backlog?
Early entry before first EPC cashflow. Most of the table is LOI or quoted, not signed EPC — we haircut accordingly. €4M prices the platform at first contract execution, not full backlog conversion. Structure B (€500k / 12.5%) available if you prefer less capital at Galascope close.
How much of the backlog is actually committed?
Near-term: Galascope (LOI + EPC in legal review, ~€3.4M). Esperia pipeline: LOI covering 315 MWh. Other lines are quoted or high-probability proposals. HESS is study-only and excluded from the total. Closing trigger is Galascope EPC signed & stamped — not LOI alone.
What is the 30% origination pool — who participates?
Up to 30% of net margin on executed projects rewards the existing team that sourced and quoted the current backlog — Cyprus origination, engineering, and delivery under current management. It is funded from project margin on client cash, not from equity proceeds. Personnel the investor places in the company would be on separate terms and outside this pool unless expressly agreed. Shareholders participate in the remaining ~70% of net margin after project costs and pool allocation.
Why wire on Galascope EPC if it is only €3.4M?
Galascope de-risks the platform (signed EPC, Linyang supply executed, first revenue path). You are buying into the EPC machine that carries the Esperia LOI pipeline and quoted book — not only one park. Longstop date and conditions to be set in term sheet if EPC slips.
What governance do I get at 25%?
Term sheet and SHA to define board observer (or seat), information rights, reserved matters (related-party spend, new debt, dividends, group entities), and standard pre-emption. Not finalised in this proposal — expect a one-page heads of terms with counsel.
Where does the €1M go?
Primarily Galascope working capital (OEM deposit, adders, delivery) and company runway — not the origination pool. Pool is funded from project margin on client payments. Use-of-proceeds schedule in subscription agreement.
Cyprus only, or Poland / group?
This investment is in Lighthief Cyprus Ltd — Cyprus BESS EPC. Poland and other group activities are separate; no cross-charge to your stake unless agreed later.
Are margin figures audited?
Management estimates, ex VAT. Data room under NDA: adder stack, Linyang supply contract, sample EPC/LOI, per-park pricing. TIM/LAM/KER/KAR re-quotes at 12.5% to be re-issued after missing group pricing window.
