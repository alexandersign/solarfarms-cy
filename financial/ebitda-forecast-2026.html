Investment Thesis

Lighthief Cyprus is executing Cyprus's largest BESS deployment — €60.6M contracted revenue generating €6.7M contract EBITDA — while building a €847K/yr recurring service platform (51.5% EBITDA margin) under 15-year LTSA contracts. LTSA revenue is retained by Lighthief in full. Portfolio updated March 2026 (ABIO removed; Spanercom added to Batch 1).

  • Contracted Pipeline: 6 client groups, fixed turnkey pricing. Batch 1 (9 parks, €14.2M) confirmed
  • Exclusive Distribution: Sole Linyang Energy BESS distributor in Cyprus
  • Recurring Revenue: €847K/yr LTSA, €436K/yr EBITDA × 15 years = €3.7M NPV
  • Self-Funding: Optimised structure eliminates need for external capital
  • First-Mover: Largest BESS portfolio in Cyprus, regulatory tailwinds from CERA
