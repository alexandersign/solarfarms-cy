Investment Thesis

Lighthief Cyprus is executing Cyprus's largest BESS deployment — €109M contracted revenue generating €11.3M contract EBITDA — while building a €1.5M/yr recurring service platform (51.5% EBITDA margin) under 15-year LTSA contracts. LTSA revenue is retained by Lighthief in full. All recurring costs are overestimated with 10% contingency for investor credibility.

  • 100% Contracted: 6 client groups, fixed turnkey pricing, advance payments secured
  • Exclusive Distribution: Sole Linyang Energy BESS distributor in Cyprus
  • Recurring Revenue: €1.5M/yr LTSA, €790K/yr EBITDA × 15 years = €6.8M NPV (costs deliberately overestimated)
  • Self-Funding: Optimised structure eliminates need for external capital
  • First-Mover: Largest BESS portfolio in Cyprus, regulatory tailwinds from CERA
