## 3. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION
Unless otherwise defined herein, capitalised terms shall have the meanings given to them in the Distribution Agreement.
For the purposes of this Agreement:
"Products"
 means battery energy storage systems, battery containers, power conversion systems, PCS skids, control panels, and related components supplied by the Seller.
"Quotation"
 means a written commercial and technical quotation issued by the Seller to the Buyer specifying scope, price, delivery terms, and payment milestones for a specific project.
"LTSA"
 means a Long-Term Service Agreement entered into between the Buyer and an end customer under which the Buyer provides preventive maintenance, corrective maintenance, monitoring, availability guarantees, and performance-related services. The Seller requires a valid LTSA to be in place as a precondition for SOH guarantees.
"EPC Agreement"
 means an Engineering, Procurement and Construction agreement entered into between the Buyer and an end customer for the turnkey delivery of a BESS project incorporating the Products.
"EMS"
 means any Energy Management System which, unless expressly supplied by the Seller under a separate written agreement, shall be deemed a third-party system.
"SOH"
 means State of Health, expressed as a percentage of the battery's original rated capacity.
"RTE"
 means Round-Trip Efficiency, being the ratio of energy output to energy input measured at the AC terminals of the complete system, including all cabling losses.
"PAC"
 means Provisional Acceptance Certificate, confirming completion of installation and commissioning.
"FAC"
 means Final Acceptance Certificate, confirming resolution of all punch list items.
"Downstream Commitments"
 means the Buyer's obligations to end customers under EPC Agreements and LTSAs, the material terms of which are summarised in Exhibit 1.

## B1. EQUIPMENT PERFORMANCE GUARANTEES

B1.1 **System Round-Trip Efficiency (RTE)**
 The Seller warrants that the Products, when installed and commissioned in accordance with this Agreement, shall achieve a full system AC-to-AC round-trip efficiency of not less than eighty-six point three two percent (≥ 86.32%), measured at the AC terminals of the complete system including all internal cabling losses.

B1.2 **PCS Efficiency**
 The Seller warrants that each Power Conversion System (PCS) unit supplied under this Agreement shall achieve an efficiency of not less than ninety-eight percent (≥ 98%) at rated power output.

B1.3 **Cycle Life**
 The Seller warrants that the battery cells supplied under this Agreement shall be capable of not less than seven thousand (7,000) full charge-discharge cycles at ninety percent (90%) depth of discharge, with end-of-life defined as seventy percent (70%) of original rated capacity, measured at a reference temperature of 25 ± 2°C.

B1.4 **Performance Guarantee Conditions**
 The performance guarantees in Sections B1.1 to B1.3 apply where:
 (a) the Products are installed in accordance with the Seller's technical requirements;
 (b) the Products are operated within the Seller's specified operating parameters;
 (c) preventive maintenance is performed in accordance with the OEM maintenance manual; and
 (d) no warranty exclusion event under Section 12.6 has occurred.

B1.5 **Remedy for Performance Shortfall**
 Where any performance guarantee in this Section B1 is not met and the shortfall is attributable to the Products (not the Buyer's EPC works, EMS, or third-party systems):
 (a) the Seller shall, at its own cost, investigate and diagnose the root cause within thirty (30) days of notification;
 (b) the Seller shall implement corrective measures (including firmware updates, component replacement, or system reconfiguration) within sixty (60) days of diagnosis; and

B2.1 **Guaranteed SOH Levels**

| Year | Guaranteed Minimum SOH |
|------|----------------------|
| 5 | ≥ 85% |
| 10 | ≥ 79.58% |
| 15 | ≥ 70% |

B2.2 **Reference Conditions**
 The SOH guarantees in Section B2.1 are based on:
 (a) Operating temperature within OEM range (0°C to +55°C charging; -20°C to +55°C discharging);
 (b) Depth of discharge not exceeding 90%;
 (c) Charge/discharge rate not exceeding 0.5C;
 (d) Maximum one (1) equivalent full cycle per day (annualised average);
 (e) Operation in accordance with the OEM User Manual.

B2.3 **SOH Measurement Protocol**
 (a) SOH shall be measured annually using an OEM-approved capacity test protocol.
