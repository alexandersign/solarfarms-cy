## Executive Summary

The 13 March 2026 draft incorporates **most** of Lighthief’s requested amendments, including the critical ones: subordination to the Distribution Agreement, CIF Limassol, installation responsibilities (5A), warranty commencement (6 months), coastal/C5 and warranty caps with manufacturing-defect carve-outs, Singapore law and SIAC arbitration with Cyprus carve-out, End-Customer Dependency, indemnities (9A), Pre-Shipment Inspection (18), Data Protection (19), Compliance/Anti-Bribery (20), Force Majeure cure/deposit return, and notice/English-prevails.

**Outstanding points:** (1) **Distribution Agreement date** — contract says 28 November 2025; Lighthief expects 22 February 2026; (2) **Payment split** — contract uses 30%/50%/10%/10% and different triggers vs Lighthief’s 25%/50%/20%/5%; (3) **Late payment interest** — contract uses EU Directive 2011/7/EU (ECB+8%) instead of Cyprus 2% per month; (4) **Force Majeure** — two formulations (90 days + negotiate vs 60 days + deposit return) need to be reconciled; (5) **Seller entity and bank** — Seller is Jiangsu Linyang (China) with Chinese bank details; confirm this is intended vs Solarfun Poland.

### 12. Other Material Points in 13.03 Draft

- **Seller identity:** Seller is **Jiangsu Linyang Energy Storage Technology Co., Ltd** (China), not Solarfun Poland. Bank details are **PING AN BANK** (China), not mBank Poland. 1A.3 still includes Solarfun Poland and Linyang as bound by the Distribution Agreement. Confirm that contracting and payment with Linyang China is intended.
- **Advance Payment Guarantee (9B.1):** Seller to provide APG for 100% of advance payments; Buyer must first call on Seller to repay with 14 days’ notice before calling the guarantee. Condition precedent: Buyer’s 6.1(a) advance not due until Seller delivers APG (9B.4).
- **Performance bond (9B.2):** Up to 5% of contract value; issued no later than one month before delivery.
- **Product liability insurance (9B.3):** EUR 5,000,000 per occurrence; AXA; certificate to Buyer within 14 days of signing and on renewal; Cyprus and BESS scope to be verified.
- **Liquidated damages (9C):** Delay LD structure (0.1% / 0.15% / 0.2% of LD value per day, 10% cap); exclusions (Buyer delay, FM, site readiness, owner/grid delays); availability LD 97% with 10 days maintenance allowance and 10% cap; total combined LD cap 10% of contract value.
- **Commissioning / PAC (Art. 10):** Scheduled commissioning and PAC dates; warranty start as per Art. 8; PAC triggers holdback payment; site readiness obligations on Buyer with extension without penalty for Seller if site not ready.

These are material commercial/legal terms; they do not contradict your requested amendments but should be reviewed for consistency with your commercial position and with the Distribution Agreement.

## Recommended Next Steps

1. **Align Distribution Agreement date** in the contract (22 February 2026 vs 28 November 2025).
2. **Confirm payment structure:** accept 30/50/10/10 and triggers or revert to 25/50/20/5 and agree exact wording (including 3-day post-FAT and 90-day long-stop for holdback).
3. **Clarify Force Majeure:** state clearly that the 60-day Buyer cancellation and 30-day deposit refund (less documented costs) apply as the primary mechanism, and that 90-day negotiation does not replace or delay it.
4. **Optional:** Reinstate Cyprus 2% per month compounded for late payment if required for Cyprus contracts; otherwise confirm acceptance of EU rate.
5. **Optional:** Add warranty exclusion (ii) “authorised representatives” and 5A.1 carve-out; add 9A.2 sentence that Buyer does not indemnify for Seller installation/commissioning.
6. **Confirm** contracting party and bank account (Linyang China vs Solarfun Poland) and that DA and guarantees (APG, performance bond, insurance) are consistent with this structure.
