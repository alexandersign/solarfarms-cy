### 2. PREAMBLE

2.1 The Company is engaged in the design, manufacture, supply, and support of battery energy storage systems and related equipment.

2.2 The Distributor is engaged in the development, engineering, integration, sales, and servicing of energy infrastructure projects in the Republic of Cyprus, including photovoltaic, hybrid, and battery energy storage systems.

2.3 The Distributor has established relationships with solar park owners, developers, and investors in Cyprus and has introduced, and will continue to introduce, clients and projects requiring battery energy storage and hybrid solutions.

2.4 The Company desires to appoint the Distributor, and the Distributor desires to act, as the Company's exclusive channel partner for battery energy storage and hybrid projects in Cyprus, subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement.

2.5 The Parties acknowledge that the Distributor's client relationships, project origination efforts, and market development activities are fundamental to this Agreement and require contractual protection.

**NOW, THEREFORE**, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements set forth herein, the Parties agree as follows.

### 4. APPOINTMENT AND STATUS

4.1 **Appointment:** The Company hereby appoints the Distributor as the exclusive Hybrid and Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) Channel Partner for the Territory.

4.2 **Scope:** The appointment covers all activities relating to the promotion, sale, integration, and delivery of Products in the Territory.

4.3 **Independent Contractor:** The relationship is that of independent contractors. Neither Party has authority to bind the other except as expressly provided herein.

---

### 5. EXCLUSIVITY AND MARKET PROTECTION

5.1 **Territorial Exclusivity:** During the Term, the Company shall not appoint any other distributor, agent, reseller, or representative for the Products in the Territory.

5.2 **Post-Termination Tail:** The Company shall not appoint any other distributor or representative in the Territory for twelve (12) months following termination, except where termination is due to an uncured material breach by the Distributor. This limitation complies with EU Vertical Block Exemption Regulation (VBER) and Cyprus Competition Law (Cap. 149).

5.3 **Dispute Buffer:** Where the Distributor disputes the validity of termination:
- (a) The exclusivity tail continues pending final resolution of the dispute;
- (b) The 12-month period commences only upon final resolution confirming valid termination;
- (c) If resolved in the Distributor's favour, the Agreement is deemed to have continued uninterrupted;
- (d) Any appointments made during a disputed termination are voidable at the Distributor's option.

5.4 **Effect of Breach:** If the Company breaches this Section 5, the Distributor is immediately released from any exclusivity obligations without prejudice to other remedies.

### 6. DISTRIBUTOR RIGHTS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

6.1 **Distributor Rights:**
- (a) Market, promote, and sell the Products in the Territory;
- (b) Coordinate client engagement, feasibility studies, and project origination;
- (c) Act as the primary interface with end customers;
- (d) Set final resale pricing at its discretion.

6.2 **Company Responsibilities:**
- (a) Manufacture and supply the Products;
- (b) Factory testing and quality assurance;
- (c) Provide OEM technical documentation;
- (d) OEM installation, commissioning, and supervision where included in project scope;
- (e) Remote monitoring services where applicable.

6.3 **Distributor Responsibilities:**
- (a) Client acquisition and project origination;
- (b) Local EPC coordination and integration;
- (c) Port-to-site transportation, customs clearance, import duties;
- (d) Civil works, cabling, grounding, foundations;
- (e) Compliance with local laws and grid authority requirements.

6.4 **EMS Exclusion:** Any Energy Management System is an external system unless expressly supplied by the Company under separate agreement. The Company has no responsibility for EMS performance beyond interface compatibility.

---

### 7. CLIENT PROTECTION

7.1 **Introduced Client Registry:** The Distributor shall maintain a register of Introduced Clients and Protected Projects. The Distributor shall notify the Company within five (5) Business Days of first substantive contact with any potential client.

7.2 **Automatic Protection:** A client becomes a Protected Client immediately upon the Distributor's written notification to the Company, irrespective of acknowledgement.

7.3 **Restriction on Engagement:** Without the Distributor's prior written consent, the Company Group shall not:
- (a) Contact or communicate with a Protected Client;
- (b) Negotiate, quote, tender, or propose any solution;
- (c) Enter into any agreement relating to a Protected Project.

7.4 **Deal Registration:** For strategic projects, the Distributor may submit a formal Deal Registration including project details, capacity, and timeline. Registered deals receive enhanced protection: the Company Group shall not engage for eighteen (18) months from registration, renewable for successive 12-month periods.

7.5 **Duration:** Protection applies for the Term and survives termination per Section 13.

---

### 8. ANTI-CIRCUMVENTION

8.1 **General Obligation:** The Company Group shall not circumvent, bypass, or exclude the Distributor from any commercial opportunity involving a Protected Client or Project.

8.2 **Circumvention Defined:** Includes any direct or indirect contact, negotiation, quotation, proposal, or commercial activity with a Protected Client without the Distributor's prior written consent.

8.3 **Affiliate Responsibility:** The Company is responsible for circumvention by any member of the Company Group.

8.4 **No Defence:** Circumvention occurs regardless of whether:
- (a) The Protected Client initiated contact;
- (b) Contact occurred via another affiliate or third party;
- (c) The Distributor was aware of such contact.

---

### 9. CONTRACTUAL LIQUIDATED DAMAGES

9.1 **Basis for Liquidated Damages:** In the event of a Circumventing Transaction, the Company shall pay the Distributor Contractual Liquidated Damages ("CLD"). The Parties acknowledge that the CLD amounts represent a genuine pre-estimate of the Distributor's loss, including lost commission, market development costs, opportunity costs, and reputational harm, which would be difficult to quantify precisely.

9.2 **CLD Structure:** Based on Total Contract Value:
- (a) Up to EUR 500,000: **EUR 75,000**;
- (b) EUR 500,001 – EUR 2,000,000: **15%** of Total Contract Value;
- (c) Over EUR 2,000,000: **20%** of Total Contract Value, capped at **EUR 500,000**.

9.3 **Minimum CLD:** The absolute minimum Contractual Liquidated Damages per violation is **EUR 50,000**.

9.4 **Cumulative Breaches:** Each Circumventing Transaction is a separate breach giving rise to separate Contractual Liquidated Damages.

9.5 **Payment Timeline:** Due within thirty (30) days of the Distributor's written notice specifying the breach.

9.6 **Determination of Value:** Where Total Contract Value is disputed:
- (a) The Company shall provide all relevant contracts and invoices within ten (10) Business Days;
- (b) Failure to provide documentation means the Distributor's calculation is deemed correct;
- (c) If Parties cannot agree within thirty (30) days, the matter is referred to an independent accountant.

9.7 **No Limitation:** Contractual Liquidated Damages are enforceable independently of any liability cap.

### 10. PRICING FRAMEWORK

10.1 **Quotation-Based Pricing:** All pricing is governed by project-specific written OEM quotations. No list pricing applies unless expressly agreed.

10.2 **Quotation Baseline Protection:**
- (a) The first quotation for a project is the Quotation Baseline;
- (b) Subsequent quotes cannot exceed the Baseline by more than 5% unless scope has materially changed or the Distributor consents.

10.3 **Incoterms:** Standard terms are **CIF Limassol, Cyprus** (Incoterms® 2020).

10.4 **Price Validity:** Each quotation remains valid for sixty (60) calendar days minimum.

10.5 **Price Adjustments:** Prices may be adjusted only where:
- (a) Raw material costs change by more than 10%, supported by verifiable indices; or
- (b) EUR/CNY fluctuation exceeds 8%.
Adjustment requires sixty (60) days' prior notice and applies only to new uncommitted orders.

10.6 **Resale Freedom:** The Distributor has full discretion on final resale pricing.

---

### 11. PAYMENT PRINCIPLES

11.1 **Default Milestone Structure:**
- (a) 30% Advance – within 7 days of contract effectiveness;
- (b) 60% Pre-shipment – prior to equipment dispatch;
- (c) 10% Commissioning – upon successful PAC.

11.1A **Group Order Milestone Structure (Confirmed per Linyang RFI Feb 2026):**
- (a) 25% Advance Payment;
- (b) 50% Ready to Ship;
- (c) 20% Arrival on Site;
- (d) 5% SAT Completion (subject to 5% Performance Bond).

11.2 **Payment Dependency:** Where Distributor payments depend on end-customer milestones, any delay in end-customer payment automatically extends the Distributor's deadline without penalty.

11.3 **No Default for Client Delay:** The Distributor is not in default, and no penalties apply, due solely to end-customer delays beyond the Distributor's control.

11.4 **Notification:** The Distributor shall promptly notify the Company of anticipated payment delays.

---

### 12. SALES TARGETS

12.1 **Year One:** No minimum sales targets, stock requirements, or purchase obligations apply.

12.2 **No Performance Termination:** The Company shall not terminate or restrict Distributor rights based on performance during Year One.

12.3 **Future Targets:** Targets for Years Two and Three apply only if mutually agreed in writing after joint performance review.

---

### 13. TERM AND TERMINATION

13.1 **Initial Term:** Three (3) years from the Effective Date.

13.2 **Termination for Breach:** During Years One and Two, termination requires:
- (a) Ninety (90) days' written notice; and
- (b) Full opportunity for the Distributor to cure.

13.3 **Termination Year Three:** From Year Three, either Party may terminate with one hundred eighty (180) days' written notice.

13.4 **Early Termination Compensation:** If the Company terminates without cause before Year Three end:
- (a) EUR 100,000;
- (b) 25% of Distributor's average gross profit over preceding 12 months;
- (c) All outstanding revenues; and
- (d) Any Contractual Liquidated Damages arising from Company contact with Introduced Clients within 24 months.

13.5 **Survival Period:** The following survive termination until the later of (i) 31 December 2027 or (ii) 24 months following termination:
- (a) Client and Project protections;
- (b) Anti-circumvention obligations;
- (c) Contractual Liquidated Damages provisions;
- (d) Confidentiality obligations;
- (e) Data protection and anti-bribery obligations.

13.6 **Auto-Extension:** The survival period is extended by 12 months for each calendar year in which the Distributor introduces at least one new Protected Client. **Maximum total survival: 5 years from termination.**

### 14. TECHNICAL SUPPORT

14.1 **OEM Support:** The Company provides remote diagnostics and OEM-level technical support.

14.2 **Response Times:**
- (a) Critical issues: initial response within **4 hours**;
- (b) Major issues: initial response within **24 hours**;
- (c) Minor issues: initial response within **72 hours**.

14.3 **On-Site Support:** Where required for critical issues, the Company provides OEM engineers within 5 Business Days.

### 15. TRAINING

15.1 **Initial Training:** Within sixty (60) days of the Effective Date, the Company provides training for up to five (5) Distributor personnel.

15.2 **Ongoing Training:** Quarterly webinars, annual refresher training, and product update training within 30 days of new releases.

15.3 **Certification:** Participants receive certification valid for 2 years. Renewal training at no additional cost.

### 16. WARRANTY AND LTSA

16.1 **Standard Warranty:** Five (5) years for Product defects in materials and workmanship.

16.2 **Exclusions:** Consumables, misuse, improper operation, unauthorised modifications, Force Majeure.

16.3 **LTSA Separation:** Availability guarantees, SOH guarantees, and performance guarantees are governed exclusively by separate Long-Term Service Agreements (LTSA) with end customers.

16.4 **Extended Warranty Cap:** The maximum extended warranty period available from the Company is fifteen (15) years from COD, subject to 1 cycle per day operating condition. Extended warranty beyond 15 years is not available. SOH guarantees confirmed: Year 5 ≥85%, Year 10 ≥79.58%, Year 15 ≥70%.

16.5 **SOH Remedy Scope:** SOH warranty remedy covers replacement components only. Service fees, on-site replacement labor, and logistics costs are not included in the OEM warranty and are the Distributor's responsibility.

16.6 **EMS Exclusion:** No warranty for third-party EMS beyond interface compatibility.

### 17. MARKETING

17.1 **Materials:** The Company provides product brochures, datasheets, specifications, case studies, and digital assets at no cost.

17.2 **Demo Equipment:** Subject to availability, demonstration equipment may be provided on loan. Insurance borne by Distributor; maintenance by Company.

### 18. FORCE MAJEURE

18.1 **Definition:** Events beyond reasonable control including war, natural disasters, pandemics, governmental actions, grid connection delays, and regulatory changes.

18.2 **Effect:** Obligations suspended and deadlines extended without penalty.

18.3 **Extended Force Majeure:** If persisting beyond 6 months, either Party may suspend (not terminate) performance.

18.4 **Survival:** Anti-circumvention and Contractual Liquidated Damages provisions remain in force during Force Majeure.

---

### 19. INSURANCE AND INDEMNIFICATION

19.1 **Company Insurance:** The Company shall maintain:
- (a) **Product Liability Insurance:** Minimum **EUR 5,000,000** per occurrence;
- (b) **Professional Indemnity Insurance:** Minimum **EUR 2,000,000** per occurrence;

with coverage valid for Cyprus and naming the Distributor as additional insured where required.

19.2 **Distributor Insurance:** The Distributor shall maintain:
- (a) **General Commercial Liability:** Minimum **EUR 1,000,000** per occurrence;
- (b) **Professional Indemnity:** Minimum **EUR 1,000,000** per occurrence for EPC services.

19.3 **Indemnification:** The Company indemnifies the Distributor against all claims arising from **manufacturing defects**. The Distributor is **NOT** liable for Product performance issues, liquidated damages, or losses attributable to manufacturing defects or software failures within the Company's Product stack.

### 20. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

20.1 **Governing Law:** This Agreement is governed by the laws of **Singapore**.

20.2 **Dispute Resolution:**

(a) **General Disputes:** All general disputes shall be resolved by arbitration under the **Singapore International Arbitration Centre (SIAC)** rules. The tribunal shall consist of **three (3) arbitrators**. The seat is Singapore. The language is English.

(b) **Cyprus Carve-Out:** Notwithstanding 20.2(a), disputes relating exclusively to **Contractual Liquidated Damages (Section 9), Client Protection (Section 7), or Anti-Circumvention (Section 8)** may, at the Distributor's sole discretion, be submitted exclusively to the **competent courts of the Republic of Cyprus**.

(c) **Interim Relief:** Either Party may apply to Cyprus courts for interim injunctions, freezing orders, asset preservation, or enforcement of arbitral awards without waiving arbitration.

20.3 **Official Language:**
- (a) The official language is English;
- (b) Translations into Chinese, Polish, Greek, or other languages have no legal effect;
- (c) No Party may reference translations in any proceeding.

20.4 **Counterparts:** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts and by electronic signature.
