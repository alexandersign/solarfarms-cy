8.4 Delay Liquidated Damages

(a) The Contractor shall achieve PAC by the date specified in the project schedule ("Target PAC Date"). The Target PAC Date shall be agreed in writing prior to commencement of works.

(b) If the Contractor fails to achieve PAC by the Target PAC Date due to causes attributable to the Contractor, the Contractor shall pay to the Client liquidated damages ("Delay LDs") calculated by reference to the contract value of the delayed or affected goods, not the total Contract Price, as follows:

(b)(i) Full Park Delay — Critical Path Equipment

Where a delay is caused by late delivery, defect, or failure of Critical Path Equipment (e.g., PCS, MV switchgear, transformer) and such delay prevents the commissioning of the entire park or a distinct section thereof:

  • The Delay LD base shall be the contract value of the entire park or section whose commissioning is prevented ("Affected Park Value");
  • Delay LDs shall accrue on the Affected Park Value at the rates set out in Section 8.4(c).

(b)(ii) Partial Delay — Non-Critical Path Equipment

Where a delay is caused by late delivery, defect, or failure of individual items of equipment that do not prevent the commissioning of the park as a whole (e.g., individual battery containers forming part of a larger park):

  • The Delay LD base shall be the contract value of the delayed goods only ("Delayed Goods Value");
  • Delay LDs shall accrue on the Delayed Goods Value at the rates set out in Section 8.4(c);
  • PAC for the unaffected portion of the park shall proceed independently.

(c) Delay LD Rates

PeriodDelay LD RateLD Basis
First 30 days of delay0.1% per dayContract value of delayed/affected goods
Days 31 to 600.15% per dayContract value of delayed/affected goods
Day 61 onwards0.2% per dayContract value of delayed/affected goods

(d) Delay LD Cap

The maximum aggregate Delay LDs payable in respect of any delayed goods shall be capped at ten percent (10%) of the contract value of the delayed or affected goods.

(e) Bankable Delay LD — Worked Examples

Example 1 — Full Park Delay (PCS): A park with Contract Price €2,000,000 cannot commission because the PCS (value €400,000) is delayed by 15 days. Because the PCS is Critical Path Equipment, the LD base is the Affected Park Value (€2,000,000). LD = 15 × 0.1% × €2,000,000 = €30,000. Cap = 10% × €2,000,000 = €200,000.

Example 2 — Partial Delay (Battery Container): A park has 4 battery containers (€150,000 each). One container is delayed 20 days. The other 3 containers + PCS commission on time. LD base is the Delayed Goods Value (€150,000). LD = 20 × 0.1% × €150,000 = €3,000. Cap = 10% × €150,000 = €15,000.

(f) Sole Remedy

Delay LDs shall be the Client's sole and exclusive remedy for delay, and the Client shall not be entitled to claim additional damages for delay beyond the Delay LDs.

(g) Exclusions

Delay LDs shall not apply to delays caused by:

  • (i) Force Majeure events;
  • (ii) Client-caused delays, including late site access, delayed approvals, or failure to meet Client obligations;
  • (iii) Grid authority or regulatory delays beyond the Contractor's control;
  • (iv) Variations or change orders requested by the Client.

(h) The Target PAC Date shall be extended day-for-day for any delays attributable to the causes set out in Section 8.4(g).

11. LTSA SEPARATION

11.1 Availability, performance guarantees, degradation limits, SOH guarantees, and penalties are not included in this Agreement but are available under the separately executed LTSA.

11.2 The standard service tier is Tier C (Availability Guarantee + SOH Guarantee), which includes:

  • (a) 97% annual availability guarantee with liquidated damages of €30 per day per MWh for each day below 97%;
  • (b) SOH guarantee against OEM degradation curve (≥85% Year 5, ≥79.58% Year 10, ≥70% Year 15);
  • (c) 24/7 remote monitoring, preventive and corrective maintenance;
  • (d) Local service team and spare parts warehouse in Cyprus;
  • (e) Module replacement and cell augmentation provisions backed by OEM warranty reserve.
SOH Capacity Shortfall LD (Bankable): Under the LTSA, if measured SOH falls below the guaranteed degradation curve by ≥1%, daily LDs accrue at €30 per Lost MWh per day after a 90-day remedy grace period. Lost Capacity = (Guaranteed SOH% − Actual SOH%) × System Capacity. Annual cap: 20% of LTSA fee. This rate is consistent with the availability LD rate, enabling banks to model a single LD metric across both availability and SOH performance.

11.3 The LTSA shall be executed as a separate agreement between the Parties, and the terms thereof shall be as set out in the LTSA template (Document Reference: LCY-LTSA-001).

12.3 Notification

The affected Party shall notify the other Party promptly of the Force Majeure event and provide reasonable information regarding its nature, expected duration, and steps being taken to mitigate its effects.

12.4 Mitigation

The affected Party shall use commercially reasonable efforts to mitigate the effects of the Force Majeure event and resume performance as soon as practicable.

12.5 Extended Force Majeure

If a Force Majeure event persists for more than six (6) months, either Party may terminate this Agreement upon thirty (30) days' written notice, without liability for such termination. In such event, the Client shall pay for all works completed and materials delivered up to the date of termination.
