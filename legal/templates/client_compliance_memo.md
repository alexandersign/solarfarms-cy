## 1. Legal Framework

### 1.1 Applicable Legislation

| Reference | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Gazette No. 5992** | Official Gazette of 16 January 2026 |
| **Special Development Order 2026** | Part III, Annex I |
| **Ν.180(I)/2025 & Ν.181(I)/2025** | Road and Building Regulations |

### 1.2 Why No New Permit is Required

Under the 2026 Order, a BESS installed within an already licensed RES (solar) power station:

✅ Does **not** require a new planning permit
✅ Is treated as a **supplementary installation**
✅ Requires only **formal declarations and drawings** (not re-licensing)
