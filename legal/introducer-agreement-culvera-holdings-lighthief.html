Recitals

WHEREAS:

(A) The Provider is in the business of supplying photovoltaic (PV) solar energy systems and Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS) for commercial and residential clients, including the design, procurement, installation, commissioning, grid connection, and ongoing maintenance of such systems in Cyprus.

(B) The Introducer has established relationships with commercial property owners, businesses, homeowners, and other prospective clients and is in a position to introduce such clients to the Provider for commercial and residential PV and BESS installation services.

(C) The Parties wish to formalise the terms under which the Introducer shall introduce clients to the Provider and the fees payable in respect of such introductions.

NOW IT IS AGREED as follows:

1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, the following terms shall have the meanings set out below:

“Introduced Client” means any natural person, company, partnership, trust, or other legal entity that has been communicated in writing by the Introducer to Alexander Papacosta at the Provider via email (alexander.papacosta@lighthief.com) or via WhatsApp to the same, where such communication identifies the prospective client and the potential opportunity for the Provider’s commercial or residential PV and/or BESS installation services. For the avoidance of doubt, an Introduced Client includes any subsidiary, affiliate, parent company, or associated entity of the introduced party, and any properties, buildings, or sites owned, controlled, or managed by such party.

“Services” means any and all products, systems, hardware, software, installations, and services supplied by the Provider within the scope defined in Clause 2 (commercial and residential PV and BESS installations), including but not limited to:

Category Description
PV SystemsPhotovoltaic solar panel systems for commercial rooftop, commercial ground-mount, and residential installations, including solar panels, inverters, mounting structures, and all ancillary equipment
BESS (Residential & Commercial)Battery Energy Storage Systems for residential and commercial applications, including battery units, hybrid inverters, battery management systems, and enclosures
Electrical WorksLV cabling, AC/DC wiring, switchboards, metering panels, earthing, protection devices, and all electrical installation works
Mounting & Civil WorksRoof-mount and ground-mount structures, foundations, cable management, and all associated structural and civil works
Grid ConnectionNet-metering or net-billing application support, EAC/DSO coordination, meter installation support, and grid compliance
CommissioningSystem testing, performance verification, handover documentation, and client training
Maintenance & SupportPreventive and corrective maintenance, system monitoring, cleaning, inverter servicing, warranty support, and spare parts supply

“Contract Value” means the total value of the installation contract and any associated service agreements entered into between the Provider and an Introduced Client for Services falling within the scope of this Agreement, excluding applicable VAT.

“Maintenance Revenue” means the total value of all recurring maintenance, service, monitoring, and support charges invoiced by the Provider to an Introduced Client under a maintenance or service agreement.

“Excluded Project” means any project that falls within one or more of the following categories: (i) PV park installations (licensed solar parks and utility-scale photovoltaic installations); (ii) BESS installations of 2 MWh or greater capacity; or (iii) any single project with a total contract value exceeding €500,000 (excluding VAT). Excluded Projects are not subject to the flat-rate commission in Clause 4 but are instead governed by the tiered commission structure in Schedule 3.

“Territory” means the Republic of Cyprus and such other jurisdictions as the Parties may agree in writing from time to time.

2. Appointment and Scope

2.1 The Provider hereby appoints the Introducer as a non-exclusive introducer of clients for the Provider’s commercial and residential PV and BESS installation services in the Territory.

2.2 The Introducer shall use reasonable efforts to identify and introduce prospective clients to the Provider. The Introducer shall not have authority to bind the Provider, negotiate prices, accept orders, or enter into contracts on behalf of the Provider.

2.3 The scope of introductions under this Agreement is limited to commercial and residential PV and BESS installations. This includes:

(a) Residential PV and BESS — rooftop solar panel systems, home battery storage, and combined PV+BESS systems for individual homeowners and residential properties;

(b) Commercial PV and BESS — rooftop and ground-mount solar systems, commercial battery storage (under 2 MWh), and combined PV+BESS systems for businesses, commercial properties, industrial buildings, and public buildings;

(c) Maintenance and support — ongoing maintenance, monitoring, and service agreements related to the above installations;

(d) Expansions and upgrades — any additional capacity, system upgrades, or new installations ordered by an Introduced Client or its affiliates within the scope of this Agreement.

2.4 Exclusions: The following are expressly excluded from the scope of this Agreement and do not fall within the Services covered herein:

(a) PV park installations — licensed solar parks and utility-scale photovoltaic installations;

(b) BESS installations ≥2 MWh — Battery Energy Storage Systems with a rated capacity of 2 MWh or greater (whether containerised, utility-scale, or commercial);

(c) Projects exceeding €500,000 — any single project with a total contract value (excluding VAT) exceeding five hundred thousand euros (€500,000).

Where the Introducer identifies a prospective client for an Excluded Project, the tiered commission structure set out in Schedule 3 (Excluded Project Framework) shall apply. The Parties shall execute a brief project-specific confirmation referencing Schedule 3 for each Excluded Project, but the standard terms and rates of Schedule 3 shall apply unless expressly varied in writing.

3. Identification of Introduced Clients

3.1 Form of Introduction: An introduction shall be deemed to have occurred when the Introducer communicates the details of a prospective client to Alexander Papacosta at the Provider via email (alexander.papacosta@lighthief.com) or via WhatsApp. The date of the earliest such written communication shall constitute the “Introduction Date”.

3.2 Minimum Introduction Requirements: A valid introduction must include at minimum: (i) the client’s full name and contact details (phone and/or email); (ii) the property or site address; (iii) the estimated project type (PV, BESS, or combined); and (iv) confirmation that the client has expressed interest in the Provider’s services. Introductions that do not meet these requirements may be rejected by the Provider without prejudice.

3.3 Acceptance and Rejection: The Provider shall, within twenty-one (21) days of receiving a valid introduction, confirm in writing whether it accepts or rejects the introduction. The Provider may reject an introduction in its absolute discretion, including where the prospective client is already in the Provider’s active sales pipeline. If the Provider does not respond within twenty-one (21) days, the Introducer may send a written reminder; if the Provider fails to respond within a further seven (7) days of such reminder, the introduction shall be deemed accepted.

3.4 Pipeline Exclusion: No commission is payable where the prospective client, or any entity directly connected thereto, is already in the Provider’s active sales pipeline at the Introduction Date. The Provider shall notify the Introducer within the acceptance period under Clause 3.3 if this exclusion applies. For the avoidance of doubt, the Provider’s determination of its own pipeline shall be conclusive.

3.5 Introduction Expiry: If an Introduced Client does not enter into a contract with the Provider within twelve (12) months of the Introduction Date, the introduction shall lapse automatically and no fee shall be payable. The Introducer may re-introduce the same client after the expiry of this period, and such re-introduction shall be treated as a new introduction subject to the acceptance process under Clause 3.3.

3.6 Affiliated Entities: An Introduced Client shall include entities directly and wholly owned or controlled by the introduced party (but not loosely associated companies, fellow shareholders, or business acquaintances). If an Introduced Client orders additional installations, capacity, or upgrades at any time during the term of this Agreement or during the Tail Period (as defined in Clause 7.3), such additional orders shall be treated as falling within the original introduction, provided they fall within the scope defined in Clause 2.3 or Schedule 3.

3.7 Register: The Parties shall maintain a written register of all Introduced Clients (accepted and rejected), which shall be updated as introductions are made. The register shall record the Introduction Date, the tier classification (where applicable), and the acceptance or rejection status.

3.8 Commencement and Prior Introductions: This Agreement applies only to introductions made on or after 16 March 2026 (the date of this Agreement). The Introducer confirms that no introductions have been made directly to the Provider under the terms of this Agreement prior to this date. The Parties acknowledge that one client was previously introduced to the Provider via a third party, for which a commission of 5% was separately agreed and has been paid in full. That prior arrangement is deemed fully settled and is outside the scope of this Agreement. The client in question is identified in Schedule 1 as a Settled Prior Introduction. No further commission is payable under this Agreement in respect of that client, unless the Introducer makes a new, direct introduction of the same client for a separate and distinct project after the date of this Agreement, in which case the standard terms of this Agreement shall apply to that new introduction only.

4. Introducer Fees

4.1 In consideration of each valid introduction that results in the Provider entering into a commercial agreement with an Introduced Client, the Provider shall pay the Introducer the fees set out in this Clause 4.

A. Standard Commission — Commercial & Residential Projects (10% Flat Rate)

4.2 For all projects falling within the scope of Clause 2.3 — commercial and residential PV installations, combined PV+BESS installations, and BESS installations with a rated capacity below 2 MWh — the following flat-rate commission applies:

Fee Component Rate Frequency Basis
Installation Contract Fee 10% One-off Of the total Contract Value invoiced to the Introduced Client for the installation, including all equipment, electrical works, mounting, commissioning, and grid connection.
Maintenance & Service Fee 10% Annually Of all Maintenance Revenue invoiced to the Introduced Client each year for system monitoring, cleaning, servicing, and support — payable for the full duration of the service agreement and any renewals thereof.
Expansion & Upgrade Fee 10% Per order Of the value of any additional capacity, panel or battery upgrades, inverter replacements, or system expansions ordered by an Introduced Client (or its affiliates) within the scope of this Agreement.

B. Excluded Projects — Tiered Commission (Schedule 3)

4.3 For all Excluded Projects as defined in Clause 2.4 — PV solar parks, BESS installations ≥2 MWh, and projects exceeding €500,000 — the tiered commission structure set out in Schedule 3 shall apply. The applicable tier rates range from 1.5% to 3.0% depending on the nature and quality of the introduction, with caps on larger contracts. Full details are in Schedule 3.

4.3.1 Project-Specific Commission: Notwithstanding Schedule 3, the Parties may agree a different commission rate for a specific Excluded Project by written confirmation via email between Alexander Papacosta (for the Provider) and the Introducer’s director, provided such agreement is made prior to the introduction of the client. Any such project-specific rate shall apply only to that individual project and shall not affect the rates applicable to any other project under this Agreement. In the absence of a project-specific written agreement, the standard tiered rates in Schedule 3 shall prevail.

C. General Fee Terms

4.4 Tier Determination: The applicable tier for Excluded Projects (Schedule 3) shall be determined by the Provider acting reasonably, based on the nature and quality of the introduction at the time of referral. The Provider’s tier determination shall be communicated in writing at the time of acceptance under Clause 3.3 and shall be final and binding absent manifest error.

4.5 Minimum Contract Value: No commission is payable on any individual contract with a value below €5,000 (excluding VAT).

4.6 All fees are exclusive of any applicable VAT or withholding tax. The Introducer is solely responsible for declaring and paying all taxes due on commission income received. The Provider may withhold tax if required by Cyprus law. The Introducer shall provide a valid tax identification number with its first invoice.

4.7 The Introducer shall issue an invoice to the Provider upon notification that payment has been received from the Introduced Client. The Provider shall pay the Introducer within thirty (30) days of receiving such invoice.

4.8 Payment Timing:

(a) Standard commission (Clause 4.2): payable pro rata as and when the Provider receives payment from the Introduced Client.

(b) Excluded Project commission (Schedule 3): payable in two instalments — 30% of the commission upon receipt of the client’s advance payment, and 70% upon receipt of the client’s final payment. Payment terms: net 30 days from invoice.

4.9 Cancellation and Clawback: No commission is payable if the Introduced Client cancels before making full payment. Where commission has been paid on an instalment basis and the client subsequently defaults, cancels, or fails to make final payment, the Introducer shall refund to the Provider any commission received in respect of client payments not ultimately collected, within thirty (30) days of written demand.

4.10 The Provider shall notify the Introducer in writing within fourteen (14) days of entering into any agreement with an Introduced Client and shall provide the Introducer with the Contract Value and Maintenance Revenue figures as applicable.

4.11 The Provider shall provide the Introducer with a quarterly statement showing all revenues received from Introduced Clients in the preceding quarter, the fees accrued, and the fees paid or outstanding.

5. Obligations of the Parties

The Introducer shall:

(a) act in good faith and use reasonable endeavours to introduce suitable prospective clients to the Provider;

(b) provide the Provider with such information about prospective clients as is reasonably available;

(c) not make any representations, warranties, or commitments on behalf of the Provider;

(d) comply with all applicable laws, including data protection regulations (GDPR), in the handling of personal data.

The Provider shall:

(a) deal fairly and professionally with all Introduced Clients;

(b) not circumvent the Introducer by dealing directly with an Introduced Client (or its affiliates) without paying the applicable fees;

(c) notify the Introducer promptly of any contract entered into with an Introduced Client;

(d) maintain accurate records of all transactions with Introduced Clients and make such records available for inspection by the Introducer upon reasonable notice.

6. Non-Circumvention

6.1 The Provider agrees that it shall not, directly or indirectly, circumvent, avoid, bypass, or attempt to bypass the Introducer with respect to any Introduced Client for the purpose of avoiding the payment of fees due under this Agreement.

6.2 If the Provider enters into any commercial arrangement with an Introduced Client (or any affiliate thereof) without paying the applicable fees, the Introducer shall be entitled to the full fee as if the arrangement had been properly notified, plus interest at 5% per annum above the European Central Bank base rate from the date the fee should have been paid.

6.3 The non-circumvention obligations in this Clause 6 shall survive termination of this Agreement and shall remain in force during the Tail Period defined in Clause 7.3.

7. Term and Termination

7.1 This Agreement shall commence on the date first written above and shall continue for an initial period of three (3) years (the “Initial Term”), unless terminated earlier in accordance with this Clause 7.

7.2 After the Initial Term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive periods of one (1) year each, unless either Party gives the other not less than ninety (90) days’ written notice of termination prior to the end of the then-current term.

7.3 Tail Period: Upon termination or expiry of this Agreement for any reason, the Provider shall continue to pay the Introducer all fees due under Clause 4 in respect of any Introduced Client for a period of three (3) years following the effective date of termination (the “Tail Period”). This includes fees on new contracts, renewals, expansions, and maintenance agreements entered into with Introduced Clients during the Tail Period, provided such contracts fall within the scope defined in Clause 2.3.

7.4 Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other Party: (a) commits a material breach that remains unremedied for thirty (30) days after written notice; (b) becomes insolvent or enters into liquidation; or (c) is convicted of fraud or dishonesty.

7.5 Termination shall not affect any accrued rights or obligations, including fees due in respect of introductions made prior to termination.

8. Confidentiality

8.1 Each Party shall keep confidential all information received from the other Party in connection with this Agreement, including but not limited to client lists, pricing, technical specifications, and business strategies.

8.2 The obligations of confidentiality shall not apply to information that: (a) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the receiving Party; (b) was already known to the receiving Party; (c) is required to be disclosed by law or regulatory authority; or (d) is disclosed to professional advisers under equivalent confidentiality obligations.

8.3 The confidentiality obligations in this Clause 8 shall survive termination of this Agreement for a period of five (5) years.

9. Data Protection

9.1 Each Party shall comply with all applicable data protection laws, including the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), in relation to any personal data shared under this Agreement.

9.2 Personal data shared for the purpose of client introductions shall be processed only for the purposes contemplated by this Agreement and shall not be used for any other purpose without the data subject’s consent.

10. General Provisions

10.1 Relationship: Nothing in this Agreement shall create a partnership, joint venture, agency, or employment relationship between the Parties. The Introducer is an independent contractor.

10.2 Entire Agreement: This Agreement (including the Schedules) constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties and supersedes all prior discussions, negotiations, and agreements relating to its subject matter.

10.3 Amendments: No amendment to this Agreement shall be effective unless made in writing and signed by both Parties.

10.4 Assignment: Neither Party may assign or transfer its rights or obligations under this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party.

10.5 Notices: All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and sent to the addresses or email addresses set out on the first page. Email shall constitute valid written notice.

10.6 Severability: If any provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

10.7 Waiver: No failure or delay by either Party in exercising any right shall constitute a waiver of that right.

10.8 Governing Law: This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Republic of Cyprus.

10.9 Dispute Resolution: Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall first be referred to mediation. If mediation fails within thirty (30) days, the dispute shall be submitted to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of Limassol, Cyprus.
