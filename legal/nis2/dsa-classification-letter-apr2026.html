2. Sector & Sub-Sector Classification

Based on our operational activities, we consider Lighthief Cyprus Ltd to fall within the following NIS2 Annex I categories:

Annex I SectorSub-SectorBasis
Energy Electricity — Operators of electricity storage facilities Owner and operator of 47 BESS (Battery Energy Storage System) installations across Cyprus with a combined capacity of 863.5 MWh, all grid-connected via EAC DSO
Energy Electricity — Electricity producers Co-located PV generation assets licensed under CERA/EAC; Lighthief Cyprus Ltd acts as EPC contractor and O&M operator for 47 licensed RES+BESS parks

We note that whilst our current employee headcount and annual turnover may place us below the standard thresholds for mandatory classification (≥250 employees / >€50m turnover for Essential Entities), we submit that the scale and grid-criticality of the portfolio — 863.5 MWh of grid-connected storage distributed across all five administrative districts of Cyprus — is likely to trigger designation regardless of size, as envisaged by Recital 61 and Article 2(2)(b) of the Directive.

We therefore request that the Authority confirm our classification and, if appropriate, formally designate us as either an Essential Entity or Important Entity under Law 60(I)/2025.

4. Relationship with Lighthief EU BESS Sp. z o.o.

We draw to the Authority's attention that Lighthief Cyprus Ltd operates in close technical coordination with Lighthief EU BESS Sp. z o.o. (EU headquarters, Republic of Poland), which is the provider of the Disperon EMS cloud platform used for management of all Cyprus sites. Lighthief EU BESS processes telemetry data from all 47 Cyprus parks and provides remote operational support.

A parallel NIS2 notification is being prepared in Poland by Lighthief EU BESS, and we are committed to maintaining a joint cybersecurity governance framework across both entities in compliance with Article 21 supply-chain security obligations. We would welcome guidance from the Authority on whether cross-entity coordination requirements apply under the Cyprus transposition law.

5. Compliance Preparedness

We wish to inform the Authority that Lighthief Cyprus Ltd has already initiated its NIS2 compliance programme:

  • An RFP for Cybersecurity Hardening & NIS2 Compliance Services (Ref: RFP-LH-CYBER-2026-002, dated 21 April 2026) has been issued to qualified vendors covering all 10 Article 21 measures;
  • Technical hardening of OT/SCADA systems is planned to commence Q2/Q3 2026, with full rollout completing by end of 2026;
  • An ISO/IEC 27001 and IEC 62443 certification project is included in the programme scope;
  • Incident response procedures including the 6-hour early warning requirement under Cyprus law are being developed and will be submitted to the Authority upon completion.
Good-Faith Notice: This letter is submitted proactively as evidence of good faith and cooperative engagement with the Authority prior to formal designation. We are committed to full compliance with Law 60(I)/2025 and welcome any guidance, clarification, or supervisory engagement the Authority deems appropriate.
