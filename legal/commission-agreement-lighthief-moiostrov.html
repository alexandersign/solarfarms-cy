1. Definitions

1.1 In this Agreement:

"Client Price" means the total price invoiced by the Company to an end client (or any intermediary on behalf of an end client) for the supply, installation, and commissioning of a BESS system at a Park, as set out in the EPC contract between the Company and such client, inclusive of all EPC works, project management, and margin.

"CIF Cost" means the landed cost of BESS equipment (battery containers, PCS/MV skids, and associated OEM hardware) delivered CIF Limassol, Cyprus, as invoiced by Linyang/Solarfun to the Company, including import duties and customs clearance.

"EPC Cost" means all costs incurred by the Company in delivering the BESS to the end client, excluding CIF Cost, including but not limited to: civil works, electrical works, EMS/SCADA (Voltus), lightning protection (DEHN), logistics, crane hire, cabling, commissioning, project management, insurance, and all other installation and compliance costs.

"Net Margin" means, for each Park or revenue stream:

Net Margin = Client Price − CIF Cost − EPC Cost

being the gross profit earned by the Company before corporate overheads, tax, and the Fee.

"Net Revenue" means, for revenue streams that do not involve CIF/EPC cost structures (e.g. management fees, O&M contracts, energy trading, fund carried interest, advisory fees), the total revenue received by the Company less any direct third-party costs specifically attributable to delivering that service.

"Origination Fee" means thirty percent (30%) of the Net Margin on the Existing Project, payable under Part A.

"Team Operations Fee" means thirty percent (30%) of the Net Margin or Net Revenue (as applicable) on all Company business, payable under Part B, and controlled and distributed by the Director at his discretion to the operational team.

"Park" means each individual solar park and its associated BESS installation, as listed in Schedule 1.

"Existing Project" means the entire portfolio of 51 Parks listed in Schedule 1, and any additional BESS parks added to the Company's EPC portfolio that were originated or introduced by Alexander Papacosta prior to the date of this Agreement.

"Future Business" means all revenue earned by the Company from any source whatsoever that does not form part of the Existing Project, including but not limited to: (i) fund-generated EPC contracts; (ii) fund management fees; (iii) operations and maintenance (O&M) contracts; (iv) energy trading or energy arbitrage revenue; (v) BESS supply margins on new projects; (vi) consultancy, advisory, or engineering fees; (vii) carried interest or performance fees from any fund vehicle; (viii) new client EPC contracts; and (ix) any other revenue stream of the Company.

"Director" means Alexander Papacosta (Cyprus ID: 1241318) in his capacity as a director of the Company and director of the Consultant.

"Protected Matter" has the meaning given in Clause 5.3.

PART A — ORIGINATION FEE (Existing Project — 51 BESS Parks)

2. Origination Fee — Existing Project

2.1 In consideration of the Consultant's role in personally originating, structuring, and securing the Existing Project, the Company shall pay the Consultant an Origination Fee equal to thirty percent (30%) of the Net Margin on each Park in the Existing Project.

2.2 The Origination Fee shall be calculated on a per-Park basis. The Company shall provide the Consultant with a detailed margin calculation for each Park within fourteen (14) days of issuing each invoice to the end client, showing: (i) Client Price invoiced; (ii) CIF Cost allocated; (iii) EPC Cost allocated; and (iv) resulting Net Margin and Origination Fee amount.

2.3 Payment Timing — Mirror Client Milestones: The Origination Fee shall become due and payable in instalments that mirror the payment milestones received from the end client. Whenever the Company receives a payment from an end client in respect of a Park, the proportionate share of the Origination Fee shall become due to the Consultant within fourteen (14) days of the Company receiving such payment.

For illustration, based on the standard EPC payment milestones:

Client Payment Milestone % of Contract Origination Fee Payment to Consultant
Advance (contract signing)30%30% of total Origination Fee due, payable within 14 days of receipt
Pre-shipment55%55% of total Origination Fee due, payable within 14 days of receipt
PAC (Provisional Acceptance)10%10% of total Origination Fee due, payable within 14 days of receipt
FAC (Final Acceptance / Retention)5%5% of total Origination Fee due, payable within 14 days of receipt
Total100%100% of Origination Fee paid pro rata with client receipts

2.4 If the actual payment milestones for any particular client differ from those shown above, the same principle shall apply: the Origination Fee shall be payable at the same proportionate rate and at the same time as the Company receives payment from the end client.

2.5 Rationale: The Origination Fee reflects the Consultant's direct and personal contribution in building the entire Existing Project pipeline from zero — including identifying all 51 parks, establishing the Linyang supply relationship, negotiating commercial terms with six separate client groups, and structuring the EPC model. Without these origination activities, the Existing Project would not exist. The 30% rate is commercially proportionate to the value created.

PART B — TEAM OPERATIONS FEE (All Company Business)

3. Team Operations Fee — All Revenue

3.1 In consideration of the Consultant's ongoing provision of team operational services (as described in Schedule 3), the Company shall pay the Consultant a Team Operations Fee equal to thirty percent (30%) of:

(a) the Net Margin on all EPC contracts and BESS supply agreements (whether Existing Project or Future Business); and

(b) the Net Revenue on all non-EPC revenue streams, including but not limited to: fund management fees, O&M contracts, energy trading, advisory fees, carried interest, and any other revenue earned by the Company.

3.2 Team Commission Pool: The Team Operations Fee is a team commission pool. The Director shall have sole and absolute discretion to determine how the Team Operations Fee is distributed among the operational team members engaged in the Company's projects. The Company shall have no right to direct, influence, or approve the internal distribution of the Team Operations Fee.

3.3 Relationship to Origination Fee: For the Existing Project, the Origination Fee (Part A) and the Team Operations Fee (Part B) are not cumulative. The 30% rate applies once to each revenue stream. For the Existing Project parks listed in Schedule 1, the fee payable is the Origination Fee under Part A. The Team Operations Fee under Part B applies to all Future Business and any revenue streams not covered by Part A.

3.4 Payment Timing: The Team Operations Fee shall become due and payable within fourteen (14) days of the Company receiving the corresponding revenue. For EPC contracts, payment shall mirror the client milestone structure described in Clause 2.3. For recurring revenue (management fees, O&M, subscriptions), payment shall be made within fourteen (14) days of each receipt.

3.6 Rationale: The Team Operations Fee reflects the market cost of retaining a high-performing operational team capable of delivering complex multi-site BESS EPC projects. The Director manages the team's composition, performance, and compensation from within this fee, eliminating the need for the Company to maintain a separate payroll, HR function, or performance management system for operational staff. The 30% rate is consistent with industry norms for outsourced project management and operational consultancy in the EPC sector.
