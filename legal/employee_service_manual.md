### 2.3 Warranty Start Date

⚠️ **CRITICAL:** The warranty period starts on the EARLIER of:
1. **Commissioning date** (when PAC is issued); OR
2. **Six (6) months after shipment** from Linyang factory

**ACTION REQUIRED:** Track shipment dates carefully. If commissioning is delayed beyond 6 months from shipment, warranty will have already started!

### 2.4 Coastal Installation Restrictions

⚠️ **CYPRUS STANDARD: C5-RATED ENCLOSURE**

For all Cyprus installations, **C5-rated corrosion-protected enclosures are specified as standard**. This is mandatory due to Cyprus's island geography and coastal climate.

**WITHOUT C5-Rated Enclosure (non-Cyprus or expressly waived):**

| Distance from Sea | Warranty Impact |
|-------------------|-----------------|
| > 5 km | Full 5-year warranty |
| 2-5 km | **REDUCED to 2 years** (PCS, transformer, switchgear) |
| < 2 km | **WARRANTY VOID** (PCS, transformer, switchgear) |

**WITH C5-Rated Enclosure:**

| Distance from Sea | Warranty Impact |
|-------------------|-----------------|
| > 500 m | Full 5-year warranty |
| < 500 m | **REDUCED to 2 years** (PCS, transformer, switchgear) |

**PRE-INSTALLATION CHECKLIST:**
- [ ] Measure straight-line distance from site to nearest coastline
- [ ] Document distance with GPS coordinates and map reference
- [ ] **CYPRUS STANDARD:** C5-rated enclosure is included in ALL Cyprus quotations
- [ ] If site < 500m from sea, note 2-year warranty reduction for PCS/transformer/switchgear
- [ ] Record coastal distance in Quotation and project file

### 2.5 Warranty Conditions

The warranty applies ONLY if:

1. **Full Payment Made:** All amounts due must be paid
2. **Proper Use:** Products used per user manuals and maintenance guidelines
3. **Authorised Personnel:** Only Linyang-certified personnel perform service
4. **No Modifications:** No unauthorised modifications or repairs
5. **Serial Numbers Intact:** All serial numbers must be visible and unaltered

### 2.6 Warranty Exclusions (DO NOT SUBMIT CLAIMS FOR)

The following are NOT covered by warranty:
- ❌ Consumables and wear parts
- ❌ Cosmetic defects not affecting function
- ❌ Damage from improper transport, handling, or storage
- ❌ Installation not following Linyang manual
- ❌ Unauthorised commissioning, repair, or modification
- ❌ Natural disasters (floods, earthquakes, etc.)
- ❌ Biological damage or industrial chemical exposure
- ❌ Salt spray/corrosion exceeding specs
- ❌ Operation exceeding voltage, wind, snow specifications
- ❌ Human damage or accidents
- ❌ Third-party equipment damage
- ❌ Force majeure events

### 2.7 Warranty Termination Events

⚠️ **WARRANTY IMMEDIATELY TERMINATED IF:**

1. **Serial number altered or unidentifiable**
   - Never remove, cover, or damage serial number labels
   - Photograph all serial numbers during installation

2. **Access refused for inspection**
   - Always ensure client provides access for Linyang inspectors

3. **Product relocated without approval**
   - Never agree to relocation without Linyang written approval
   - Linyang must inspect new site before relocation

### 2.8 Linyang Liability Cap

**Linyang's maximum liability = 10% of product value**

This means if warranty claims exceed 10% of what was paid for defective products, Linyang is not obligated to pay more. Plan LTSA pricing accordingly.

- O&M Manager reviews and approves submission

**Step 2: Submit to Linyang (Within 24 Hours)**
- Email to Linyang official warranty contact
- Include all required documentation
- Request confirmation receipt

**Step 3: Linyang Response (48 Hours)**
- Linyang will respond within 48 hours (excluding weekends)
- Response will be: Accept, Reject, or Request More Information

**Step 4: Track Resolution**
- Log claim in warranty tracker
- Follow up if no response within 48 hours
- Document all communications

## 6. MAINTENANCE PROTOCOLS

### 6.1 OEM Safety Precautions (CRITICAL)

**Reference:** Linyang Power Atlantic 5MWh User Manual V2.0 & Maintenance Manual

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
⚠️ ELECTRICAL SAFETY - DANGER
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

| Hazard | Precaution |
|--------|------------|
| **LETHAL VOLTAGES** | Lethal voltages present inside device at all times |
| **GRID CONNECTION** | Touching contact points/terminals may cause electric shock |
| **RESIDUAL ENERGY** | Wait 5 minutes after power disconnect before any work |
| **VOLTAGE CHECK** | Always use standard voltmeter to confirm zero voltage |

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
⚠️ WARRANTY-VOIDING CONDITIONS - CRITICAL
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

**The following conditions VOID the warranty immediately:**

| Condition | Threshold | Action Required |
|-----------|-----------|-----------------|
| **Low Cell Voltage** | < 2.8V for 120 consecutive hours | Charge immediately |
| **Zero SOC** | 0% for 120 consecutive hours | Charge immediately |
| **Under-Voltage** | Cell ≤ 2.5V (any occurrence) | Escalate to OEM |

**Monitoring Alert Configuration:**
- Set CRITICAL alert at cell voltage < 2.9V
- Set CRITICAL alert at SOC < 5%
- Never leave BESS in low SOC for extended periods
- Before planned shutdown, charge to 30-50% SOC

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
⚠️ PERSONNEL REQUIREMENTS (Per OEM User Manual)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

All personnel must:
- Be qualified professional electricians per local regulations
- Know electronic, electrical wiring and mechanical expertise
- Be familiar with ESS composition and working principles
- Have received professional training on ESS installation/commissioning
- Be able to respond to hazards or emergencies
- Be familiar with Cyprus/EU regulations and standards

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
⚠️ MAINTENANCE PRECAUTIONS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

| Precaution | Requirement |
|------------|-------------|
| **Weather Conditions** | Do NOT open container door in rainy, humid, or windy conditions |
| **High Humidity** | Avoid opening door in rain, snow, or fog conditions |
| **Door Seals** | Ensure seals do not curl when closing door |
| **MSD Disconnection** | Disconnect ALL MSDs in rack before maintenance |
| **Pack Unplugging** | Ensure plug locking structure is UNLOCKED before unplugging power cord |
| **Scope Limitation** | Do NOT perform maintenance beyond this manual – contact Linyang |
| **Snow Clearance** | Clear snow from equipment top and surrounding area promptly |
| **Dehumidification** | In fair weather, open container door to dehumidify equipment |

⚠️ **ELECTRIC SHOCK HAZARD:** Do not perform any maintenance operations beyond this manual. Contact Linyang customer service if required.

## 12. CYPRUS REGULATORY COMPLIANCE

### 12.1 Regulatory Framework Summary

**KEY PRINCIPLE:** A BESS in Cyprus does NOT require a new building permit when:
- Installed within an already licensed RES (solar) power station
- All safety, fire, access, and environmental conditions are met
- A Responsible Engineer Declaration (Υπεύθυνη Δήλωση Μελετητή) is submitted

⚠️ **If conditions are violated, exemption is automatically invalidated.**
