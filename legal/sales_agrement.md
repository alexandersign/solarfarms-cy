2. BACKGROUND AND CONTRACTUAL CONTEXT

2.1 The Seller is engaged in the manufacture and supply of battery energy storage systems and related equipment.

2.2 The Buyer is engaged in the development, integration, and delivery of photovoltaic, hybrid, and battery energy storage projects in the Republic of Cyprus and elsewhere.

2.3 **Subordination to Distribution Agreement:** This Sales Agreement is entered into pursuant to, and shall be governed by the terms of, the Distribution Agreement between the Parties (and/or their Affiliates) dated November 28, 2025 ("Distribution Agreement").

2.4 This Agreement governs the sale and supply of Products by the Seller to the Buyer on a project-by-project basis, pursuant to written quotations issued by the Seller and accepted by the Buyer.

2.5 **Conflict of Terms:** In the event of any conflict or inconsistency between this Sales Agreement and the Distribution Agreement regarding exclusivity, client protection, anti-circumvention, liability, penalty fees, or survival provisions, the Distribution Agreement shall prevail.

2.6 **Affiliate Coverage:** For the avoidance of doubt, references to the Seller in this Agreement include Solarfun Renewable Energy Poland sp. z o.o., Jiangsu Linyang Energy Storage Technology Co., Ltd, and their respective Affiliates, all of whom are bound by the Distribution Agreement.

3. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION
Unless otherwise defined herein, capitalised terms shall have the meanings given to them in the Distribution Agreement.
For the purposes of this Agreement:
“Products”
 means battery energy storage systems, battery containers, power conversion systems, PCS skids, control panels, and related components supplied by the Seller.
“Quotation”
 means a written commercial and technical quotation issued by the Seller to the Buyer specifying scope, price, delivery terms, and payment milestones for a specific project.
“LTSA”
 means a Long-Term Service Agreement entered into separately with an end customer governing extended warranty, availability guarantees, state-of-health guarantees, performance guarantees, and any related penalties.
“EMS”
 means any Energy Management System which, unless expressly supplied by the Seller under a separate written agreement, shall be deemed a third-party system.

4. SCOPE OF SUPPLY AND COMMERCIAL BASIS
4.1 The Seller shall supply the Products strictly in accordance with the applicable Quotation accepted in writing by the Buyer.
4.2 No obligation to supply Products shall arise unless and until a Quotation is accepted by the Buyer.
4.3 Each accepted Quotation shall form an integral part of this Agreement and shall define the technical scope, pricing, delivery schedule, and payment milestones for the relevant project.
4.4 Unless otherwise stated in the applicable Quotation, supply of Products shall be CIF Limassol, Cyprus (Incoterms® 2020).
4.5 This Agreement does not impose any minimum purchase volume, minimum order value, or exclusivity-based purchasing obligation on the Buyer.

5. ENERGY MANAGEMENT SYSTEM (EMS)
5.1 EMS is not supplied by the Seller unless expressly stated in the applicable Quotation or in a separate written agreement.
5.2 Any base EMS interface cost included in the Buyer’s EPC pricing does not constitute EMS supply, licensing, or performance responsibility by the Seller.
5.3 Advanced EMS functionality, customisation, licences, analytics, or integrations shall be contracted separately by the end customer and are outside the scope of this Agreement.
5.4 The Seller’s responsibility in relation to EMS is strictly limited to interface compatibility between the Products and the EMS.
6. PRICING
6.1 Quotation-Based Pricing
 All prices under this Agreement shall be governed exclusively by written Quotations issued by the Seller and accepted by the Buyer. No list pricing shall apply unless expressly agreed in writing.
6.2 Scope-Based Pricing
 Each Quotation shall specify the scope of supply, technical configuration, pricing, delivery basis, and payment milestones applicable to the relevant project. Any change to scope, configuration, capacity, or specifications shall require a revised Quotation.
6.3 Price Validity
 Unless otherwise stated in the applicable Quotation, prices shall remain valid for sixty (60) calendar days from the date of issuance.
6.4 Price Adjustments
 Prices may be adjusted only if:
 (a) raw material costs change by more than ten percent (10%), supported by verifiable public indices; or
 (b) currency exchange fluctuation between EUR and CNY exceeds eight percent (8%).
Any price adjustment shall require not less than sixty (60) days’ prior written notice and shall apply only to new orders not yet contractually committed.
6.5 No Unilateral Deviations
 Additional configurations or features requested after quotation acceptance shall be quoted separately and shall not materially deviate from the original quotation unless the project scope is modified.

7. PAYMENT TERMS AND MILESTONES
7.1 Milestone-Based Payment Structure
 Unless otherwise stated in the applicable Quotation, payment for Products shall be made as follows:
 (a) thirty percent (30%) advance payment by bank transfer (T/T) payable within seven (7) days of contract effectiveness or purchase order signing;
 (b) sixty percent (60%) payment by T/T payable prior to shipment of the Products from the manufacturing facility;
 (c) ten percent (10%) final payment by T/T payable upon successful installation, commissioning, and provisional acceptance of the Products on site.
7.2 End-Customer Dependency
 Where Buyer payments are contractually linked to end-customer milestone payments, any delay in end-customer payment shall automatically extend the Buyer’s corresponding payment deadline without penalty.
7.3 No Default for Client Delay
 The Buyer shall not be deemed in default, nor shall shipment suspension, interest, penalties, or termination rights arise, due solely to delays in end-customer payment beyond the Buyer’s reasonable control.
7.4 Notification Obligation
 The Buyer shall promptly notify the Seller of any anticipated payment delay and provide reasonable supporting details.

8. TAXES, DUTIES, AND VAT
8.1 Pricing Basis
 All prices under this Agreement and under any Quotation are exclusive of value-added tax (VAT), unless expressly stated otherwise.
8.2 Import Duties and Customs
 Unless otherwise stated in the applicable Quotation, the Buyer shall be responsible for import duties, customs clearance, and local taxes in Cyprus. Where the Quotation is expressly stated as turnkey EPC, such costs shall be included in the contract price.
8.3 VAT Treatment
 VAT shall be charged to the Buyer in accordance with applicable Cyprus law. The Buyer shall be entitled to recover VAT from the end customer where contractually agreed.
8.4 Regulatory Changes
 In the event of changes in customs regimes, import duties, mandatory grid requirements, or applicable laws affecting the scope or cost of supply, the Parties shall negotiate in good faith any necessary price or schedule adjustments.
9. DELIVERY AND INSTALLATION
9.1 Delivery Basis
 Unless otherwise stated in the applicable Quotation, delivery of the Products shall be CIF Limassol, Cyprus (Incoterms® 2020). Title and risk transfer shall be governed by Section 11.
9.2 Delivery Schedule
 The Seller shall use commercially reasonable efforts to make the Products ready for shipment in accordance with the delivery schedule stated in the applicable Quotation, typically within approximately ninety (90) days from receipt of the advance payment and contract effectiveness, subject to Force Majeure and Buyer compliance with contractual obligations.
9.3 Inland Transport and Site Delivery
 Unless expressly included in the applicable Quotation, inland transport from port to site, offloading, and site logistics shall be the responsibility of the Buyer.
9.4 Installation and Commissioning Responsibilities

 (a) **Seller (Linyang) Scope — Installation and Commissioning:**
  The Seller shall be responsible for:
  (i) Equipment installation and assembly by factory-trained technicians;
  (ii) OEM commissioning and system configuration;
  (iii) Connection of battery modules, PCS, BMS, and cooling systems;
  (iv) OEM testing protocols and verification;
  (v) Installation supervision and quality assurance;
  (vi) Installation workmanship warranty (5 years from PAC).

 (b) **Buyer (Lighthief) Scope — EPC Works:**
  The Buyer shall be responsible for:
  (i) Civil works, foundations, and site preparation;
  (ii) AC/DC cabling from BESS to grid connection point;
  (iii) Grounding and earthing systems;
  (iv) Auxiliary electrical systems;
  (v) Site access and security;
  (vi) Coordination with local authorities and grid operator.

 (c) **No Cross-Liability:**
  (i) The Seller is NOT liable for defects in the Buyer's EPC works;
  (ii) The Buyer is NOT liable for defects in the Seller's installation and commissioning.

9.5 Site Readiness
 The Buyer shall ensure that the site is ready for installation in accordance with the Seller's technical requirements. The Buyer shall provide:
 (a) Completed foundations and civil works;
 (b) AC connection point ready for termination;
 (c) Grounding system installed;
 (d) Clear access for equipment and personnel;
 (e) Secure storage facilities if required.
 Delays arising from site unavailability or non-compliance shall automatically extend the installation and commissioning schedule without penalty to the Seller.

10. COMMISSIONING AND PROVISIONAL ACCEPTANCE
10.1 Commissioning
 Commissioning shall be performed in accordance with the Seller’s standard commissioning procedures and technical documentation, following completion of installation and site readiness confirmation.
10.2 Provisional Acceptance
 Upon successful completion of commissioning and demonstration that the Products operate in accordance with agreed specifications, the Parties shall execute a Provisional Acceptance Certificate (PAC).
10.3 Effect of Provisional Acceptance
 Provisional Acceptance shall:
 (a) confirm completion of installation and commissioning;
 (b) trigger the Buyer’s obligation to make the final ten percent (10%) payment in accordance with Section 7; and
 (c) mark the commencement of the base warranty period under Section 16 of the Distribution Agreement, unless otherwise stated in the applicable Quotation.
10.4 Minor Defects
 Minor defects that do not materially affect system operation shall not prevent Provisional Acceptance, provided such defects are recorded in a punch list and remedied within a reasonable time.
10.5 EMS Interface Confirmation
 Commissioning and acceptance relate solely to the Products and their interface compatibility with the EMS. EMS performance, analytics, or optimisation are expressly excluded unless separately contracted.

11. RISK TRANSFER, TITLE, AND FINAL ACCEPTANCE
11.1 Risk Transfer
 Unless otherwise stated in the applicable Quotation, risk of loss or damage to the Products shall transfer from the Seller to the Buyer upon delivery in accordance with CIF Limassol (Incoterms® 2020).
11.2 Title Transfer
 Title to the Products shall pass to the Buyer upon receipt by the Seller of full payment for the Products, unless otherwise agreed in writing.
11.3 Final Acceptance
 Final acceptance, if applicable, shall occur upon resolution of any punch list items identified at Provisional Acceptance. Final acceptance shall not delay or condition payment obligations unless expressly agreed in writing.
11.4 LTSA Independence
 Availability guarantees, performance guarantees, state-of-health guarantees, liquidated damages, or service-level commitments shall apply only if an LTSA is executed with the end customer and shall be governed exclusively by the LTSA. No such guarantees are implied under this Agreement.
11.5 No Set-Off
 The Buyer shall not withhold, defer, or set off payments under this Agreement due to performance or service matters governed by an LTSA or by third-party EMS arrangements.
12. WARRANTY
12.1 Base Manufacturer Warranty
 Unless otherwise expressly stated in the applicable Quotation, the Seller warrants that the Products supplied under this Agreement shall be free from defects in materials and workmanship under normal operating conditions for a period of five (5) years.
12.2 Warranty Commencement
 The warranty period shall commence on the earlier of:
 (a) the date of commissioning (PAC); or
 (b) six (6) months after the shipment date of the Products from the Seller's factory.
12.3 Standard Warranty Coverage by Product
 Unless otherwise specified in the applicable Quotation:
 (a) Power Conversion System (PCS): 5 years;
 (b) Battery Container: 5 years;
 (c) Auxiliary Equipment (HVAC, Fire Suppression): 5 years;
 (d) Transformer and Medium Voltage Switchgear: 5 years;
 (e) DC/AC Container/Enclosure: 5 years;
 (f) Uninterruptible Power Supply (UPS): 5 years.
12.4 Coastal Installation Restrictions
 IMPORTANT: The following restrictions apply to installations near coastal areas:
 (a) **Cyprus Standard - C5 Enclosure:** For all installations in the Republic of Cyprus, C5-rated corrosion-protected enclosures shall be specified as standard in all Quotations. This is due to Cyprus's island geography and coastal climate.
 (b) For systems WITHOUT C5-rated enclosure (non-Cyprus or where expressly waived):
  (i) Installations within 2-5 km straight-line distance from the sea: warranty for PCS, transformer, and switchgear reduced to 2 years;
  (ii) Installations within 2 km straight-line distance from the sea: WARRANTY VOID for PCS, transformer, and switchgear.
 (c) For systems WITH C5-rated enclosure (Cyprus standard):
  (i) Installations more than 500 metres from the sea: full 5-year warranty applies;
  (ii) Installations within 500 metres straight-line distance from the sea: warranty for PCS, transformer, and switchgear reduced to 2 years.
 (d) The Buyer shall confirm the installation site distance from the sea in each project Quotation request. For Cyprus, C5 enclosure is presumed unless expressly stated otherwise.
12.5 Warranty Scope
 The warranty under Section 12.1 is limited to repair or replacement, at the Seller's option, of defective Products or components. Replacement parts shall be new or equivalent to new in performance. Replaced parts shall remain the property of the Seller.
12.6 Warranty Exclusions
 The warranty does not apply to:
 (a) consumables, wear parts, and reasonable wear and tear;
 (b) cosmetic defects that do not affect normal functioning;
 (c) defects arising from misuse, negligence, improper operation, or failure to follow Seller instructions;
 (d) incorrect transportation, handling, or storage by the Buyer;
 (e) improper installation by third parties (not applicable where installation is performed by Seller under Section 9.4(a));
 (f) commissioning, repair, or modification performed without Seller's written authorisation;
 (g) unauthorised modifications or repairs;
 (h) defects caused by third-party equipment, including any EMS not supplied by the Seller;
 (i) damage caused by natural disasters, biological activity, or industrial chemicals;
 (j) salt spray or corrosion exceeding design specifications;
 (k) operation exceeding voltage, wind, snow load, or other operating specifications;
 (l) human damage, accidents, or third-party actions;
 (m) damage caused by Force Majeure events; or
 (n) normal degradation consistent with Product specifications.
12.7 Warranty Termination
 The warranty shall be deemed terminated if:
 (a) the serial number of any Product is altered or unidentifiable;
 (b) the Buyer refuses to provide conditions for inspection, testing, or repair; or
 (c) the Product is relocated without prior inspection and written approval by the Seller.
12.8 Warranty Claim Procedure
 (a) The Buyer shall submit warranty claims to the Seller immediately upon discovery of a defect, via official contact information, including:
  (i) Product serial number;
  (ii) Proof of purchase and delivery;
  (iii) Installation serial number;
  (iv) Operation and maintenance documentation or logs;
  (v) Detailed defect description with photos or videos.
 (b) The Seller shall respond to warranty claims within forty-eight (48) hours (excluding weekends and public holidays).
 (c) The Seller reserves the exclusive right to determine the cause and nature of any product defect.
12.9 Payment Requirement
 The warranty shall apply only if the Buyer has fully paid all amounts due under this Agreement. The Seller reserves the right to require advance deposit prior to warranty service if any payments are overdue.
12.10 EMS Exclusion
 The Seller provides no warranty, guarantee, or representation regarding the performance, availability, analytics, optimisation, or functionality of any EMS, except that the Products shall be interface-compatible with the EMS as specified in the applicable Quotation.
12.11 Liability Cap for Warranty
 (a) Subject to Section 12.11(b), the Seller's aggregate liability for warranty services and related costs shall not exceed ten percent (10%) of the total payment received for the defective Products. This limitation is in addition to the general liability cap in Section 13.
 (b) **Manufacturing Defect Carve-Out:** Notwithstanding Section 12.11(a), the Seller's aggregate liability for warranty services shall cover the full cost of repair or replacement of defective parts, including shipping to CIF Limassol, where the defect is confirmed as a manufacturing fault under Section 14.1. The 10% cap shall not apply to defects confirmed as manufacturing faults.
12.12 Warranty Dispute Resolution
 In the event of a dispute regarding liability for equipment defects, the Parties shall mutually agree to engage a third-party testing agency. Prior to testing, the Parties shall agree on the test protocol and defect criteria. If the test demonstrates the defect is not attributable to the Products, all testing costs shall be borne by the Buyer.
12.13 LTSA-Based Enhancements
 Any extension of warranty duration, availability guarantees, state-of-health guarantees, performance guarantees, or related penalties shall apply only if an LTSA is executed with the end customer and shall be governed exclusively by such LTSA. No such guarantees are provided under this Agreement.

13. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
13.1 Exclusion of Consequential Damages
 To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, the Seller shall not be liable for any indirect, incidental, special, consequential, or punitive damages, including loss of profit, loss of revenue, loss of use, or business interruption, arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
13.2 Liability Cap
 Except for liability arising from wilful misconduct or fraud, the Seller’s total aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed one hundred percent (100%) of the total price paid by the Buyer to the Seller for the Products giving rise to the claim.
13.3 Carve-Outs
 The limitations set forth in this Section shall not apply to:
 (a) Seller obligations expressly stated to survive termination; or
 (b) indemnification obligations under Section 14.
13.4 No Double Recovery
 The Buyer shall not be entitled to recover damages more than once for the same loss under this Agreement, any LTSA, or any other agreement with the Seller.

14. INDEMNITIES AND REMEDIES
14.1 Product Defect Indemnity
 The Seller shall indemnify and hold harmless the Buyer against third-party claims arising directly from manufacturing defects in the Products, provided such claims are not caused by Buyer acts or omissions. The Buyer shall NOT be liable for Product performance issues, liquidated damages, or losses attributable to manufacturing defects or software failures within the Seller's Product stack.
14.2 Buyer Indemnity
 The Buyer shall indemnify the Seller against claims arising from:
 (a) EPC works performed by or on behalf of the Buyer, including civil works, foundations, cabling, and grounding;
 (b) Site conditions not meeting Seller's technical requirements;
 (c) Operation of the Products in breach of Seller instructions; or
 (d) Third-party systems, including EMS, not supplied by the Seller.
 **Note:** The Buyer does NOT indemnify for claims arising from the Seller's installation and commissioning works, which are the Seller's responsibility under Section 9.4(a).
14.3 Indemnification Procedure
 The indemnified Party shall promptly notify the indemnifying Party of any claim and provide reasonable cooperation. The indemnifying Party shall have control over the defence and settlement of the claim, subject to the indemnified Party’s reasonable consent.
14.4 Exclusive Remedies
 Except as expressly stated in this Agreement, the remedies set forth herein shall be the Buyer’s exclusive remedies against the Seller for claims arising out of or in connection with the Products.
15. FORCE MAJEURE
15.1 Force Majeure Events
 Neither Party shall be liable for any delay or failure to perform its obligations under this Agreement (other than payment obligations already due) if such delay or failure results from events beyond its reasonable control, including but not limited to acts of God, war, terrorism, riots, fires, floods, earthquakes, pandemics, epidemics, governmental actions, changes in laws or regulations, grid connection delays, port congestion, transportation disruptions, labour disputes, shortages of materials, or delays in financing beyond the reasonable control of the affected Party.
15.2 Notification and Mitigation
 The affected Party shall promptly notify the other Party of the Force Majeure event and shall use commercially reasonable efforts to mitigate the effects of such event and resume performance as soon as practicable.
15.3 Suspension of Obligations
 During the continuance of a Force Majeure event, the affected obligations shall be suspended and the relevant deadlines extended for the duration of the Force Majeure event.
15.4 Extended Force Majeure
 If a Force Majeure event continues for a period exceeding six (6) consecutive months, either Party may suspend performance until the event is resolved.

15.5 **Buyer Cure Right and Deposit Return**
 (a) If a Force Majeure event affecting the Seller's delivery obligations continues for a period exceeding sixty (60) days, the Buyer may, upon written notice, cancel the affected order without liability.
 (b) Upon such cancellation, the Seller shall refund the Buyer's advance payment (30% deposit) within thirty (30) days, less any documented costs for materials already procured and non-recoverable.
 (c) The Buyer's right to cancel under this Section 15.5 is without prejudice to any other rights or remedies available under this Agreement or applicable law.
 (d) The Seller shall provide reasonable evidence of claimed non-recoverable costs within fourteen (14) days of the cancellation notice.

15.6 **No Abuse of Force Majeure**
 Force Majeure events shall not be invoked for normal commercial risks including factory scheduling, routine material procurement, or internal production delays not caused by external events.
