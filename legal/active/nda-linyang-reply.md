Dear Kamil,

Thank you for sharing the Non-Disclosure Agreement. We have reviewed the document and are happy to proceed with signing, subject to a few clarifications and amendments that we believe are reasonable and in the interest of both parties.

**1. Effective Date Correction**

The Duration clause (Section 6) references an Effective Date of "…….2025", while the preamble references "……… 2026". We would ask that this is aligned to reflect the correct date of execution in 2026.

**2. Definition of "Business Purpose"**

The Agreement references "Business Purpose" in several clauses (Sections 3, 6, 7, and 8) but does not define it. To avoid ambiguity, we suggest adding the following definition to Section 1:

> *"Business Purpose means the evaluation, negotiation, and potential execution of agreements relating to the supply, distribution, and deployment of battery energy storage systems (BESS) and related equipment, components, and services in the Republic of Cyprus and any other territory agreed between the Parties."*

**3. Dispute Resolution (Section 9)**

The current clause submits all disputes to arbitration at the Court of Arbitration of the Polish Chamber of Commerce in Warsaw, with a waiver of the right to appeal on legal issues.

Given that our business relationship is governed by a Distribution Agreement with its own dispute resolution provisions, and that the practical context of this NDA relates to activities in Cyprus, we would propose one of the following alternatives:

- **Option A (preferred):** Arbitration under the ICC (International Chamber of Commerce) Rules, with the seat of arbitration in a neutral jurisdiction (e.g., London or Vienna), conducted in English.
- **Option B:** Arbitration at the Cyprus Chamber of Commerce, consistent with the territory in which the commercial relationship operates.
- **Option C:** Retain Warsaw arbitration but remove the waiver of the right to appeal on legal issues, to preserve both parties' access to judicial oversight.

We are flexible on this point and happy to discuss what works best for both sides.

**4. Return or Destruction of Confidential Information**

The Agreement does not include a provision for the return or destruction of Confidential Information upon request or at the conclusion of the business relationship. This is standard in most NDAs and protects both parties equally. We suggest adding the following clause:

> *"Upon written request by the Disclosing Party, the Receiving Party shall promptly return or destroy all Confidential Information (including all copies, extracts, and summaries) and shall confirm in writing that it has done so. This obligation shall not apply to copies retained in routine IT backup systems or as required by applicable law, provided such retained copies remain subject to the confidentiality obligations of this Agreement."*

**5. Duration (Section 6)**

We note that the NDA has no fixed expiry — obligations continue for as long as information qualifies as Confidential Information or trade secrets under applicable law. While we understand the rationale, it is more common in European commercial practice to define a fixed survival period (typically 3-5 years after the last disclosure). We are comfortable proceeding with the current wording, but wanted to flag this for transparency.

---

**In summary**, we are ready to sign promptly once the above points are addressed. Items 1-4 are the substantive requests; item 5 is noted for the record but is not a condition.

We look forward to finalising this so we can continue moving forward on the commercial discussions.

Kind regards,

**Lighthief Cyprus Ltd**
