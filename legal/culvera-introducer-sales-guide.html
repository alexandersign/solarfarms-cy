Sizing rule of thumb: Most Cyprus PV parks need BESS with 2–4 hours of storage at the park’s peak output. A 5 MW park typically needs 10–20 MWh of battery storage, depending on curtailment exposure and revenue targets.

  • Electrical (MV/LV cabling, earthing, protection)
  • CIF Limassol shipping + customs + transport

  • Full commissioning + DSO coordination

Excluded (Client’s responsibility)

  • EAC/DSO testing fees
  • DSO connection fees and metering
  • Land lease or acquisition
  • VAT (19%)

6. Client Qualification Checklist

Before sending us a referral, please confirm the prospective client meets these minimum criteria. This ensures we spend time only on genuine opportunities.

DO NOT REFER if any of these apply:

  • Client has no budget or cannot confirm a realistic budget range
  • Client is “just exploring” with no timeline to proceed
  • Client does not own or lease the property / site
  • For PV parks: client does not hold a valid CERA / EAC licence
  • Client expects prices significantly below the ranges in this guide
  • Client is already in discussions with Lighthief (pipeline exclusion applies)

6.1 Residential PV — Minimum Qualification

Question to Ask Qualified Answer Not Qualified
Monthly electricity bill? €100+ per month Under €80 (poor ROI)
Owns or rents property? Owns (or long-term lease 10+ yrs) Short-term rental
Roof type and condition? Concrete flat roof or tiled (good condition) Asbestos / structural issues
Budget awareness? Understands €7K–€25K range Expects it for €1,000–2,000
Decision timeline? Within 1–3 months “Maybe next year”

6.2 Commercial PV — Minimum Qualification

7. How to Refer a Client

Step 1 — Qualify

Use the checklists in Section 6. Confirm the client has a real need, realistic budget, and decision authority.

Step 2 — Send Introduction

Email alexander.papacosta@lighthief.com or WhatsApp with:

  • Client’s full name and contact details (phone and/or email)
  • Property or site address
  • Project type (residential PV, commercial PV, PV+BESS, PV park BESS)
  • Approximate monthly bill or park capacity (MW)
  • Confirmation the client has expressed interest

Step 3 — We Confirm

Lighthief will confirm acceptance or rejection within 21 days, including the tier classification for BESS/park projects.

Step 4 — We Handle Everything

Lighthief manages the full sales process, technical proposal, site survey, design, contract, installation, and commissioning. You do not need to be involved in the technical process.

Step 5 — You Get Paid

Commission is paid as the client pays us. For residential/commercial: pro rata. For parks/BESS: 30% on advance, 70% on final.
