7. Non-Competition

Section Z – Mutual Distribution Rights

Z.1 Distributor Rights and Obligations: Distributor may be entitled to distribute other BESS brands while offering Linyang products a fair opportunity to compete and maintaining separation of technical and pricing materials.

Z.2 Company Exclusivity: Company shall not appoint any other distributor, agent, reseller or representative in Cyprus during the Term and for twenty-four (24) months following termination, except termination of the agreement due to the Distributor's failure to achieve target set up in written annex and in case of for material breach of agreement.

LIGHTHIEF COMMENT — CRITICAL: Please amend Z.2 to reduce the post-termination tail from twenty-four (24) months to twelve (12) months.

REASON: Under EU Vertical Block Exemption Regulation (VBER) and Cyprus Competition Law (Cap. 149), post-term non-compete obligations are generally limited to one year.

Suggested revised wording:

Z.2 Company Exclusivity: Company shall not appoint any other distributor, agent, reseller or representative in Cyprus during the Term and for twelve (12) months following termination, except where termination is due to an uncured material breach by the Distributor. This limitation complies with EU Vertical Block Exemption Regulation (VBER) and Cyprus Competition Law (Cap. 149).
LIGHTHIEF COMMENT: Please add Z.2A – Dispute Buffer:

Z.2A Dispute Buffer: Where the Distributor disputes the validity of termination:
  • (a) the exclusivity tail continues pending final resolution of the dispute;
  • (b) the 12-month period commences only upon final resolution confirming valid termination;
  • (c) if resolved in the Distributor's favour, the Agreement is deemed to have continued uninterrupted;
  • (d) any appointments made during a disputed termination are voidable at the Distributor's option.

Z.3 Release from Non-Compete: If Company appoints another Cyprus distributor, Distributor is automatically released from all non-compete obligations.

LIGHTHIEF COMMENT: Z.3 accepted as drafted.
