## 2. BUSINESS DESCRIPTION

Lighthief Cyprus Ltd is an Engineering, Procurement, and Construction (EPC) contractor specialising in:

- Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS) — utility scale (1MW to 50MW+)
- Hybrid solar + storage installations
- Grid-connected renewable energy projects
- Long-term service and maintenance agreements (LTSA)

**Role in Supply Chain:**
- **Distributor** for Linyang (Chinese OEM) battery storage systems in Cyprus
- **EPC Contractor** for end customers (solar park owners, developers, investors)
- **Service Provider** for ongoing monitoring and maintenance

**NOT a Manufacturer:** Lighthief does not manufacture any equipment. All BESS equipment is manufactured and installed by Linyang (OEM). Lighthief provides EPC works (civil, cabling, coordination) and project management.

## 7. EXCLUSIONS AND LIMITATIONS

### What Lighthief Insurance Does NOT Need to Cover

Based on contractual protections, Lighthief's insurance policies do NOT need to include coverage for:

| Exclusion | Reason |
|-----------|--------|
| OEM manufacturing defects | Covered by Linyang's Product Liability |
| OEM installation defects | Covered by Linyang (they do installation) |
| OEM equipment performance | Covered by Linyang's Professional Indemnity |
| Battery degradation | Backed by OEM warranty reserve |
| Client misuse or modifications | Excluded from Lighthief liability |
| Force Majeure events | Excluded from all agreements |
| Third-party EMS issues | Excluded from Lighthief liability |
| Grid/utility failures | Excluded from Lighthief liability |

## 8. SPECIAL ENDORSEMENTS REQUIRED

### 8.1 Contractual Liability Extension
All policies should include coverage for contractual liability assumed under:
- Distribution Agreement with Linyang
- Sales Agreement with Linyang
- EPC Agreements with end customers
- LTSA Agreements with end customers

### 8.2 Additional Insured Status
Policies should allow adding the following as additional insureds when required:
- End customers (project owners)
- Financing institutions (banks, investors)
- Grid operator (EAC Cyprus)

### 8.3 Waiver of Subrogation
Policies should include waiver of subrogation in favour of:
- Linyang (OEM)
- End customers

### 8.4 Cross-Liability Clause
For projects with multiple parties, policies should include cross-liability coverage.
