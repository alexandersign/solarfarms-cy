Market Pain Points — Ranked by Severity

Every sales conversation should start with the client's pain. These are the five biggest problems in the Cyprus energy market.

Pain Point #1: Curtailment Crisis — 47% and Growing

YearCurtailmentEnergy Lost
2021~0%~0 GWh
20223.5%~23 GWh
202313.4%~89 GWh
202429%167 GWh
202547%~306 GWh

For a 5 MW PV park, 47% curtailment means:

  • €873K–€1M/year in lost revenue
  • €21–22M over a 25-year project life
  • System-wide: ~€58M+/year lost at €190/MWh

Sales angle: BESS captures curtailed energy at €0, discharges at peak prices (~€183/MWh). Net revenue after 86.3% RTE: ~€161/MWh.

Pain Point #2: DSO/SCADA Compliance

  • 100kWp+ = DSO Category C licensing required
  • EN 50549-2 grid code compliance mandatory
  • IEC 60870-5-104 SCADA communication
  • TSOC requires real-time telemetry, SOC/SOH reporting
  • Most operators lack in-house capability

Sales angle: Turnkey SCADA/EMS via our Voltus partnership. We remove all compliance complexity.

Pain Point #3: Net Billing Transition

  • Jan 2026: net metering replaced by market-based self-consumption
  • Exported solar earns wholesale rates (near €0 at midday)
  • Without battery: buy expensive evening power at €183/MWh
  • With battery: use own solar 24/7
  • 12,000 net-metering applications filed Dec 2025

Sales angle: Deye kits are 36–42% cheaper than Huawei. Every PV owner needs a battery now.

Pain Point #4: High Electricity Costs

  • Among the highest electricity prices in the EU
  • Residential PV payback: 3–5 years
  • Commercial self-consumption saves €0.20+/kWh
  • ~340 sunny days/year in Cyprus

Pain Point #5: No Local BESS Supply

  • Only Apollon PV (Sungrow) deployed: 3.3 MWh
  • 33 CERA licensees — 1,000+ MW — almost none deployed
  • Our 882 MWh pipeline = 267x the only operational system
  • Exclusive Linyang distribution in Cyprus
