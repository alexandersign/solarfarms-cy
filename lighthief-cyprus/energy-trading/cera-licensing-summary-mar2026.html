1. Licensing Framework Overview

Under Article 26 of the Cyprus Electricity Market Regulation Law, the following activities require a CERA license: construction/operation of power stations, electricity supply, transmission/distribution system operation, market operation, aggregation, energy storage installation/operation, and interconnection line ownership/operation.

CERA provides three tiers of authorisation:

  • General License — automatic grant via notification form (smallest installations)
  • Exemption from License — simplified application for mid-range RES / conventional plants
  • Full License — complete application process for large commercial installations

5. Application Process — General Steps

1. Prepare Application

Complete the relevant CERA application form. Attach all supporting documents (technical specs, company registration, financial capability evidence, land/site permits).

2. Pay Application Fee

Deposit/transfer the fee to one of CERA's two bank accounts. Send the receipt to regulator.cy@cera.org.cy. Registration date is determined by date of payment.

3. CERA Registration

Application is registered in CERA's Register and assigned a serial number. CERA sends an invoice and receipt to the applicant's stated email.

4. Publication & Consultation

Application is published (Official Newspaper of the Republic and/or CERA website). Interested parties may inspect and submit representations/objections within the stated period (min 30 days).

5. Examination & Decision

CERA examines the application per the Licensing Regulations (R.A.A. 538/2004) and relevant regulatory decisions. May request additional information.

6. License Granted

License issued with standard terms and conditions. Annual fees become payable to maintain validity.

General License (simplified): Submit Notification Form electronically to gl@cera.org.cy with subject "Notification Form for General License". Granted automatically 1 month after receipt unless CERA requests corrections.

6. RES System Open Market

The Cyprus electricity market is transitioning from a single-buyer model (EAC) to a competitive market. Under the Detailed Planning regulatory decision (RAA 164/2015), the following participants will operate in the new market:

  • Cyprus Transmission System Operator (CTSO)
  • Market Operator
  • Distribution System Operator (DSO / EAC)
  • Producers with thermal units (> 5MW on distribution system, or on transmission system)
  • RES stations operating outside national grant schemes
  • Aggregators (up to 20MW total RES served)
  • Retailers and Wholesalers
  • Balance Responsible Parties

Key Amendment Rules

  • Technology type changes are not allowed (RES to conventional or vice versa)
  • RES technology sub-type changes are not allowed (e.g. wind to PV)
  • Manufacturer/model changes are allowed
  • Parcel expansion to adjacent land may be allowed (case-by-case)
  • Operating regime changes not allowed (commercial to own-use or vice versa)
  • Extension requests must be submitted ≥ 3 months before expiry
  • Post-expiry extension requests are not accepted (Decision 95/2017)
