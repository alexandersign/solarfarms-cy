Applications

What BESS Does for You

Grid Services & Frequency Regulation

Earn revenue providing frequency containment reserves (FCR), balancing energy, and voltage support to the TSO/DSO. Response time <200 ms from IEC 104 dispatch command.

FCRaFRRVoltage Support

Solar Firming & Curtailment Recovery

Store excess daytime solar generation and dispatch at evening peak or on grid request. Eliminates curtailment losses and maximises PPA revenue for co-located PV assets.

PV + BESSCurtailmentPeak Shift

Energy Arbitrage

Charge during low-price hours, discharge during high-price periods. DisperonEMS integrates with day-ahead and intraday market data to optimise charge/discharge cycles automatically.

DAMIntradayPrice Signal

C&I Peak Shaving

Reduce maximum demand charges for commercial buildings, hotels, factories and data centres. Deye MS-GS215 with on-site PV delivers 24/7 power reliability and bill savings.

Demand ChargeC&IHotel / Factory

Full off-grid and islanded operation for remote sites, islands, or grid-independent microgrids. Generator integration (STS, dry contacts) included as standard — no additional inverter required.
