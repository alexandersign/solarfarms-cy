## 1. Market Pain Points — Ranked by Severity

Understanding why clients need us is the foundation of every sales conversation. These are the five biggest pain points in the Cyprus energy market today.

### 1.1 Curtailment Crisis — 47% and Growing

Cyprus solar curtailment has escalated from 0% in 2021 to 47% in 2025 (~306 GWh lost). For a 5 MW PV park, this means approximately €873K–€1M per year in lost revenue. Over a 25-year project life, that is €21–22M of stranded value.

**Sales angle:** Every PV park owner with curtailment is a BESS prospect. BESS captures curtailed energy at €0 and discharges at peak prices (~€183/MWh). Net revenue after 86.3% round-trip efficiency: ~€161/MWh.

### 1.2 DSO/SCADA Compliance Burden

Installations above 100 kWp require DSO Category C licensing, EN 50549-2 grid code compliance, and IEC 60870-5-104 SCADA communication. Most PV operators do not have the in-house capability to manage this. TSOC requires real-time telemetry, remote curtailment signal compliance, and SOC/SOH reporting for utility-scale BESS.

**Sales angle:** We handle the full SCADA/EMS integration through our Voltus partnership. Turnkey compliance is a differentiator that removes complexity for the client.

### 1.3 Net Billing Transition (January 2026)

The shift from net metering to market-based self-consumption means homeowners and commercial operators now face time-of-use pricing. Without battery storage, exported solar earns wholesale rates (often €0 during peak solar hours due to curtailment). With storage, they consume their own solar and avoid buying expensive grid power in the evening.

**Sales angle:** Every homeowner with a PV system installed before 2026 needs a battery retrofit. Every new installation should include storage. Our Deye net billing kits are 36–42% cheaper than the Huawei alternative.

### 1.4 High Electricity Costs

Cyprus has among the highest electricity prices in the EU — a residential PV system pays back in 3–5 years. Commercial electricity costs make solar+storage economically compelling at almost any scale.

**Sales angle:** ROI calculators should be the centrepiece of every commercial proposal. Show the payback period, not just the system cost.

### 1.5 No Local BESS Supply Chain

Before Lighthief, Cyprus had effectively zero utility-scale BESS deployed. Apollon PV (Sungrow) commissioned the first system at just 3.3 MWh in May 2025. Our 882 MWh pipeline is 267x larger than the only operational system in the country.

**Sales angle:** We are the only company in Cyprus with a proven BESS supply chain — exclusive Linyang distribution, Kehua PCS, Voltus EMS, and a 51-park portfolio under contract.
