Cyprus is rapidly building its renewable energy infrastructure. Property investors and real estate companies are ideally positioned to participate:

  • Land Utilisation: Agricultural or underused land converted to solar/BESS revenue

Referral Commission Structure

  • PV Development Referral: Connect landowners or investors to solar farm opportunities
  • Equipment Volume Referral: Refer installers or developers who purchase equipment — earn 2% on first-year purchases
  • Ongoing Relationship: Annual recurring commissions on O&M contracts

Real estate companies with investor networks can generate €5,000–€50,000+ in referral commissions annually — with zero capital commitment.

How We Work With Developers

A streamlined process from first meeting to commissioned systems — we handle everything.

1. Discovery & Site Assessment
Meet with your development team. Review architectural plans, roof areas, electrical infrastructure. Identify optimal solar and storage solutions per project.
2. Technical Design & Proposal
Detailed system design with equipment selection, energy yield modelling, ROI projections. Volume pricing tailored to your project scale.
3. Permitting & Approvals
Handle EAC applications, building permit coordination, grid connection paperwork. Net-metering registration per unit for apartment buildings.
4. Equipment Procurement & Staging
Source equipment from our 7SUN warehouse or direct EU delivery. Kit preparation and quality check at Ypsonas depot before site delivery.
5. Installation & Commissioning
Our certified installation crews handle mounting, wiring, inverter setup, grid connection, and system testing. Coordinate with your construction schedule.
6. Handover & O&M
System documentation, monitoring setup, warranty registration. Optional long-term O&M contract for ongoing performance assurance.
