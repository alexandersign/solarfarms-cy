System in brief (Deye route)

This option supplies a ~75 kWp photovoltaic array using REC Alpha Pure-R 410 W modules, paired with a Deye 215 kWh commercial battery system that includes integrated power conversion and solar MPPT (MS-GS215 class). A Deye MS-EMS station controller coordinates operation; the automatic switching board interfaces grid (when available), battery, and your backup generator (generator set supplied and connected by the client per our design). During the day, solar powers the site and charges the battery; overnight the battery discharges; when solar and stored energy are insufficient, the generator starts automatically according to the control strategy. The layout is suitable for later grid connection subject to EAC approval and protection upgrades.
