7. Client Acknowledgment — Regulatory Status

A. System Classification

The Client acknowledges and understands the following:

  1. The System is installed as a private self-consumption installation operating in zero-export mode. No energy is intended to be exported to the EAC distribution network at any time.
  2. No application has been submitted to the Electricity Authority of Cyprus (EAC) or any Distribution System Operator (DSO) for grid connection approval, Net Billing participation, or any other form of feed-in authorization for this System.
  3. This decision was made at the Client's express request. The Contractor has advised the Client that under Cyprus Distribution Rules and the RES Technical Guide (Edition 2023.2 / 2025), any generating installation operating in parallel with the distribution network — regardless of export setting — requires prior DSO approval, technical submission, and inspection before energization.
  4. The Client has elected to proceed without DSO authorization, having been fully informed of the regulatory requirements and the consequences of non-compliance.

B. Regulatory Risks

The Client acknowledges that operating a photovoltaic system without DSO/EAC authorization carries the following risks:

Risk Description
Non-compliance notice EAC may issue a formal notice requiring immediate disconnection of the system
Supply disconnection EAC may disconnect or seal the meter supply to protect the network
Future application refusal EAC may refuse Net Billing or other RES applications for this installation until compliance is achieved
Contractor reporting The licensed electrical contractor may be reported to ETEK or EAC for non-compliant installation
Administrative penalties Fines or penalties may be imposed under Cyprus electricity law for unauthorized parallel generation
Insurance implications The Client's property or liability insurance may be affected if the installation is found to be non-compliant

C. Client Obligations

The Client agrees to the following obligations:

  1. Not to modify the zero-export settings or any protection/safety configuration of the system without prior written approval from the Contractor.
  2. Not to tamper with, bypass, or disable the export limitation equipment, current transformers (CTs), or any hardware safety interlocks installed as part of this system.
  3. To inform the Contractor immediately if contacted by EAC, the DSO, CERA, or any regulatory authority regarding this installation.
  4. To bear sole responsibility for any regulatory penalties, fines, disconnection costs, or remediation works arising from the absence of DSO authorization.
  5. Not to hold the Contractor liable for any consequences arising from the Client's decision to operate the system without DSO/EAC authorization, provided the system has been installed in accordance with applicable electrical safety standards and the zero-export configuration is functioning as designed.
  6. To maintain the diesel generator interlock and transfer switch in proper working order and not to modify the electrical topology without the Contractor's involvement.

D. Safety Measures Installed

The Contractor confirms that the following safety measures have been designed and shall be installed to ensure the system operates safely and does not export energy to the distribution network:

# Safety Measure Description
1 Zero-export controller CT-based real-time power measurement at the point of common coupling (PCC), dynamically limiting inverter output to prevent any grid export
2 Hardware fail-safe Independent reverse power relay and/or AC contactor that physically disconnects the PV system if export is detected or if the zero-export controller loses communication
3 Generator interlock Automatic transfer switch (ATS) and control logic ensuring proper coordination between grid supply, PV system, and diesel generator — preventing uncontrolled parallel operation
4 Anti-islanding protection Active loss-of-mains detection on all inverters, preventing the system from energizing the grid during a power outage
5 DC and AC isolation Lockable isolators on all DC string inputs and AC inverter outputs, allowing complete physical disconnection for maintenance or emergency
6 Surge protection Type 1+2 surge protection on DC side and Type 2 on AC side
7 Labeling and signage All switchgear labeled per EN 60204-1, including dual-supply warnings at the main distribution board

E. Future Regularization

The Client acknowledges that:

  1. Regularization is possible. Should the Client wish to bring the installation into full DSO compliance at a future date, the Contractor can assist with preparing and submitting a Net Billing application to EAC, including all required documentation, protection settings, and inspection coordination.
  2. Regularization is a separate service and will be quoted independently. It typically involves additional equipment (ripple control receiver), EAC inspection fees, and a processing timeline of 2–6 months.
  3. The Contractor has maintained full installation documentation (single line diagram, protection settings, commissioning records, test certificates) to facilitate future regularization if required.
