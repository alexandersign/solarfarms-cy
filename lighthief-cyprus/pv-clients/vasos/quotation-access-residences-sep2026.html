1. Project Understanding

Lighthief Cyprus Ltd is pleased to submit this technical and commercial proposal for the photovoltaic system at The Access Residences, Limassol — comprising Blocks A1 and A2.

The system is required to satisfy the project's Energy Performance Certificate (EPC), which specifies a minimum installed PV capacity of 38.5 kWp, based on a reference area of 255 m² at 15% module efficiency. Using modern high-efficiency N-Type modules (≥ 22.9% cell efficiency), the corresponding required panel area is approximately 170 m².

EPC Compliance Achieved: Our proposed 66-panel array occupies exactly 170.0 m² of roof area (66 × 2.28 m × 1.13 m), delivering 38.94 kWp — meeting and marginally exceeding the 38.5 kWp EPC threshold.

The currently allocated roof area across both blocks is approximately 120 m². To reach the required 170 m², we propose utilising additional available roof surfaces including staircase roof areas and roofs above enclosed roof-garden structures, as indicated in the updated roof drawings. Our preliminary panel layout demonstrates how this can be achieved while maintaining adequate access and maintenance clearances.

4. Preliminary Panel Layout

The following layout is based on the architectural roof drawings provided. Panel rows run E–W (perpendicular to the building's N25°E long axis), with panels tilted at 25° facing due south. The configuration uses 3 rows × 1 panel deep per row in each block, with zero inter-row spacing.

Location Description Panels Area (m²) Capacity (kWp)
Block A2 — Main flat roof (NE portion) 11 panels wide (E–W) × 3 rows deep (N–S), south-facing, 25° tilt. Positioned on the upper flat section of Block A2 as indicated in the architectural drawing, clear of staircase and MEP equipment. 33 77 19.47
Block A1 — Main flat roof (NW portion) 11 panels wide (E–W) × 3 rows deep (N–S), south-facing, 25° tilt. Positioned in the upper flat section of Block A1 as indicated in the architectural drawing, clear of the staircase enclosure and roof access. 33 77 19.47
Total — Blocks A1 + A2 Combined 66 154 m² 38.94 kWp
Layout method — 3 rows × 1 panel deep @ 25° tilt, zero inter-row spacing: Each of the 3 rows is a single panel deep (2.28m inclined), tilted at 25° facing south. Back height per row = 0.96m (within the 1.20m architectural constraint). Shadow from the top of each row lands exactly at the base of the next row at 10am on 21 December — the mathematical zero-gap condition. No inter-row spacing required. Total N–S footprint per block: 3 × (2.28 × cos 25°) = 6.19m. Total E–W footprint: 11 × 1.13m = 12.43m. Total footprint per block: 77 m² — comfortably within the main flat roof areas indicated in the architectural drawing.
Confirmed on architectural drawing: Panel array positions match the zones allocated for PV on the roof drawings provided by the Architect (Roof Block A1 and Roof Block A2). Final exact positions, row offsets from parapets and MEP clearances to be confirmed at site survey. A panel layout drawing to be issued by Lighthief Cyprus's ETEK Electrical Engineer (Costas Hadjikyriacou) as part of the EAC application.
