## Summary & Observations

1. **Current portfolio: 6.000 MW** across 9 applications, plus a future ~90 MW project not yet filed.

2. **Two companies, one controller:** Both PENTASOL LTD and SOLAREVO LTD appear to be controlled by or connected to Demetris Andreou via SunraysTreat Ltd. Corporate structure and beneficial ownership need verification.

3. **Paramytha site is the anchor asset** — 4.450 MW on a single plot is the main value. The 3.95 MW application alone represents 66% of the current portfolio.

4. **Ormideia is fragmented** — 7 small applications (60 kW to 500 kW) spread across 6 plots. This suggests rooftop or small ground-mount systems rather than a utility-scale park.

5. **All applications submitted 17–19 Dec 2025** — very recent. Only one (App 5961, Plot 99, 0.300 MW) has been officially registered in the CERA Licenses Registry. The remaining 8 are still pending review.

6. **Application type is "Exemption"** — these are applications for exemption from holding a Construction License (for RES under 5 MW), not full construction licenses. This is the lighter regulatory path. The future 90 MW project would need a full license.

7. **No energy storage** on any current application.
