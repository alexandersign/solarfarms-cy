6.2 Sequencing — What Must Happen First

  1. CERA approves the PV exemption applications (currently pending for 8 of 9)
  2. PV parks are built and connected to the grid (DSO/EAC connection agreement needed)
  3. BESS applied for under Category B — CERA notification + DSO technical approval
  4. BESS installed under planning permit exemption (Gazette 5992/2026 conditions)
Bottom line: BESS cannot be installed until the PV parks are licensed and operational. These parks are still at Step 1. Timeline to BESS-readiness is likely 12–24 months from now.

7. Environmental Permits — Seller's Exemption Claim

7.1 The Claim

The seller states that because the parks are small, they should be exempt from environmental permits.

7.2 Assessment

FactorAnalysisVerdict
EU EIA Directive (2011/92/EU) Solar PV falls under Annex II (projects requiring screening). Member states set their own screening thresholds. Framework
Cyprus EIA Law (N. 127(I)/2018) Projects listed in Schedule II require EIA screening. Solar PV installations are included. The competent authority (Environment Department) decides on a case-by-case basis whether a full EIA is needed. Screening required
Small-scale threshold Cyprus does not have a blanket MW-based exemption from environmental screening for solar PV. However, very small installations (typically residential/commercial rooftop under net-metering) are generally exempt. Ground-mount solar on agricultural/rural land — even at 60 kW — may require at minimum an environmental screening opinion (Γνωμάτευση Τμήματος Περιβάλλοντος). Partially true
Location sensitivity Both sites (Paramytha, Ormideia) are rural. If near Natura 2000 sites, wetlands, or protected areas, EIA screening is mandatory regardless of size. Ormideia is near Larnaca Salt Lake SPA — proximity needs verification. Verify
"Exemption" application type The CERA application type used ("Exemption from obligation to hold a Construction License – RES") is a licensing exemption, not an environmental exemption. These are separate regulatory streams. Different thing
Conclusion on environmental permits: The seller's claim is partially correct but misleading. While small PV installations face a lighter regulatory path, there is no automatic exemption from environmental screening in Cyprus for ground-mount solar on rural/agricultural land. The CERA "exemption" these parks applied for is a construction licensing exemption (under 5 MW), not an environmental one. An environmental screening opinion from the Environment Department may still be required, particularly for:
  • The 3.95 MW Paramytha site (significant scale for a single ground-mount installation)
  • Any site near environmentally sensitive areas (Ormideia/Larnaca Salt Lake)
Recommendation: Request evidence of environmental screening clearance or formal exemption letters from the Environment Department for each site.
