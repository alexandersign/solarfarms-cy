Executive Summary

We are evaluating the acquisition of the Avdellero 45 MW PV project in Larnaca as a Lighthief-developed asset. We buy the RTB/SPV, self-perform the full EPC (PV + BESS), and operate long-term. An equity partner provides €25M for 25% of the SPV. Total project cost is €73.5M. The project generates €10.55M EBITDA/year from Day 1 (current DAM prices, 15% aggregator).

Two loan structures are analysed: amortising (pay principal + interest throughout) and balloon/interest-only (pay interest only, return principal at term end — roll or refinance). For the equity partner, three payment structures are modelled: waterfall (all cash first), 8% interest-only with balloon, and 8% amortising. All six combinations are costed and compared on DSCR, developer residual, and equity partner IRR.

Two structures are available for each facility. Amortising: pay principal + interest throughout (no end exposure). Balloon (interest-only): pay only interest during the term; full principal returned at maturity — can be refinanced, rolled, or repaid from project sale proceeds.

8. Key Risks and Open Items

Balloon repayment risk (Scenarios B1–B3, A2): If balloon structure is chosen, a Y15 refinancing is required for €25M (EP) and/or €33.9M (AB). Mitigation: (1) by Y15, project has 15yr DAM track record — highly bankable; (2) accumulate reserves from surplus cash flow during term; (3) project sale or partial disposal generates capital. Plan the exit strategy at deal inception.
EAC substation (€7.2M) ownership: Confirm whether asset remains on SPV books (depreciable) or transfers to EAC after construction. This affects tax shield and balance sheet.
Agri-PV planning (13.7 MW, 4 parks): Expected in ~6 months. Phase construction: Solar PV 31.5 MW first, Agri-PV follows. Revenue model already uses 50% curtailment so additional MW are upside.
DAM price assumption: Model uses current 339-day TSOC sample (Oct 2025–Sep 2026). Cyprus average €200/MWh, evening peak €212/MWh, daytime €151/MWh. This is real market data — conservative scenario has been dropped. Monitor DAM quarterly.
EP preferred return vs pure equity: If EP insists on a legal debt instrument (shareholder loan) rather than equity for the 8% return, interest becomes tax-deductible in the SPV, saving ~€300K/yr in CIT. Structure accordingly.
PV EPC procurement cost: Model uses €0.50/Wp (Lighthief self-perform reference). Actual procurement should be confirmed — if panels/labour come in lower, CAPEX drops and your equity requirement falls.

9. Recommendation for Arkadiusz

Primary: Scenario A2 — AB Amortising + EP 8% Interest-Only
  • EP gets clean 8%/yr yield — straightforward to negotiate
  • Developer gets €4,635K/yr from Day 1 (best ongoing cash in amortising group)
  • DSCR of 2.08× — very comfortable coverage
  • Single balloon: €25M to EP at Y15 → refinance from mature bankable asset
  • Developer payback: 3.2 years
  • From Y16: all FCFE free (both AB and EP capital resolved), ~€6–7M/yr
Alternative: Scenario A3 — AB Amortising + EP 8% Amortising
  • Zero balloon exposure — simplest legal structure
  • EP guaranteed 8% IRR, capital fully returned by Y15
  • DSCR 1.76× — solid but lower than A2
  • Developer cash €3,714K/yr — slightly lower than A2
  • From Y16: complete clean slate (EP capital repaid, AB repaid)
  • Best if EP requires certainty of capital return without refinancing risk
Bottom line: The project works under all six structures. Choose A2 for maximum developer cash flow with manageable balloon risk. Choose A3 if you want the cleanest deal with no refinancing discussions in 15 years. Both scenarios give the equity partner a fair, market-rate return (8% p.a.) and give Lighthief a 100%+ IRR on its invested capital.
