Key Risks and Open Items

EAC substation (€7.2M) — UNCONFIRMED: KPMG factsheet states EAC will build a new transmission substation in the area. Confirm with seller (K. Ellinas / G.P. Rodik Services Ltd) whether this €7.2M is in the SPV CAPEX. If yes, total CAPEX rises to €71.4M.
Agri-PV planning permits (13.7 MW): Avdellero 2, 3, 8, 11 are pending town planning permits. All 4 have CERA licence + environmental approval. No building permit applications submitted yet. Consider phased construction: build the 31.5 MW Solar PV first, then add Agri-PV when permits arrive.
Land leases — Agri-PV not signed: Annual rental cost €40K/yr for Agri-PV sub-projects is estimated (PV at €80K/yr is signed and paid). Lease negotiation ongoing.
BESS CIF — 3 parks derived: Avdellero 2, 6, 8 use a decomposed price (3 BESS containers + T4 skid). Variance ±5% vs formal Linyang quotation for these specific configs. Request formal quote from Linyang before signing term sheet.
DAM price sensitivity: Conservative scenario (4.5% partner IRR) uses frozen Feb 2026 rates. Current DAM (Sep 2026 avg ~€200/MWh) gives 6.5% partner IRR. 5-year average DAM price will be the real determinant. Consider PPA hedging.
BESS replacement at Y15: Assumed €13.7M at technology-reduced future cost (~€70K/MWh). Actual replacement cost will depend on battery market in 2041. New debt at then-prevailing rates.
