1. Executive Summary

The Lighthief group is structured as a Cyprus holding chain (BSH → Lighthief International → Lighthief Cyprus) with an active Polish operating company (Lighthief Sp. z o.o.) and several dormant or pre-revenue SPVs. Substantive revenue and balance-sheet scale sit in Poland; Cyprus entities serve as holding, contracting, and BESS/solar EPC platform.
